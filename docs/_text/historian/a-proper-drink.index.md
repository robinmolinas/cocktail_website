# Index: *A Proper Drink* (Robert Simonson, 2016)

The history of the cocktail revival, from the Rainbow Room in 1987 to the mid-2010s: the bartenders, the bars and the modern classics they made. It's written by a New York Times drinks writer from dozens of dated interviews (listed on p. 327–333, "People and dates"). **That makes its quotes a source for motives on the record:** what a bartender said about why they made a drink counts as fact, cited to the page.

- **Text:** `a-proper-drink.md` (PDFKit text layer, clean; the contents pages p. 11–13 are garbled, so use this index instead).
- **Cite:** `A Proper Drink, p. N`, using the printed page from `=== pdf N | printed M ===` (printed = pdf − 14).
- **Tier:** reliable secondary (journalism from first-hand interviews). The primary tier is for pre-revival history (Oxford Companion, Imbibe!); this book is the best source the library has for **drinks created after about 1985 and the people who made them**.
- **Search:** `library.py search "Penicillin" --book proper` · `library.py page proper 170 --printed`

## Why it matters to the studio
- **People as mirrors** (Robin, 2026-09-26): every modern classic here has a named creator, a bar, a year and often a reason in the creator's own words. That makes it good ground for "the person it was made by, or for".
- **Tomás:** each recipe page gives the creator's original spec in ounces. Convert it, then balance it with `balance.py`.
