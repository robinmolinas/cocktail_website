# The original journey film (retired 2026-10-17)

`journey.webm` (VP9, 2560×1440) and `journey.mp4` (H.264, 1920×1080) are the
15 s film that shipped until 2026-10-17. They were replaced by the "eased both"
cut after the designer playtest. To go back, copy both files into
`dionysus-experience/public/` and restore the old timings in `TheDepths.tsx`:
holds 0.6, 1.5, 3.1, 5.7, 8.2, 9.4, 10.7 and 11.9 s, with `SURFACE_CUT` at 12.95.
