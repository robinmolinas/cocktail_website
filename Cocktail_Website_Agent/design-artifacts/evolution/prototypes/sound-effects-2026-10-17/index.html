Your name

Typing a name, then Enter. The first pass sounds only the first letter and cuts the seal at its loudest point, 0.82 s in, when the next question arrives.

Quick pairs (H4)

Four quick choices, left and right. The first pass retunes each one to the chord, so the note jumps when the harmony turns. The new pass keeps her note, D#, which every chord holds, and leans a little to the side she tapped.

Gathering words (H5)

Three words gathered, one taken back, another gathered. In the new pass each gathered word climbs a tone of the chord sounding, so you hear the world filling up. Taking one back sinks.

Flavours, then leaving out (H6)

Two flavours, sealed; then two left out, sealed. The first pass uses one sound for choosing, sealing and leaving out, and the final seal is cut by the next stage.

Writing the sentence (H7)

A sentence typed in the hush, then sealed. The first pass brushes once every 1.6 s whatever she types. The new pass answers every stroke, softer as she speeds up, fuller after each pause.

The four sounds alone

At journey level they are very quiet on their own; the button lifts them so you can hear their shape.
