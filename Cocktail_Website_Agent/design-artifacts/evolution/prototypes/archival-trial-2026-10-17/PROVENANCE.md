# Archival image trial: explorer-ruler ("Far Enough")

Designer review 2026-10-17, Batch 3: one archival image per true story, trialled on one pour first. Robin liked it and asked to keep it, with every claim checked by Hester.

- **File:** `Nimrod_South_9_Jan_09-original.jpg` (1384×872, greyscale), from Wikimedia Commons, [File:Nimrod South 9 Jan 09.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nimrod_South_9_Jan_09.jpg). It was scanned from the plate in Shackleton's *The Heart of the Antarctic* (William Heinemann, London).
- **What it shows:** the southern party at their furthest south, 88°23′S, 9 January 1909, with the Union flag. Shackleton's own book captions the plate "Farthest South, January 9, 1909". Four men went south and one took the photograph. Sources disagree on who is in the frame, so the caption names nobody.
- **Verified:** it is the same exposure as the plate in the 1911 edition (Project Gutenberg #69138, img_pg_149), compared by eye on 2026-10-17. The Commons scan is a tighter crop.
- **Rights:**
  - **US:** public domain, published 1909, before 1931.
  - **UK/EU: likely expired, but not proven.** The photographer is unsettled; Marshall is most often named, and he died in 1963. The Copyright Act 1911, which still governs photographs taken before 1 June 1957, ran 50 years from the negative, so the copyright ended in 1959. However, the 1995 Duration Regulations could have revived it to life + 70 (to 2033) if a known author's work was still protected elsewhere in the EEA in 1995. That hinges on who took it. Commons lists the file as public domain. For a public launch, either get a rights opinion or confirm the photographer.
- **Rejected:** `rejected-Shackleton_nimrod_42.jpg` (AWI archive, 3488×3016). Hester found it is a different exposure from the published plate, so "published in 1909" can't be shown for that negative. Commons also names Shackleton as photographer while he is in the frame.
- **In the app:** `public/archival/explorer-ruler.jpg` (1384w) and `-900.jpg`. No tint, no grain, no AI retouching.
- **Hester audit:** caption and alt pass; credit corrected to the book. See `hester-audit-2026-10-17.json`.

## Decisions (Robin, 2026-10-17)

They do not apply to a real archival photograph: this plate is the record of the moment the story tells ("planted the flag"). Archival photos are still bound by everything else in the Sonnet search prompt.
