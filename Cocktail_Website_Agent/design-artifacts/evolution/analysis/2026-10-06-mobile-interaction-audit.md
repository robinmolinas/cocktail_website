# Dionysus experience: mobile interaction audit (2026-10-06)

Read-only audit of the committed app at `c6a48cd` (branch `experience-revamp-2026-09`). It ran against a frozen git worktree served on :5181, not the live :5180 dev server. No app source was changed. Screenshots are in `2026-10-06-audit-shots/`. Names follow `<hold>-<viewport>.png`. `390rm` means the 390 reduced-motion run.

## 1. Method

| Run | Viewport | Input | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1440 | 1440×900, DPR 1 | mouse + keyboard | full uninterrupted run, plus a keyboard probe |
| 375 | 375×667 (iPhone SE), `isMobile`, `hasTouch`, DPR 2 | `touchscreen.tap`, CDP touch drag | full uninterrupted run |
| 390 | 390×844 | same | full run, plus probes (background, accidental taps, landscape) |
| 430 | 430×932 | same | full run |
| 390rm | 390×844, `reducedMotion: reduce` | same | full run |

- **Full run:** landing → "Discover my cocktail" → H1 lines (waited out, not skipped) → name → lens → H2 → H3 (5 real touch drags, to 15/85/50/30/95%) → H4 practice + 9 pairs → H5 ×2 → H6 flavours + leave-out → H7 typed trace → H8 → H9 → H10, scrolled to the bottom with synthesized touch scrolls.
- **H4 timing:** each pair was tapped after a realistic delay counted from when it became visible: 1.2, 1.8, 2.5, 0.9, 3.2, 1.5, 4.0, 1.1 and 2.0 s.
- **DevNav:** hidden with CSS during full runs. The probes used it to jump to holds, and it is visible in the landscape shots. It is dev-only.
- **Backgrounding:** emulated with CDP `Page.setWebLifecycleState frozen` for 5 s. This stops timers and rAF the way a backgrounded mobile tab does.
- **Rotation:** emulated by swapping the viewport to 844×390 on the 390 phone.
- **Console:** 0 errors and 0 warnings across all five runs and both probes.
- **Naming note:** the landing CTA in the build reads "Discover my cocktail". "The Threshold" only appears as the Velvet Rope label.

**Not verified:** the iOS virtual keyboard (Playwright does not emulate it), real Safari/WebKit (only Chromium was used), real-thumb ergonomics, and audio. Contrast judgements are visual, from the screenshots, at the frames captured.

## 4. Top 8 issues, ranked

1. **H3 commits on any touch-up** (major, all phones). A single tap, a vertical scroll attempt, or a tap on the pole word records a gravity value and advances. There is no confirm, and the back dot is a ~23×27 target. Evidence: probe C, `h3-gravity-committed-390`.
2. **H4 backgrounding silently records a "secret"** (major). After a 5 s freeze the pair is expired on return and the next one starts (probe A, `h4-after-background-390`). Anyone who takes a notification mid-H4 alters their own hidden-self data without knowing.
3. **H2 colour names are hover/focus-only** (major, every touch device). Phone users pick a named liqueur ("Pamplemousse Rosé") without ever seeing its name until after the commit, and that name returns in the breath and the reading (`h2-seed-390`).
4. **H5 Q1 hint printed over the Comfort sphere at 375×667** (major). Both texts become illegible once 3 are chosen (`h5-q1-chosen-375`). Q0 and H1 lens also touch the head at that size (`h5-q0-375`, `h1-lens-375`).
5. **Landscape mid-journey breaks H5 (blocker for that orientation) and H6.** The cluster is clipped at the top, sits over the question, and overlaps Continue. The rope only guards the threshold, so a mid-journey rotation lands here (`h5-landscape-390`, `h6-landscape-390`).
6. **H4 blind taps register** (major). A tap at opacity 0.04 at the start of a pair recorded an answer (probe B). The practice embers are also tappable during their invisible reading beat (code-derived).
7. **Nav mark exits the journey with no confirmation** (major consequence, low likelihood). It is live on every hold, sits top-left, and is 106×24.
8. **Sub-44 px targets and small clipping** (minor, cumulative): every "Continue" pill is 142×40, "Let them cool" 182×40, "Start again" 63×29, back dots ~23×27. Also the H7 single-line trace hides its own start, an H8 echo is clipped at the left edge at ≤390, and the H10 body text scrolls under the unscrimmed nav mark.

Positives worth keeping:

- **H4 is catchable with a thumb at every phone size** (9/9, 145–176 px targets at mid-screen, window effectively 4.25 s).
- **H6's rising flavours are catchable** (3/3).
- **Reduced motion gives a clock-free H4** ("Let them cool") and a still H6.
- **Desktop keyboard reaches every hold.** Exceptions: H3 needs one Tab on arrival, and H4 loses focus every pair.
- **No console errors.**

Unverified: the iOS keyboard overlap at H1/H7, WebKit rendering of `backdrop-filter`/`color-mix`, and Tab order on H5/H6 from a clean focus start. In H6 the off-band flavour buttons are opacity 0 but still focusable (code-derived).
