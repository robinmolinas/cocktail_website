## Per-hold fine-design (locked hold by hold)

### H1 · The Threshold — LOCKED 2026-06-12 ("The Quiet Depths")

- After "Cross the Threshold": screen sinks to black, opens on the suspended amber depths (S1 ~0.6s frame, breathing hold).
- Two cold-open lines, fading in one at a time (Robin cut the duration line and the name preamble — keep it simple):
  1. *"Somewhere, a cocktail that does not exist yet is waiting to be made."*
  2. *"There are no right answers — only honest ones."*
- Name: bare text input in the existing prototype's style — single glowing line, quiet placeholder, no preamble copy. Each keystroke releases one tiny bubble that drifts up and freezes.
- Lens ("Who should this cocktail capture?" ×6): six pale glass bubbles drifting almost imperceptibly among the frozen ones, each holding a phrase. Hover: bubble swells and brightens. Choose: the other five dissolve upward as fizz; the chosen one sinks toward the viewer.
- No game mechanics here — H1 is the calm; orientation through atmosphere, not instruction. Ambiguity total (user must not yet know they're inside a drink).

### H2 · The Seed — LOCKED 2026-06-12 ("The First Drop")

- Option A chosen, kept simple: **8 fixed drops, no drag-blend** — but each colour is anchored to a real pour (Robin's call): Campari Red `#c8102e`, Aperol Orange `#ff6f1f`, Galliano Gold `#f2c24e`, Midori Green `#58b947`, Curaçao Blue `#1287c8`, Violette Purple `#7b5aa6`, Pamplemousse Pink `#f2789f`, Cassis Plum `#5c2447`.

- **Touch = commit**: the drop bursts (scale + blur + ripple ring in its colour), the others dissolve, the grade deepens and stays for the rest of the journey. Then time un-freezes: footage 1.5s → 3.1s, freezing on the interlocked clouds (the H3 Gravity hold).

**Rev 2 (2026-06-12, Robin's review):**
1. **Droplets, not candy** — the opaque glossy spheres read cartoonish against the footage. Redesigned as droplets *of the world*: translucent glass that refracts the footage behind them (backdrop blur + saturate), the colour glowing inside (tinted core gradient), liqueur-tinted rim, varied sizes (0.86×–1.12×) so the ring feels found, not arranged.

3. Question stays at H2 (not moved to the end): it is the moment that teaches *answering changes the world*, and the early instinctive read is what the LLM wants.
