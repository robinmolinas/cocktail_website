## Technique Execution Results

### 1. Metaphor Mapping

* **Interactive Focus:** Connecting Suminagashi printmaking (dropping, swirling, lifting) to the 6-page quiz flow.
* **Key Breakthrough:** "The Liquid Alchemist's Glass" — bridging abstract ink marbling with concrete mixology steps (pouring, stirring, serving) inside a glassware silhouette.

#### Ideas Generated:

**[UX Motion #1]**: The Liquid Alchemist's Glass
_Concept_: The quiz progress is visualized inside a floating glassware silhouette. Background questions drop colored, layered liquids into the silhouette, while trait sliders stir and marble the fluids like Suminagashi ink, culminating in the liquid bleeding into watercolor paper to reveal the final hand-painted cocktail.
_Novelty_: Blends abstract Japanese marbling directly with the physical steps of drink crafting (pouring, stirring, serving) inside a shifting glassware canvas.

---

### 2. Reversal Inversion

* **Interactive Focus:** Inverting progress bar conventions to invent a better progress visualization.
* **Key Breakthrough:** "The Ink Wash Clearing" — screen starts flooded in dark sumi ink; each completed page washes away a layer, revealing paper beneath. Selected by client.

#### Ideas Generated:

**[UX Motion #2]**: The Ink Wash Clearing *(Selected)*
_Concept_: Screen starts completely flooded in dark sumi ink. Each completed page washes away a layer, gradually revealing bright watercolor paper beneath. By the final page, the canvas is clean and ready for the cocktail to be "printed" onto it.
_Novelty_: Progress = removal, not addition. Uncovering who you are rather than building it.

**[UX Motion #3]**: The Descent into the Glass
_Concept_: Camera descends through cocktail layers (garnish → foam → spirit → ice → amber). Progress = going deeper. User becomes the liquid.
_Novelty_: Progress = going deeper, not higher.

**[UX Motion #4]**: The Ice Carve
_Concept_: Ice block sculpted/melted away to reveal cocktail glass frozen inside. Progress = sculpting away what doesn't belong.
_Novelty_: The cocktail was always there; you just unearth it.

**[UX Motion #5]**: The Ingredient Constellation
_Concept_: Scattered ingredients drift inward centripetally, converge, and fuse, releasing a burst of sumi-e ink.
_Novelty_: Progress is centripetal, moving from chaos to focus.

---

### 3. SCAMPER Refinement (On "The Ink Wash Clearing")

* **Interactive Focus:** Polishing, detailing, and layering the selected "Ink Wash Clearing" concept using mixology metaphors.
* **Key Breakthrough:** Integrating fluid density strata, cursor bar-spoon physics, and a final "overflow reveal" to make the clearing tactile and dramatic.

#### Ideas Generated:

**[UX Motion #6]**: The Clearing Glass (Combine)
_Concept_: Dark sumi ink floods the entire screen. A faint, translucent cocktail glass silhouette glows at center. As each quiz page completes, ink washes away in layers — but the cleared space reveals marbled, vibrant colors forming *inside* the glass while the surrounding canvas lightens toward watercolor paper. The glass fills with life as the world around it clears.
_Novelty_: Combines subtractive progress (ink clearing) with additive revelation (colors emerging inside the glass).

**[UX Motion #7]**: Stratified Density (Adapt)
_Concept_: Each of the 6 quiz pages corresponds to a specific mixology layer in the glassware (spirit base, liqueur, bitters, citrus/acid, garnish, foam). As the outer screen washes away, the glass silhouette is revealed layer-by-layer from bottom to top, with each page displaying a distinct fluid density and animation style (e.g., heavy, slow spirit vs. sharp, thin bitters).
_Novelty_: Progress is mapped directly to the density strata of physical cocktail layering.

**[UX Motion #8]**: The Bar Spoon Cursor (Modify/Magnify)
_Concept_: The user's cursor behaves like a mixologist's bar spoon. Hovering and dragging over the glass silhouette swirls sumi-e ink droplets in real-time with fluid physics. Clicking "Next Page" triggers a brief zoom-in on the swirl, creating a whirlpool that lifts ink outward, washing away the outer layer and transitioning to the next density stratum.
_Novelty_: User input becomes a physical mixing gesture — answering questions literally stirs your cocktail into existence.

**[UX Motion #9]**: The Overflow Reveal (Put to Other Uses)
_Concept_: After the quiz, the marbled colors inside the completed glass spill outward in a controlled sumi-e ink explosion, flooding the clean paper canvas. The spreading liquid paints the cocktail name in calligraphy, bleeds the archetype illustration into existence, and settles into the recipe card.
_Novelty_: The reveal is an inversion of the inversion — the cocktail finally breaking free and painting itself onto the blank canvas you uncovered.

**[UX Motion #10]**: Chrome-Free Quiz (Eliminate)
_Concept_: Eliminate all traditional web form UI — no progress bars, no page numbers, no card containers. Questions float as handwritten-style calligraphy on the clearing canvas. Answer options feel like ink marks the user chooses to make. The ink level itself is the only progress indicator.
_Novelty_: The interface disappears entirely into the metaphor.

**[UX Motion #11]**: Subtractive Soundscape (Rearrange)
_Concept_: Audio mirrors the visual arc. Starts dense (resonant bowls, ice clinks, liquid gurgle). Each page strips a layer of sound away. By the final page: near-silence, a single crystal glass ring. On the overflow reveal, the sound rushes back in as a warm, full-bodied wave.
_Novelty_: Conventional quiz audio builds up; this strips down.
