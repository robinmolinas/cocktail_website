# 01: Celeste's Descent

Created 11 June 2026 · Refreshed 8 October 2026

## Transaction and context

Celeste arrives during a quiet desktop break or on her phone in the evening. She expects a brief distraction; the craft earns her attention. She wants sensory discovery and a result that explains why the cocktail is hers. She worries about an arbitrary quiz, unclear controls and generic prose.

Success is a completed journey, a personally meaningful reading and a recipe she wants to make or save. The original completion/save percentages are aspirations, not measured results.

## Path

1. **Entrance:** the spotlight artwork and Spirit Within headline introduce the promise. **Discover my cocktail** begins the descent; the note promises about five minutes and a recipe plus reading.
2. **H1 Threshold:** enter a name and choose a perspective. Another side of me keeps her identity.
3. **H2 Seed:** choose a colour carried as light and lettering.
4. **H3 Gravity:** place the mote across five continuous polarities.
5. **H4 Hidden Self:** read the centred instruction, try the unscored 1 / 2 example and catch up to nine binary choices. Letting both fade is valid; Still Water removes the deadline.
6. **H5 Resonance:** gather what others come to her for, then mark what draws her. Each round allows three reversible choices. Carry and tap agree.
7. **H6 Finish:** choose flavours and veto ingredients; the selected pour supplies its fixed glass.
8. **H7 Trace:** leave an optional line that stays in the browser.
9. **H8–H9:** the breath resolves and the room appears through dark.
10. **H10 Reading:** meet the cocktail and read the portrait, personal passage and recipe. On portrait phones the unobscured photograph precedes the title and text.
11. **Keep or share:** bottom-only Share your cocktail / Save the recipe. Printing creates two clean sheets.

## Implementation boundary

The UI is built; the primary app still reveals Down the Line / The Visionary. The required outcome is a pour selected from her actual answers. Component builds do not close this scenario until desktop/phone walkthroughs show different authored results for different fixtures and privacy/fallback checks pass.

The twelve-word drawnToward edit is approved but awaits ticket 1.9. Sound is still a decision and integration task. No separate chapter pages, fluid marbling quiz or white overflow reveal should be restored.
