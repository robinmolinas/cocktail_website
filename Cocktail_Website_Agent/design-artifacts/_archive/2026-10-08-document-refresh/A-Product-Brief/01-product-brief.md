## Vision

Create a world-class, serene web application that guides users on a magical journey of self-discovery through a sumi-e and Suminagashi-themed personality quiz, leveraging a collaborative multi-agent AI backend to generate and visually display deeply personalized cocktails representing the user's core personality.

**Key Insights from Discussion:**
- **AI Showcase Ambition:** The project is a non-commercial, high-fidelity portfolio piece designed to showcase the pinnacle of what is possible when human creative direction partners with AI engineering.
- **Serene, Immersive Aesthetics:** Visuals and transitions draw heavy inspiration from Japanese sumi-e ink wash paintings, water marbling (Suminagashi), and Pottermore-style page-turn animations.
- **Agentic Backend:** Multiple specialized AI agents (Historian, Mixologist, and Storyteller) operate in the backend to craft the recipe and a bespoke, heartfelt narrative explaining the recommendation.
- **Web-First Output with PDF Export:** The output cocktail and story will be presented dynamically on the website, with the option to download the final recipe as a beautifully formatted PDF.
