## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Arrest Celeste's attention within seconds and promise magic without explaining it — convert a skeptical thirty-second visit into a willing descent.

**Entry Context:** First view of the site. She arrives from a Slack link shared by a fellow designer; no navigation, no prior state (except a faint Color Seed tint in the opening wash if she has visited before).

**Exit Action:** Clicks the calligraphic CTA "Discover the cocktail within you" — this click also unlocks the Web Audio context, letting the Subtractive Soundscape fade in as the descent begins.

**Secondary Exit (the side door):** A quiet calligraphic aside — *"taste one already poured"* — leads to a Poured Elixir exemplar (the Trickster; see Scenario 03 Design Resolution). Whisper rule: it must never compete visually with the primary CTA.

**On-Page Interactions:**
- **Ink marbling on cursor movement** — the dark canvas responds to her very first mouse motion with Suminagashi ripples; the cursor is already the bar spoon before she knows it
- **Idle breathing** — with no input, the ink swirls slowly on its own; the page is alive before she touches it
- **CTA reveal** — the calligraphic invitation emerges from the ink rather than sitting in a button container; no borders, no chrome
- **Soundscape unlock** — first click/gesture enables audio (mobile autoplay constraint honored by design)
- **Return-visit tint** — if a previous session exists, the opening wash carries a faint trace of her last chosen color

## Entry Context (First Step — Full Detail)

**User Situation (Q3):** Celeste, 28, senior brand designer at a boutique studio. A quiet mid-afternoon lull at her desk; headphones already on from a focus session; aesthetically fatigued after a day of reviewing boxy, conversion-driven layouts.

**Mental State (Q4):**
- **Hope:** A moment of genuine magic — a drink that *is* her, justified so beautifully she feels seen.
- **Worry:** Nineteen questions of clinical checkboxes ending in a generic "You're a Margarita!" with AI-flavored platitudes.

**Discovery Method (Q6):** A fellow designer drops the link in their studio's inspiration Slack channel: *"okay this is the most beautiful thing I've seen this year."* She clicks it between tasks, expecting to give it thirty seconds.
