## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Prove that failure, accessibility, and edge cases are *designed*, not patched — the states only Edward goes looking for, which quietly serve every user who needs them.

**Entry Context:** Not a destination — a collection of cross-cutting states reachable from any view. Edward arrives by probing: toggling OS settings, killing the network, mistyping a URL.

**Exit Action:** Edward returns to the main flow with his three fears retired; the audit continues toward the Verdict.

**On-Page Interactions (the four states):**

- **Still Water mode** — the `prefers-reduced-motion` variant as a designed experience: ink becomes slow paper-fade washes, gestures become taps, full keyboard navigability. Every view has a Still Water twin; the metaphor survives without the physics. (Origin: questionnaire brainstorm QX #16.)
- **Agent-failure recovery** — if the multi-agent backend fails during the Distillation, the apology stays in-fiction and offers a Poured Elixir exemplar as a graceful second act — the visitor still leaves with something beautiful. No raw error text, ever.
- **The poetic broken state** — 404 / hard failure rendered in-world: *"The ink has settled. This glass was never poured."* with a quiet path back to the Entrance.
- **Resize & touch parity** — viewport resizes mid-animation do not break the fluid sim; every desktop gesture has a calibrated touch twin (stir = drag, flick = swipe, tap = tap) with strata viscosity applied identically.

**Phase 4 note:** Each Scenario 01/02 page spec receives an "Edward lens" acceptance-criteria block (60fps floor, zero console errors, <4s transitions, touch parity). This page spec is where those cross-cutting states are designed once and referenced everywhere.
