## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Convert a friend's gift into her own curiosity — let Danielle admire Celeste's elixir, then make beginning her own descent irresistible.

**Entry Context:** Cold arrival from an iMessage/WhatsApp/Slack link. The link preview (Open Graph image) already showed the painted cocktail — the page must exceed that promise, not merely repeat it.

**Exit Action:** Taps "Discover the cocktail within you" — the ink swallows the screen and she lands at 1.1 The Entrance to begin her own ritual.

**On-Page Interactions:**
- **The miniature overflow (on load)** — a condensed ~2s replay of the Overflow Reveal: ink blooms into Celeste's painting, the cocktail's name brushes itself in calligraphy. Same component as view 1.10's climax, shortened choreography
- **Narrative reading** — Celeste's full story and recipe, identical to the owner view (Storyteller privacy constraint guarantees the "one true thing" is never echoed verbatim)
- **"Preserve this recipe"** — available to guests; the recipe is a gift Danielle can make for Celeste
- **Guest-state CTA hierarchy** — "Discover the cocktail within you" is the primary action in guest-arrival state (owner state leads with "Preserve this recipe")

**Implementation Note:** This page IS the Overflow Reveal view (1.10) in guest-arrival state — not a separate build. Requires server-persisted results with unique URLs and per-result OG images (see Design Resolution in `02-the-gifted-elixir.md`).

## Entry Context (First Step — Full Detail)

**User Situation (Q3):** Danielle — "the next Celeste," a creative peer in Celeste's group chat. Evening, on the sofa, half-watching something, phone in hand.

**Mental State (Q4):**
- **Hope:** A glimpse into her friend's inner self — and the itch of *"what would mine be?"*
- **Worry:** Another quiz-spam time sink; and quietly — that her result won't be as beautiful as Celeste's.

**Discovery Method (Q6):** An iMessage from Celeste: the link unfurls into a hand-painted cocktail with a calligraphic name, captioned *"this is so me it's scary."* Danielle taps it during the ad break.
