## Physical fastening and tag

The brass top plate appears seated on and riveted to the leather cover; the hinged front piece/turned lip hangs over that cover edge. It reads as mounted folder fastening rather than a free brass symbol. The mating catch is not clearly visible, so precise released-clasp mechanics remain a little ambiguous; I would not certify its open/closed state from this source alone. Enough adjacent layered leather/corner must remain to preserve that practical reading.

The blank cream tag is physically connected to burgundy cord around the lower-middle glass and remains higher than the old tabletop versions. Its right-hand writable area is clean of generated writing and distinct from the left hole. Fine crease/etched-looking paper detail is stronger than in v11, but the larger concern is that the field is relatively small; actual long-name glyph fit and lower-page authored-copy clearance cannot be certified from the source photograph.

## Recipe and materials

One straight Collins, clear pale gold/light amber liquid, full ordinary ice, one interior orange peel, no foam/cherry/straw: serving direction remains coherent. The blue bowl still reads empty. Jaggery stays opaque, golden brown and matte/crumbly rather than transparent amber resin, retaining the important v11 correction.

The drink's wet detail regressed versus v11: dense thin looping/etched highlights recur across cube faces, glass and peel, with few genuinely unobstructed patches. Broad cube outlines remain, but their surfaces again read as engraved/generated wet texture rather than simple smooth melting ice. This is a material hold, not merely a request for less condensation. The tag and parts of the timber/leather also pick up extra fine crackling; the drink is the clearest regression.

Room identity stays coherent: historic dark walnut, burgundy chair, neutral brown-black shadows and right-side warm practical. Timber is still flatter and less pathological than v5, though glossy microtexture is returning in highlighted areas. Correct atmosphere and opaque jaggery do not outweigh the failed ice/glass realism gate.
