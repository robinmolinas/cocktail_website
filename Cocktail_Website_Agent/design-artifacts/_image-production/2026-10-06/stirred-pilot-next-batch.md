## 2. Any Day — ordinary ice, thin honey film

Source: `design-artifacts/pours/lover-regular-guy.md`, complete dossier including applied fact decisions and image brief; complete `_studio/specs/lover-regular-guy.json`.

- Recipe: 60 ml bourbon (Elijah Craig Small Batch recommended; straight bourbon 40–50%), 22.5 ml fresh lemon juice, 15 ml honey syrup. Syrup is **three parts light runny wildflower/light-clover honey to one part water by volume**, shaken in a closed jar until smooth. Fill shaker three-quarters with ice, shake hard for 30 seconds, strain into the rocks glass over one large plain cube. Nothing on top; no egg and no garnish. The current 15 ml honey measure is intentional, not the older printed 22.5 ml. Final liquid approximately 153.6 ml.
- Vessel/appearance: one heavy rocks glass, one large ordinary freezer cube — the biggest the freezer makes, **not clear and not cut**. Faint cloudy trapped air, broad wet faces and softly melting corners, not faceted/gemstone/etched ice. Hazy pale amber-gold drink with a **thin film of fine bubbles** from honey proteins, not a thick egg-white head or sculpted foam. The dossier explicitly calls the colour its author's unsourced visual read; treat it as appearance direction, not a sourced exact colour value. Cold, sparse uneven droplets rather than decorative all-over beads.
- Personality evidence: `whoYouAre` hears a loved one's passing mention of a car noise, then is under the car fixing it by Saturday; love is repeated practical help without an ask, with a fear that the important sentence might be left too late.
- Two proposed essential traces: one worn leather work glove with natural palm creases/localised wear; a small adjustable wrench resting partly on that glove. Both appear in the current image brief and express the repair habit in `whoYouAre`. Functional steel is permitted; no car, mechanic costume, money, wallet, theatrical scars or industrial metal counter.
- Reconciliation: the honey jar glowing from within like a lamp is impossible: omit the jar, or let it reflect the single practical light without emitting light. A shaker's exact frozen hand silhouette is not essential; omit it or allow ordinary irregular thaw patches. The old workshop setting becomes the house walnut reading-room/bar, not an industrial garage. Newspaper stacks are secondary and unnecessary; no pseudo-print. Preserve the plain cloudy cube and current recipe, not a clear sculpted cube borrowed from another pour.
- Risks: ice microtexture and orange advertising grading are material risks; the liquid must remain hazy, not transparent. Fine bubbles must read as a restrained film without creating an egg sour. Glove and wrench must remain separately recognisable within the moving phone window; their union cannot extend across a wide workbench.
