## Anti-pattern verdict

Material treatment FAILS the observed-photograph rule. Dense etched-looking
loops inside the wine read as invented surface detail, not sparse natural
carbonation. Paper and cloth repeat an embossed/damask-like treatment; the
wallet has crocodile emboss. Those objects are not inherently impossible,
but repeated decorative microtexture and heavy amber finishing undermine
photographic believability and house-control continuity.

This is not a complaint about the existing Playfair/Jost system or immersive
darkness. No generic card grid, gradient text, dashboard template, neon palette,
or ubiquitous glassmorphism appears in the reviewed reveal. The brand's
purposeful darkness and glass language stay. No ban on functional metal is
introduced: the lamp's hardware is legitimate; timber remains the surface.

## Overall impression / what works

The composition conveys a prepared, caring person through the coat collar
and ready folded cloth, not merely an ingredient still life. A correct chilled
no-ice flute and violet-red-to-gold-rose liquid retain the recipe. Desktop copy
has an intentional quiet left zone; loaded type and name-on-paper fitting work.
Arrival-to-breathing handover is smooth, and reduced motion retains the scene.

On phones the application overwrites its most personal moment: the name is
physically on the paper but cannot be read through hero copy and the veil.
Geometry and visual legibility are distinct gates. Before the hero enters,
the scene reads; after it enters, the phone layout fails. That earlier frame
does not establish accessible contrast.

   Experience owner: adapt/arrange a shared phone layout that reserves a
   non-overlapping image/name region. Moving ink off-paper or faking coordinates
   is not a fix. Do not simply lift copy into the glass/coat region.
2. **P1 — Phone vignette suppresses name visibility.** Reported ink/paper
   contrast is approximately1.05–1.08:1 on phones. Experience owner: audit the
   actual rendered name and reduce/reposition phone darkening while retaining
   readable hero copy. Desktop's reported1.4–2.0:1 is not accessibility proof.
   Keep seed-colored ink; test the actual selected colors rather than assume
   one red-name result covers all seeds.
3. **P1 — Wine filigree and patterned materials disclose synthetic generation.**
   Image producer: new version only, physically ordinary clear flute, sparse
   irregular bubbles, plain handled paper, natural folded cloth and restrained
   neutral brown-black shadows. Preserve the recipe and recognisable care
   evidence. Do not repaint/grade the drink or change serving format.
4. **P2 — Early phone scroll overlaps the fading hero and recipe.** Experience
   owner: arrange the transition so incoming recipe labels/items do not cross
   the outgoing tagline/cue. Preserve motion handover and reduced motion.
