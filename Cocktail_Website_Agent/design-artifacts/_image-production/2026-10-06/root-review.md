## Material-calibration lesson

Excessive instructions for “worn”, “patina” and “tactile detail” produced a
repeating synthetic pattern: embossed/alligator varnish, densely etched-looking
glass/ice and a broad advertising sheen. Texture quantity is not realism.
Use plain flat long wood grain, small local contact marks, sparse irregular
droplets and distinct material reflections. Compare finished exports directly
against Half a Rim/Down the Line/Off-Label house controls, not just a prompt
or another equally over-rendered generated scene.

Functional metal is allowed. This is not a renewed ban on shakers, clasps,
cups, keys or lamp hardware. The problem is room/table material and treatment,
not ordinary useful metal objects.
