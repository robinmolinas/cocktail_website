## Tone of Voice Definition

For a portfolio piece of this caliber, the copy must feel premium, evocative, and cohesive. It mirrors the Sumi-e brushstrokes and water marbling aesthetic by being serene and alchemical rather than typical dry app microcopy.

### Tone Attributes

1. **Serene & Immersive**: Calm, minimalist phrasing that doesn't scream for attention. Space is left for the visual ink wash and sensory cues.
2. **Evocative & Alchemical**: Framing the user's journey as an alchemical distillation of their inner self, using mixology terms (distill, stir, preserve, ingredient).
3. **Gentle & Guiding**: Poetic, non-demanding instructions that treat the user as a partner in discovery.
