## Deep Dive

### Core Structural Idea

**The founding principle:**
**"Distillation of the Unseen Self" (The Cocktail Within You)**. The app operates as an interactive, calligraphic alchemy laboratory. Instead of "building" a recipe from scratch through input selections, the application *uncovers* a complex liquid identity that already exists inside the user's mind, history, and preferences.

**Concrete example:**
A user lands on a quiet, ink-swirling canvas. They click "Begin" and answer 19 questions across 6 pages using rapid emotional associations. Their mouse acts as a bar spoon, swirling sumi-e ink washes that represent density layers of a drink. 
On completion, the ink clears to white watercolor paper, then overflows in a splash of watercolor paint. Out of the splash, calligraphy letters render the name of their cocktail, a beautiful hand-painted visual of the drink in its selected glassware appears, and a narrative by the Historian agent details why this recipe fits their personality.

### Why This Matters

**User's rationale:**
Typical cocktail sites are utilitarian index pages (select whiskey → get whiskey drinks). Typical quizzes are clinical and generic (fill form → get extrovert badge). 
Dionysus organizes the journey around sensory aesthetics and emotional discovery. By focusing on the visual beauty of the drink and the storytelling "why" behind the choice, the experience becomes an art piece that people feel connected to and want to show others.

## Concept Documentation

**Core concept:**
A visually led, multi-agent AI personality alchemist that distills the user's inner self into a deeply personalized, calligraphic cocktail recipe and historical narrative.

**Implementation principles:**
1. **Visual-First Narrative:** The climax is driven by a beautiful visual of the cocktail and a heartfelt story, not just text data.
2. **Subtractive Discovery:** The quiz is an act of clearing away noise (ink) to reveal the self.
3. **Tactile Mixology Metaphors:** Motion is structured around drink preparation physics (shaking, pouring, density, stirring, spilling).
4. **Physical Export Utility:** A clean, print-ready PDF card bridges the digital experience to real-life usage.

---

## Related Features

Features that stem from this concept:
1. **Interactive Bar Spoon Cursor:** A WebGL/Canvas fluid simulation that marbles background inks in response to mouse movement.
2. **Density-Layered Quiz Transition:** A page-turn mechanic styled as sinking through 6 distinct fluid layers.
3. **The Ink Overflow Climax:** An animation of colored inks overflowing from a glass silhouette to paint the results card.
4. **Agent Storytelling Engine:** Backend API orchestration where a Historian agent researches historical drink origins and a Storyteller agent writes the personalized client narrative.
5. **Print-Ready PDF Recipe Card:** A beautifully formatted, downloadable layout of the cocktail, ingredients, method, and history.
