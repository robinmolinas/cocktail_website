# Project Brief: Dionysus

This is the entry point to the [canonical product brief](project-brief.md), refreshed on 8 October 2026. Maintain the strategic foundation there rather than keeping two competing briefs.

Dionysus is a non-commercial creative portfolio experience: **Discover your Spirit Within**. The Suspended Pour journey leads to one of 132 authored cocktails and a reading that makes the guest feel seen. The collection is authored offline; runtime matching selects an eligible pour and may tailor only its personal `yours` passage.

The experience is built around native video holds, glass spheres, dark photographic rooms and a printable keepsake. Desktop and phone share the same answer meanings. The owner's reading stays private; the shared gift contains the cocktail and recipe.

Read the [current experience specification](../2026-07-08-experience-master-spec.md) for the flow, the [continuation status](../2026-10-06-continuation-status.md) for delivery progress, and `dialog/` for dated discovery history.
