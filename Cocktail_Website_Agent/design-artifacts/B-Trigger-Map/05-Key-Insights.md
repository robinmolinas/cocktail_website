# Key Insights & Strategic Implications

Refreshed 8 October 2026 from the existing trigger map and later product decisions.

1. **A result must feel earned.** The journey needs to reveal a cocktail selected from the guest's answers. The fixed pilot demonstrates presentation, not personal fit. Auditable matching and playtests are the next proof.
2. **Clarity carries the atmosphere.** H4's example and H5's two distinct movements make the interaction readable without turning it into a conventional form. Mystery should sit in the world, not in uncertainty about how to answer.
3. **Authored craft makes the promise credible.** History, recipe and core reading are written and checked offline. Runtime tailoring has a narrow remit: the personal yours passage. A failed call still serves a complete authored pour.
4. **Phones need their own composition, with the same meaning.** Protect the physical tag before introducing the title and reading. Preserve continuous gravity values and identical answers across mouse and touch.
5. **The keepsake extends the experience.** Bottom-only share/save actions and clean two-sheet printing let guests use the recipe. A shared gift invites discovery while withholding the owner's private reading.
6. **Portfolio proof follows guest quality.** Edward sees the design and engineering in the same journey. Dev navigation serves testing; house pours are the planned public side door.
7. **Approval and delivery are separate facts.** A settled recipe, accepted tagline, generated image, exported image and integrated final are different states. So are a finished shared component and a working end-to-end reveal.

Preserve the dark world, Playfair typography, native video and reduced-motion alternatives. Do not restore the retired fluid solver, white-bloom reveal, vessel question, generated recipes or full private reading in shared links.
