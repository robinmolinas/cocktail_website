# Celeste the Curious — Primary Persona

Strategic persona created 11 June 2026 · Experience references refreshed 8 October 2026

Celeste is a creative self-explorer who appreciates craft, cocktail history and a moment of reflection. The original persona pictured a brand designer in her late twenties; that sketch is a design lens, not audience research. She arrives during a desktop break or a quiet evening on her phone.

## Motivation

She wants discovery that feels thoughtful rather than arbitrary: a cocktail whose reading names something recognizable about her, with enough recipe detail to make it. The art earns her attention; the meaning makes it worth keeping.

## Driving forces

| Wants | Fears | Response in the current experience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sensory wonder | Another flat, clinical form | Native video, motes and glass spheres with clear interactions |
| Feeling seen | A generic label or repetitive AI compliments | Distinct authored personalities, real anchors and bounded personal tailoring |
| An enjoyable brief ritual | Unclear mechanics or too much reading before a choice | H4's 1 / 2 example; distinct gathering/ringing H5 rounds |
| A useful keepsake | A beautiful result she cannot recreate | Fixed authored recipe and clean two-sheet print |
| Sharing the discovery | Exposing a private confidence | Recipe-only gift; trace and personal reading remain private |

## Transformation

She arrives aesthetically fatigued and mildly curious. She leaves with a drink and reading she recognizes, a recipe she plans to make, and a reason to invite someone else. Her delight drives the sharing flywheel and Robin's portfolio credibility.

## Experience implications

The Suspended Pour replaces the original nineteen-question ink-marbling concept. Preserve the intimacy, sensory play and low cognitive load, while avoiding ambiguity about how to answer. Support phones and Still Water with the same answer meanings. Missing H4 choices are valid; completion must not require catching every word.

The normal journey must select an authored pour from her answers before personal-fit success can be claimed. The fixed pilot proves presentation only. The original >80% completion and >40% save targets are aspirations until measured; demographic detail and persona quotes are not interview findings.
