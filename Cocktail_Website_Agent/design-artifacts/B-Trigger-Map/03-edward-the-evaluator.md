# Edward the Evaluator — Secondary Persona

Strategic persona created 11 June 2026 · Experience references refreshed 8 October 2026

Edward is a creative director or engineering lead assessing Robin's portfolio within a few minutes. The original sketch pictured a lead in his late thirties. It is a design lens, not audience research. He explores the same product as Celeste, then probes it on a phone and with developer tools.

## Driving forces

| Wants | Fears | Evidence he can assess |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creative engineering | A standard AI wrapper with decorative effects | Coherent native-video choreography and purposeful interactions |
| Reliable craft | Jank, clipped text and poor touch adaptation | Resize, orientation, Still Water and keyboard/touch checks |
| Real mixology and meaning | Invented facts or unmakeable recipes | Authored dossiers, source anchors, recipe and content checks |
| Architectural depth | An unbounded model inventing the result | Deterministic shortlist, bounded choice, shared contracts and authored fallback |
| A convincing destination quickly | Spending his whole attention budget before seeing quality | Planned authored house-pour side door; dev shortcuts remain for testing |

## Success

The experience earns confidence in Robin's design and implementation judgment, enough to recommend the work to a team. Celeste's coherent journey is the strongest portfolio proof; no technical dashboard or recruitment-only flow is needed.

## Acceptance implications

Check the connected result, not just a sample. Different fixtures should reveal different authored pours, and shared gifts must withhold the private reading. Silent failure, strict data boundaries and clean print contribute to the assessment.

The 60fps ambition concerns UI smoothness, not a claim about source-video frame rate. Approved choreography can exceed the original four-second transition target. Server persistence, OG, house pours and live tailoring remain delivery claims requiring their own evidence.
