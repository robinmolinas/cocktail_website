## 9. Not Only the Way — The Craftsman

Pairing: caregiver-creator (Caregiver × Creator).

**Persona:** You show care through how you make things, including details nobody will notice. Your method has affection in it, although you hesitate to call it pride.

**Cocktail and why you:** A gin Gimlet with homemade lime and caraway cordial. The preparation happens before anyone asks, and the small, unseen addition changes the finished drink. It mirrors the care in your process.

**Current:** You'd never call it pride. You'd call it doing it properly.

**Approved 2026-10-08:** Doing it properly is how you show you care.

## 10. Serviceable — The Researcher

Pairing: caregiver-explorer (Caregiver × Explorer).

**Persona:** When someone is hurting, you read, ask questions and investigate possibilities. Curiosity and care keep you looking after others have stopped.

**Cocktail and why you:** A Scotch highball with hops, soda and lemon peel. The reading connects its bubbles to an experiment intended to help people, which proved useful in an unexpected way. The hopped whisky nods to where that experiment began.

**Current:** You care about people you'll never meet.

**Approved revision, 2026-10-08:** For someone you care about, you'll keep looking as long as it takes.

Robin's correction: this persona is defined by grit in exploring because they care and will not stop. The earlier proposal, “When someone says nothing can be done, you start looking”, captured starting rather than staying with the search.

## 11. Off Duty — The Rescuer

Pairing: caregiver-hero (Caregiver × Hero).

**Persona:** Trouble makes you clear and decisive. You notice who needs help and act before others have worked out what to do; the emotional aftermath comes later.

**Cocktail and why you:** A hot Irish whiskey drink with honey and thyme, served in a wide teacup. The thyme needs two minutes, and the drink needs to be enjoyed while hot: the reading gives someone else a chance to care for you while you sit down.

**Current:** Everyone else is still deciding. You've already gone.

**Approved 2026-10-08:** When someone needs help, you're already on your way.

## 12. Before the Room — The Angel

Pairing: caregiver-innocent (Caregiver × Innocent).

**Persona:** Your presence settles a room. People soften, start talking or remember they like each other; your own wishes are less visible.

**Cocktail and why you:** A sparkling Kir variation with blackcurrant liqueur and fresh basil. The bubbles bring the unexpected basil forward. The reading uses that first impression to invite the less familiar sides of you into view.

**Current:** Everyone breathes easier when you arrive. Nobody asks what you left at the door.

**Approved 2026-10-08:** The room feels kinder once you're in it.

## 13. Worth the Trip — The Reassurer

Pairing: caregiver-jester (Caregiver × Jester).

**Persona:** You read someone carefully before finding the joke that helps. The laugh is how you show you understand the seriousness of what they are going through.

**Cocktail and why you:** Gin and brandy with lime, ginger and toasted cumin, finished with soda. It can travel to someone before the fizz is added: the visible lift comes with care underneath it, just as your humour does.

**Current:** You always make them laugh at the wrong moment, but it's actually always the right one.

**Approved 2026-10-08:** Your best jokes come when someone needs them most.

## 14. Standing By — The Understanding Spouse

Pairing: caregiver-lover (Caregiver × Lover).

**Persona:** You understand the difficult days of people you love without taking everything personally. With you, being loved does not depend on being at one's best.

**Cocktail and why you:** A Scotch Old-Fashioned variation with passion fruit syrup, bitters and lemon peel, served without ice in a small wineglass. The sweetener also brings sharpness: the drink carries the reading's idea that love and honesty can belong together.

**Current:** You've seen them at their worst. You're still here.

**Approved 2026-10-08:** People don't have to be at their best to be loved by you.

## 15. Till Spring — The Healer

Pairing: caregiver-magician (Caregiver × Magician).

**Persona:** You believe people can recover before they believe it themselves. You keep turning up with small, ordinary invitations that make help easier to accept.

**Cocktail and why you:** A bitter orange amer with pomegranate and sweet orange syrup, soda and a cognac float. The first sip is gentle and the bitterness follows. Sweetness sits beside the hard part, matching the way your help makes it more bearable.

**Current:** You believe they'll be all right before they do.

**Robin's replacement wording, 2026-10-08:** You believe in people before they believe in themselves.

The chosen wording celebrates belief in the person, beyond reassurance about a particular difficult period, without giving away the later proposal about the limits of responsibility.

## 16. Not Too Polite — The Campaigner

Pairing: caregiver-outlaw (Caregiver × Outlaw).

**Persona:** You speak first when someone is being forgotten, even when doing so makes you nervous. Care for that person matters more than your own comfort.

**Cocktail and why you:** A gin Collins with lemon, soda and jaggery replacing white sugar. The reading makes that single change an expression of conviction: an everyday choice that stands for someone else.

**Current:** Someone had to say it. It's usually you.

**Approved 2026-10-08:** You speak up for the people who aren't in the room.
