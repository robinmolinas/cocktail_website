# Fact card: Brian Shebairo and the phone booth door

Tiers: *A Proper Drink* (Simonson 2016; `proper`, printed pages) and the Oxford Companion (OUP, © 2022; LCCN 2021) = primary (library). *Get Smart* rows = archive.org full-text-search OCR highlights from period books (secondary, labelled; cut-off highlights are incomplete). ## Conflicts and limits

| # | Note |
|---|---|
| C1 | Whose door: Proper p. 270 says the idea was Shebairo's; Oxford pdf 521 has Meehan opening the bar "behind" it (who opened the bar, not whose idea the door was). Not a conflict of fact, but never a credit claim either way (Wren: credit is the Straight Man's). |
| C2 | No page says anyone was surprised, delighted or caught by the booth. Its fame (F5, "arguably") cuts the other way: many arrive knowing. "Still catching strangers" is our reading: "I like to think". |
| C3 | How the booth works (picking up the receiver, a host answering, a panel opening) is on no page we read. The photo shows a phone inside; nothing says it is used to get in. Out. |
| C4 | "Opening titles" of *Get Smart* (Wren's memory): unsourced. G4 is a quiz answer, question unread; "dropped through" has no object on screen. Safe wording: the hero "steps into a phone booth and dials a number". |
| C5 | "Still in use today": not checked past Oxford (F10). Date it ("more than a decade later"), never "today" or an undated "still". |

## Guards
- Agency: the page gives Shebairo the *idea*. Never "he built", "he put in", "he installed".
- Never "speakeasy", Prohibition, "please don't tell", secret, password, reservations, "getting in", "a way in" (*Fine by Me*'s phrase).
- Never Meehan's firing, Saunders, the $14,000 joke, the credit-to-Meehan line (F4, F12), Deragon's Daiquiri, Don Lee or bacon (`don-lee-bentons-old-fashioned.md`, Explorer's).
- No date on any Shebairo quote (F6). Year 2007 is safe if needed (three sources), but one clause at most.
- *Get Smart* is the reading's one outside touchstone; no Mel Brooks name-drop needed.

## Use (fence)
- jester-magician only. The Trailblazer (*Fine by Me*, explorer-outlaw) is set in the same bar and fences the booth out of its own reading (its image brief: "no phone booth or door"). Any pour naming PDT owns that overlap in one clause.
