# Fact card: The Penicillin and Lucinda Sterling (runner-up, caregiver-magician)

Written 2026-09-29 by Hester.

- F1. Sam Ross, 2005, Milk & Honey, New York; a riff on the Gold Rush, with honey and ginger sweetening and a smoky Islay float. *A Proper Drink* p. 170.
- F2. Ross, as Simonson reports him: he "went to bed that night not thinking much of it"; "nine months later" (Ross's figure), at Little Branch, the waitress Lucinda Sterling told him every table needs to experience one. p. 170.
- F3. 2007: LA consultancy spread it; "as close to a household word as any cocktail since the Cosmopolitan"; by 2014 some assumed it was an old classic. pp. 170–171.
- F4. Sterling later tended bar and opened Middle Branch with Petraske (*Proper* p. 277; Oxford MILK & HONEY pdf 1303; Middle Branch 2012, Oxford pdf 1500).
- C1. No source explains the name. Never explain it, and never play it as a cure.
- Spec: *Codex* p. 282; *Proper* pp. 170–171.
