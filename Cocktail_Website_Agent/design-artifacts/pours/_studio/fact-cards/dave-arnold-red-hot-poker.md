# Fact card: the poker, not the pan

Our words throughout. Arnold is alive: nothing about him or his bar in the present tense without a date (*Liquid Intelligence* 2014; *A Proper Drink* 2016). Arnold is already the story person of *Overnight* (creator-magician, the milk wash): own it in one clause if he's named. Every quote below was read on the page in the same call.

## Out (and why)
- St. George Fighting the Dragon, the burns, his wife's three weeks (*Proper* p. 223): injury played for laughs, Wren's trap.
- The illegal loft, "hookers", drugs (p. 224); the Nagasaki gingerbread (p. 225).
- "Mad Hobbyists Without Degree" (Simonson's coinage, p. 223): dossier only.
- His hard-shake tests ("It didn't", p. 225): Uyeda is *Not Only the Way*'s person.

## Sibling fences
- ***Overnight*** (creator-magician): Arnold is its story person, and its lesson is "the part you didn't mean" / "unintended side benefit". Here the over-building is deliberate and the surprise is that it works, never that it came out differently.
- ***The Entertainer*** (magician-regular-guy): a hot drink, a crowd and fire ("People came to watch the fire"). Here no flame, no watching; the iron is for the taste, and the room's reaction is to the smell (F5).
