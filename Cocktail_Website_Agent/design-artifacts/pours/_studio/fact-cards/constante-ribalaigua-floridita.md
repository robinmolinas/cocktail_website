## Conflicts
- **C1 Start year:** 1912 or 1914 (Oxford pdf 1655); Fuentes (1984) and a 2001 Havana book say 1914 (OCR highlights). No year.
- **C2 Booklet years:** Oxford pdf 821 "between 1935 and 1948"; *Joy* pdf 317 a "1934 recipe book from La Florida"; *Joy* pdf 300 a 1937 booklet. No years for the booklets.
- **C3 How influential:** Oxford pdf 1655 "unquestionably the most influential mixologist of the twentieth century"; Oxford pdf 853 "perhaps the most influential"; *Imbibe!* pdf 216 "perhaps the greatest". The same book hedges itself: never a superlative in guest text.
- **C4 Founder:** Kurlansky (*Havana*, 2017, OCR highlight) says Constante founded the Floridita in 1912; Oxford: founded by the Sala Parera family's circle, he bought it in 1935. Kurlansky is wrong on the page we have; don't use.
- **C5 Name:** Oxford heads him "Constante" but calls him "Constantino" elsewhere and "Constantí" (BOADAS pdf 303); Woon's "Constantino". Use "Constante".

## Guards
- The Daiquiri is the Problem Solver's (Cox). The Floridita cocktail is a different named drink in the Daiquiri's family (Oxford pdf 821): never "Daiquiri", "Daiquiri variation" or "master of Daiquiri variations" in guest text. The family already holds another Daiquiri-family drink (the Mai Tai, *Hold the Shark*), so the root family isn't what the guard protects.
- Trader Vic: one clause, pdf 821's facts only (1938 visit, first menu, printed it later). Never his leg, the shark, the Mai Tai, tiki, Jeff Berry or Donn Beach. *Hold the Shark*'s card reserved this visit for this row.
