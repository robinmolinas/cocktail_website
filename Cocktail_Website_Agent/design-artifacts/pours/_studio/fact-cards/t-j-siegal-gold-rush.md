## Conflicts
- **C1, the year.** *PUNCH*: "sometime in 2000". *Proper* p. 96 header: 2001. No year in the reading.
- **C2, the bourbon.** First night Knob Creek; then Elijah Craig 12-year-old (*PUNCH*) vs "Elijah Craig single batch recommended" (*Proper*). Tomás picks and the dossier says which.
- **C3, the lemon (resolved for Proper).** Page image of *Proper* p. 96 (pdf 110), opened 2026-09-30: 2 oz bourbon, **¾ oz lemon, ¾ oz honey syrup**; "three parts honey to one part water", no unit. *PUNCH* gives Petraske's general sour formula that night as 2 : 1 : ¾. The two still differ; our glass is Tomás's measures, so the reading states no measures as Siegal's or the house's.
- **C4, Siegal's role.** Oxford: longtime employee. *Proper*: investor, "not a drink creator". *PUNCH*: not primarily a bartender. Keep role labels out.
- **C5, the honey syrup.** Petraske (Siegal, p. 96) and Maloney (*PUNCH*). Say "the house honey syrup", or give Siegal's account as his.
