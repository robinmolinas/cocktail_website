## Legends / cautions
- **"Invented in 1990."** Oxford gives 1990, Joy "circa 1990", Proper "early 1990s" and says agave syrup only reached the US in the late 1990s. See C1.
- **"His father slapped him."** Proper p. 81 says his father "slapped his son's ears back" after a 1999 *WSJ* piece. The phrase may be figurative (a telling-off); don't turn it into a physical punishment. Keep out of the reading unless Wren wants the father, and then only as "wasn't impressed", which is what the source plainly says.
- **"Tommy's tequila is Herradura."** True at the start; not today (F14).

## Conflicts
- **C1, the date.** Oxford 1990 / Joy c. 1990 / Proper early 1990s, with agave syrup in the US only late 1990s (Proper p. 84) while Joy says agave was "pretty new" when he made it. The drink evolved; no year in the reading.
- **C2, the recipe.** Proper: 2 oz reposado, 1 oz lime, ½ oz agave syrup. Joy: 2 oz 100% agave tequila, 1 oz lime, 1 oz diluted agave. Oxford specifies neither. Tomás's call; never "his recipe" if we riff.
