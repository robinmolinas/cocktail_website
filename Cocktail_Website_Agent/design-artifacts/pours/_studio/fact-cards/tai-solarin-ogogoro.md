# Tai Solarin and ogogoro

## Legends

|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | He "walked into a Police Station with a bottle of ogogoro and demanded to be arrested", which got the product unbanned, "in the early 1960s". | Josef Omorotionmwan, "'Ogogoro don dey sour' but its ban is not a remedy (2)", *Vanguard*, 9 Jul 2015 (opinion column, fetched) | One columnist, no source, and the date contradicts Oxford's 1968 and the 1968 headline (F6).
