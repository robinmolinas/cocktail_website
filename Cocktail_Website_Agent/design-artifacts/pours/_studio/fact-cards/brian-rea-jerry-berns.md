# Fact card: Brian Rea and Jerry Berns (21 Club) · Southside at 21

Hester, 2026-10-02, for sage-caregiver (The Mentor). Tiers: Oxford = primary (library); DeGroff and *NRA News* = archive.org full-text OCR highlights (cut off, so incomplete).

| ID | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Brian Rea, 1927–2021: American bartender, collector, historian of the craft. | Oxford REA, BRIAN, pdf 1614 |
| F2 | First bar job 1947. His career "began in earnest" when he was hired at the 21 Club, midtown Manhattan. | pdf 1614 |
| F3 | Jerry Berns, 1907–2006. Oxford: he founded 21 with his brother Charlie and cousin Jerome Kreindler (contested, see C1). | pdf 1614 |
| F4 | Berns "schooled the enthusiastic student in the bar and restaurant business". | pdf 1614 |
| F5 | Rea rose to head bartender while 21 was "arguably" the world's most famous bar. Undated. | pdf 1614 |
| F6 | Then the Little Club (Billy Reed; where Doris Day began). In the 1960s, West Coast: Scandia (Ken Hansen), the Playboy Club, Frascati. | pdf 1614 |
| F7 | 1969: he "dreamed up" a new hospitality position, beverage director, to separate bar and beverage operations from food. | pdf 1614 |
| F8 | He spoke with six major hospitality corporations. Host International was willing to try it and appointed him director of beverage services. | pdf 1614 |
| F9 | Left Host in 1977 to consult (Marriott, TGI Friday's, the US Department of Defense and others). | pdf 1614 |
| F10 | Books: *Brian's Booze Guide* (1976), *Brian's Bartender Guide* (1978), described as humour, common sense and strong technique. *B.A.S.T.A.R.D.S.* (2017). A "barchivist" with one of the world's largest drinks libraries. Entry by Dale DeGroff. | pdf 1614–1615 |
| F11 | Rea suggested to Wondrich how Jerry Thomas made his $16,000 (staking miners). Rea is also thanked in the acknowledgements. | *Imbibe!* pdf 28, 255 |
| F12 | Southside: a tall drink of gin, lemon, sugar, mint and soda. Its Chicago origin is a rumour, and Oxford calls it incorrect. It grew out of Snedecor's Tavern juleps on Long Island and the Southside Sportsman's Club. The country-club drink. "For many years" the house drink at 21. Recipe: 45 gin / 30 lemon / 22.5 simple / about 6 mint leaves, shaken, strained over ice in a highball, soda, mint sprig. There's a 2–3 day steeped mix, and rum in Maryland's Hunt Country. | Oxford SOUTHSIDE pdf 1851 (Felten) |

## Contested and leads
- **C1** Founders: Oxford names Jerry with Charlie and "Jerome Kreindler". DeGroff, *The Essential Cocktail* (2008, OCR highlight) has "Jack and Charlie's. Jack Kriendler and Charles E. Berns, cousins". The name and spelling conflict, and no founding date is in the library. Don't call Jerry a founder.
- **L1** *NRA News / Restaurants USA* 1986 (archive.org `micro_IA40706920_0459`, OCR highlights): "CIA Hall of Fame honors Jerry Berns (Oct., p. 9)". Berns among operators sharing "secrets for success". "instructor Brian Rea" teaching a bar-business course. These may be different issues. Not verified.
- **L2** Rea's own words on Berns: not found. Gap: *B.A.S.T.A.R.D.S.* (2017) and his 2021 obituaries.
