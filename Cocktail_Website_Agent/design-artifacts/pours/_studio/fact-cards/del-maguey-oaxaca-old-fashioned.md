## Conflicts
- **C1, Cooper's dates.** COOPER, RON (pdf 573): found the real thing in Oaxaca in 1986; launched in 1995 with 54 cases from two villages (Chichicapa, San Luis del Río). DEL MAGUEY MEZCAL (pdf 638): first tasted it in Tlacolula in 1990; importing by 1996, four village mezcals. Same book, two entries. **No dates, no counts** in the reading.
- **C2, the Oaxaca Old-Fashioned's year.** Proper 2007 only; no second source checked yet. Say "at Death & Co" and no year unless Oxford confirms.
