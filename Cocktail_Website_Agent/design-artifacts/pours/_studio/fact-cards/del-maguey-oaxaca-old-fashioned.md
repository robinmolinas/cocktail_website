## Conflicts
- **C1, Cooper's dates.** COOPER, RON (pdf 573): found the real thing in Oaxaca in 1986; launched in 1995 with 54 cases from two villages (Chichicapa, San Luis del Río). DEL MAGUEY MEZCAL (pdf 638): first tasted it in Tlacolula in 1990; importing by 1996, four village mezcals. Same book, two entries. **No dates, no counts** in the reading.
- **C2, the Oaxaca Old-Fashioned's year.** Proper 2007 only; no second source checked yet. Say "at Death & Co" and no year unless Oxford confirms.

- **C3, the moment of the find.** Oxford COOPER (pdf 573): 1986, on holiday with friends. Oxford DEL MAGUEY (pdf 638): 1990, a café in Tlacolula. Proper p. 248: 1990, an art project, visiting farmers. His own account (VinePair 2021) has **two trips**: 1986 with friends (the couple of litres brought home) and 1990 (the villages, the border). The café is in none of his telling: **dropped**. Tell the 1986 holiday scene, no year; the 1990 trip only as "he went back".
- **C4, the border allowance.** "1 liter" (VinePair, his memory of the official) vs "only a three-liter bottle" (SevenFifty narration). **No number.**
- **C5, "discovered".** Oxford uses it twice (pdf 573) and quotes an off-the-record "It took an American… to discover" (pdf 640). Never in the reading (Wren's condition c); his own dicho (F22) says the opposite.
