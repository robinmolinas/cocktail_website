# Fact card: Kazuo Uyeda and the hard shake

Our words throughout. He is alive (b. 1944): nothing about him in the present tense without a date. *The Bartender* manga shares this world: feeling only.

## Conflicts
- **C1:** did he claim superiority? Oxford pdf 2078 "he claims" it rounds off flavours, colder and smoother. Klemm (*Proper* p. 208): he "never said it was a superior shake". Keep "better" out; quote only what each said.
- **C2:** New York date: 2009 Hiro Ballroom demo (Oxford pdf 1998) vs 2010 two-day symposium (*Proper* p. 208). Possibly two events. No year in the reading.

- Klemm's "the show his customers liked" (p. 208) leans to performance: dossier only (Wren, round 2).
