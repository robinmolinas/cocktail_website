## Counterweights (carry openly)
- He didn't want the trade at 17 (F2). The gift wasn't a dream he hid; it grew in a job he was sent to.
- SB13: asked about craft gin, "Everything they are trying we've tried before" and craft is "a little bit of money to get by". Two years later he took a stake in a craft distillery (DG15). That's the "irascible" on record; don't tell it as hypocrisy, and don't use it to mock small makers.
- BN: "I wear my heart on my sleeve." He didn't hide himself. Only the work was unseen. Never "he kept it quiet".
