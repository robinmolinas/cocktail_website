## Legends
- **L1 The shark.** He lost the leg as a child (F2); the shark is his telling (F7) and a popular belief (F8). Grant it first; never "lie", "fake", "made up", "far from the truth".
- **L2 "He invented the Mai Tai" (flat).** *Joy* pdf 69 says it flat ("gave us the Mai Tai in 1944"); Oxford hedges (F15). Follow Oxford.

## Conflicts
- **C1 Cause.** Oxford: tuberculosis (with a kidney). Difford's: an accident with a cable car. Beyl: a childhood accident left him with tuberculosis of the knee. Three versions: no cause in guest text, and no "sickly" (it names an illness by the back door).
- **C2 Age.** Six in Difford's and Beyl; Oxford gives none. Wren rules it out of guest text.
- **C3 Search summary vs page.** The 2026-10-04 search summary said Beyl wrote that Vic "would tell his customers" about the shark. Beyl's page says "many believed". The summary is not a source.
- **C4 Ice pick vs fork.** The plan noted a 1996 Emeryville essay with a fork (unread). Beyl has the ice pick. Two objects: out of guest text either way (also Wren's rule).
- **C5 "Good" vs "the best".** Not a conflict: Oxford glosses the single word *maita'i*; Vic quotes the longer phrase with *roa ae* and glosses that. No Tahitian page read, so we can't say he inflated it. Never set Oxford's "good" against his "the best".
- **C6 Age at the shack.** 31 (born Dec 1902, opened Nov 1934), not "about thirty-two".
