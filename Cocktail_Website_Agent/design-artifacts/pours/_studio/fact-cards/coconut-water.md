## Legends / cautions
- **"Whisky with coconut water is a Brazilian habit."** Only sourced for the north-east (F6), and only as "common" per one consultant. Pages claiming Diageo data about coconut-water ice cubes in Recife are brand or lifestyle sites: not trusted, not used.
- Tasting note "nutty" (F2) is a flavour description, not an allergen fact. The allergen class (nuts) comes from STUDIO-RULES, not from here.

## Conflicts
- None between sources. Scope only: Oxford = Caribbean and neighbours; Punch = Puerto Rico to Trinidad; Valor = Recife.
