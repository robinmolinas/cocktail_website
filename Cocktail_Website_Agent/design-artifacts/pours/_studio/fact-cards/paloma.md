Our words throughout.

| F2 | Its roots go back to a 1950 brochure for Squirt, a US grapefruit soda, suggesting it with tequila; Squirt launched in Mexico in 1955; who adapted it to Mexican tastes is unknown. | Oxford, PALOMA (pdf 1470) | primary | So never "traditional", "ancestral" or "old Mexican" drink. |
