## Conflicts
- **C1, the title.** DNB: *A Word or two on Port Wine*; Barr (FTS): *A Word or Two on Port Wine!* (London, 1844); Oxford pdf 707: "A Word or Two about Port". Two against one: if a title is told, use *A Word or Two on Port Wine*; better, leave it out.
- **C2, whom he accused.** Ludington, *The Politics of Wine in Britain* (2013, FTS highlight): Forrester "charged his fellow port shippers with adulterating" (cut off). The DNB frames it as abuses plus the monopoly. So the accused may well have included his own trade. The DNB's attackers stay "the persons interested" (people with a stake); the reading never names them as shippers, and never says his trade stood with him either.
- **C3, whether he was right.** Barr (FTS highlight): his account of adulterated port in the 1840s "has generally been written off by wine" (cut off, the end of the sentence unread). *Edinburgh Review* 1867 (FTS): a writer "goes out of his way to disparage" him after his death, and disputes his treacle claim for the present day. Oxford pdf 707 sides with him on elderberries. Sources disagree: no "he was right", no "the port was full of elderberries" as fact.
- **C4, his own stake.** Simpson, *Creating Wine* (2011, FTS highlights, three separate fragments): "the port-wine shipper J. J. Forrester, believed that if duties were cut sufficiently" (cut off); a different fragment, "“everyone” to be able to afford to drink it" (subject and "it" unread); and "Forrester, as many others in the debate, was not a disinter-" (cut off; almost certainly "disinterested"). Safe level: he shipped port and was not a disinterested party in the 1852 debate on Britain's wine import duties. **Struck (mine, r2):** "he wanted duties cut far enough for everyone to afford port", which merged two cut-off fragments. Never tell him as a pure, selfless lone truth-teller.

## Legends and inferences
- L1: Why he published anonymously: **not on record** (no page read gives a motive). Signposted interpretation only.
- L2: That he was isolated or lost standing: **not on record**. The page gives one attack sentence, then warm support, a prize and honours.
