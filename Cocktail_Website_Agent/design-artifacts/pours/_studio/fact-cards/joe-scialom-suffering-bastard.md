## Conflicts
- C1: **Date.** Oxford (two entries) places it in WWII (the story in October 1942); *A Proper Drink* p. 264 says "the late '40s". Keep the year out of the reading; "in wartime Cairo" rests on Oxford only, so prefer "at a hotel bar in Cairo" or tell the 1942 story as Berry's.
- C2: **Recipe.** Cordial 15 ml (Oxford) vs 30 ml (Proper); shaken vs stirred; Collins vs rocks. Never "his recipe" for either.

## Legends
- L1: the drink helped win El Alamein. Oxford calls it highly unlikely. Only as the journalists' joke, labelled.
- L2: the name's origin (officers' complaint, a joke by Scialom): not in our sources.

## Watch
- Lime cordial's lineage (Rose, 1867, sea voyages) is caregiver-creator's clause. Not again here.
- The drink's name contains a swear word. Wren's call whether the reading names it.
