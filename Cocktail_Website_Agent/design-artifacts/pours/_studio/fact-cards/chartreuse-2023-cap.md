## Guards
- Medicine: the "long life" elixir stays out.
- The 1903 expulsion and the Tarragona years are the Rule Breaker's backup ground: leave them out, or give them one clause.
- Religion is told respectfully, never as a pun. Never "quiet quitting" (Yahoo's headline).
- Hero reserves the Chartreuse Swizzle. Hero-regular-guy's herbal drink is Bénédictine, not this.
- Present tense is dated: "in 2023, they wrote…". Nothing says the cap still stands.
- Counterweight, on no page: scarcity raises a bottle's value, and a cynic reads the cap as hype. Don't deny it, and don't claim it.
