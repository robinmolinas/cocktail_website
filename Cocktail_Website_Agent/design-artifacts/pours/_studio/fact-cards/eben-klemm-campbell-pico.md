# Fact card: Eben Klemm, from the Campbell Apartment to Pico

Our words throughout. Quotes under 15 words, all read on screen on 2026-10-01 (*A Proper Drink*, printed pp. 134-136 = pdf 148-150; Oxford MARASCHINO pdf 1227-1229). Simonson is a reliable secondary; lines in quotation marks from Klemm are on the record in his voice, and the rest is Simonson's narration. Klemm is also quoted (dossier only) in *Not Only the Way* (caregiver-creator) on Uyeda, *Proper* p. 208.
