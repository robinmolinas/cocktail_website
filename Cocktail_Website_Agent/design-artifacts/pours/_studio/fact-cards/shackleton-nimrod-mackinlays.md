| F2 | Evening of New Year's Day (1909): within 172½ miles of the Pole. | 3532 |
| F3 | Same passage: he "compelled myself to look at the matter sensibly and to consider the lives of those who were with me"; going on too far would make return impossible "and then all the results of our efforts would be lost to the world". **Dated to New Year's Day, days before the turn**, and it has two halves (lives and results). | 3538–42 |
| F4 | "But all this was not the Pole. And how sadly I realised that I need not say." | 3546–47 |
| F5 | After F3 he still resolved to depot on the 4th "and then to dash for the Pole"; the depot was "a risk". | 3554–57 |
| F6 | He had to "abandon all hopes of getting nearer than 100 geographical miles to the Pole". | 3584–85 |
| F7 | 6 January: 57° of frost, blizzard; that day they reached 88° 7′ S, and "at night I had to admit that this must be our last outward march", with one more push south with the flag. Stated reason: nature and "our scanty supply of food had conquered us". | 3593–3605 |
| F8 | **Then** two days in the bags, "shrieking blizzard", food going without marching; fear the tracks to the depot would drift up. Notes chapter: blizzard from the night of 6 January to the morning of 9 January. | 3607–11; 4307–08 |
| F9 | 9 January, 9 a.m.: 88° 23′ S, 162° E, without the sledge; flag hoisted; "we turned our backs upon the Pole"; "though failure was ours, we had done our best to avoid it". | 3633–52 |

## Pairing pages Tomás may quote (checked round 3)
- *Flavor Matrix* pdf 137 (printed 127), the Grain wheel, rendered: **plum is on it**, in the Fruity arc (between apple and coconut). The Grain text page (pdf 136) doesn't list plum in its Best or Surprising Pairings. So "plum is on the Matrix's Grain wheel" is OK. Don't say "a best pairing".
- *Flavor Matrix* pdf 236, Stone Fruit: best pairings include "fruit brandy, corn … wine"; surprise pairings "Beer, sage, soy sauce". No whisky.
- *Flavor Matrix* pdf 119: a Bourbon-Roasted Fennel and Plums dessert (bourbon, plums, brown sugar, butter). That's bourbon, not Scotch, in a dish, not a drink.
- *Codex* p. 47 (printed): the cold-blended method is the **Blended Strawberry Syrup** (equal weights of fruit and sugar, blended, sieved, up to 2 weeks), not a plum recipe. "Adapted from the Codex's strawberry syrup", never "the Codex's plum syrup".
- Strength: the 47.3% abv is scotchwhisky.com's figure for the **salvaged original bottles**. The replica's own strength is only on retailer and auction pages (not trusted). The recommended relaunch is 40% (W8).
