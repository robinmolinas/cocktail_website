## Conflicts
- **C1, spelling:** *Proper* p. 241 (our extraction) reads "Seestredt"; Tales of the Cocktail and the brand press give "Seestedt". Check the printed page before the name enters any text; prefer "Seestedt" (the company founder's usual spelling) once confirmed.
- **C2, date:** "2006" for the lunch and a "two-year" development came from a search summary, and the fetched Tales text doesn't show them. Unverified: no year for the lunch. Oxford's 2008 (reaching market) stands alone.

## Guards
- **Test 4 gap:** no source says a plain gin would have cost Seestredt anything. "Better off" rests on F5 and F7 (first wave, recommended by others); the counterfactual is our reading and must be signposted.
- The verb is "advised". Never "refused", "forbade" or "said no" as quotation.
- Maraschino is `nuts`. Wondrich's curaçao-for-maraschino note is on the Improved Cocktail page (*Imbibe!* pdf 180), which is *Beside the First*'s drink: don't borrow it.
- Jerry Thomas's Martinez (1887) touches *Beside the First*'s man: keep Thomas out of the reading.
