## Contested (keep the contested detail out of the reading)
- **C1. What was in the San Francisco original.** Selvin (via Oxford): a Kamikaze with cranberry. Cecchini (Proper): Rose's lime and Rose's *grenadine*. So "he swapped the grenadine for cranberry" is his account only; "he added the cranberry" contradicts the 1987 newspaper. Safe: "he kept the name and the pink, and remade the rest", or signpost his version.
- **C2. Who made it over.** DeGroff may have made similar changes; Cheryl Cook (Florida) claimed it (Proper: improbable, Citron not out until 1988). Oxford: "most likely" Cecchini. Never "the only one".
- **C3. The year.** "Most likely 1988." Not "in 1988" flat unless signposted.
- **C4. "Sex and the City made it."** The show made it international (Oxford); it was already ubiquitous in New York by 1990. Don't credit the show with the rise.

## Pitfalls to watch
- (creator-outlaw, round 4) **Not on record:** "before everyone sweetened it" (others made it "wrong and badly", p. 43; nobody says sweeter). "Tart" rests only on F19 ("I like it shrill", Lascelles). "The people whose opinion he cared about" is our reading of "young bartenders" (p. 42). "Not to start anything" is inference. "Fashioned it as a staff drink" IS sourced (*Proper* p. 44). Codex p. 183 has its own "version from Cecchini's original", and p. 154 puts the Cosmo beside the Sidecar (p. 106: a daisy).
- (creator-outlaw) The method on record (F4): he fixed it with what his own bar already used, the Margarita treatment and the Cape Codder's cranberry. A spark taken from the guest's own shelf follows his method. A spark that changes the drink voids "made the way he makes it".
- (creator-outlaw) He shirked the credit, not the making: nothing on record says he stopped serving it. Don't write "he walked away from it" or "stopped making it". And the reason for the shirking (F15) is the peers' hatred, not the crowd.
- Not "invented": he remade something that existed (F1–F3, F7).
- "Hated" belongs to the bartenders of the 2000s, not to drinkers.
- Living person: quotes only as printed.
