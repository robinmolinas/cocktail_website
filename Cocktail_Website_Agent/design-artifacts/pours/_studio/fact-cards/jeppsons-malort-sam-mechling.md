## Legends and keep-outs
- **Carl Jeppson and Prohibition:** Oxford hedges ("reportedly proffered … as a medicinal alcohol"); NPR23 (opinion) says he convinced officials it treated stomach worms; RING says he liked offering Feds a taste. Murky, medicinal, and Outlaw ground: **out** (Wren's guard).
- **Brode's "1 out of 49 men" label line** (RING calls it "dubious"): out; also gendered.
- **Atkinson's first-sip quote** (RING) contains a swear and isn't a welcome: out. His "it's fun to make fun of it" (WBEZ) is usable colour.
- **The Halloween couple** (RING): out.

## Conflicts
- **Dates:** WBEZ puts the dare in the late 2000s and the meeting "around 2012"; RING has the comedy show in 2011 and no meeting date. **Keep years out** of the reading.
- **Gabelick's own taste:** NPR13 (2013) says it "has since grown on her"; RING (2019) says she refused to try a drop for decades and still doesn't love it. **Keep her palate out.**
- WBEZ spells him "Mechlin" once, "Mechling" elsewhere; NPR13 and RING: Mechling.
