## Guards
- The lobbying is real and large: Erenzo "launched and managed a three-year lobbying effort" for the 2007 Farm Distillery Act (*Hudson Valley Magazine*, 27 Dec 2016); "running up to Albany to lobby" (*Hudson Valley One*). It's the Campaigner's ground (Wren's Out). The result for others (F15–F17) partly runs through that lobbying, so the reading may state the counts, but never that Tuthilltown caused them, and never the Act.
- William Grant: bought the Hudson brand in 2010 and Tuthilltown in 2017 (*Hudson Valley One*). Never the ending.
- Gable's death, his foster children and his COVID hand-sanitizer effort are all on the obituary page: none of it is ours.
- "First since Prohibition" is a date, never a story (Outlaw ground).

## Conflicts
- C1 **The licence year:** "a little-known law from 2000" (*HV Magazine* 2016); "a year earlier" than his research (Erenzo, 2017); "in 2002" (AP 2007). The Farm Distillery Act is 2007 (*HV Magazine*) or "2008" (Erenzo via *Stateline*). No year goes in the reading.
- C2 **The fees:** $1,450 against $50,800 (AP 2007); $1,500 for three years against $65,000 (Erenzo 2017). No figures.
- C3 **The first whiskey:** Baby Bourbon in 2006 (*HV Magazine*; Erenzo 2017, "our first whiskey expression"; AP: on sale from April 2006); Oxford dates only the Manhattan Rye (2007) and doesn't say which whiskey came first. "Started Tuthilltown in 2006" (Thomas) vs founded 2003 (Oxford, Erenzo). Say "a few years later", never "their first whiskey was the rye".
- C4 **Who learned what:** Ralph (no prior knowledge, the internet; F9, F11), Lee (fermenting, the still; F10), Gable (from the ground up; F13). Any "they learned" sentence names which.
