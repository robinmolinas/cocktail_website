# Fact card: the Aviary's ice-sealed Old-Fashioned, and Charles Joly

Our words throughout. Quotes under 15 words, all read on screen on 2026-10-01. *A Proper Drink* (Simonson 2016), printed pp. 223, 234-235, 281-283, 328, 333 (pdf 237, 248-249, 295-297); Oxford COCKTAIL RENAISSANCE (pdf 522). Web, fetched with curl and stripped: Mike Sula, *Chicago Reader*, 26 May 2011 ("The Aviary: a defining Chicago experience"); *Time Out Chicago*, 17/18 Feb 2014 ("Know your bartender: Charles Joly at the Aviary"); *PUNCH*, 7 Nov 2017 (review of the Aviary NYC; byline metadata conflicting: Simonson / Baiocchi, cite as *PUNCH*). Tiers: Oxford primary; Simonson reliable secondary (quotes in his book are on the record, in the speaker's voice; the rest is his narration); web secondary. *Reader* 2011 is the closest to the event: a review written weeks after opening.
