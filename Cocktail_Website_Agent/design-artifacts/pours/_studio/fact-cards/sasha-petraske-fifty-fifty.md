Our words throughout. Facts are free; the sources' sentences are not. Petraske died in 2015: tell him plainly, and keep his death out of the reading.

## Conflicts
- **C1, who brought the 50-50 back from London.** Schwartz and Maloney remember Petraske bringing it back; he didn't remember it that way (Proper p. 115). Keep it out.
- **C2, whether the hidden speakeasy was intended.** Petraske said his landlord forced it; Siegal says "He wanted that" (Proper p. 89). Keep it out, or give only his account, signposted.

## Not on record (stays out)
- Whether he kept pouring the 50-50 at Milk & Honey after 2005. Nothing in the library says so either way. **Library gap for Robin:** *Regarding Cocktails* (Petraske with Georgette Moger-Petraske, Phaidon 2016; 85 house recipes arranged by family) would show whether it stayed his house Martini.
