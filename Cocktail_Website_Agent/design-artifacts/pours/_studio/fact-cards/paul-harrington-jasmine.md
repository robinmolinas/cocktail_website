## Conflicts (keep the contested detail out)
- **C1 Year.** 1990: Proper p. 58, Oxford HARRINGTON pdf 971, and Proper's chronology (pp. 54–55). "Mid-1990s": Oxford JASMINE pdf 1099 (Clarke, from Harrington's own emails, 2015–16). Two tellings by the same man. No year in the reading.
- **C2 Who said the grapefruit line.** Proper p. 58: the man it was made for, after "Wow, that's good." Oxford pdf 1099: Evan Shively, "a chef and acquaintance", the second taster, "Congratulations—you've invented grapefruit juice." Safe: "one of the first to taste it".
- **C3 Architecture school.** Proper p. 58 calls Jasmin a friend from architecture school; Proper p. 57 has Harrington begin his architecture degree *after* leaving the Townhouse; Berkeleyside (Farrell, 15 Sep 2017) has him at UC Berkeley in the early '90s, engineering then architecture. Keep "school"/"classmate" out; "a friend" stands.
- **C4 What Campari replaced.** "Angostura bitters" (Proper) vs "two types of bitters" (Oxford). Say "the bitters".
- **C5 Chez Panisse / customer.** Oxford only, inside the contested account. Keep out.
