## Conflicts
- **C1, which drink:** *Proper* p. 11 says the Bloody Bull; Oxford pdf 374 says the Bullshot. Both are DeGroff's accounts. Reading: "his beef-broth drink", or name neither.
- **C2, what the fix was:** *Proper*: lemon and lime out, orange twist and a dash of orange juice in. Oxford: orange peel as garnish, Worcestershire out. Common ground: lemon/lime was wrong, orange was right, found via beef with orange at a Chinese restaurant, and Baum then accepted it.
- **C3, the year:** Oxford "In 1984"; *Proper* has DeGroff moving back to New York in 1985 for Aurora. No year in the reading ("the mid-1980s" is safe).
- **C4, the menu order:** *Proper* p. 13 doesn't make clear whether the twenty-six-drink menu failed in service before Baum's "too ambitious" or whether "disaster" is hindsight. Say only: Baum turned it down as too ambitious; DeGroff later called his twenty-six a disaster and cut to sixteen.

## Guards
- Baum is not "kind" on the record; DeGroff calls him a son of a bitch in the same breath as the story. Never soften him into a mentor who "cared".
- "Protected" is not a source word. What's sourced: Baum refused (F5, F6, F8), it held (Baum's OK, F5; no sour mix, F6/F9), and the refused man's own verdict on what was refused (F8 "a disaster"; the orange still in his printed recipe, F12).
- Jerry Thomas (F3) stays out of the reading: *Beside the First*.
- DeGroff also appears in *Making the Calls* (Saunders under him; C1 there on the unpaid years): own it in one clause if he's named.
