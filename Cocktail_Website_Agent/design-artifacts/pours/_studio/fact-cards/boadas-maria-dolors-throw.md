## Conflicts
- **C1 Husband's name:** Oxford pdf 301 "Pep Maruenda"; bar page "José Luis Maruenda". Guest text: "her husband".
- **C2 Her name:** Oxford pdf 303 "Maria Dolors" (Catalan spelling, stated); pdf 301 and caption "Maria Dolores"; bar page "María Dolores". Use "Maria Dolors" (Oxford's stated policy).
- **C3 The bartenders' club:** Oxford pdf 301 Miquel "founder"; pdf 303 "first president"; bar page: José Luis founded it in 1962, Miquel first president. Out (Wren bans the club).
- **C4 Miquel's birth / Floridita dates:** Oxford pdf 301 "born in Cuba", put to work at the Floridita in 1908 "then owned by his cousins"; RIBALAIGUA pdf 1655: the Sala family bought it in 1918. Out (Day Job's ground).

## Not found
- **What Boadas throws.** Neither Oxford nor the bar page names a house drink or a Martini; the bar page speaks of "the cocktail shaker" and "the glass mixer". Any drink is ours. Library gap: Maria Dolors Boadas, *Los cóctels del Boadas Cocktail Bar* (Muchnik, 1990), Oxford pdf 304's source.
