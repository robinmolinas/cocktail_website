# The Clover Club of Philadelphia, and its cocktail

Sources read on the page this session: Oxford Companion CLOVER CLUB pdf 481 (whole entry), BRONX pdf 358, ALEXANDER pdf 92, JACK ROSE pdf 1089, BUREAUCRACY pdf 1643; *Imbibe!* pdf 239-240 (whole entry); *Joy of Mixology* pdf 49, 268. Web (2 visits, budget spent): archive.org search, then the djvu text of *The Clover Club: From a Club Poet's Point of View* (Philadelphia: The Clover-Leaf Company, 1884; privately printed, edition limited to about 125 copies), archive.org `cloverclubfromcl00phil`. Tier: **primary, period, the club's own book** (self-description; a humorous, flattering account of itself, so its claims about its own wit are the club's claims, not outcomes).

## Legends
- **"We have with us this evening."** The 1884 book credits the club's president, Moses P. Handy of *The Philadelphia Press*, with inventing the phrase. A club joke-book's boast about its own man: legend-grade, never told as fact.
- **Yeats's three in a row** (*Imbibe!* pdf 239, "some who even say" he kept going through dinner): out by Wren's ruling (drinking excess) and the "some say" is itself a legend marker.
- **The club's wit "took root"** (F8): the club's claim about itself. Tell as "the book claims", never as a habit or a result.

## Conflicts
- **C1, the hotel.** Oxford (pdf 481) and *Imbibe!* (pdf 239) say the club met at the Bellevue-Stratford "from the late 1880s". *Joy* (pdf 268) says that hotel opened in 1904, and the club's own 1884 book says it dined at the Hotel Bellevue. Guest text: never "the Bellevue-Stratford" for the club's early years. Say "a Philadelphia hotel", or "the Hotel Bellevue" for the 1880s.
- **C2, the founding.** *Joy* pdf 268: the Bellevue hosted the club "in 1881"; the club's own book: 1882 (after a year-long forerunner); *Imbibe!*: "from the late 1880s". Guest text: "in the 1880s", or "1882, by its own account".
- **C3, who it was.** Oxford: "an association of prominent journalists"; the club book: journalists founded it and were half; *Imbibe!*: "more lawyers". Say "founded by journalists", never "a club of journalists" alone if the members' mix matters.
- **C4, the return.** 2007 or 2008 (pdf 481) vs 2008 (pdf 1643). Say "the late 2000s".
- **C5, fences.** George Boldt is in all these pages, and the club book lists his daughter as one of its "babies" (Louise Clover Boldt, 1883): out, Regular Guy struck him. Reiner and her bar: out of guest text. Wondrich's own jokes and comparisons: never paraphrased as ours.
