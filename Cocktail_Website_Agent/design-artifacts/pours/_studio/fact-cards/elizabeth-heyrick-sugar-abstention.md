## Stand-in ruling (round 3)
- Jaggery for "East India sugar": **close, not the same.** Heyrick names only where the sugar comes from (F5), never its kind or refining; what 1820s East India sugar actually was hasn't been seen. Safe: "an Indian sugar, made the old unrefined way" (F15–F16). Never "the sugar she asked for" or "what they put in the bowl".
- Whether East India cane was worked by free labour: **contested** (the campaign said so, F17; A. Major, *Slavery & Abolition* 31:4, 2010, "'Unfree' Labour in India, 1820–1833", abstract not fetched). Out as fact.
