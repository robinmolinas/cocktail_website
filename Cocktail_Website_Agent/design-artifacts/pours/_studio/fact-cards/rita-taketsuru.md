## Legends and labels
- **L1** "The mother of Japanese whisky": a 1989 *Financial Times* line (OCR highlight), a tribute, not a fact. Never in the reading as a title.
- **L2** Anything from *Massan* (how they met, what she said, what she gave up): drama, never fact.
- **L3** Rita's feelings, reasons or words: **none on any page read.** Every motive ("she went all in", "she chose him over home") is our reading and must be signposted.

## Conflicts (keep the contested detail out)
- **C1** Inside Oxford: to Scotland 1918 (pdf 1975) vs 1919 (pdf 2156); back in Japan 1920 (pdf 1975; Itoh "autumn of 1920") vs 1921 (pdf 2156). Reading: no year for the voyage or the arrival.
- **C2** Leaving Torii: 1934 (pdf 1975) vs 1933 (pdf 2156). The move to Yoichi is 1934 in two entries (pdf 1391, 1975). Safest: no year.
- **C3** Who founded the firm: pdf 1391's sentence has "he and his Scottish wife moved … and founded"; pdf 1975 calls it "his company". Say "his company", and "they moved north together".
