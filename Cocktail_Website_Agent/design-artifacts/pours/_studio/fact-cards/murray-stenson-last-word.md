## Legends (labelled only)
- **L1, the 1605 manuscript:** d'Estrées bequeathing a "long life" formula to the Carthusians in 1605 is *brand lore* in Oxford's own words (pdf 439). "The story goes…" only; no anchor.
- **L2, Frank Fogarty:** the only link is an unnamed Detroit Athletic Club source writing in 1951 ("about thirty years ago"); Oxford: no other print source (pdf 1166). *Proper* p. 70 names him "Frank Farrell". Tell as "the book says a vaudevillian brought it", or leave out.

## Conflicts (keep the contested detail out)
- **C1** start: *Proper* "early 1970s"; Oxford first bartending job 1976 at Benjamin's, Bellevue. Say "the 1970s".
- **C2** revival year: Oxford LAST WORD 2003; Oxford MARASCHINO (pdf 1228) 2004; *Joy* (pdf 319) 2009, which looks like the date of the *Seattle Times* article it quotes. Say "the early 2000s".
- **C3** lime or lemon: Oxford and Proper's recipe lime; Proper's prose lemon. Use lime (two against one).
- **C4** Il Bistro: Oxford "nearly a decade", hired to Zig Zag 2001; *Proper* twelve years, quit 2002. Say "a long stint"; no year.
- **C5** how it reached New York: Magarian, eGullet/Kinsey, or Hess via Saunders (Proper p. 71). Keep out.
