## Legends and framings
- **L1** "Ticket to freedom" (PR p. 79): Simonson's framing, signposted if used, and never "ticket" in guest text.
- **L2** "First Mexican restaurant/cantina in Europe / the UK" (SB21 "said to be"; Pacifico's own site via TO26): a claim, not checked. Out (also *Nothing to It* owns "first").
- **L3** Mexicans rediscovering tequila through Pacifico London (Bayly relaying Hajnal): hearsay of hearsay. Out.

## Conflicts
- **C1 Restaurant or tequila bar?** Oxford's contributor note (pdf 2260) says "a tequila bar"; Oxford's own entry (pdf 743, "his first restaurant"), *Proper* p. 79 ("a Mexican restaurant"), Estes himself (DI17: "a cantina-style restaurant – big bar") and SB21 ("restaurant and bar") say a restaurant with a bar. **Ruling: a Mexican restaurant with a big bar.** The note is a one-line bio; never "tequila bar".
- **C2 Teaching**: resolved (F2).
- **C3 When Bayly met him**: Oxford 1979; Bayly himself 1980. No year in the reading.
- **C4 How many places**: 17 (OX-E, SB21), 15 more with Bayly (OX-B), 18 (DI17, BAY21). No number; "more than a dozen" at most.
- **C5 Ambassador**: 2003 (all) vs 2003 and 2006 (Bayly, different body). 2003 only.
- **C6 Motive framing**: DI17's writer says by 1976 he was "ready to share his love for Mexican culture with Europe"; *Proper* says no mission, "to support myself". Use Estes's own words only (F3, F4).
