## Legends and inferences
- **"Livy was a teetotaller who stocked the whisky anyway."** Teetotal: secondary summaries only (not verified). That she did stock it: **not on record.** Allowed only as "I like to think…".
- **"Twain's favourite cocktail was an Old-Fashioned"** (listicles): the letter calls it a cock-tail; the build (scotch, sugar, bitters, lemon) is the old whisky cocktail family, but how the lemon was used (peel or juice) is **not stated**. Don't claim either.

## Conflicts
- None between the letter and Oxford. The letter is the closer source; cite it first.
