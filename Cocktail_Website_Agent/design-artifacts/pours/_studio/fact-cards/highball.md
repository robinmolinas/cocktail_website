## Legends
- **"Duffy brought the highball to America in 1895."** His own claim; contradicted by earlier record (F3, F8). Tell it only as his boast, or not at all.
- **"Named for the railway signal" (or baseball, or poker).** Possible, never proven (C1).

## Conflicts
- **C1, the name.** Oxford favours the tall-glass reading (four fingers) on the weight of evidence but says nothing is settled. In the reading: "the best clue we have", "the barkeepers of the day said", never "the name means".
- **C2, soda quantity.** Oxford's 60–90 ml vs. modern longer builds. No proportion is "the original".

## Curiosity (dossier only)
- The earliest printed "Scotch High Ball" headline in Oxford's list is from the *Wilkes-Barre Weekly Dollar News* (1896), the town where Hugo Ensslin later worked (hugo-ensslin-aviation F-card). Coincidence only.
