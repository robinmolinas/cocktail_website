## Legends
- **The mismatched label (F4)** is true, but it's an accident-turned-good story: dossier only for creator-explorer (Wren, round 2).
- None caught yet. Watch for "Gonzalez invented using bitters as a base" (Bolognese's drink came first, F7) and for "Reiner banned it" (only his account, C3).

## Conflicts
- **C1, the year.** Oxford: 2009. *A Proper Drink*: 2008. The reading gives no year ("about fifteen years ago" is itself a rounding: avoid).
- **C2, the inspiration.** Oxford says Bolognese's recipe inspired Gonzalez. Gonzalez's own account (Proper) credits a StarChefs competition and doesn't mention Bolognese. Don't say "he took it from Bolognese" or "he came up with it alone". Safe: two bartenders, within about a year, poured Angostura by the ounce.
- **C3, the menu story.** Reiner doesn't remember it and doesn't recall the drink on Clover Club's menu (Proper p. 315). Tell it only as his account, signposted, or keep it in the dossier.

- **C4, dash vs drop.** Oxford says recipes use "a few dashes". A dash isn't a drop: say "by the dash" (or "a few shakes"), not "by the drop".
