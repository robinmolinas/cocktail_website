## Legends / cautions
- **"Botany notwithstanding"** isn't in the opinion (checked: the word doesn't appear). It's our phrase; never put it in the Court's mouth.
- **"The Supreme Court ruled the tomato is a vegetable."** Only for the tariff, and while saying it's botanically a fruit. Never "legally a vegetable" without "for tax".
- **Unanimous?** Not checked. Don't say it.
- **Poison legends** (Europeans thought tomatoes were poisonous; the man who ate one on the courthouse steps): nothing in our library. Keep out.
- **"The first tomato cocktail"**: no. Tomato juice drinks go back to the 1910s–1920s (F8), fresh-tomato cocktails are a modern category (F9).
