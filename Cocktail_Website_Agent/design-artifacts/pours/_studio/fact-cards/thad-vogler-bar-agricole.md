## Consequences for any pour
- **No "still"** about Bar Agricole, his bars or his refusing brand work. As of 2025 he sells his own label and has put his name to a canned cocktail.
- The safe, sourced endpoint is the 2019 award (A2), or the book's own time, dated ("in 2016, a book on the revival...").
- Opening year: none (A1).
