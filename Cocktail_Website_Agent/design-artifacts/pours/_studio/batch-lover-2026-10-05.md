## What the rooms found hard

**When the page and the craft disagreed**
- The rooms kept running into a choice between what the page says and what balances.
- *In Your Own Hand* and *Wide Open* kept the record and went OUT.
- *By Design* changed the rum instead of shipping a flag.
- *In Kind* departs from the ode twice (it's churned, and the ice is pounded rather than cracked), and owns both in Checks.

**When the plan was overturned**
- *Only Half Joking* put jasmine back, against the plan's own Claims line.
- *By Design* released Bacardi.
- Both are noted in `plans/lover.md`, so the claims stay true.

**Holding the line on facts**
- Hester's audits struck claims the page wouldn't carry: Zaric's "very few ever left", McMillian "intrigued", not "fell for", and no recipe "as the bar made it".
- Many fixes in the last four pours were quoted old → new and landed in a single step.

**Names**
- Holding picks until the vote settled every name in one round.
- *Worth Finding*, *Accomplices* and *In Kind* went 2–1.
- *By Design* was 3–0.

**Process**
- In lover-sage, an API content-filter error cut off Tomás's first round-2 run before he wrote anything. A fresh Tomás took the round from the same prompt.
- In lover-outlaw, close ran before Hester's step-4 turn was filed. The tick was re-filed afterwards.
