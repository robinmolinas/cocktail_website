## Robin's review and second pass

- **17–19:** accepted.
- **20:** the first rewrite had the right idea but lacked power. The revised line expresses making as a love language; accepted.
- **21:** Robin preferred the force of the original. The restored original is accepted.
- **22:** the first rewrite was weak. The revised line follows Robin's direction about the gravity of the person's dreams, drawing others into joining and building; accepted.
- **23:** Robin rejected the first rewrite as unnatural English and endorsed the original. The original is restored and accepted.
- **24:** the first rewrite lost the humour and force of the shift in perspective. The revised line puts laughter and a lasting change in perception together; accepted.
- **25:** Robin asked for more power or insight. The revised line presents sustained devotion as an entire world to discover; accepted with “I love 25 it hits”.
- **26:** the first rewrite felt nice but cheesy, and the second, “You change almost everything, and the person comes into focus”, still did not hit. A further pass offered recognition and transformation directions. Robin chose transformation and supplied the exact final wording: “You see what makes someone themselves, and change everything around to let it shine.” This wording is accepted and applied without alteration.

## Final direction for 26

The persona finds the part that makes something someone's own, then builds the transformation around it. The guest-facing reading hopes for “That's so you”, and values the feeling of being seen as worth moving everything else for.

- **Accepted transformation line:** You see what makes someone themselves, and change everything around to let it shine.
- **Earlier recognition candidate, superseded:** You make people feel like themselves for the first time.
- **Earlier transformation candidate, superseded by Robin's wording:** You see what makes someone themselves, then change everything around it.

Robin's final line connects the maker's instinct and process to its purpose: allowing the person's own identity to shine.

Earlier draft lines, superseded by this pass: 20, “The things you make tell people how much they matter to you”; 21, “You love trying things just to see what happens”; 22, “Your ideas make people want to build something bigger together”; 23, “You give the things that matter room to be noticed”; 24, “You see connections that make people look at familiar things again”; 25, “The longer you spend with something you love, the more you discover”; 26, “You see the best in someone and know how to bring it out”.
