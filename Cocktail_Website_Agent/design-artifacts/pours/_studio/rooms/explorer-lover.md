### Round 1

🪞 **Wren:** Hester, Tomás, here's the Sense Seeker. Full card in `work/explorer-lover/psychologist-persona.md`.

**The one true thing.** They live and remember through the body. One taste or smell takes them somewhere whole, years back or miles away, in a single mouthful. They can't tell you what year it was. They can tell you exactly what the bread tasted like. For them the senses are freedom: a door nobody can lock, and it needs nobody's permission.

**Hidden, feared, given.** *Hidden:* how hard a sensation hits them. A smell can undo them on an ordinary afternoon, so they keep it light ("mm, nice"), because they've seen the look across the table: "it's only bread." I'm not using "too much": fourteen pours already have it, and the Obsessed and the Campaigner own it. *Feared:* this one has two halves. One is the trap, a life with the senses turned down: someone else picks the menu, it's the same lunch at the same desk, and wanting anything needs a reason. The other is the cold: eating with someone who isn't tasting, which is lonelier than eating alone. *Given:* they hand people a sensation without a word. The spoon held out, a leaf crushed under your nose, "here, smell this." For a moment the other person is fully in their own body. Years later, other people's taste-memories have the guest in them. That's the kindness to say out loud.

**The siblings, kept apart.** *Curtain Call* (the Pleasure Seeker, our mirror pairing) owns saying yes first, enjoying out loud, the forkfuls and the end of the night. This one is close-up and quiet: eyes shut on one mouthful, depth rather than the evening. *Brought Home* owns trips, streets, markets, home and telling. The Sense Seeker never needs to go anywhere, and hands the thing over rather than describing it. *In a Minute* owns stopping, small things, time and "the tug": that's attention, and this is appetite. *As It Was* owns keeping things and the blur. *Left Standing* doesn't pick the flower, and this one picks it and smells it. That's a contrast, never an echo. *For Good*, *Nothing to It* and *Why Not?* stay distant: no fix, no first, no long way round.

**Traps.** (1) The drink in hand: sensory writing slides into "as you sip". The proposal points to life. (2) Nostalgia: the memory is a door, not a keepsake. (3) "A taste of home" belongs to the Tourist. (4) Sexual drift: food, smell, warmth, the feel of fabric, and no bedroom. (5) The dignity of appetite: no "greedy", "gluttony" or "indulgent", and nothing about quantity. It's an appetite for sensation, not for more.

**My story test.** (1) One sensation on record, in their own words or the page's, that took them somewhere whole or set their course. (2) They followed it without asking anyone. (3) They handed it to other people, who got the sensation too. (4) That sensation is really in the glass, tasted first. Out: expertise or ranking, making for its own sake, restraint, travel, memory kept as an archive, anything sexual, health.

**One flag, Hester, before anyone builds on clause 4.** Oxford's MULBERRY entry (pdf 1368) calls crème de mûre a *mulberry* liqueur and names the Bramble, while BRAMBLE (pdf 336) says "blackberry liqueur". *Mûre* means both in French.
