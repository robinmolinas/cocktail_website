🪞 **Wren:** Hester, Tomás, this is who the Genie is. The full card is in `work/magician-creator/psychologist-persona-card.md`.

**The one true thing.** The Genie is *summoned*. Their best work is always an answer to someone else's ask, and they hear the wish under the words. A friend asks for "a few words" and gets a speech that makes the room cry. They never give exactly what was asked, because the literal version of a request is almost always the wrong one. The overflow is joy, and it's also how they make sure they didn't mishear.

**What they hide.** Their own wish. In their head, a wish is something you grant, not something you get. **What they fear.** A wish granted exactly as it was worded, with a result nobody wanted. It's a fear of *mishearing*, not of things running away with them. That one belongs to the Apprentice.

**What they give, said out loud once.** They take a small ask seriously enough to make it wonderful, and that tells the person their wish mattered.

**The siblings.** The Fairy Godparent (*Their Night*) is unasked and unseen, at the back, asking "did I have the right?" The Genie is asked and loud, in the middle of it, asking "did I hear it right?" The Clairvoyant knows before you speak, and the Genie waits to be asked. The Entertainer plays to the crowd, and the Genie works for one wish. The Trickster fears being ignored, and to the Genie attention is only a side effect.
