🍸 **Tomás:** Wren, here's my answer to your question, and it comes from physics. Espresso can't be kept waiting. Arnold says its texture "dies fairly quickly", and a shaken iced espresso "must be consumed very quickly" (LI pp. 352–353). The *Codex* says fresh coffee "can rapidly deteriorate" (p. 177). So Naples can't be keeping a cup. It keeps the *payment*, and whoever asks gets a cup made fresh, like anyone else at the bar. That's the drink. It has two times in its method, and no second glass:

- **The part that waits:** a roasted parsnip syrup, made days ahead. One jar is about 400 ml, enough for twenty-odd drinks, and most of it sits in the fridge for whoever's next. It's also the spark: the *Flavor Matrix* puts coffee among the best pairings for parsnip and the other root vegetables, "most commonly roasted" (pdf 224). Carrot-cake-and-coffee territory, with brandy.
- **The part made only when someone's there:** one espresso, pulled at that moment, then shaken hard with 60 ml brandy, 15 ml of the syrup and 2 drops of salt water. It's strained into a chilled coupe and drunk straight away. That's the Italian *caffè shakerato* with a spirit in it (LI pp. 353–354), and the shake brings the espresso's foam back.

Numbers (`balance.py`, shaken): **15.0% ABV, 5.83 g sugar/100 ml, 0.29% acid, 51.9% dilution.** Strength, sugar and dilution are in range. The acid reads OUT against the sours' citrus band, but there's no citrus here. Arnold's own shaken coffee drinks run 16.1% / 8.0 g / 0.39% (Café Touba, p. 206) and 10.2% / 3.9 g / 0.29% (Boozy Shakerato, p. 354), and this sits between them. The worst case, a hot shot melting extra ice, comes out around 13.4%. `allergens.py`: **veto-free.** I've added four rows to the ingredient table: espresso (LI p. 137), aged grape brandy, salt water and the parsnip syrup. The syrup's sugar figure is my own estimate, not from a source. One flag that isn't sourced: parsnip is in the carrot and celery family, like *Not Only the Way*'s caraway.

Two guards for you and Hester:
- Never use the parsnip as a picture of people. It's in the glass because it's the thing made ahead, and never "the vegetable nobody looks at".
- In shape, this is a cousin of the Espresso Martini. It has no vodka and no coffee liqueur, and it comes from the shakerato, but the reading should never name the Espresso Martini.

The spec is in `_studio/specs/regular-guy-caregiver.json`. The draft (recipe, method, checks) is in `work/regular-guy-caregiver/mixologist-draft.md`. The closing line waits for your position, and the image brief and names come next round.
