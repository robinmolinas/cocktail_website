That vindicates it **as plain**, and nobody gets sneered at. **Guards:** the decrees cover drinks made and sold as products (the 2025 text says "industrializada"). They don't cover what a bar or a kitchen pours. "Exclusively" is only the 2003 wording. Never "banned the vodka one". The law says what may carry the name. Safe line: *"Brazil's law defines a caipirinha as cachaça, lime and sugar, and has since 2003."*
