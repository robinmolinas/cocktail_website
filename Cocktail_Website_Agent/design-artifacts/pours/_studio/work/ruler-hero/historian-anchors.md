## Guards
- Never "gave up" (*One Table*'s verb), never "Colonel", and never the "Boys" line quoted.
- The Blums are never villains, and never owners in guest text (C1). They were "of Chicago" (pdf 1105), and the drink was first attested in Chicago: if both ever appear, own the coincidence in one clause. Better, keep the Blums out.
- *Nothing to It* owns a bourbon carrying its maker's own name: keep the reveal on the lost name, and give the overlap one clause.
- Repeal gets one date clause, never the opening, and never the trade or the bars coming back (Duffy's ground).
- The Old Tub name back on a bottle today: unsourced, so it stays out (C9). That rules it out as the bottle.
