# The Nurse, rework: Wren's brief (round 1, 2026-09-30)

## Why Robin's note is right, from the person's side
The reading tells the Nurse that the people closest to them "get exactly what everyone gets. Not a bit more." Then the pour hands the Nurse exactly what everyone gets: the Irish Coffee any bar makes. The drink acts out the very thing the reading names as their quiet cost. So the disappointment Robin predicts isn't only about the drink being plain. It's the Nurse being given the house pour, one more time.

## What stays
- The person (persona read, v2 in `psychologist-persona-read.md`): care nobody has to earn; the same you for everyone; the closest people get no more.
- The story: Sheridan, a few important visitors, then everyone off the plane (F1, F2); fame spoiled it (F4).
- The position (y5): let someone be special. Closing line (Tomás): *Two cubes for everyone. For one person, ask how they take it.*
- The title block, unless the drink change forces a move.

## What changes: the brief for Tomás
The drink turns from "the house pour, done right" into **the house pour with one thing that's only the guest's.** Sheridan's drink is for everyone. This one is the version made for the person who never gets more. In the reading, y4 changes from "Nothing extra for the important guest" to something like: this one has one thing Sheridan's doesn't, because you're the one who's been getting the house pour.

Tests for that one thing (the person's side; the drink is Tomás's):
1. **A treat, not a job.** It must not add work or a skill the Nurse would have to perform for others. Make-ahead is fine if it's easy; it must not be the gesture (the family already owns made-ahead: the Craftsman's cordial "made before anyone asked", the Healer's syrup jar, the Nanny's syrup).
2. **Not in the sugar.** The sugar is the Campaigner's argument (*Not Too Polite*) and the Nanny's allowance (*Fair Measure*), and our cubes carry the closing line. Leave the two cubes alone.
3. **Not a remedy.** No honey, thyme, menthol, bark or anything that reads as medicine (Rescuer, Healer, Doctor). The persona's brands (Vicks) point there; don't follow them.
4. **Not F4 again.** Not bigger, not syrup, nothing from a can. A garnish on top that you'd only give the VIP was the old objection; it doesn't apply the same way now, but the thing should belong *in* the drink, not sit on it as decoration.
5. **Ordinary and warm.** Regular Guy: something from a kitchen cupboard, a flavour a layperson knows, nothing precious or costly. It should taste like being looked after, not like being impressed.
6. **Where I'd look:** the cream. It's the part everyone meets first, the soft top, and no sibling has touched cream. A cream that carries one quiet flavour (infused ahead, still whipped fresh per glass) keeps "whipped fresh each time" true. The coffee is the other place. Tomás's call.

## Fences on words (swap test, before drafting)
- *Quite Alive* (the Sculptor) owns "what made it someone's" and made-to-measure for one person. The Nurse's version is not made to one person's measure; it's the one time the carer is on the receiving end.
- *The Rest of You* (the Angel) owns "an evening that's only yours". Don't lean on "only yours".
- *Four Shares* owns "allowed to be looked after too". Don't say it.
- "nobody sees", "whoever", "shortcut" stay out (first pour's lessons).
