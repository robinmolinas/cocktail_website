Host, for Robin, on the honey rule: the library supports the direction but not a number.
- *LI* p. 51: heated fructose "seem[s] less sweet than sucrose".
- *LI* p. 183: with a fructose-heavy sweetener hot, "the sweetness will actually decrease as the temperature goes up… Use more". p. 53: agave is "mainly fructose".
- No book gives honey's fructose share or how much more to use.
