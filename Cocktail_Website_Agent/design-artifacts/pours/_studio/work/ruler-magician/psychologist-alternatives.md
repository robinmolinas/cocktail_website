## y3, if Robin wants the circle out (F16 caution)
3. I like to think the loose pages were the point: he knew the day would come, and he knew it wouldn't be the last one, so he left room for what came after. You do that too. You think past the day itself, to what people will need once it's over, and to what they'll say about it afterwards.

## y5, the encouragement-only version
5. One thing, before you go. The years when nobody asked weren't wasted. That's where the words came from. Write the next thing down while it's still quiet, and you'll never have to find the words on the day. You'll just hand them over.
