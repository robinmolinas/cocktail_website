# The People's Champion: Tomás's draft (rework v1, rounds 2-4, 2026-10-06)

Spec: `_studio/specs/regular-guy-ruler.json`. The first pour's drink (the bourbon Rickey) and its draft are archived as `mixologist-spec-rickey-2026-10-01.json` and `mixologist-draft-rickey-2026-10-01.md` in this folder. Robin's note: the drink must not be a Rickey (*Somewhere to Land* is). Story kept as Wren ruled in rework round 1: Bobby Heugel and the packer's cases (*PUNCH*, 2 April 2014). This drink carries her five conditions: fresh lime measured, never counted; fruit you can taste; a reason the measure matters; the lemon as the honest alternative; no Key lime.

**Glass:** highball glass (about 350 ml), ice cubes added last
**Contains:** veto-free

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 60 ml | vodka | any vodka at 40% (37.5% also holds) |
| 22.5 ml | fresh lime juice | measured, not counted: any size of lime will do, so buy the odd-sized ones if your shop sells them cheaper. Or use lemon, if the lemons taste better that day |
| 20 ml | simple syrup | equal weights of sugar and water, stirred until clear. Measure it as carefully as the juice |
| 50 ml | soda water | straight from the fridge |

## Method

1. Keep the soda water in the fridge until the moment you pour it. Warm soda loses its bubbles before you get to drink them.
2. Squeeze a lime and a lemon. Taste a drop of each juice. Use whichever tastes better today, and if anyone asks, tell them why.
3. Measure 22.5 ml of that juice into a shaker with 20 ml of syrup and 60 ml of vodka. Measured out, it makes no difference what size the lime was.
4. Pour 50 ml of the cold soda water into the empty glass.
5. Shake with ice for about five seconds, just long enough to chill it, and strain it onto the soda. Pouring it in mixes the drink, so there's nothing to stir.
6. Fill the glass with ice cubes last. No garnish. Drink it while it's cold.
