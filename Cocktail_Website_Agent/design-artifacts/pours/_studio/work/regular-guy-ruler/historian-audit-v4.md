- In one clause, if history is wanted: one of three drinks that, early in the 1900s, made it acceptable to put citrus juice in a cocktail (pdf 1089).

- "I make it with apple brandy" (C1: the classic on Oxford's page is applejack).
- Never the 1912 murder case, never "named after" as fact (the Jacqueminot rose is "apparently", F5), never Haas as inventor.
