# The Little Prince (innocent-ruler): drink v1, Tomás r2, Checks and closing line revised r3, Hester's T1–T4 landed r4 step 1 (2026-10-04)

Spec: `_studio/specs/innocent-ruler.json`. A dry gin Martini, thrown: poured from an ice-filled tin into an empty one and back, four or five times, the technique Miquel Boadas brought to Barcelona, which his bar kept going after it had fallen out of use (Oxford pdf 301, 303). Nothing on record says what Boadas throws, and there's no house Martini on the page (Hester, card F1–F17), so this is never "her recipe" or "the Boadas Martini": it's a dry Martini made their way. Measured with a kitchen tablespoon, three of gin to one of dry vermouth, no jigger. Served small, in a small wine glass, with a thin strip of lemon peel dropped in, as the thrown cocktails of the 1840s were.

**Glassware:** small wine glass (about 120 ml), chilled in the freezer, no ice
**Contains:** veto-free

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 45 ml (3 tablespoons) | London dry gin | any London dry gin of 40 to 47% |
| 15 ml (1 level tablespoon) | dry vermouth | from a bottle opened recently and kept in the fridge; a full tablespoon, not less |
| 1 thin strip | lemon peel | about 5 cm, no white pith |

## Method

1. Put the wine glass in the freezer for 10 minutes. Take the dry vermouth from the fridge.
2. Fill one shaker tin three-quarters with ice and fit the strainer on it. Measure in the gin and the vermouth with a tablespoon.
3. Stand over the sink. Hold the empty tin low in one hand and the full one just above it in the other. Pour the drink through the strainer into the empty tin, lifting the full tin a little as you pour, so the stream gets longer. A forearm's length is plenty. The ice stays behind the strainer.
4. Bring the tins back together and pour the drink straight back through the strainer onto the ice.
5. Do it four or five times in all, until the tin with the ice is cold to hold.
6. Strain it into the cold glass. Squeeze the strip of lemon peel over the top so its oils fall on the drink, then drop it in.

**closingLine:** *Four or five throws, then stop while it's cold. Make it for someone, and watch their face at the second sip.*
