## whoYouAre

You rewrite a message three times, and every time it gets shorter. The last version looks like it took a minute. Most of what you make is what you said no to.

You don't want less. You want to feel each thing properly, and too much of anything drowns it out. Some people mistake the quiet for coldness, or for plain. The ones who know you know it's room: what you make leaves space, and people find their own meaning in it.

## yours

In the mid-1980s, Julio Bermejo was back behind the bar of his family's restaurant in San Francisco, an ordinary neighbourhood place, pouring Margaritas the usual way: fresh lime, but sour mix too, cheap tequila, orange liqueur. Then he tasted a tequila made only from agave, and decided the bar would pour nothing but tequila made that way, at about four times the cost. He made that call, he says, "as a young person with no authority".

Over the years the sour mix went, and then the orange liqueur, with agave syrup in its place. What was left was tequila, lime, a little agave, and nothing to hide behind. He called it the house Margarita. Other people gave it the restaurant's name.

I like to think you'd recognise those years: all that work, to end up with less. Nobody thanks you for what isn't there.

So this one keeps his three ingredients and his habit of thinning the syrup with water, so none of it clings to the spoon. The measures are mine. The syrup is blue agave, the same plant the tequila comes from.

Sometimes you cut one thing too many, and it's usually the one that was only there because you loved it. The salt on this glass isn't needed. It's there on one side, in case you want it. Leave one thing like that in whatever you make next. Not because it's needed. Because you like it.

## Choices and doubts
- **Shortest in the set:** five short paragraphs in `yours`, two in whoYouAre. History is paragraphs 1–2 (under half).
- **Denny's left out** of v1. It lengthens the story and, read cold, sounds like the sting. Hester, if you think it earns its clause, argue it; otherwise dossier.
