# Tomás: drink draft, regular-guy-creator (The Hidden Talent), v2, round 4 (gin re-keyed to 41.3% per Hester T3; page fixes T6, T8; band labelled as the tool's, T2; nuts reason restated; "liquorice" in all guest text; the drink is unchanged)

Spec: `_studio/specs/regular-guy-creator.json` (v1). A gin Old-Fashioned built in a plain kitchen tumbler: Tanqueray London Dry, a liquorice root syrup, Angostura. The syrup brings forward the liquorice that's said to be one of the gin's four botanicals.

**Glassware:** plain kitchen tumbler (about 250 ml), three or four ordinary ice cubes from the freezer tray
**Contains:** nuts (the liquorice root, on the safe side; see Allergens)

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 60 ml | Tanqueray London Dry gin (recommended) | the gin Tom Nichol looked after; or any juniper-led London dry gin, 40% to 47%. If your bottle says 47% or more, use 50 ml |
| 10 ml (2 teaspoons) | liquorice root syrup, homemade (below) | brings forward the hint of liquorice the gin is said to have already |
| 2 dashes | Angostura bitters | |
| | *for the syrup:* 100 g white sugar, 100 ml water, 5 g dried liquorice root (about two finger-length pieces), chopped or snapped small | makes about 160 ml; keeps two weeks in a closed jar in the fridge. Dried liquorice root is sold in health-food and tea shops. Check the pack for a nut warning. Liquorice sweets won't do: they aren't the root |

## Method

1. Make the syrup: put the sugar, water and liquorice root in a small pan and bring it just to a simmer, stirring until the sugar is gone. Let it simmer gently for 5 minutes, then take it off the heat and leave it for 30 minutes. Strain out the root and let the syrup cool.
2. Put the syrup and the bitters in a plain tumbler first, then pour in the gin.
3. Add three or four ordinary ice cubes and stir for about 20 seconds, until the outside of the glass feels cold. Then stop: the cubes keep melting as you drink.
4. Nothing goes on top.

**closingLine:** *Make it where everyone is, not in the other room. Someone will ask about the finish.*
