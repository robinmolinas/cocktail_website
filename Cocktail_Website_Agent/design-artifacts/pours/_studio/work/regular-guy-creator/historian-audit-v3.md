## Name (final position)
I'd pick ***Right Here***, with *Close to Home* a close second. The case: *Close to Home* is true to the record (he lived a couple of miles from where he was born, F14), and Wren's double meaning is real. But it shares its key word with the same family's *Brought Home* (explorer-regular-guy, The Tourist), a pour whose whole reading is about home ("Home is half the trip for you"). Two Regular Guy siblings would then carry "Home" in the title. *Right Here* has no echo of that kind, and the record supports it (the gin team "there", F6). It's a taste call, not a fact, so I won't hold up the room over it. My sign-off covers both names, and Robin picks.

## Lessons for the sanctum
- "Of his own" (my R4 wording) needed a second source before I could pass it: my own fix words get audited like anyone's.
- The trade's own definition of a title (Oxford MASTER DISTILLER) can carry the counterweight to a "being seen" moment. Read the entry for any title a story leans on.
