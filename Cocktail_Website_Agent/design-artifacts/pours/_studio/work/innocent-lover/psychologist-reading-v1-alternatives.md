## y5 (the drink paragraph) if the flamed peel stays

The cocktail I've made for you is a close relation of the Manhattan, one of the world's best-known cocktails. It starts from the Brooklyn Club's: aged rum with sweet vermouth, the version that apparently never left the room. Where Grohusko's had a little bitter orange aperitif, mine has a spoonful of a dark Italian amaro that tastes of burnt orange. It's stirred cold, rich and warm from the rum, with a bitter-orange edge, poured into an ice-cold cocktail glass. I've added one thing, and it comes last, in front of whoever's there: a wide orange peel, squeezed through a lit match, so its oils flash for an instant and land on top. If the first match doesn't take, you strike another. As a nod to you, the Brooklyn that stayed indoors is the one that now announces itself.
