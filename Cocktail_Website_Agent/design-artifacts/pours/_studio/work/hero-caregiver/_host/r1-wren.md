**The one true thing.** The Saviour takes on the cost in someone else's place, calmly and on purpose: the bill, the harder half, the phone call nobody wants to make, the mistake that was only partly theirs. Plenty of people owe them, they never collect, and any kindness done to them gets paid back, with interest, by Friday. No sibling has this: generous without limit, and unable to owe for even a day.
