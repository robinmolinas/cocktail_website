# The Wise Kid (innocent-sage): persona card, Wren r1 (2026-10-04)

## Who this is
Source: essence "a naive observer who sees the truth of the matter"; story: says the thing everyone else is too clever to say, asks the simple question and waits for an honest answer; Marcus seeing through Will's cool and teaching him to care. Goal safety, fear punishment. Wound: "being lied to by people they trust, or told that the truth is rude."

**The one true thing:** they tell people what they'd want to be told themselves. Everyone at the table saw the spinach, the price tag, the colleague's name said wrong for a year. They're the one who says so, quietly, to that person, with no point to score. They never learned the rule that you let it go.

**What they hide:** the half-second after. They've said it, and they wait to find out if they're in trouble, because once (more than once) they were told it was rude. And the matching wound: finding out that people they trust saw them getting something wrong and let them carry on, to be nice.

**What they fear:** being told off for the truth (punishment); being humoured.

**What they give (say it out loud, Robin's rule):** the chance to fix it while it's still small. Being told by them doesn't feel like being caught; it feels like being treated as someone who can take it.

**Position (Ryu, earned):** stop waiting for the frown, and ask for the same back: tell the people you trust you'd rather know.

**"This is me" line (draft):** "You tell people what you'd want to be told. Then, for half a second, you wait to see if you're in trouble." (check "half a second" against 4 pours that use it; may need "for a moment".)
