4. The cocktail I've made for you is a Martini, one of the two classics he built that bar on, made the generous way: more vermouth than gin, like one of the ten Martinis on his first list. (Vermouth is wine steeped with herbs and spices.) A bartender from New Zealand, Jacob Briars, remembered Gin Palace as the first place he'd seen orange bitters, and also the first time he'd seen anyone use "more than a dropper" of Noilly Prat, a French dry vermouth. So both are here, and the vermouth gets the bigger share, as a nod to all the Martinis it was rationed in. It's stirred long over ice, then poured into a big, chilled V-shaped glass, gentler and silkier than most Martinis.
