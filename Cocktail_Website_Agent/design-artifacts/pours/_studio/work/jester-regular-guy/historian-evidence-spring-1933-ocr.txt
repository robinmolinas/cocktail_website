¶ THIS and THAT By William A. Spring The Bartenders Guyed
¶ The army of bartenders is ready. With the repeal of prohibition, that artist whose background is brightly-shining glassware, whose anecdotes are of the highest order, and whose hair in the old days always sported fancy twists and turns given only by clever manipulation of the comb, returns to his former prominence. Not since the days when the boys left for Over There to save Democracy have the mixed-drink specialists anticipated such a heavy business. Of course here in Yonkers we have many talented performers, some veterans and some newcomers. But, if the licenses come through, and the folks have the money, all sorts of queer concoctions will be served up whether they must be quaffed sitting, standing, or lying, The boys are ready.
