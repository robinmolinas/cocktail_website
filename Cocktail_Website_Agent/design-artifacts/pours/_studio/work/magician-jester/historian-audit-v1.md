## Watch, for the reading (round 4)
- The glass: ours is "about 75 ml". Oxford's 1903 glass is "about 45 ml" (pdf 1558), and its sample recipe says a 2- or 3-ounce glass (pdf 1559). Never "the 1903 glass" for ours.

- Port as "a red wine" is Tomás's gloss (fortified). Never "port is a red wine" as fact in the reading; "port, a fortified wine" is safe.
