# Tomás: draft v1.3, ruler-lover (The Magnetic Leader)

Round 5 (v1.3: Hester's H4–H5 landed in Checks; drink unchanged). Round 4. Story ruled by Wren (r3): Ciro Capozzi's plain Manhattan, and the regular who brought something of his own. Facts per Hester's card (`fact-cards/ciro-capozzi.md`, F1-F20) and her r3 rulings. The drink is unchanged from v1.1. This version updates the Checks wording (spark sources, counterweights), the closing line and the names. Spec: `_studio/specs/ruler-lover.json`. **The drink:** the Manhattan Ciro brought to Europe (Oxford CIRO'S pdf 460; MANHATTAN pdf 1224), on rye, stirred, served up, lemon peel. One change: a small square of fresh pineapple, from a cocktail of the prince's own (London *Evening Telegraph*, 21 July 1887, per Oxford pdf 1562; *Imbibe!* pdf 231), pressed in the mixing glass with the half teaspoon of sugar syrup it needs to sit in a stirred drink, then strained out.

**Glassware:** stemmed cocktail glass (about 180 ml), chilled, no ice.
**Contains:** veto-free.

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 small square (about 2.5 cm, 15 g) | fresh ripe pineapple | cut from near the base, where it's sweetest; no bigger, or the drink turns sharp |
| ½ tsp (2.5 ml) | rich sugar syrup | 2 parts sugar to 1 part water, stirred until clear |
| 60 ml | straight rye whiskey | any straight rye, 40% or stronger; the numbers were run on 50% |
| 20 ml | sweet vermouth | a red Italian-style vermouth |
| 2 dashes | Angostura bitters | or any aromatic bitters |
| 1 | strip of lemon peel | squeezed over the top, then dropped in |

## Method

1. Put the cocktail glass in the freezer, or fill it with ice and water while you mix.
2. Put the pineapple and the syrup in a mixing glass or jar. Press the pineapple with a muddler or the end of a rolling pin until its juice runs.
3. Add the rye, the vermouth and the bitters. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
4. Stir for about 30 seconds, until the outside of the glass feels very cold.
5. Empty the cocktail glass. Strain the drink into it through a small tea strainer, so no pineapple gets through.
6. Squeeze the lemon peel over the top so its oils fall on the drink, then drop it in.
