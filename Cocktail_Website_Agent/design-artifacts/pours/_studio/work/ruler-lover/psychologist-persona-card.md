# Persona card: The Magnetic Leader (ruler-lover)

Wren, round 1, 2026-10-01. Sources: persona.py row (essence, story, goal Control, fear Revolution, drivers "provide structure / BUILD CONNECTION"), the Ruler plan row, my sketch (`plans/work/ruler/psychologist-sketches.md`), sibling readings, and Oxford CIRO'S read whole this session (pdf 460–462, plus pdf 491 and 1331).

## The one true thing
They lead with their attention, one person at a time, and they know exactly what it does. The name remembered, the question about last time, the second longer: it's real, and it's also how everything holds together. Both are true at once, and they've never thought that was a contradiction.

## Hides
- That it costs. Being fully there for each person is effort, and there are nights they'd give anything not to be "on".
- The question they never say out loud: **if I stopped, would they still come?** Do people follow me, or the way I make them feel?

## Fears
The night it doesn't work. Someone looks past them, the warmth doesn't land, the room drifts, and the order that rested on it comes apart. (Their "revolution" is the room turning away quietly, not a riot.)

## Gives
People leave feeling they were the one who mattered, and around them people feel important enough to offer something of their own. Say it out loud once (Robin's rule): that is a real gift, and few people can give it.

## Story test
1. A real person whose hold on people was face-to-face hospitality, on the page.
2. The warmth was real, and the order or fortune of the place rested on it.
3. Cost or doubt: on the page, or signposted ("I like to think…").
4. What holds: it was more than a spell. What they started outlasted their being there to perform it.
Out: seduction, a con, charm as manipulation, a stage performance, a flourish the drink acts out.
