Story: Ciro Capozzi's little bar in Monte Carlo, the regulars who brought him their own cocktails, and the house that kept going after he sold it. Everything read on the page this round in the Oxford Companion (primary). Card: `_studio/fact-cards/ciro-capozzi.md` (F-numbers). No web source read (Gallica 403; both visits spent).

## Guards
- Never the 1912 Paris bar-room fact as his (F10, Wren's strike). Never "where he isn't, they leave".
- Royalty: "the Prince of Wales" may appear once (later references by pronoun), as a name; no other royal word anywhere (no king, crown, court, royal, reign). Never "sitting kings", "one-percenters", "royalty". The Prince of Wales cocktail (pdf 1562) is never named and never linked to Ciro.
- Jerry Thomas: his boast, disputed, or nothing (C1). *Beside the First* holds Thomas.
- Ciro's London and its head bartender (*For Kicks*), Leo Engel, the wildcat Ciro's: out.
- No face-to-face scene is on the page (C3): "one at a time", the name remembered, the eye contact, all signposted.
- His drink's champagne (Wondrich's "probably sabered" magnum is a guess, pdf 230), the mistresses (pdf 230), the 1901 book's title and the Monte Carlo gin-pineapple Prince of Wales (pdf 1562, unlinked to Ciro): out.
