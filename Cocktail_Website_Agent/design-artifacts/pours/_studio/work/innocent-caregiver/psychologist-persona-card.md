# Persona card: The Big Sibling (innocent-caregiver)

Wren, round 1, 2026-09-30. Sources: `persona.py innocent-caregiver` (xlsx row and both archetype lines), the family plan row (`_studio/plans/innocent.md`), the sibling pours in the registry, and a phrase sweep of every pour and plan (below).

## Core truth
They started looking after someone before they knew how to look after themselves, and they learned how by doing it for someone smaller. The care isn't a role they chose as adults. It's a reflex from when they were small too, and it never switched off.

## Shows / hides
- **Shows:** the smaller hand held at the crossing, the last biscuit saved, the calm voice when something goes wrong, the spare jumper.
- **Hides:** that they were scared too, and still are. And that when anything goes wrong for the people they love, part of them is already working out what they should have done. That's the Innocent's *Punishment* fear, read properly: not fear of being punished by someone else, but the verdict they'd pass on themselves if anyone got hurt on their watch.

## Hidden fear -> hidden gift
- **Fear:** someone hurt on their watch, and it being their fault.
- **Gift:** the people they love have always had someone calm in the dark with them, so the dark got smaller. Say it plainly once (Robin's 2026-09-30 rule): the calm they give is how the people around them learned to be calm.

## The "real me" line to aim for (draft)
> You were never less scared of the dark. You just had someone to be calm for.

Kept off "brave" on purpose (see traps). To be ngram-checked against every pour before it's used.

## Truth vs. legend
The kind story, and a true one. The Innocent wants honesty and trust, so no con and no big reveal: a mirror that's simply true, told gently. Legends are fine when labelled, but the anchor has to be real.

## Tone
Honest, optimistic, straightforward (Innocent), with compassion (Caregiver). Tender with a spine: the fear is real, so the reading can say so. A little warmth about the habit is allowed (they know they're like this). No sugar: the imagery on the sheet (Disney, Dove, blankets) mustn't make the reading sweet.

## Distinct from siblings
- **The True Friend** (*Four Shares*, approved): makes sure nobody is left out; struggles to receive. Its ending ("keep one cup back for yourself") and the landed line "You're allowed to be looked after too" are the obvious Big Sibling ending. Both are off limits.
- **The Nanny** (*Fair Measure*): structure and limits so the fun lasts; its whoYouAre already has "who asks for a text when they're home".
- **The Rescuer** (*Off Duty*): acts first in a crisis; owns "You don't feel brave", hands shaking once "everyone's safe and home", lying awake afterwards.
- **The Campaigner** (*Not Too Polite*): owns "the people who didn't have to be brave on their own" and shaking hands after speaking up.
- **The Little Prince** (Innocent plan): owns "kept it alive". **The Expedition Leader** (Explorer plan): owns "everyone gets home" and "let someone else count heads".
- **This one:** the watch began in childhood, for someone smaller, and their calm was learned for that person. Nobody else in the 132 carries the child keeping watch over a child.

## Traps
1. The True Friend's ending (receiving care, keeping one back).
2. "Getting home" as the heart. The sheet says they still check that everyone got home, but the Nanny (the text), the Expedition Leader (the headcount) and the Rescuer (safe and home) already hold it. A passing scene at most.
3. "Brave" / "scared too" framed as the Rescuer's or the Campaigner's (shaking afterwards, not facing it alone).
4. Birth order. An only child or a youngest can be this person: never "brother", "sister", "your siblings", "eldest".
5. Diagnosis or blame: "you grew up too fast", "you never had a childhood", absent or careless parents. A guess, and it wounds.
6. Sacrifice as the gift: being used up so others can grow.
7. Rules, limits, the killjoy who's really why it's safe (the Nanny).
8. Anyone hurt, ill or dying in the story; medicine (the Healer's).
9. Phrases already owned: "before anyone asked" (*Not Only the Way*'s epigraph), "breathe easier" (*Before the Room*'s tagline), "You've already gone" (*Off Duty*), "Someone had to…" (*Not Too Polite*).

## Story test
1. Someone or something looks after the younger or smaller, by nature, unasked and unappointed.
2. A watch kept over time (nights, winters, years), not one rescue.
3. The ones kept safe come through and go on into themselves, carrying something of the watcher, perhaps without knowing it.
4. A cost or effort on the page that isn't self-erasure.
Out: crisis rescue, rules and rationing, medicine, the shared bowl, "kept it alive", hosting, Petraske (the Lover family's), smuggling, ships, anyone hurt on the watch.

## Verdict on the family plan row
- **Rye lead: replace as the story.** Oxford RYE (pdf 1714-1715), read. It's the Underdog's shape (scorned as "a very inferior grain", taken for a weed, grows on any soil, found to be useful), which is Vadrna's row one over. It protects nobody smaller. The "volunteers" hinge is our pun, and the plan says so. The cost on the page, being ploughed back in "as a manure", is sacrifice as the gift (trap 6). And the people it fed thought little of it; they didn't grow on from it (clause 3). Rye may still be poured if Tomás wants it.
- **Robertson backup: no.** Heirs, not watchers; the motive is contested, so the reading couldn't say why; a company, not a watch.
- **Standing candidate, read on the page: flor.** *Cocktail Codex* p. 245: sherry casks are filled only four-fifths full, so a layer of yeast called flor grows on the young wines, a protective barrier over them that keeps the air off; it feeds on the wine and gives sherry its taste (fino, manzanilla); for amontillado it's the first step, and then the wine ages on in the air. Passes (1) a cover over the young, (2) for years, (3) gone from the glass but still in its taste, and the wine goes on without it, (4) it's fed as it feeds, with no sacrifice. **Costs:** thin history on our shelves (Oxford mentions flor once, BRANDY DE JEREZ pdf 346), so Hester needs a web source that it forms on its own and for how long fino ages under it; and it's a living thing in the barrels, next to the Free Spirit's black fungus on warehouse walls, in the same family. Guards: never "mould" or "fungus"; no cellar walls, no darkness-as-wonder; no solera talk (*A Brother's Care*); nothing about the flor dying.
- **Preferred if found by round 2:** a person who passes all four clauses (the guest sees themselves more easily in a person). Web sources are fine at secondary tier.

## Position (the reading's, to be settled with the story)
Not rest (*Off Duty*), not being looked after (*Four Shares*). Two candidates:
- **Tell one of them you were scared too.** Honest, Innocent, and it gives something back: being known, and not carrying it alone.
- **Trust what you taught them.** The watch worked; the proof is that they don't need it. (Mirrors flor: what you gave goes on with them.)
