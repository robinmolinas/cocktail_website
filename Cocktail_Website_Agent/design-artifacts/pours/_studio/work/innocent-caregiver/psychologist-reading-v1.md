## whoYouAre

You were small too. People forget that part. You were the one holding the smaller hand at the crossing, saving the last biscuit, staying awake because someone littler was frightened of the dark. No one had asked. It just seemed obvious that the people you love should be safe, and if nobody else was watching, you would. You were never less scared of the dark. You just had someone to be calm for.

You still do it. When something goes wrong, the calm voice comes out before you've decided anything. And underneath it, whenever someone you love gets hurt, part of you is already asking what you should have seen, as if it had been your watch. Here's what that gives them, and it deserves saying out loud: the people who grew up near you learned from you that the dark can be got through. Some of them are calm in it now, and they couldn't tell you where they got it.

## yours

1. Fino is the driest sherry there is, and it's made in a way that surprises most people. Its barrels are never filled to the top. Soon after the young wine goes in, a pale layer of yeast grows across the surface. Nobody adds it: the people who make sherry say it comes from yeasts already in the air of that part of Spain. It's called flor, and it stays over the wine for years.
2. What it does is keep the air off. A little air always finds its way into a barrel, and air is what would spoil a young wine. The sherry makers put it plainly: the air that gets in is used up by the yeast's breathing, and the wine underneath stays protected. So whatever would harm the wine reaches the flor first. I like to think that's the whole job. Not a rescue. Just being the one it reaches first, every day, for years.
3. That's what you've done since before you were tall. The fright, the bad news, the thing that would have spoiled their night: it gets to you first, and by the time it reaches them, it's smaller. Most of the time they never knew it was there. That's how you've always cared. The people you love have felt safe without ever having to think about it, and that was you.
4. The cocktail I've made for you is built on fino. Fino is delicate: pour a loud spirit beside it and it disappears, so everything else in the glass is there to sit around it and never drown it out, the way the Bamboo, a famous classic from the 1880s, was built. The flor itself is left in the barrel. You can still taste it, though. The fresh-bread smell and the faint almond at the finish are its work. *(Provisional: the spark and the one clause tying it to the story come once Tomás has a spec.)*
5. So pick one of the people you watched over, one who's grown now, and tell them about a time you were scared too, back when you were the one being calm. They can carry it now. I think they'll be glad to know what reached you first, and prouder of you than you'd guess.
