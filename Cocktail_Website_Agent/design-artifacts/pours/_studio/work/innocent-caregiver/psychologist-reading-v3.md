## whoYouAre

You were small too. People forget that part. You were the one holding the smaller hand at the crossing, saving the last biscuit, staying awake because someone littler was frightened of the dark. No one had asked. It just seemed obvious that anyone you loved should be safe, and if nobody else was watching, you would. You were never less scared of the dark. You just had someone to be calm for.

You still do it. When something goes wrong, the calm voice comes out before you've decided anything. And underneath it, whenever someone you love gets hurt, part of you is already asking what you should have seen, as if it had been your watch. Here's what that gives them, and it deserves saying out loud: the ones who grew up near you learned from you that the dark can be got through. Some of them are calm in it now, and they couldn't tell you where they got it.

## yours

1. Fino sits at the driest end of sherry, and it's made in a way I still find surprising. Its barrels aren't filled to the top. Once the young wine is in the barrel, a pale layer of yeast grows across its surface. It forms by itself: the people who make sherry say it comes from yeasts already in the air of that part of Spain. It's called flor, and it stays over the wine for years.
2. What it does is keep the air off. There's always air in the space left at the top, and a little more comes through the wood, and air would turn a fino into a different wine, darker and heavier. The flor sits between that air and the wine and breathes up most of the oxygen itself, so the wine underneath stays protected. The air that would change the wine reaches the flor first. I like to think that's the whole job. Not a rescue. Just being the one it reaches first, every day, for years.
3. That's what you've done since before you were tall. The fright, the bad news, the thing that would have spoiled their night: it gets to you first, and by the time it reaches them, it's smaller. Most of the time they never knew it was there. That's how you've always cared. Everyone close to you has felt safe without ever having to think about it, and that was you.
4. The cocktail I've made for you is built on fino. It's delicate, and beside anything loud it can get lost, so nothing strong goes in with it: just a little dry and a little sweet white vermouth around it, the way the Bamboo was built, a famous classic already on record by 1886. The flor itself is left in the barrel, but you can still taste its work: the dry, yeasty edge and the hint of almond in the smell are mostly the flor's. I sweetened it with a teaspoon of honey steeped with chamomile, the tea people make at bedtime, for all the nights you stayed awake so someone smaller wasn't alone in the dark.
5. So pick one of the people you watched over, one who's grown now, and tell them about one time back then when you were scared too, while you were the one being calm. They can carry it now. I think they'll be glad to know what reached you first, and prouder of you than you'd guess.
