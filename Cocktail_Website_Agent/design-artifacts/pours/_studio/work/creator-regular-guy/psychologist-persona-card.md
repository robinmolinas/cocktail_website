# Persona card: The Photographer (creator-regular-guy)

Wren, round 1. Written from `persona.py creator-regular-guy`, the ten Creator siblings, the approved Visionary and True Friend (the other Regular Guy pour), the Sculptor draft, the registry and my voice ledger.

## Core truth
The Photographer makes nothing up. Their creativity is noticing: they see that an ordinary Tuesday was beautiful while it's still happening, and they keep it. Not to be called an artist (they'd hate that), but because they can't bear for a good ordinary day to go by as if it never happened.

## Shows / hides
- **Shows:** "Hold on, don't move." The phone out at the kitchen table, not at the landmark. Friends tease them for it. They remember what everyone ordered, the light that evening, the joke nobody else kept.
- **Hides:** two things. First, the fear underneath the habit: that days blur, that a whole year could go by and look like one day. Keeping them is how they hold on. Second, how much it matters to them. They'll say "it's just a photo", "it's nothing". Making a fuss of what they do feels like showing off, and they'd rather not stand out.

## Hidden fear → hidden gift
- **Fear:** the blur. Sameness, the status quo, life passing unnoticed (Creator: mediocrity, conformity), and, from the everyday half, standing out, being "the artist" (Regular Guy fears individuality). So they create from behind the camera, never in front of it.
- **Gift:** they hand people their own lives back and show them it was worth keeping. Affection without flattery: they love things as they are, not prettier than they are.

## The "real me" line to aim for
Draft: *"Everyone keeps the big days. You keep the Tuesdays."*
Alt (the funny one, a scene): *"You'd never call it art. You'd call it 'hold on, don't move.'"*
Position seed, *not* an ending yet: they undersell it. "It's never just a photo." Show people what you saw.

## Truth vs. legend
The true story, **unretouched**. They'd distrust a legend the way they distrust a filter. The unglamorous detail is the one they'd love. Kind, because they're affectionate, but never prettier than it was.

## Tone
Creator (inspirational) + Regular Guy (conversational, genuine, supportive) → warm, plain, a little amused. The bartender talks like a friend who's noticed them noticing. Humour allowed (the teasing about the phone). No grandeur, no "artist" talk.

## Distinct from siblings
- **The Sculptor** (creator-caregiver, draft) *makes* something new for one person. The Photographer adds nothing: they keep what's already there, for anyone.
- **The Visionary** (creator-hero, approved) lives in the future, big and handed on. The Photographer lives in the present, small, right now.
- **The Minimalist** (creator-innocent) takes things away; the Photographer frames what's there. **The Old Master** (creator-sage) chases depth of craft; the Photographer would refuse the title. **The Obsessed** (creator-lover) is in love with the making; the Photographer is in love with the subject: ordinary life.
- Cross-family: **the True Friend** (innocent-regular-guy, approved) owns belonging, "nobody left at the edge", "you're allowed to be looked after too". Not our ground.

## Traps
- **The literal photographer.** The name says it; most guests don't own a camera. Scenes must be phone-in-hand or simply *noticing* (the light on a kitchen table). No lenses, apertures, darkrooms as metaphors for the guest's life.
- **The label.** "You're the one who takes all the pictures" as a title, the way "the unofficial sommelier" failed. Show the scene, don't hand them the role.
- **"Put the phone down and be present."** A horoscope, a lecture, and it attacks the gift. Never.
- **The ending that's already taken three times.** "Step into the picture / let yourself be seen" collides with the True Friend ("be looked after"), the Sculptor ("let them have you, making it") and, in spirit, the Visionary. The Photographer's position has to be something else.
- **Nostalgia as sadness.** The ordinary is loved, not mourned. Wistful, never melancholy.
- **Flattery:** "you have such an eye". Say what they *do* with it.
- **A dressed-up drink or story.** Smoke, glitter, a showpiece garnish, a legend passed off as fact: all filters.
