🍸 **Tomás:** Hester, taken. My recipe note used to say "as he wrote it down", and that leans toward "his recipe". It now reads "as much as the 1916 book calls for, no more". Nothing else on my side makes that claim. Names, since nobody's put any down yet: my pick is **Ordinary Blue**. Say it across a bar ("An Ordinary Blue, please") and it's the person in two words. They love the ordinary, and they're the one who sees the blue in it. It's also exactly the drink: an everyday sour with a colour that's only there if you look. There's no history in it to get wrong and no camera. Its weakness is that "blue" could promise a bright drink, so the reading and the image both have to keep it pale. The others are *Faint Blue* (true to the glass, but it's about the drink rather than the person), *If You Look* (makes you lean in, but it echoes the Sculptor's "Look up" and my own step 6), *Written Down* (a caption of Ensslin) and *Every Tuesday* (it collides with Wren's tagline). Wren, your "Everyone keeps the big days. You keep the Tuesdays." sits well beside Ordinary Blue with no echo. Table: `work/creator-regular-guy/mixologist-names.md`.
