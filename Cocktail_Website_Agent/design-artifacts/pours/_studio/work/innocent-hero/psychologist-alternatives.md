# Alternatives: The Underdog (innocent-hero), Wren, round 3

## Taglines
- **Everyone else could see how far you'd come. You were the last to hear.** (pick: intrigues, a compliment they'll half-refuse)
- You never played it any other way. (short; risks echoing the name *Straight Up*)
- You got there the long way. Nobody can take that off you. (speaks to the fear directly, but reads as reassurance before trust is earned)

## Epigraphs (about the cocktail, cold, ≤16 words)
- **Mango and juniper, from far apart, have one note in common. It was always there.** (pick: 15 words. The pine link is Tomás's spark; it teases "it was always yours" without depending on the story)
- The first thing asked for was a shaker. Everything else came later. (story-side; the shaker was asked for, not got, so it holds)
- Set aside: Tomás's "A shaker, asked for the day after a film. The package came fourteen years later." It's false and dated: 1994 to 2007 is thirteen years, Simonson's 2008 is the slip (Hester C1), and the room tells no year anyway.

## Names
- **Straight Up** (Tomás's pick, and mine): served up, and how this person has always played it. Sayable at a bar. The reading says "straight" once (whoYouAre ¶3), so the name doesn't spend a line.
- **No Catch**: the position, too plainly. Better left implied by the closing line.
- **Last to Hear**: spends the tagline.
- **The Long Way**: the road, honestly told. "long way" is in y5, so it would spend that.

## y5 (the position), other ways in
- (v1) So the next time something good finds you, don't search it for the catch. You remember how you got there, and nobody handed you any of it. Someone near you is watching to see whether the long way pays off. Show them it does: let the happiness come first. The checking can wait, and I'd bet it never finds anything.
- (shorter, encouragement only) Next time something goes your way, say thank you before you say how. You know how. You always have.
- Set aside: "tell the road as a story, never as a defence" (explorer-jester's proposal owns "sounds like a defence").
