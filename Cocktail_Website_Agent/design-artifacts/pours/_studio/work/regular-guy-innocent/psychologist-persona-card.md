# Persona card: The Bumpkin (regular-guy-innocent)

Wren, round 1, 2026-10-01. Source: persona.py (xlsx row), the Regular Guy plan row, sibling readings on file.

## The one true thing
Their pleasure shows, and it's a choice. They know what the fancier option is; they aren't pleased because they don't know better. Plain taste, held openly, in rooms where taste is how people decide who belongs.

## Fear (goal: belonging · fear: exclusion)
Not being shut out: being let in as the sweet one. The half-smile, the "aw", their delight filed as not knowing better. Included as the mascot, not as one of them.

## Hidden
They've learnt the words and can nod through the wine talk. In some rooms they turn the delight down, and once or twice they've ordered the clever thing to keep their seat. It costs more than anyone guesses.

## Gift (to be said plainly, Robin's rule)
When they don't turn it down, everyone else can stop performing. Enjoying things gets cheap to join: no knowledge required at the door.

## Position
Keep saying it first. Gain: they get to enjoy it, and find out how many were waiting for someone to.

## Working "this is me" lines
- You know what the fancy one is. You just like this one more.
- It isn't that you don't know better. You know what you like.

## Siblings / neighbours (keep apart)
- True Friend (*Four Shares*): makes sure nobody's left out (others). Bumpkin: their own taste costing the seat.
- Naive Artist (*The Way It Felt*): unaware of the rules, the "how it's usually done" explainer, things they make. Bumpkin: fully aware, chooses plain; no explainer scene; tastes, doesn't make.
- Discoverer (*In a Minute*): small wonders in nature, stopping to look. Bumpkin: enjoying, not attending.
- Photographer (*As It Was*): ordinary days kept. Minimalist (*Half a Rim*): stripped back, "plain". Hidden Talent (*Right Here*): secretly gifted. Tourist (*Brought Home*): food finds. Stand-up: "nobody's the only one".

## Traps
- Reverse snobbery: never sneer at the fancy thing or its fans.
- Our own condescension: no "simple", "naive", "easily pleased", "childlike", "uncomplicated", "bless"; never "bumpkin" in the reading; no rural life assumed.
- "Out loud" is in 40+ pours. "The bread" is in two (Minimalist, Sense Seeker).

## Story test
1. A plain thing enjoyed openly by ordinary people.
2. On the page, the fashionable try to improve it or look down on it.
3. It stays plain and is vindicated as plain, never as secretly sophisticated.
4. Its people are never the butt; the improvers aren't sneered at either.
5. Best: it takes the one new thing that's actually better and leaves the show.
