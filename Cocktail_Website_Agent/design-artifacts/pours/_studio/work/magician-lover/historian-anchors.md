## Lines the page doesn't hold (struck or signposted)
- "The strongest Martini in town / in London" (Wren's read): no comparison on the page. Use A3.
- "Its effect worried even its makers" (Wren's read): Oxford says only that the strength "necessitated" the rule.
- "Quiet" room: Oxford says "intimate"; "tranquil" is the 1988 headline's. Use "intimate", or credit the review.
- "Said out loud" / "told you the truth" / "the house never made it weaker to make it safer": our reading, signposted.
- "As famous as the drink": no; "a famous house rule".
- "Long a refuge" (my plan): no span on the page. Mine, struck.
- Any maker named; any date for the rule; Mayfair; Palazzi in the present tense.
