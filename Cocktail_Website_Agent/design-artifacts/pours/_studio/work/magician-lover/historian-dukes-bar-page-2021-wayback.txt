DUKES Bar
The DUKES Bar martinis are legendary and as quoted by the New York Times, ‘one of the world’s best.’
If the walls of this intimate space could tell a thousand stories…
Every Martini or personalised cocktail served at the internationally acclaimed bar is an occasion, with our
world class bartender team at the helm. Whether visiting for the first time or more, a special rendezvous
with
friends, a romantic meeting with a lover or a relaxing moment after a meeting, DUKES Bar provides the
setting
for an unforgettable experience.
From here, retire to the Drawing Room or the
Cognac and Cigar Garden
for the final finesse of a perfect
Mayfair
evening.
The DUKES Bar team look forward to welcoming you for your very own DUKES Martini Experience on your next
visit.
OPENING TIMES
Monday to Saturday: from 3pm to 10pm (Last order at 21:15)
Why not dine with us prior to visiting the DUKES Bar? Guests can reserve a table in our
Great British
Restaurant (GBR) and Bar
