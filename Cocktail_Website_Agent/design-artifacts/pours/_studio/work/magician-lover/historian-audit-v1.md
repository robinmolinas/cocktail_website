| # | ask | ruling |

| T1 | *Codex* page for the vodka-vermouth line | The line sits in ch. 2's "The Classic Recipes", after the Gin Martini and before the e-text's p. 61 marker. | T2 | "A home freezer runs about −18°C" | **Fix.** Arnold gives "typical freezer temperatures" as −20°C (*LI* p. 155). He calls −18°C the too-warm end, and his own home freezer is at −23.5°C (p. 140). Say "about −18 to −20°C" or "about −20°C (*LI* p. 155)". |

The page covers over 40%, not exactly 40%. In Checks, cite p. 140 with that scope and keep the exactly-40% case labelled as yours, unsourced. "Pours thick" has support: high-alcohol drinks left in the freezer go "syrupy" (*LI* p. 305). |
