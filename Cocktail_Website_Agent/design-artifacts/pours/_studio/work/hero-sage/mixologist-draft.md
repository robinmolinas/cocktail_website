# hero-sage · The Problem Solver · Tomás's draft (v1, round 2, 2026-10-04)

Spec: `_studio/specs/hero-sage.json` (serves 6). Story ruled by Wren in r1 (Cox and the first Daiquiri, Oxford DAIQUIRI pdf 621, 623); Hester's facts audit runs in parallel, so any story word below is held until she rules.

**Glassware:** six small tumblers (about 180 ml), chilled in the freezer; a pitcher (about 1.5 litres)
**Serves:** 6
**Contains:** veto-free

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 300 ml | white rum, light and dry (Spanish-style, unaged or lightly aged, 37.5-45%; not spiced, not sweetened) | the kind of rum that was there when the gin wasn't |
| 100 ml | fresh lime juice (about 3 to 4 limes), measured | |
| 50 g | caster sugar (granulated works, it takes longer) | dissolved in the lime, no syrup: rum, lime and sugar, nothing else |
| 4 | makrut lime leaves, fresh or frozen | lime's scent, without more sour; they come out before you pour |
| about 500 g | crushed ice (a bag of cubes in a tea towel, hit with a rolling pin) | |

## Method

1. Put six small tumblers in the freezer for 10 minutes.
2. Pour the lime juice into the pitcher, add the sugar, and stir until no grains are left on the bottom: about a minute.
3. Tear each makrut leaf in half and drop the halves into the lime. Press them against the bottom of the pitcher with a muddler or the end of a rolling pin, five times each, until they smell strongly of lime.
4. Add the rum.
5. Fill the pitcher about two-thirds with the crushed ice. Swizzle for 30 seconds by the clock: push a long spoon down to the bottom and work it up and down through the ice, turning it as you go.
6. Lift the leaves out with the spoon.
7. Pour straight away into the six tumblers, ice and all, along the row and back, so every glass gets the same.

**closingLine:** *Pour all six in one go, along the row and back. And the next time the plan falls apart, admit you're having fun.*
