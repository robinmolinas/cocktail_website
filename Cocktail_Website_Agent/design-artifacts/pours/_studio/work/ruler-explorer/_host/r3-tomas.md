**The drink.** The Lemon Drop, served the "more elegant" way the original was (F19): up, in a sugar-rimmed coupe.
- **Recipe:** 60 ml vodka (40% or stronger, never citrus), 22.5 ml fresh lemon, 22.5 ml simple syrup, shaken.
- **The glass:** half a teaspoon of dry apricot eau-de-vie, the clear barack pálinka or Marillenbrand kind, as in the Bell Ringer (Joy pdf 41). It wets the outside of the rim so the fine sugar sticks. What's left goes into the glass and is swirled until the inside is coated. Nothing gets tipped out.
- **Numbers:** balance.py says **all in range, no edges**: finished 15.3%, 8.67 g sugar, 0.82% acid, dilution 52.3. Poured on after the shake instead, it's 15.4 / 8.76 / 0.83, still in the shaken range. Without the apricot, the same drink sits on two edges, so the coating also pulls it into the middle.
- **Versions rejected:** Cate's printed 60/30/45 goes OUT (acid 1.35, 12.2%). Vodka at 37.5% gives three edges, so the floor is 40%. The sweet apricot-flavoured brandy edges on sugar (9.15), so the recipe says eau-de-vie.
