# Drink v3 · jester-ruler (Tomás, round 4 step 1; v2 round 3, v1 round 2)

Spec: `_studio/specs/jester-ruler.json` (drink unchanged since v1; v3 changes wording, the closing line and the dossier). Built because Wren accepted the plan's lead in r1, with conditions.

**glassware:** large plain tumbler (about 350 ml), three ice cubes
**contains:** `[]` (veto-free)

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 50 ml | straight rye whiskey | ideally about 50%; 43% at the least |
| half | a fresh lime | half a normal-sized lime, squeezed; the squeezed half goes in too |
| 105 ml | Apollinaris sparkling mineral water | recommended, plain (unflavoured), cold; or any plain sparkling mineral water with a noticeable mineral taste |
| 3 | ice cubes | ordinary cubes, not crushed |

## Method

1. Squeeze half a lime into a large tumbler, then drop the squeezed half in after the juice.
2. Add three ice cubes, then the rye.
3. Top up with the cold mineral water, poured gently so it keeps its bubbles. Stir once.
4. No sugar, and nothing on top.

**Owned near-misses.** *Word Gets Round* (the People's Champion) is a bourbon Rickey: bourbon, 15 ml measured lime (or lemon), soda, crushed ice, highball glass, nothing in the glass. Ours differs in five places: rye, not bourbon; the colonel's mineral water, not soda; half a lime by eye with the shell in, no measure and no lemon; three cubes, not crushed ice; a large tumbler, not a highball glass. The category is shared, which Robin allows; the cocktail isn't. *With the Bite In* (outlaw-jester, this family) closes on "Squeeze the lime and drop it in", and its method drops the shell too. Here the shell is the Rickey's own definition (Oxford pdf 1661), not our gesture: it stays in the method and out of the reading's emphasis and the closing line. *Day Job* closes on "Half the lime, squeezed by hand": my method says neither "by hand" nor makes the half a gesture. *Fresh Air* is a heavy tumbler with two or three cubes, but aged rum and no lime. *Serviceable* owns "Pour the soda slowly, down the side": my step 3 says "poured gently". *Worth the Trip* (gin · highball · tall glass) and *Next One's Mine* (rye · tall glass): off by base or glass.
