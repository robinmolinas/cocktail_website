# Tomás, round 2: drink leads (no spec, no balance until Wren rules)

## The card in the photo (his handwriting)

Source: Kathryn Price, "The 'Juice of a Few Flowers'", *Gastronomica* 7:2 (2007), fetched page, second image:
`https://gastronomica.org/wp-content/uploads/2007/05/juice_flowers_image2.jpg` (orig. 2131x1594).
Caption on the page: "Gerald Murphy's recipe for the 'Juice of a Few Flowers,' his signature drink, and his recipe for a Manhattan. Gerald and Sara Murphy papers, Yale Collection of American Literature, Beinecke..."

My transcription of the left card (Hester to verify against the image):

> Juice of A Few Flowers
> 1 oz. orange
> 1 oz. grapefruit
> 1/2 oz lime
> 1/2 oz lemon
> 1 oz. gin
> shake & pour into: moisten rim of glass w/ lemon and dip in coarse sugar

The right card: "Manhattan. Dry: 2/3 whiskey, 1/3 Italian Vermouth, 2 dashes Angostura, Olive, Twist lemon peel. Sweet: same as above add sugar + cherry + orange peel twist." It's on printed stationery: "The Westbury, A Knott Hotel, Madison Ave. at 69th St., N.Y." (the card isn't dated; the page gives no date).

The Bailey (text, footnote 4: Murphy to Woollcott, 15 February 1939, Beinecke): gin (Booth's House of Lords), grapefruit juice, fresh lime juice, fresh mint, ice ("a great deal"). The page gives no amounts and no sweetener.

## The two drinks, as a maker's drink

| | Juice of a Few Flowers (card) | Bailey (letter) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| His measures | yes, all five | none; mine would have to be invented |
| Sweetener | coarse-sugar rim, moistened with lemon (his) | none on the page; I'd have to add one |
| Bottle | "gin" (a London dry style, no named bottle) | Booth's House of Lords (current bottling unverified) |
| His own words | the name, in his hand | "invented by me as were a great many other good things" |
| Collisions | rim: *Half a Rim* (name/motif), Old Master's lemon-wiped rim (method) | mint: the Romantic's julep; Joy pdf 73 has a Gordon's "Major Bailey, a sort of gin-based Mint Julep" (Hester: name coincidence to own or leave out) |

My lean: the card. Every measure in the glass is his, so the drink carries the maker without my hand in it.
Possible single touch (not decided): the Bailey's fresh mint, shaken in and strained, so every ingredient still comes from one of his two recipes.

## Head-count only (not a balance run)

1 : 3 gin to juice, 120 ml before ice, about 10% ABV before ice; four citrus, so sour. I expect a long, light, juice-led drink below any sour range, with the rim bringing the sugar sip by sip. Proof comes after the ruling: `freeform` or `shaken`, rim sugar modelled as a range.

## Guards for Checks, later

- Grapefruit interacts with a number of medicines (Oxford CITRIC ACID, pdf 468, cites Bailey, Dresser and Arnold, "Grapefruit-Medication Interactions", CMAJ 2013). It isn't a veto; a plain note in Checks.
- The rim goes in the method, never in the name or the motif (*Half a Rim*).
- Glass: the plan reserves a nick-and-nora. About 170 ml once shaken, so a larger one; to size at spec.
