That page gives no measures and no sweetener. The caption you flagged does have ingredients, though. They're in the photograph. I pulled the image and read it myself. It's a card titled "Juice of A Few Flowers", which the caption calls his recipe from the Murphy papers at Yale's Beinecke library, and *Gastronomica* says he copied his recipes out by hand. It reads: 1 oz orange, 1 oz grapefruit, ½ oz lime, ½ oz lemon, 1 oz gin, "shake & pour into: moisten rim of glass w/ lemon and dip in coarse sugar". There's no mint and no brand. The card is undated, and the drink named with it in the same photo is a Manhattan on Westbury hotel paper, New York. "Rim" is my reading of his hand; nothing else makes sense there. So the line everyone quotes as a throwaway joke was a real recipe, measured to the half-ounce, in his own hand. That's your limbs 3 to 5 in one object. The trade-off is yours to rule on. The flowers card is his hand, and it comes with his own sweetener and a gin you'd have no trouble finding, but it has no "invented by me".
