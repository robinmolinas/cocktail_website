## Recipe

**glassware:** two nick-and-nora glasses (small, stemmed), chilled, each rim dipped in coarse sugar on the outside
**contains:** `[]` (veto-free)
**serves:** 2 (his measures fill two small glasses, about 97 ml each)

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 ml (1 oz) | London dry gin | any; the card names none |
| 30 ml (1 oz) | fresh orange juice | |
| 30 ml (1 oz) | fresh grapefruit juice | |
| 15 ml (½ oz) | fresh lime juice | |
| 15 ml (½ oz) | fresh lemon juice | plus the cut lemon for the rims |
| 22.5 ml (¾ oz) | coarse-sugar syrup (below) | the same sugar as the rim, some of it dissolved in the glass |
| a saucer | coarse sugar | for the rims |

**Coarse-sugar syrup:** stir equal weights of the same coarse sugar and hot water until clear, then let it cool. It keeps for a couple of weeks in the fridge.

## Method

1. Make the syrup: stir equal weights of coarse sugar and hot water until clear, and let it cool.
2. Put two small stemmed glasses in the freezer.
3. Cut a lemon in half. Wet the outside edge of each glass with the cut side, then dip that edge into a saucer of coarse sugar. Keep the sugar on the outside, so none falls into the drink.
4. Squeeze the orange, grapefruit, lime and lemon fresh, and measure them into a shaker with the gin and the syrup.
5. Fill the shaker right to the top with ice and shake hard for about ten seconds.
6. Pour it through the strainer, half into each glass.

**closingLine:** *Pour both glasses. Hand one over with something you've made, and let them ask about it.*
