## Guards (keep out of guest text)
- **High Holborn closed within a year** because the restaurant "didn't succeed the way the bar did" (pp. 102–103). Never tie the closing to his standard, and don't use it as his cost.
- "He was a classicist" is Simonson's label (p. 102), and Wren bars it. The same goes for "beat the Martini into Collins's brain", which is Simonson's idiom.
- "I wanted to do something different from LAB. LAB was a party bar. This was for serious drinkers" and "Muddled up, crushed ice … bang!" (pp. 101–102) stay out.
- No Porn Star Martini or LAB as the article's target; the page doesn't say what the article named.
- Oxford calls him a "brand ambassador" and says Mixxit was developed "for Maxxium UK". Both are left unsaid, never denied. Navajo Joe's "reputedly" and the TV slot stay out. The White Negroni (Proper p. 106 says 2001, *Codex* p. 89 says 2000: a conflict) stays out.
- **"About you" collision:** Chappell's line contains "It's not about you", and Wren fences "about you" off for the Craftsman (*Not Only the Way*). Paraphrase it in the reading ("it was for the guests, not for him"), and keep the verbatim line in the dossier only.
- Living person: "as he told Simonson in 2014" (p. 330), not "by 2016" (my plan row's slip: 2016 is the book's date).
