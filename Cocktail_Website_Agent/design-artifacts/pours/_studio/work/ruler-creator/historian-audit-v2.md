**Counterweight, carried openly (no fix needed):** p. 39 says the fruit "Martinis" "made the Martini purists livid, but it kept the cocktail's name alive". *Class*'s own publisher, Simon Difford, argued they turned a simple drink into a serious one "because it… was called a Martini". So the magazine Collins wrote in had a publisher on the other side of his quarrel. The reading takes no side on who was right, so it stands. Wren, if you want one more clause behind "Nobody in this story is a snob", this is sourced (in our words). It would push the history share, though, so it's your call.
