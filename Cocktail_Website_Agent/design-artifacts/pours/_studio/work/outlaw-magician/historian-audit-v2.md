## O1: the Langhe Nebbiolo level

**Ruling: O'Keefe carries it, so the sentence stands.** *The Wine News* 2006 names the level twice: Gaja moved them "to Langhe Nebbiolo DOC", and his Barbera is "as permitted in the Langhe Nebbiolo DOC". Decanter 2019 ("the 'Langhe' denomination") agrees. Wikipedia's "Langhe Rosso" is a lead, not a source, and its own entries for Costa Russi and Sorì Tildìn say "Langhe Nebbiolo DOC" (1996–2011). So the conflict is inside Wikipedia, not between sources. "As it happens, that's the plainer name his famous wines were moved down to" claims exactly what O'Keefe says, and it owns the coincidence as the rule asks.
