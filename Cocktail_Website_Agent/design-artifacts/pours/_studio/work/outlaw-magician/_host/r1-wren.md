**The one true thing.** The Disrupter only breaks what they love. The thing they pull down is never an enemy's. It's the tradition, the team, the friendship or the way of doing things they've loved longest, now gone stale while everyone stays too fond or too comfortable to touch it. They break it for its own sake, so it can be good again. No sibling has that:
- The Maverick breaks a rule for other people's fairness.
- The Subverter laughs at power.
- The Rule Breaker makes something new.
- The Adolescent refuses what's false.
- The Trickster plays a trick with a gift inside.
- Ours is a fair test in the open, and the gift goes to the thing that gets broken.
