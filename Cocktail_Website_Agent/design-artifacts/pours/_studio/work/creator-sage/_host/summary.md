- **The persona in one line:** the Old Master is respected enough that nobody questions their best work, so they question it themselves; what they dread isn't being wrong, it's settling into being right.
- **Story chosen:** Jerry Thomas, who wrote down the standard Cocktail in 1862 and, in the 1876 edition, added "Improved" Cocktails at the back of the same book: maraschino in, a dash of the fashionable absinthe (*Imbibe!* pdf 17, 173, 179–180); the Improved Whiskey Cocktail is, apart from the rinse, the Sazerac, now New Orleans's official cocktail (Oxford pdf 1750, 1753). **Runner-up:** Murray Stenson and the Last Word (Hester's first pitch).
- **What failed and why:** the story swapped twice. In round 2 Wren moved to Stenson as Hester moved to Thomas; in round 3 each took the other's. The room settled on Thomas once Tomás showed a corrected Last Word would pour *As It Was*'s twin (gin, lime, coupe, a 1916 drink rescued from a book). Tomás's first v0 (a rebalanced Last Word) was withdrawn. His passion fruit spark (*Flavor Matrix*, Grain, pdf 136) was dropped after Wren called it a second change: "change one thing". Hester struck two of her own shorthands ("new bottle of the decade": absinthe was on record in 1843; "printed beside": the Improved went in a supplement at the back) and cut superlatives. Tomás's *Imbibe* pdf 176 find (no curaçao in Thomas's whiskey cocktail) corrected p4. Lint: a we/ours voice error and overlaps with *Still Yours* and *As It Was*, all fixed by Wren.
- **"This is me":** *"What you dread isn't being wrong. It's settling into being right."* Position: change the thing you're best known for, keep the old one beside it, and let people choose.
- **Names:** *Beside the First* (pick, all three), *Fourteen Years On* (Hester; alternative for Robin), *Not Settled*, *The Supplement*, *Still Improving* (dropped: echoes *Still Yours*), *Second Edition*, *One Change*, *The Spoonful*, *The Improved One*, *Second Printing*.
- **The drink:** an Improved Whiskey Cocktail after Wondrich's composite: 60 ml rye, 7.5 ml plain syrup, ½ tsp orange liqueur (Wondrich's swap for the maraschino, which keeps it veto-free), 2 dashes Angostura, and 1 tsp green Chartreuse where Thomas put his dash of absinthe; shaken and strained into a small chilled coupe with lemon on the rim. 26.9% / 5.11 g, balanced, no edges; veto-free; the registry's first rye base. Closing line: *"Taste it next to the one you already know. Then decide."*
- **Edges:** no *Flavor Matrix* spark in the glass (consulted and set aside; the spark is the Chartreuse slot), the same kind of flag as *Half a Rim*; the serve is Wondrich's reconstruction; *Rosetta*'s image also has an open book.
