## Dossier only (true, kept out of the reading)
- *Flavor Matrix* Grain → passion fruit (pdf 136, verified) was consulted and set aside for the person (a second change). Spark = the Chartreuse in the dash slot.
- The "Bogart's" to "Boker's" fix was a printer's slip (Oxford BITTERS pdf 274): never "he fixed his mistake".
- Thomas "differed with himself" on shaking vs stirring (pdf 178): unused.
- The 1887 revision is by an unnamed bartender after his death (pdf 17); *Joy* is wrong to call it his second book (C1).

## Wording guards for the reading
- "Fashionable bottle", never "new bottle" (F18). Also in Tomás's recipe note ("his dash of the new bottle").
- "Grew out of a monks' medicine", sourced to Oxford pdf 439, not *Codex* p. 176 (that page describes the herbal-liqueur family: "most of these").
- "Printed beside" = in the same book (a supplement at the back). Never "right beside" / "on the facing page".
- "One change" is true only against Thomas's recipe *with Wondrich's curaçao swap* (F9): say "one change of mine" and credit the orange to the historian. Never "the orange liqueur his first cocktails used" in a whiskey drink: his whiskey cocktail had none (pdf 176).
- "Wrote down the standard" or "wrote the book on the standard", not "wrote the standard" (he didn't invent it).
- Serve: Wondrich's composite is **shaken** (F11). Dash sizes (⅛ tsp etc.) are Wondrich's readings (F12): never "his ⅛ teaspoon".
- "Monks still make": no. "Still made under Carthusian monks" (F19).
- No absolutes about his shelf ("never had passion fruit"): not on record.
