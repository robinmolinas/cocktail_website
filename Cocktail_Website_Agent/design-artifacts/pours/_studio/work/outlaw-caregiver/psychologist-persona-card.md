# Persona card: The Gentle Giant (outlaw-caregiver) — Wren, round 1

**Essence:** could flatten the room, and chooses to hold it. Tenderness is the rebellion.

## The one true thing (no sibling has it)
Their gentleness is not temperament. It is a decision, made every time, by someone who knows exactly how much force they have. Everyone else in the family pushes outward; this one's rebellion is refusing to use what they've got.
- "This is me" candidate: *People think gentle comes easily to you. It doesn't. You decide it, every time, and you've never told anyone how often.*

## Hidden / fear / gift
- **Hides:** the effort, and the anger under it. They are angry at bullies and at unfairness, and they keep it on a very short lead. Also hidden: they know the room reads them as the one who *could*, and they use that without ever cashing it.
- **Fears:** powerlessness, specifically the day their strength isn't enough to protect someone. Not being hurt: being unable to stop it. (Second, quieter fear: being mistaken for soft, as though holding back meant they couldn't.)
- **Gives:** safety without a fight. Bullies go quiet, nobody has to shout, the smallest person in the room relaxes. Say it out loud once (Robin's batch-caregiver rule): *that calm is what you give people, and it's worth more than any fight you'd win.*

## Siblings and neighbours (swap test)
- **Campaigner** (*Not Too Polite*, "Someone had to say it"): speaks up, loudly. The Giant rarely raises their voice; the quiet is the point.
- **Big Sibling** (*Night Light*, "You were small too"): protects from being small. The Giant protects from being strong.
- **Gangster** (Olmstead, "rather lose a shipment than a life"): a code against force belongs to that row. Ours never frames restraint as a rule or a code.
- **Maverick** (Solarin): breaks a law for others and takes nothing. Ours doesn't break the law; it holds power and won't use it on the wrong people.
- **Understanding Spouse** (*Standing By*, "You're still here"): staying with one person. If our position is about staying, it's for a room, a night, never a partner.
- **Saviour** (*Next One's Mine*): takes anything on. Ours is restraint, not taking on.

## Traps
- **Never physical size or the body** ("big hands", "your frame", "you tower"). Power = presence, conviction, rank, reputation, the thing they hold. Many guests are small.
- **No gender** (the giant is not "a big man"). BFG stays out of guest text (it invites both).
- **Pity toward the protected:** the people shielded are never "the small ones", never weak (my plan guard, kept).
- **Cliché diminutives:** no "big softie", "teddy bear", "wouldn't hurt a fly". They condescend.
- **No violence, no fists, no near-fights**, not even averted ones.
- **Registry words:** "door" (a dozen pours), "Someone had to", "still here", "anyway", "You're allowed to", "hold back" (*Left Standing*), "held back". No "the easy thing" in guest text more than once.

## My story test (four points)
1. The person holds real power over the situation (a licence, a rank, a reputation), and the people affected don't.
2. The easy course was to use it against them or step aside; they chose to protect, quietly, without a scene.
3. It cost them something real.
4. **New for this read:** the story reaches the moment the power ran out, and shows what they did with nothing left. That's the fear met, and it's where the position comes from.

## Verdict on the plan's lead (Stoumen, the Black Cat)
**Accept, and it's stronger than the plan knew:** point 4 is on the page. The licence (his power) was taken on Halloween 1963, and he stayed open that last night serving soft drinks. That is powerlessness, met with tenderness: nothing left to fight with, and he still kept the room for them.
- Reading lands on two beats only: he could have shown them out (it was his licence), and the last night.
- The 1963 soft-drinks night must have a second page before it carries a paragraph (plan condition kept). If it falls, the lead loses point 4 and the drink with it; the backup (Roosevelt's six cents) passes restraint but fails points 1 (protects no one) and 3, so it would be a weaker story, not a rescue.
- Guards kept: his sexuality never stated; no raids; Holocaust detail out; the customers are adults whose rights were taken, never "the small ones"; Sarria a clause at most.

## Position (my lean, to earn in round 2)
The fear is the day strength isn't enough. The story answers it: the strength was never the licence. **When you can't stop it, stay with them.** Keep the room warm on the last night. Ends on what they give: being there is protection too, and people remember who stayed open.
Guard: not *Standing By* (a partner, "still here"): ours is a room, a night, a crowd.

## Tagline directions (person, positive, invites)
- You never need to raise your voice. Everyone knows you could.
- You could clear any room. You'd rather keep it.
- The strongest thing about you is what you don't do.
