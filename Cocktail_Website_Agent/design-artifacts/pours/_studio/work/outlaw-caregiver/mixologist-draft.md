# Tomás: drink draft, outlaw-caregiver (The Gentle Giant), v1.2, round 6 (Checks and image wording fixed per Hester's audits: A11, A16, A14, T1, T2, T8, T9; the drink is unchanged)

Spec: `_studio/specs/outlaw-caregiver.json` (v1). A Horse's Neck: VSOP-style cognac made long with pale dry ginger ale, and the whole lemon peeled in one spiral.

**Glassware:** tall glass (highball, about 300 ml), filled with ice
**Contains:** spice

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | whole lemon, unwaxed | for the peel only: one unbroken spiral |
| 45 ml | VSOP cognac | any VSOP; deeper than a VS, so you taste it under the ginger ale |
| 120 ml | pale dry ginger ale, cold | not ginger beer, and not a sweet golden ginger ale |

## Method

1. Peel the whole lemon with a vegetable peeler in one long, unbroken spiral, from top to bottom, taking as little of the white as you can. Go slowly: if it breaks, it still works, but the one long piece is the drink.
2. Lower the spiral into a tall glass and hook its top end over the rim. Fill the glass with ice, which holds the peel against the sides.
3. Pour in 45 ml of cognac.
4. Top with 120 ml of cold ginger ale, then lift the drink once, gently, with a long spoon.

**closingLine:** *Take your time over the peel. When strength can't help, your time still can.*

## Names

- ***In One Piece*** (**the room's pick, all three, round 6**). The gesture is the whole lemon peeled without a break, and it's also what this person gives: everyone in the room gets home in one piece. It names the person, not the night, so it doesn't repeat the epigraph, and it spends neither the tagline's "raise" nor its "could". It claims nothing the glass can't keep (Hester). Said across a bar ("What are you having?" "In One Piece") it sounds like a promise. In no pour, checked.
- ***Nothing Stronger*** (Wren's first pick, now second). Good cold, but three flags moved the room: a second "Nothing…" name after *Nothing to It*; it says what the epigraph says; and read under the title, or ordered out loud, it says there's nothing stronger in the glass, which holds 45 ml of VSOP (the "weak drink, weak person" reading Wren ruled out in round 1).
- ***Winter Warmer***: Oxford's own words for cognac and ginger ale (pdf 548). Warmth, not heat (Wren r1). It captions the drink more than the person.
- ***Long Twist***: the Horse's Neck's own signature (*Imbibe!* pdf 117). A bartender's name, and too much about the garnish.
- *Set aside:* *Never Raised* (spends the tagline's "raise your voice" twice); *Keep the Room* (too close to *Before the Room*).
