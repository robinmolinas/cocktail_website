## III. Names (Hester's pick)
**My pick: *In One Piece*.** The reason is a fact about our glass: read cold under the title, *Nothing Stronger* says there's nothing stronger in this drink, and it holds 45 ml of VSOP. That's true of the 1963 night and false of the cocktail I've made for you. It's also the second "Nothing…" name (after *Nothing to It*), and it repeats the epigraph. *In One Piece* makes no historical claim. The unbroken peel is ours, and the method says so.
