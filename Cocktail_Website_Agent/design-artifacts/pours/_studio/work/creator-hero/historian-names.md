## Round 6: I move to Down the Line
My case for *Well Shaken* rested on "well shaken" being the only instruction on effort in his 1925 text. It isn't: he also told the reporter to shake until it was smooth and snowy white (*Imbibe!*, pdf 112; card F18). With that premise gone, Wren's objection stands: it's about the drink, not the person. *Down the Line* is now in the reading's own words ("passing the shaker down the line"). It is fact twice over (F7, the 1915 chain; F15, still being carried on), and the future is where this guest lives (lens: *My future self*). It promises no ending. *Well Shaken* and *The Whole of It* go to Robin as options.
