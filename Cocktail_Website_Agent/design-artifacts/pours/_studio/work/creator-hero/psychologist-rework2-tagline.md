# Rework 2: tagline (Wren, round 13)

Seeds only (no sanctum yet). Robin's brief: build the tagline around whoYouAre's "because other people are building on your belief". Positive: they own the dream, they hold it.

## Pick
**The dream is yours. Everyone else is building on it.**
- Ownership is said outright ("yours"); holding is implied (it's what everyone stands on).
- Positive, and it's a recognition, not a compliment: it says what they carry, not how good they are.
- The echo with whoYouAre is deliberate. The tagline gives the gift; whoYouAre later shows its price ("You never do it out loud, because other people are building on your belief"). The guest meets the same fact twice, first as pride, then as the reason for the silence. whoYouAre stays unchanged.
- Siblings: the Dreamer (innocent-magician) has the dream but nobody builds on it; the Architect (sage-creator) builds it themselves; the Auteur needs it signed. "Everyone else is building on it" is only the Visionary's.
