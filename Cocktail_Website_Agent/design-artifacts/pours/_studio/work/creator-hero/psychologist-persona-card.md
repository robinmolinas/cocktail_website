# Persona card: The Visionary (creator-hero)

Wren, round 0. Written from `persona.py creator-hero` and the seed templates (no sanctum yet).

## Core truth
The Visionary's real enemy isn't failure, it's the smaller version: the temptation to shrink the thing until it fits in a lifetime, a budget, a room's patience. They would rather build something they may never see finished than finish something nobody will look at twice.

## Shows / hides
- **Shows:** certainty. The whole finished thing, described so vividly that people sign up to build it.
- **Hides:** that the belief *does* waver (the spreadsheet's "never wavers" is the cliché to go beneath). The 3 a.m. arithmetic: how far away the end is, how tired they are, and the private fear that they are the one thing holding it back. They'd never say "I'm tired"; they'd say "we're nearly there".

## Hidden fear → hidden gift
- **Fear:** ordinary. Settling. Making it smaller so it can be done (Creator: status quo, mediocrity; Hero: weakness, timidity).
- **Gift:** because they refuse the smaller version, they give other people something bigger than themselves to belong to, and a reason to keep going after them. The gift is the *handover*: a vision clear enough that someone else can carry it on.

## The "real me" line to aim for
Draft: *"What frightens you isn't that it won't get finished. It's that you'll make it smaller so it can be."*
Alt: *"You'd rather leave something unfinished than something ordinary."*

## Truth vs. legend
The true story, unfinished and all. They respect a long haul honestly told, and they'd spot a tidy legend as the smaller version. Note Robin's own margin on the source: "lol @ 'through the end'". The Sagrada Familia isn't finished. Don't write "followed through to the end". The truth of this persona is carrying on without the ending in sight.

## Tone
Inspirational + assertive → the bartender believes them, and dares them. Not gushing, not motivational-poster. Grown-up, a little challenging, with warmth for the tiredness underneath. Room for one dry smile (the unfinished cathedral is quietly funny).

## Distinct from siblings
- **The Obsessed** (creator-lover) is in love with the making, the detail in the hand. The Visionary is in love with the finished thing they may never see.
- **The Auteur** (creator-ruler) needs it to be *theirs*, signed and controlled. The Visionary will hand it over if that's what it takes to get it built.
- **The Old Master** (creator-sage) seeks depth of craft; **The Experimentalist** (creator-explorer) seeks the trying; **The Trend Setter** (creator-outlaw) seeks the new, now. The Visionary seeks the *lasting*, later: scale plus time.

## Traps
- Flattery: "genius", "iconic", "you see what others can't" as a compliment. Say what they *do* and what it costs.
- "Legacy" / "leave a mark" said as praise. It's a driver, not a line.
- Job labels: architect, founder, entrepreneur, dreamer.
- The finish line. Never promise the ending; never pretend it's done.
- "Never wavers". The truth is: it wavers, and they carry on.
- Motivational-poster cadence ("dream big"). The Hero half makes this easy to fall into.
- An unresolved, open-ended drink story would be fine here (unlike the Sage), but it must feel *deliberate*, not unfinished work.
