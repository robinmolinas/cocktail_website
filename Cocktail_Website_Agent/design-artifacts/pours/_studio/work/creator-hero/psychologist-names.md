Room picks: The Whole of It (Wren), Well Shaken (Hester), Down the Line (Tomás).
Wren concedes to **Down the Line**. What convinced her (Tomás): it works on its own, cold, across a bar, and it's true in both directions: the chain the drink needed (p2 already says "passing the shaker down the line", so the guest meets the name inside the story) and where this person lives, years down the line.
