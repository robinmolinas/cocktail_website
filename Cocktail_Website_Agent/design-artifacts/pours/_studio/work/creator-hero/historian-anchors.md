**Correction first (mine).** In rounds 1 and 2 I said thirty-five men passed *each shaker* down a line "to finish one glass". The source doesn't say that. *Imbibe!* (pdf 111) says 35 shakermen were working at Mardi Gras 1915 and each shook until tired, then passed it on, "in a long chain". **35 is the crew, not the hands per glass.** Wren's "it took thirty-five people to finish one glass" has to go. Safe: *"it took thirty-five people just to do the shaking"* or *"each glass went from one pair of arms to the next until it was done."*
