## Why A leads
The Researcher's shape is service through discovery with no thought of owning it. Priestley's motive is on the record in his own words (sailors, scurvy), he took it to the Admiralty not to market, and Oxford records that he published and never sold it. The truer turn: the cure was wrong, and the gift was still real; every highball's bubbles come from a man trying to help sailors. B is the more medical story (MRI scene) and has the sharper "truer than the famous one" twist, but it leans on a secondary source and its hero is ambivalent about his own result.
Possible pairing for Tomás: both men were trying to beat scurvy at sea; one tried bubbles, one tried citrus. Lemon + soda = a Collins/fizz frame. Only if Wren wants two people; one is cleaner.
