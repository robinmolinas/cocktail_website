# Tomás: drink draft for The Researcher (caregiver-explorer), v2 (round 7)

Glassware: highball glass, filled with ice
contains: veto-free

## Recipe
| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 50 ml | blended scotch whisky, steeped with dried hops | Any unpeated blended scotch. Steep 4 g of dried aroma hops (from a home-brew shop) in 250 ml of the whisky for about two hours at room temperature, then strain it through a coffee filter. This makes five drinks. |
| 125 ml | soda water, very cold, from a freshly opened bottle | Poured last. It's the part that began with him. |
| 1 | long strip of lemon peel | Twisted once as it goes in, and left in the glass. |

## Method
1. Fill a highball glass with ice, then pour in the hopped whisky.
2. Pour the soda water in slowly, down the inside of the glass, so it keeps its bubbles.
3. Stir once, gently, only to bring them together.
4. Twist the strip of lemon peel once, as if wringing it, and slide it down into the glass. It stays there and scents the drink as you sip.
