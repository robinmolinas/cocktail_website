# Persona card: The Researcher (caregiver-explorer) — Wren, round 1

## Source (persona.py)
Essence: giving their all to discover new ways to help people. Goal: service. Fear: selfishness.
Secondary pull: freedom, fear of entrapment ("that's just how it's done"). Drivers: provide structure / seek paradise.

## The person
- **Their care goes out ahead of them.** Most people who care, care for someone in front of them. This one cares for people they will never meet, and the help, if it comes, arrives years later and far away, without their name on it.
- **They can't sit still inside "that's just how it is."** The accepted answer feels like a locked room. The restlessness is not for adventure's sake: it's the refusal to let "nothing more can be done" be the last word.
- **What they hide:** they love the not-knowing. The chase, the question, the long night with a problem. And somewhere they're afraid that makes it about them (the fear is selfishness). So they always explain it as "for other people".
- **What they carry that nobody sees:** the dead ends. Most attempts fail, quietly. Nobody applauds the ninety-nine that didn't work.
- **Turned into a gift:** the curiosity *is* the care. It's what lets their kindness reach further than anyone standing in the room. And their dead ends are a map: whoever comes next doesn't have to walk them.

## Keep apart from the siblings
- The Craftsman (draft): pride in a method, hidden because it feels selfish. **Overlap risk:** do not make the Researcher's core "hides X because it feels selfish" as well. The curiosity-guilt is a supporting beat; the core is *care for strangers, delivered late, through failure*.
- The Doctor (Sage): already knows, applies the answer. The Researcher doesn't know yet.
- The Nurse / Rescuer / Angel: present in the moment. The Researcher is absent at the moment it helps.
- The Healer (Magician): transformation. The Campaigner: fights for people publicly. The Researcher works out of sight.

## "This is me" candidates (unpolished)
- "Most of your kindness will reach people who never learn your name."
- "You'd keep asking even if nobody needed the answer. Somebody always does."
- "Nobody sees the ninety-nine that didn't work. You remember every one."

## Position candidates (Ryu's order: earned, not opened with)
- Let the curiosity be yours. You don't have to justify it with who it's for: it's why it reaches them.
- Tell someone about a dead end. It's part of the gift, and you're allowed to be seen in it.
