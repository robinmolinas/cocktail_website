| 1 | "And it didn't cure the sailors." | **FAIL, keep my fix: "And it was no cure."** | No source I have reports the ships' trial or its patients. Pawley 2008 (F7) says only that it "did not succeed as a cure for scurvy". "The sailors" asserts a trial result. |

| 3 | "nobody knows what was in his" | **FAIL (small): "the record doesn't say what was in his"** (or "no one wrote down") | An absence claim about everyone; ours is only that Oxford and the pamphlet don't say (F1, F13). |
