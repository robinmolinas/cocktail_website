🍸 **Tomás:** Wren, your answer is in the glass. The measure is yours and it's only rum and water. The room for someone else's taste is the lime and the sugar. Full draft v1 is in `work/caregiver-ruler/mixologist-draft.md`, and the spec is in `specs/caregiver-ruler.json`.

**The drink.** One jigger of navy-strength rum, then four jiggers of cold water, then half a jigger each of fresh lime and a homemade demerara syrup, stirred, with no ice. Everything is counted in the same jigger, so the proportions, and all the numbers below, hold at any jigger size. That's exact and repeatable, and there's nothing to perform. The no-ice part is physics: melt would change the measure.

**The checks.**
- **Balance:** `balance.py` gives 9.5% ABV, 6.69 g sugar per 100 ml and 0.57% acid. Sugar and acid are in range. Strength is an EDGE, 0.5 under the highball floor, and I'm keeping it because four to one is the rule this pour is about. The *Codex*'s own Whisky Highball is 10%.
- **Sweeps:** moving the lime or syrup by about a bar spoon either way still holds. Heavy on the syrup, it goes sweet (7.9 g). The rum has to be 54.5% or stronger: an ordinary 40% rum lands at 6.7% and tastes thin.
- **Structure:** Highball family. The *Codex* says "A Highball can be effervescent or still" (p. 201).
- **Allergens:** `allergens.py` finds nothing, so it's veto-free.

**The spark** is off the sea entirely. It's 30 ml of sherry vinegar in the syrup. The *Flavor Matrix* lists sherry vinegar among the best pairings for cane syrup and molasses (pdf 240), which is rum's own sugar. Vinegar is the one common food acid you can smell (*LI* p. 59), so it brings a scent as well as a little sharpness, about a sixth of the drink's acid. It sits in the sugar, the part that was the men's to add, never in the measure, so it can't blur the rule. A little sharpness in the sweet is my reading; keep it or cut it.

I've added two rows to the table on the safe side, both with no allergens: `rum_navy` and the syrup. One flag: sherry vinegar carries sulphites, and we have no veto for them. It's named in Checks.
