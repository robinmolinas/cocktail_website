**The one true thing.** For the Free Spirit, "nobody's sure" is good news. Most people hear it and feel let down. This person lights up. Draft "this is me" line: *When someone says "actually, nobody's sure", most people sigh. You light up.*

**Hidden.** Some of their questions got them told off: "because I said so", "don't be cheeky", an eye-roll in a meeting. That's the sheet's fear of punishment at everyday size. So they've learned to ask lightly, as if it didn't matter much. It does. And when they ask why a rule exists, they really want to know, but people hear it as a challenge.

**Fear.** Getting the label instead of the answer: "it's just a…", said to close the subject. A world so labelled there's nothing left to wonder at. Gently, they also half-fear that finding out spends the wonder, so sometimes they don't look.

**Gift.** With them in the room, "I don't know" stops being embarrassing and becomes the start of the fun. We say that out loud once.
