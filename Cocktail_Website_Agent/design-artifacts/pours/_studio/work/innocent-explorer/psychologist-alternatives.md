## A1: y4 if the peppercorn's "not a pepper" has no page (taste only)
The cocktail I've made for you is stirred over ice until it's icy, then poured without it, the way a Martini is, from two bottles of Cognac's own: a cognac aged for years in oak, and Pineau des Charentes, the region's aperitif, sweet grape juice kept from fermenting by a dash of young cognac. Into part of the cognac I've steeped a few Sichuan peppercorns. You'll feel them as a faint tingle on your lips before the warmth of the cognac comes through, a small question in the glass, because a drink for you should have one. It's poured into a tulip-shaped glass, which narrows at the top to hold the scent rising off the drink, the same escaping spirit those black walls live on, kept for you this time.
