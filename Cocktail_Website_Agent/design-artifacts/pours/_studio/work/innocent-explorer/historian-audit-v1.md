## For Tomás (his ask): white Pineau's sugar and acid
- **Sugar: a legal floor.** The AOC control plan (INAO/Qualisud PCPineau/P300-8, 2019) treats glucose plus fructose **below 120 g/L** in the finished Pineau as a critical failure. The juice must be over 170 g/L when it's stopped (INAO CNAOV-2017-525, §VII). So 12 g/100 ml is the sourced floor. Your 13 g sits in range, and your 11 g sweep point is below the legal minimum (drop it). There's no ceiling on the page I read, and the "125–150 g/L" in a search summary isn't confirmed on a page, so don't use it.
- **Strength:** 16–22% (EU single document C 248/32, 2019; Oxford pdf 1518). The 17.5% is unsourced *within* that band.
- **Acid: no page.** The EU single document's "Acidité totale minimale" line is **blank**, and the INAO's 2017 note records a disagreement over whether total acidity should be tested at all. The 0.45% stays *unsourced*, and the acid flag stays owned in Checks.
- **White Pineau's ageing:** at least 18 months, of which at least 12 in oak (INAO CNAOV-2017-525). That supports "both spend time in oak" (D5).
- Image brief colour ("amber-gold"): no page has the colour. Say "golden" only as our description, or drop it.
