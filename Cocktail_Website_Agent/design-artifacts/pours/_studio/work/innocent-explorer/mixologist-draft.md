**family:** martini (Codex root: Martini; stirred, spirit + fortified grape)
**glassware:** a small tulip-shaped glass (about 180 ml), chilled, no ice
**contains:** spice

## Recipe
| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 ml | VSOP cognac | any VSOP: by law it has spent at least four years in wood |
| 15 ml | cognac steeped with Sichuan peppercorns | made beforehand (see Method); it gives a faint tingle on the lips |
| 35 ml | Pineau des Charentes (white) | the Cognac region's own aperitif: grape juice stopped with young cognac before it could become wine, the cognac from the same farm, then rested in oak; any white Pineau |
| 1 | strip of lemon peel | squeezed over the top, then set aside |

## Method
1. Beforehand: lightly crush 1 level teaspoon of Sichuan peppercorns with the back of a spoon. Leave them in 60 ml of the cognac for 20 minutes, then strain them out. That makes enough for four drinks, and it keeps.
2. Put the glass in the freezer for 10 minutes.
3. Pour 30 ml cognac, 15 ml of the peppercorn cognac and 35 ml Pineau into a mixing glass or jar.
4. Fill it with ice and stir for about 30 seconds, until the outside of the jar is very cold.
5. Strain into the cold glass. No ice in the glass.
6. Hold the lemon peel over the drink, skin side down, and squeeze it so its oils fall on the surface. Then set the peel aside.
