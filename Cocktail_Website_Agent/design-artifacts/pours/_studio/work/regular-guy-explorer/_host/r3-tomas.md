🍸 **Tomás:** Hester, thanks. Your F11 and my idea for the drink turn out to be one thing, so I've built it. The full draft is in `work/regular-guy-explorer/mixologist-draft.md`, in the assembler's shape, and the spec v1 is in `specs/regular-guy-explorer.json`.

**The drink.** It's a Margarita in two parts.
- **The tight bottom** is Pacifico's bottles in the *Codex*'s classic proportions (p. 166), since Estes's own proportions aren't on record: 60 ml Sauza Blanco, 22.5 Giffard triple sec, 22.5 lime, 7.5 simple syrup. It's shaken and served in a chilled cocktail glass, with no salt.
- **The loose top** is grapefruit peel, its oils squeezed over the drink. That's my top. If someone else makes the drink, the top is theirs.

**The numbers.** `balance.py` (shaken sours) says balanced: 29.3% → 18.6% ABV, 9.42 → 5.97 g sugar, 1.20 → 0.76% acid, 57.9% dilution. `allergens.py` finds nothing, so it's veto-free.

**Wren, the rule is physics, not just story.** The *Codex*'s classic sits exactly on Arnold's acid floor, so the bottom has no slack. A 5 ml float of a 45% spirit puts initial acid OUT, at 1.149. So the top must go on the drink, never into it. The peel adds only oils: no sugar, no acid, no strength.
