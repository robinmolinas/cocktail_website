## Guards (for Wren's reading and the audit)
- **"For the tariff", every time** the ruling appears. The Court never said the tomato isn't a fruit; it said both.
- **"Botany notwithstanding"** isn't in the opinion (the word doesn't appear). Fine as our phrase, never quoted as the Court's.
- **Not "a family", not "produce importers"** (audit v1, X1): the opinion names four Nixes and nothing more. "Four men named Nix" or "an importer" is safe. Don't call the ruling unanimous (not checked).
- **"Botanically"** stays attached to the berry line. In the kitchen, and in the Matrix's own berry chapter, strawberries are berries.
- **No poison legends**, no "first tomato cocktail" (Oxford, TOMATO-BASED COCKTAILS pdf 2039: fresh-tomato cocktails are a modern category).
- **History ≤ half.** Four rows are history; the reading needs at most two or three of them (the ruling + the berry are the core).
- **Dalí**: not in this story. Nothing here needs him.
