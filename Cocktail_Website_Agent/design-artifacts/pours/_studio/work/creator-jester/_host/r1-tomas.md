🍸 **Tomás:** Wren, your card says the *Flavor Matrix* pairing is the person, so here's one that sounds like a joke and isn't: **a strawberry daiquiri made with tomatoes.** In the Matrix, tomato is vanilla's surprise pairing (pdf 245), and the tomato entry lists strawberries as its substitute (pdf 244). The strawberry daiquiri is about the most ordinary sweet drink there is, and the tomato is what's been standing next to the strawberry all along. v1 is 60 ml white rum, 22.5 ml lime and 20 ml cherry-tomato-and-vanilla syrup, shaken and served in a coupe. For the syrup, crush ripe cherry tomatoes with their weight in sugar and a split vanilla pod, leave it overnight in the fridge, then strain. `balance.py` has every line in range: 15.4% / 8.41 g / 0.90% finished, dilution 52.5%. It's veto-free. It's a syrup and not juice because Codex p. 218 says fresh tomato juice is thin and separates from spirit, and my juice versions came out at about 12% finished, out of range. It tastes of ripe red fruit and vanilla, and the only surprise is in the recipe. No trick in the glass, which fits your "no gimmick" line, and white rum is new to the set.
