**The drink.** Croswell's four things and no fifth: 60 ml aged Jamaican rum, 20 ml banana liqueur (Giffard Banane du Brésil, recommended, or any banana liqueur of about 25%), 2 dashes Angostura, 30 ml ice-cold water measured from a jug, orange peel.
