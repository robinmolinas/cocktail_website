# Hester: mirror hunt for The Auteur (creator-ruler), round 1

Persona: in control of every element of the production, meticulous, commands respect. Goal innovation; fears mediocrity / status quo (Creator) and chaos, vulnerability, failure (Ruler). Driver: provide structure.

The shape to mirror: **invention held inside a structure they set themselves**, and the fear that anything less than exact is mediocre.

## A. Audrey Saunders, Pegu Club (New York, 2005): my pick

**What:** a person (the bartender who built a bar as one piece of work).

**Story in three sentences.** When she agreed to open Pegu Club she set one condition, in her own words: "I want a project where I'm making the calls." She worried over every detail, the logo for months and the staff's shirts, vests and ties, and nothing reached the menu, classic or new, until it survived her testing, where an eighth of an ounce was a serious matter. Her own account of building a drink is the Auteur's method exactly: keep the ratios, and riff "within the parameters".

**Why it mirrors the Auteur.** Innovation (her originals) held inside a structure she sets (ratios, testing, consistency). The fear of mediocrity is on the record in the book's summary ("perfect, or garbage"). The vulnerability is on the record too: Jim Meehan, one of her own bartenders, later said the method's flaw is that palates differ ("There is no perfect drink"), and still develops drinks her way. That's the turn Wren may want: the standard is theirs, the perfection isn't guaranteed, and the people trained under it carry it on.

## B. Donn Beach, Don the Beachcomber (Hollywood, 1934): runner-up

**What:** a person (and the Zombie).

**Story.** A former bootlegger born Ernest Raymond Beaumont Gantt renamed himself, built a South Seas bar in a Hollywood hotel lobby and invented a whole category of drinks from the Planter's Punch. When rivals poached his bartenders to get the recipes, he encoded them: ingredient names replaced by numbers matching the labels on his bottles. He refused more than two Zombies per customer, took the formula to his grave, and it was found in 2006 in his former headwaiter's notebook.
