## Wording guards for the reading
- Maloney was head bartender; Meehan held a single Monday shift at opening (*Proper* p. 162). Call Meehan "one of her bartenders", never "her head bartender".
- "Eighth of an ounce" / "perfect, or garbage" are Simonson's words, not hers.
- "Give them the ratio instead of the answer" is the bartender's proposal. She did the opposite (she gave the answer), so the reading can't say she gave the ratio.
- Present tense: nothing about her.
