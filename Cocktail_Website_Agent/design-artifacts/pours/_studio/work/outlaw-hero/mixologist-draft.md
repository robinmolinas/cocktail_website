# Tomás: draft, The Maverick (outlaw-hero), v1.3, round 5 step 2 (matches spec v1.3 and reading v3)

Story as Wren ruled (r3) on Hester's anchors (`work/outlaw-hero/historian-anchors.md`): Tai Solarin, a teetotaller, got himself arrested in 1968 for possessing ogogoro, to show how unfair it was to ban a local spirit and allow foreign ones (Oxford OGOGORO pdf 1417); "Solarin Freed of Gin Charge", *Lagos Daily Times*, 14 September 1968, p. 3 (Oxford's bibliography, pdf 1418; unread). The drink tells no scene. It puts the two spirits under one rule: equal measures. Spec: `_studio/specs/outlaw-hero.json`.

**Glass:** Nick & Nora glass (about 150 ml), chilled
**Contains:** veto-free

## Recipe
| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 ml (1 oz) | Pedro's Premium Ògógóró, unaged (recommended) | a Nigerian spirit distilled from palm sap, 40%. Buy the unaged Premium, not the 5-year aged bottling. If you can't find it: another branded, unaged West African palm-sap spirit, never a homemade or unlabelled one |
| 30 ml (1 oz) | London dry gin | any London dry gin of 40% or more |
| 30 ml (1 oz) | sweet vermouth | an Italian-style red vermouth |
| 2.5 ml (½ barspoon) | demerara syrup | one part demerara sugar dissolved in one part warm water, cooled |
| 1 dash | orange bitters | |
| 1 strip | orange peel | squeezed over the top |

## Method
1. Put the glass in the freezer for ten minutes, or fill it with ice and water while you mix.
2. Measure 30 ml of the ogogoro and 30 ml of the gin into a mixing glass or a jar.
3. Add the vermouth, the half barspoon of demerara syrup and the dash of orange bitters.
4. Fill with ice and stir for 20 to 30 seconds, until the outside of the mixing glass feels very cold.
5. Empty the glass, strain the drink into it, then squeeze the orange peel over the top so its oils fall on the drink, and drop it in.

## For Wren's y4
- Spark tie, in one clause: **"so neither one gets the bigger share, which is how you've always wanted it, yourself included."**
- Glass: **a chilled Nick & Nora** (a small stemmed glass with a deep, rounded bowl).
- The demerara is in the recipe (half a barspoon). v2's "to draw out the toffee and black pepper" claims more than the sugar does: it's there to lift the sugar off the floor, and it can sit with the toffee, not the pepper. Suggest: "a little demerara syrup, which sits well with the toffee tasters find in Pedro's". Never the spark.
- Spark clause: Wren's v3 wording joins hers and mine ("a nod to the point Tai Solarin got himself arrested to make: one rule for both, so neither gets the bigger share, the way you'd always have it"). Agreed, and "yourself included" rightly cut.
- "refined in Lagos" holds: Difford's says it's redistilled there "to produce a refined ògógóró". "made from palm sap in villages": Difford's says fermented *and distilled* in villages, so "made" is fine.
- "strong": 23.6% finished is a strong drink.
