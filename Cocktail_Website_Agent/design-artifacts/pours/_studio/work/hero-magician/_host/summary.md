- **Persona:** The Superhero: can do something most people near them can't, keeps it out of sight so nobody feels small, brings it out only for someone who needs it, and privately believes the quiet self is the disguise. The reading turns that round: the ability is the jacket.
- **Story:** the plan's lead, Constante Ribalaigua (1888–1952): rich and known abroad by the late 1930s, he still put on the white jacket every morning and went behind the bar; the Floridita cocktail is his and delicious, yet for reasons we don't know it isn't in any of the bar's booklets. Wren accepted it in r1 with conditions (it's a new drink every day "as self discipline", not one drink a day; the crown in one clause at most; "seemingly casual" left to the siblings who own it). Hester passed the Floridita cocktail against the family's Daiquiri guard with fences. Runner-up: Doudoroff and Bar Asterie (thin).
- **Drink:** the Floridita (white rum 30, sweet vermouth 15, white crème de cacao 7.5, dry orange curaçao 5, grenadine 5), shaken, nick-and-nora, with two changes: half a lime squeezed by hand (the printed whole lime is OUT on ABV, acid and dilution), and the lime's own peel as the twist (*Flavor Matrix*). All seven balance numbers in range. Veto-free.
