# hero-magician · The Superhero: drink v1 (Tomás, round 2, 2026-10-04; wording fenced to Hester's r2 card, round 3)

Spec: `_studio/specs/hero-magician.json`. The Floridita cocktail as Oxford prints it (pdf 821; the entry's only cited source is Trader Vic's 1972 *Bar Guide*), a drink Oxford counts among Constante's creations (pdf 1656), and one that isn't in any of his booklets (pdf 821; card F14). No recipe of his own is on record, so never "Constante's recipe" (card F13; audit H1). Every printed measure is kept except the lime, which the numbers halve, and the twist is lime peel instead of orange. So it's never "his recipe": it's "the Floridita, at half a lime, with a lime twist".

**Glassware:** nick-and-nora glass (about 150 ml), chilled
**Contains:** veto-free

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 ml | white rum, Cuban style (light and dry, unaged or lightly aged), 40% or stronger | the recipe asks for Cuban or Puerto Rican; below 40% the drink goes thin |
| 15 ml | sweet (red) vermouth | |
| 7.5 ml | white crème de cacao | clear as water, and it tastes of chocolate |
| 5 ml (1 teaspoon) | orange curaçao (a dry style, about 40%) | |
| 5 ml (1 teaspoon) | grenadine | |
| 15 ml | fresh lime juice: half a lime, squeezed by hand | measure it: a whole lime makes the drink too sharp and too weak |
| 1 | wide strip of lime peel, cut from the same lime | |

## Method

1. Put the glass in the freezer for ten minutes.
2. Before you cut the lime, take one wide strip of peel off it with a vegetable peeler, green only, none of the white underneath. Set it aside.
3. Cut the lime in half. Squeeze one half over a jigger with your fingers, nothing else, until you have 15 ml.
4. Put the rum, vermouth, crème de cacao, curaçao, grenadine and lime juice in a shaker. Fill it with ice and shake hard for about twelve seconds, until the outside is too cold to hold.
5. Strain it into the cold glass.
6. Hold the strip of peel over the drink, green side down, and squeeze it so its oils fall on the top. Lay it on the rim.

**closingLine:** *Half the lime, squeezed by hand. When you're next introduced, bring up something the introduction doesn't cover.*
