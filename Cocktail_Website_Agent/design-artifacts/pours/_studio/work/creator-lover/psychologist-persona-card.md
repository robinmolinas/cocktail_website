# Persona card: The Obsessed (creator-lover)

Wren, round 1. Source: `persona.py creator-lover` (Creator x Lover; goal innovation; fears status quo / mediocrity, and the Lover's isolation / loneliness; example *Shakespeare in Love*; tone inspirational + passionate, sensual, inviting).

## Core truth
They aren't working on the thing. They're in love with it. The work is the place they feel most alive and most close to something, and the same love is what leaves them alone in it: nobody else cares this much, and they know it.

## Shows / hides
- **Shows:** the intensity. They talk about it too long, lose the evening to it, go back to the same thing again and again (the same song, the same dish, the same drawing), and each time it's a little different to them and identical to everyone else.
- **Hides:** that it's romantic, not technical. They explain it in details because "I love it" sounds too much. And they hide how much a lukewarm answer hurts when they finally show it to someone.

## Hidden fear -> hidden gift
- **Fear:** not failure. Being alone in how much they feel it. Showing the thing they love to someone and getting back "nice". (Creator's fear of mediocrity meets the Lover's fear of loneliness: an ordinary answer to an extraordinary feeling.)
- **Gift:** near them, people remember that it's allowed to care this much about something. They make devotion look like a pleasure, not a burden. (Not the Experimentalist's gift, which is "failure is fine, try something": this one is "loving something too much is fine".)

## The "real me" line to aim for
- A: *"The word that hurts you most isn't 'no'. It's 'nice'."*
- B: *"You've made the same thing more times than anyone knows. To you, it's never been the same thing twice."* (careful: "more than anyone guesses" is Off-Label's motif; reword)
- C: *"You explain it in details, because 'I love it' sounds like too much."*

A is my lead. It's the fear and the Lover in one word, and nobody else in the family carries it.

## Position seed (Ryu's order: only once trust is earned)
In their interest: let someone in *before* it's ready. They only show it finished, and then wait to be understood. The intimacy they're missing is being seen while it's still being loved into shape. Closing-line seed: something the guest can do with another person, not alone at the bench.

## Truth vs. legend
The true story, told with feeling. They love the romance of devotion (they'll forgive "I like to think…"), but a Creator hates the fake. A real maker who couldn't leave one thing alone, not a myth of genius.

## Tone
Warm, a little breathless where the story allows, amused with them (they know they talk about it too much). Sensual in the drink and the language of taste. Never the tortured-artist gravity.

## Distinct from siblings
- **Sculptor (Quite Alive):** makes for one person, to say what they can't; the 2 a.m. present. The Obsessed is in love with the *making*; the thing itself is the beloved. No "2 a.m.", no "vanish", no "perfectionism" (all Sculptor's).
- **Experimentalist (Off-Label):** never the same thing twice, loves finding out, walks on. The Obsessed stays and goes deeper into one thing. Opposites; keep it that way.
- **Visionary (Down the Line):** sees the finished thing first, size and time. The Obsessed doesn't want it finished; being in it is the point.
- **Old Master (unauthored):** mastery by discipline, respected, years of craft. Protect it: the Obsessed's depth comes from love, not practice or reputation. No "mastery", no "years of expertise".
- **Muse (lover-creator, unauthored):** inspires others' creativity through desire. The Obsessed's love points at the work, not at people.

## Traps
- "Artist", "genius", "craftsman": labels.
- Diagnosis: "obsessive", "workaholic", "you forget to eat". Also romanticising self-neglect. Seen, not solved.
- "Passion" as horoscope ("you're passionate about what you do" = anyone's LinkedIn).
- Shakespeare / *Shakespeare in Love* as mirror.
- A drink that's austere or a performance. The Lover wants it sensual; the Creator wants one real, original touch.
- Off-Label's "more than anyone guesses"; Sculptor's "rarely let anyone see what it costs".

## speaksTo hints (journey words)
- lens: `The real me`
- drawnToward: `Beauty`, `Pleasure`, `Mastery` (the Obsessed's reach, not its reason)
- soughtFor: `Taste`, `Ideas`
- texture: `Out of control`, `Excited`, `Dark`, `Rough` / `Smooth` (open)
- gravity: `Solitary`, `Instinctive`, `Wild`
- flavours: `Bitter`, `Fruity`, `Spicy`; seed `Campari Red` / `Cassis Plum` (the profile's deep red, Pantone 186 C)
- vessel: `Short & strong` or `Poised & ceremonial`

## Asks
- **Hester:** a real maker who couldn't leave one drink (or one ingredient) alone for love of it, remaking it for years, where the drink is the beloved, not the customer. Bonus if the world shrugged at first ("nice"), or if it stayed unfinished. Not Ada Coleman (Sculptor's).
- **Tomás:** a drink that rewards staying with it (changes as you sip, reveals a layer), sensual and deep red if it's honest to the drink. The spark from the Flavor Matrix should feel like a love affair, not a trick. Nothing that's a performance.
