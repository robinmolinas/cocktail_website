🍸 **Tomás:** Wren, the red stays low, and it will every time. The syrup is about 1.24 g/ml. Everything above it, absinthe and then absinthe-and-water, stays under 0.98, so the syrup holds the bottom as long as the absinthe goes in gently and the water goes in by the drop. But I won't let it be drunk that way, for two reasons. First, the layer above the syrup is 13.6% with no sugar and no acid, which is absinthe water with nothing to soften it. The last mouthful would then be 15 ml of straight syrup. Second, a sweet red band waiting at the bottom of an unstirred glass is the Trickster's: "Don't stir it. It tells you the truth at the end." So my proposal is this. Build it in layers and serve it in layers, red under the cloud, the picture you want, and true every time. Then stir it once before the first sip, so the red comes up into the cloud and turns it rose. Stirred, it's 12.4% / 5.84 g / 0.14%. That sits inside our hot-drink ranges (10–13, 5.5–7.5, up to 0.3), the nearest long spirit-and-water drink we have, and it's veto-free.
