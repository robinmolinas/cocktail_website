Spec: `_studio/specs/innocent-magician.json` (v2). Lead: Bill and Lyn Lark and the still-size rule (Hester's card `fact-cards/bill-lark-tasmania.md`). **v1 → v2:** the pepperberry water is gone. **glass:** tall glass (highball, about 300 ml), filled with ice
**contains:** veto-free

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 45 ml | Lark Classic Cask Tasmanian single malt whisky (43%), recommended | or any Tasmanian single malt, 40-46%; not cask strength |
| 110 ml | cold still water | from the fridge; not sparkling |
| 10 ml (2 teaspoons) | Glenfarclas single malt Scotch whisky, recommended | or any Scotch single malt; added last |
| to fill | ice | ordinary cubes |

## Method

1. Fill a tall glass with ice.
2. Pour in the Lark, then the cold water, and stir a few turns, until the outside of the glass feels cold.
3. Add the two teaspoons of Glenfarclas last, over the top, and give it one more turn.

**closingLine:** *The Glenfarclas goes in last. Then find out what's really in the way.*

(The gesture: the help from Scotland arrives after the yes, as Grant's call came after the licence. The position is Wren's y6 ("find out what's really in the way"). Step 3: changed from "ask who said it couldn't happen, and when" at Wren's crossing. In the story the "no" was a rule, not a person (D9), and "who said" invited one. Grepped: "goes in last" and "really in the way" appear in no sibling closing line. "Two teaspoons" is kept out of the line, because *On Their Behalf*'s closing line has it.)
