## Names (my pick)
**On One Condition.** It's the only name that is literally on the page: he'd improve the bar *if*. And it holds the Genie's hidden move, the wish said inside the offer, without echoing the tagline's "wish". *Name Your Wish* is the warmer line, but it sits close to the lamp-and-genie register Wren's trap 6 warns about, and it tells rather than shows. Runner-up: *Name Your Wish*.
