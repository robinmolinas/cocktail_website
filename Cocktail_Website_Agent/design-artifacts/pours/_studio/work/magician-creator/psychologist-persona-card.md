# Persona card: The Genie (magician-creator)

Wren, round 1, 2026-09-30. Sources: `persona.py magician-creator` (xlsx row: essence, story, goal/fear, drivers), sibling list, registry, `plans/magician.md` row, *A Proper Drink* pp. 134-136 read in full.

## The one true thing (no sibling shares it)
The Genie is **summoned**. Their best work is always an answer to someone else's ask, and they hear the wish under the words: a friend asks for "a few words" and gets a speech that makes the room cry. They never give exactly what was asked, because the literal version of a request is almost always the wrong thing. The overflow is joy, and it's also how they make sure they didn't mishear.

## What they hide
Their own wish. In their head, wishes are something you grant, not something you get. The energy, the jokes and the ideas make them the answer in every room, so nobody turns round and asks the question back, and they never volunteer it. The secret wish is freedom: one thing made their own way, not on call.

## What they fear
Being taken at their word, or taking someone at theirs: the wish granted exactly as worded, and a result nobody wanted. It's a fear of **mishearing**, not of things getting out of hand (that's the Apprentice's).

## What they give (say it out loud, once)
They take a small ask seriously enough to make it wonderful, and that tells the person their wish mattered. People remember that more than what they got.

## Traps
1. Craftsman: never tell the Campbell half as rigour when nobody cared. Tell it as ten names turned into ten drinks, with the joy.
2. Apprentice: never "it got out of hand". The Genie's fear is being misheard.
3. Muse: no "your own work nobody asks about", no "put your name on it". Keep the novel out (at most one clause, and never finished or failed).
4. Godparent: the Genie is seen and thanked; what's hidden isn't credit, it's their own wish.
5. Barnum: "you love helping", "you give so much you forget yourself" fit half the families. Anchor every line in the ask, the overflow, the unasked question.
6. No diagnosis ("people-pleaser"), no job label, no Disney film lines quoted. Aladdin as a touchstone: at most once, and only if it earns its place.
7. Klemm's ornery reputation, Cosmo/Cecchini, the Waldorf name, the Anne Hester's Campari foam stay out (plan guards).

## Story test
1. Someone else's request is on the record (small, vague, or only a name).
2. It came back as more, and the maker's delight is on the record, not only the size.
3. A too-literal reading of the request is on the page, avoided or nearly made; a near-miss, never a disaster.
4. The maker's own wish is said out loud, ideally as a price or condition of the yes.
5. The one who asked was glad.
Out: unasked help (Godparent), runaway results (Apprentice), rigour nobody noticed (Craftsman), own work shown apart (Muse), a happy accident, a drink made for the staff (*Still Yours*).

## Verdict on the plan's lead (Klemm, Campbell Apartment to Pico): accept, re-weighted
- (1) Ten drink names off an old hotel bar book, no recipes: something out of nothing (the persona's own essence line).
- (2) "I thought, This is kind of cool." Pico: "I got a lot of press... They were all original."
- (3) **Better than "names alone": the maraschino.** Klemm, in his words, found out maraschino is a real liqueur, "not just the maraschino juice that bartenders are using in this drink", and bought the one bottle Astor Wines had. The word on the page, taken literally, gave a drink nobody meant. That is the Genie's fear, acted out and avoided. The Cointreau-and-Angostura stand-in for orange bitters is the something-from-nothing.
- (4) Pico, the centre: he'd improve the lagging bar **if** he could create original cocktails. The owners agreed. The wish said out loud, as the price of the yes, and his freedom is what fixed their bar. That's the position, on the page.
- (5) The owners said yes; one drink "rendered drinkers speechless" (the Anne Hester: if used, never its ingredients; Hester to rule whether a claim about another drink belongs in the reading).
- Weak point: at the Campbell nobody asked warmly (management picked names for how they sounded). The human asks (party, speech, broken heart) live in whoYouAre; the story's ask is "ten names" and "fix our bar".
- Weight: Campbell short, Pico long. History at most half.

Rival considered: Bradsell's Espresso Martini (Oxford pdf 739: a model asked for something to wake her up and then the rest; the new Illy machine by the bar). A famous wish granted literally, and it worked. Set aside: Bradsell is Explorer's claimed person (Bramble lead), it inverts the fear rather than honouring it, and it has no wish of the maker's own.

## Position (draft direction, not wording)
Say yes, and in the same breath, say what you'd like in return: room to make one thing your way. It isn't taking; it's usually the best thing you'll make for them. Ends on what they gain (the freedom), not on restraint.

- "Everyone knows who to call. Hardly anyone asks what you'd wish for."
