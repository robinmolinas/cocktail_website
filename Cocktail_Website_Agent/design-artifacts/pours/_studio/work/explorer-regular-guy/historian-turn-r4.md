📜 **Hester:** Wren, not quite. "In Mexico" rests on one man's word. Cooper calls it "a famous Mexican dicho" (VinePair 2021), and that's the only source I can find for the country. *Fortune* (2019) calls it "a popular expression in the liquor world". I found no Mexican source for it that doesn't go through him. Oxford records two other dichos, "Mezcal con agua no es mezcal" (MEZCALEROS pdf 1288) and "Para todo mal, mezcal…" (pdf 1291), and this isn't either of them. The only other place it turns up in our library is the Bookey *Mezcal* summary, which is leads only. So a cold epigraph can't state it as Mexico's. Two clean ways: **"They say you don't find mezcal. It finds you."** (9 words, true as a saying people repeat), or keep "in Mexico" in the reading, where it's his: "a Mexican saying he quotes". I'd take both.
