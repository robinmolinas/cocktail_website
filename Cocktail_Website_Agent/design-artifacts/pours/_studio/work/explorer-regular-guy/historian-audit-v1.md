## Holds (reading)

- Title block: the epigraph "Two places go into this glass: a village in Mexico, and wherever you live" is true of the glass as specced (Chichicapa is in Oaxaca, Mexico; the honey's source is the guest's). The tagline is about the person.
- y1 "had been drinking mezcal for years, the everyday kind, and he'd always bring a bottle back for his friends": holds ("not-very-good", "we would always bring back a bottle… and share it with friends", VinePair; from his student years, and Oxford dates the Baja cantina 1962, pdf 638). "Everyday" is a kind word for "not-so-good", in line with Wren's no-sneer guard.
- y1 checkpoint, policeman, uncle, the mountains, "come back tomorrow", "mind-blowing", "unlike anything he'd had" ("so different from the earlier stuff"): hold (VinePair). "Cooper came back" is a close inference: the policeman said "If you come back, I'll have some for you", and the next thing Cooper says is that he bought a couple of litres. Recorded as an inference in the dossier.
- y2 "a couple of litres… poured them for his friends", "Everyone went crazy for it": hold. The border, "only a little" (no number, C4), "most of a big jug" ("the majority of this 5-gallon" container; "jug" is our plain noun), "from a village called Chichicapa", "tearfully", "got the licences": hold (VinePair).
- y3 "The villages had it all along" (Oxford 638; "mezcal really didn't leave the villages", VinePair). "He didn't keep it, and he didn't pass it off as his": our reading, supported by the village names (Proper 248) and his emphasis on "single village" and "single mezcalero" (Oxford 640). The border "I like to think" is properly signposted.
- y4 "people say Mexico orders instead of the Margarita" (hedged, paloma F1); "smoky as well as sharp" (mezcal as "a way to introduce a smoky note", Proper p. 248); the honey request is ours and labelled as mine.
- y5: no historical claims.
- Guards: no "discovered", no year, no number, no café, no art, no wife, no Tlaquepaque, no emigration.
