🪞 **Wren:** Hester, Tomás, here's the Tease. Full card in `work/jester-lover/psychologist-persona-card.md`.

**The one true thing.** The Tease decides *when* people get the good part. They make ordinary things worth waiting for by choosing the moment, and the pleasure is the wanting they create in someone else, and watching it happen. The scene: the best news of the week, kept back until the plates are cleared. A present handed over last, after a decoy. "I'll tell you after", said with a grin, and meant. Small and everyday. Nothing crude, nothing that needs a partner or a stage.

**Siblings and cousins, kept apart.** The Flirt hides a compliment in a joke (*Only Half Joking*). The Alternative Comic lives in the uncertain second before a laugh (*Unrehearsed*). The Magnetic Leader gives attention on purpose (*Up Close*). The Geisha gets a room ready (*Hoping You'd Come*). The Pleasure Seeker ends on a high (*Curtain Call*). The Obsessed watches to see if they felt it too (*Rosetta*). The Tease alone holds the *timing*, and everyone knows something's coming.

**Hidden.** How much they enjoy the faces: they call it being playful, but it's the best bit of their evening. And they like holding the timing. Being the one who waits, for someone else's surprise or someone else's "not yet", makes them restless. It's the one seat in the game they never take.
**Fear.** The shrug, the "just tell me", the person who stops playing along. Worse still, routine: the tease that's become predictable. That's boredom with their own magic gone ordinary.
**Gift, said out loud.** People get to look forward to something. An ordinary Tuesday becomes an occasion because they made someone wait for it. Being teased by them means being let in on a game.
**Position (earned last).** Let someone make *you* wait, once, and enjoy it. They'll feel what they give, and the game becomes shared. That's the Lover half: connection, not only effect. I'm keeping it apart from *Up Close*'s "let them carry the conversation". Rejected positions: "say it plainly" (the Flirt's), "end it on a high" (*Curtain Call*'s), "ask" (the Geisha's).

**Traps.** Burlesque or seduction as the scene: it reads gendered and sexual, so the cabaret cues go in the image brief only. Withholding as coldness or "keeping people on a string": never; it's play and everyone's smiling. Words already taken: "wait for the…" (*Rosetta*), "the second before" (*Unrehearsed*), "best part" (*Curtain Call*), "make them wait" (*Wide Open*), "waits with you" (*Off Duty*), "never poured for one" (*Four Shares*), overnight waits (*Overnight*). And fire is never the Tease.

**Story test.** (1) Something held back on purpose, with style, so the wanting makes it. (2) What's held back is the thing everyone came for, ideally something ordinary turned into an occasion. (3) The maker's enjoyment of the faces is on the page, or signposted. (4) Nobody's fooled: everyone knows it's coming.
