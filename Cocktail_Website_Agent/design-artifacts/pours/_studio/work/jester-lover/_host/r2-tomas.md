Cognac 30 and kirsch 15 (Oxford's two to one) steep for a few hours with lemon and orange peel and half a cinnamon stick. Then they're warmed with 17.5 ml demerara syrup in a small pan until they just steam, never lit, and run down a clove-studded orange spiral into the cups. The coffee with chicory goes in last, 50 ml a cup, **poured by someone else, whenever they like**. No cream, no beans. **balance.py, hot style: 11.3% / 6.67 g / 0.062%, all three in range.** Syrup 15-20 ml, coffee 90-110 ml and kirsch at 40-45% all stay in. One flagged edge: warming costs alcohol, which balance.py can't model. At 10% loss it's 10.1%, at 15% it's the edge, at 20% it's OUT, so the method says "under a minute, never bubbling". I'm sitting toward the sweet end because coffee and chicory are bitter and the numbers can't hear that.
