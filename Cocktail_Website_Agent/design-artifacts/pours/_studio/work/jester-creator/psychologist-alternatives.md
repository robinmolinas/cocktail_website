# Alternatives: The Wacky One (Wren)

## Taglines
- **You made it up. Now everyone says it.** (v1 pick: the positive frame, ownership, invites; it's the persona's own "invent a word… until everyone else does")
- It started as a joke. You never saw a reason to stop. (Truer to the story, but echoes whoYouAre ¶1's last line; use only if ¶1 changes.)
- Everyone's still waiting for you to grow out of it. (Recognition, a smile; weaker as an invitation.)
- Struck: "You're why it never stopped" (the Nanny's "You're why it lasts").

## Names
- **For Kicks** (v2 pick, Tomás's): the persona's motive, pleasure, with the drink's name inside.
- Not a Phase (v1 pick, struck in v2): repeats the tagline and whoYouAre ¶2, and borrows a line many people heard when coming out.
- Where You Left Off: the position, and Oxford's hinge. Strong, but the story's words more than the person's.
- Little Blue Bottlefly: Harry's listed title. Delightful and daft; it's his, not the guest's.
- With the Fly: the glass (sambuca's "con la mosca"); menu-friendly, a little cryptic.

## Epigraph
- **Three coffee beans float on top. They're meant to be flies. It's a long story.** (v1)
- A Scotch and an Irish whiskey, shaken together. The flies on top are coffee beans. (gives the joke away)

## y5 (lighter backup: plain affirmation)
- People will keep waiting for you to grow out of it. Let them wait. The word, the ritual, the thing you always wear: keep every one of them going, the way Harry kept his. (risk: "keep" edges toward *Still Yours*.)
