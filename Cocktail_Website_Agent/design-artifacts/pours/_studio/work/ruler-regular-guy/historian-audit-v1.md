## Wren's two questions

1. **"a renowned New Orleans classic" (y4): PASS.** Better anchors than F1: Oxford BÉNÉDICTINE pdf 256 names it among the "classic cocktails" Bénédictine is "essential in" (F13); *Proper* p. 255 heads it "Classic" and says the revival "has been very good for the reputation" of such drinks; *Proper* p. 283 lists it beside the Sazerac and the Ramos Gin Fizz as classics invented in New Orleans; *Joy* pdf 402 calls it "This classic drink". "Renowned" is today's reputation, after the revival, and it stays clear of the "nearly forgotten" guard. Note row: cite pdf 256 and Proper pp. 255, 283.
2. **The rest of Arthur's sentence: yes, it gives a reason, but not the one y3 needed.** He made the drink "to do honor to the famed Vieux Carré". That's the drink, not the pairing. So A4. It also helps you: the name and the honour point at the old quarter, which is your "colours kept on". Quote it in our words ("to honour the old quarter"); Arthur's "he says" is the channel, so "He said" is right, never a bare "He made it to…".
