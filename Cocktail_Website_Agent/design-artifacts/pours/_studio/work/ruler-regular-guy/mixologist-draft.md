# Tomás: draft v1.2 (final for sign-off), ruler-regular-guy (The People's Representative)

Round 3. Story: the Vieux Carré (Wren r1, Hester r2, fences C1-C10 on `_studio/fact-cards/vieux-carre-walter-bergeron.md`). Spec: `_studio/specs/ruler-regular-guy.json`. **The drink:** Oxford's Vieux Carré (pdf 2102) at its own measures: rye, cognac and sweet vermouth at 30 ml each, 5 ml Bénédictine, 2 dashes each of Peychaud's and Angostura. **One change:** the sweet vermouth is steeped overnight with green cardamom. Of the three equal measures, the vermouth is the one that isn't either side's (Hester H1), and cardamom pairs with both the grain behind the rye and the grape behind the cognac (*Flavor Matrix*, below). Neither spirit is touched. Both go in exactly as bottled, at the same measure.

**Glassware:** large rocks glass (about 300 ml), one large ice cube.
**Contains:** nuts (the nutmeg in Bénédictine, Oxford pdf 256).

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 ml | straight rye whiskey | 40% or stronger; a 50% bottled-in-bond rye is what the numbers were run on |
| 30 ml | cognac | any VS or VSOP cognac, 40% or stronger |
| 30 ml | sweet vermouth steeped with cardamom | made the night before (see Method); start from any Italian-style sweet vermouth, as the 1937 recipe asks |
| 1 tsp (5 ml) | Bénédictine D.O.M. | recommended, as the old recipe names it; or a honeyed French herbal liqueur. Bénédictine contains nutmeg: check any substitute's label for nuts |
| 2 dashes | Peychaud's bitters | recommended, as the old recipe names it; or any New Orleans-style aromatic bitters |
| 2 dashes | Angostura bitters | or any aromatic bitters |
| 1 | strip of lemon peel | squeezed over the top, then dropped in |

## Method

1. The night before, put about 3 g of whole green cardamom pods (roughly 15) into 250 ml of sweet vermouth in a clean jar. Leave it at room temperature for about 12 hours, then strain it back into its bottle and keep it in the fridge. It's enough for eight drinks.
2. Pour the rye and the cognac into a mixing glass or jar, just as they come from the bottle.
3. Add the cardamom vermouth, the Bénédictine and both bitters. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
4. Stir for about 30 seconds, until the outside of the glass feels very cold.
5. Put one large ice cube in the rocks glass and strain the drink over it.
6. Squeeze the lemon peel over the top so its oils fall on the drink, then drop it in.
