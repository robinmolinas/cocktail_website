## Guards for the y4 spark clause (pending)
- Sourced: citrus is among vanilla's best pairings (*Flavor Matrix* pdf 256); Arnold adds "a drop of vanilla extract" to orange juice in the Dr. J, his Orange Julius variant (*LI* p. 271); vanilla comes from an orchid "native to Mexico" (Matrix pdf 256), optional.
- Ours: "orange and vanilla from the ice-cream counter" is Tomás's reading. Say it as the bartender's borrowing ("I borrowed…"), never as a fact about ice cream.
- Martinez borrowed a machine, not a flavour: "the way he borrowed a machine", and never suggest his drinks had vanilla.
