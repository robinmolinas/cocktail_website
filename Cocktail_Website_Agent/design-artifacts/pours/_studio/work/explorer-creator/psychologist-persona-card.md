# Persona card: The Innovator (explorer-creator)

Wren, round 1, 2026-09-30. Read fresh from the sheet (`persona.py explorer-creator`), my family sketch (`plans/work/explorer/psychologist-sketches.md`), the plan row, *Left Standing* and *Off-Label*.

## Core truth
They'll happily spend a weekend so they never have to spend ten minutes on something again. It starts as irritation, not inspiration: the everyday thing everyone else sighs at and puts up with gets under their skin and stays there. What they build to get out is, oddly, **structure**: a tool, a system, a way that works the same every time, so nobody has to think about it again (the sheet's drivers: seek paradise / PROVIDE STRUCTURE). The freedom they want is the freedom to stop fighting the same small thing. And the answer rarely comes from where the problem is: it's borrowed from somewhere unrelated and bent to fit (the sheet's essence, "a new angle").

## Shows / hides
- **Shows:** the fix. Calm, practical, "I just made it easier".
- **Hides:** how much of it starts as plain annoyance. It sounds like complaining, or ingratitude ("it was fine as it was"), so they show the solution and never the gripe. Also how long the "quick fix" actually took.

## Hidden fear → hidden gift
- **Fear:** not the cage itself (all eleven siblings fear entrapment: a horoscope sentence in this family). It's the **settled** answer: "that's just how it comes out". And under that, the day they'd hear themselves say it, and mean it.
- **Gift (say it out loud, per Robin's caregiver edits):** their best work disappears. People stop having the problem, forget it ever existed, and never know who to thank. Time given back to people who'll never know they lost it.

## The "real me" line to aim for
- Lead: "You'll happily spend a weekend so you never have to spend ten minutes on it again."
- Runner-up: "The best thing you make, nobody notices. They just stop having the problem."
- For the whoYouAre's hide: "You'd rather be called clever than difficult, so you show people the fix and keep the grumbling to yourself." (check vs "You'd rather" frames before use)

## Truth vs legend
The true story, and the mechanism. They want to know how it works and where the idea came from. Grant any legend, then correct it; never build on one.

## Tone
Direct, brisk, a little funny (the weekend-for-ten-minutes line is a joke on them they'll enjoy). Inspirational only in the ending. No reverence, no "genius" talk.

## Distinct from siblings (and near neighbours)
- **The Conservationist (*Left Standing*):** leaves the world as it found it and grieves change in advance. The Innovator can't leave it as it is. Opposite poles of one family, so none of its ground: no returning to a place, no "counting what's left", no "Everyone else… You…" opener, no "You'd rather X than Y" tagline (also *Making the Calls*).
- **The Experimentalist (*Off-Label*, the reverse pairing):** hates repeating ("being asked to repeat yourself feels like a door closing"), plays to find out. The Innovator has one target and wants the fix to repeat perfectly for ever. *Off-Label* owns "door": the sheet's "door nobody knew was there" stays out of our words.
- **The Mad Inventor:** the elaborate way on purpose, for joy. The Innovator: the simplest way out, for relief.
- **The Genius:** holds a question in the head, for truth. The Innovator holds a problem in the hands, for a way out.
- **The Trailblazer:** permission and nerve. The Innovator: the gripe and the build.
- **The Campaigner (*Not Too Polite*):** says it. The Innovator doesn't say it; builds it.
- **The Visionary (*Down the Line*):** a dream of scale that stays theirs. The Innovator's fix vanishes into ordinary use.

## Traps
- Freedom or the cage as the frame; "curious" (family-wide).
- Inventor, entrepreneur, disruptor labels; Dyson, vacuums, "thousands of prototypes", the Post-it (*Serviceable*), "outside the box".
- The failures you keep (the Researcher's and the Experimentalist's).
- The gadget as the point (the Mad Inventor's pleasure).
- "Door" imagery (*Off-Label*).
- Treating the annoyance as a flaw; the word "lazy" anywhere (Oxford's MARGARITA page says "lazy bartending" beside Martinez: keep it far away). Be gentle.
- A drink that reads cheap: never "slush", "slushie" or "machine drink" in the copy.

## Story test
1. An **everyday shortcoming others accepted**, with the complaint about it **on record**.
2. The fix **borrowed from somewhere unrelated**: a new angle, on record.
3. **Built, not argued.**
4. **Others now use it without thinking**, still today.
Out: a lucky accident, play, a dream of scale, a failure kept, medicine, a secret.

## Verdict on the plan's lead (Mariano Martinez and the frozen Margarita machine)
**Passes all four.** (1) Customers complained the drink was "inconsistent and not even cold" (AP 2005, web, secondary); in his own words, "we kept burning out the blenders as fast as we could buy them", bartenders "threatening to quit" (*VinePair* 2021). (2) The idea came from "a Slurpee machine at a 7-Eleven" (Smithsonian NMAH 2005.0226.01); the thing he bent was a soft-serve machine. (3) Built, "through trial and error", with others ("we developed"). (4) "A fixture of the age" (Oxford, MARGARITA pdf 1237: this page gives only the soft-serve machine and the spread; the complaints and the Slurpee are web, per Hester r2, who corrected my citation). And a machine that makes the drink identical every time is exactly the structure-for-freedom this person builds.
Conditions for round 2:
- (a) The Slurpee and soft-serve need a second page (ideally the Smithsonian's own object record): the recognition rests on them.
- (b) Martinez's own words about the problem, if they exist. If not, the reading says "complaints" and signposts his feeling.
- (c) Oxford's sniff stays out (the "anything-goes crowd pleaser", FROZEN DAIQUIRI pdf 854; the fruit flavours; the grain-alcohol scandal). Never "a better Margarita". The mirror is the fix, not the drink's reputation. He's a living person: dignity.
- (d) No year (1971 vs 1973). Tommy's and Bermejo out (*Half a Rim*).
- (e) If he never patented it, dossier only: "built it and didn't hold it" is close to *Left Standing*'s dossier line and the Visionary's handing-on.
Backup (Reiner's jigger): reserve only.
