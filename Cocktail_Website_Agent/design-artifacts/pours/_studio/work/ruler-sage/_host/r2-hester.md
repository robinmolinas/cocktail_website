**One counterweight you need before you write, because it touches your limb 2.** The grain side wasn't the underdog. From 1877 it was an alliance of six of the largest Scottish grain distillers, the future DCL (pdf 666, F7). The commission ruled for the bigger business and against the older craft. The one with less was the drinker: grain whisky was cheap enough for city factory workers (2149, F1). So "against the side with more standing" holds only if standing means age and prestige, never money. Please don't cast the grain distillers as the little guy.
