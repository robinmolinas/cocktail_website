## Guards
- **Cause:** three versions (Oxford tuberculosis; Difford's a cable-car accident; Beyl an accident that led to tuberculosis of the knee). No cause, and no "sickly" (it names the illness by the back door).
- **Age:** none in guest text. If Wren uses his age at the shack, it's 31, not "about thirty-two" (born 10 Dec 1902, opened Nov 1934); safer "in his early thirties".
- **His telling is single-sourced:** Beyl is a second source for the *belief*, not for Vic telling it. The search summary said otherwise; the page doesn't (C3).
- **Ice pick:** now on a page (Beyl), but a 1996 essay is said to have a fork (unread). Out of guest text (Wren's rule, and the objects conflict).
- **"Good" vs "the best":** not a tall tale and not a conflict (C5). Don't set them against each other; if his gloss is used, it's his ("he said it meant…").
- Never "lie", "fake", "made up", "far from the truth", "inconvenienced" (Beyl's disability framing), "despite".
- Copy: Oxford's "selling everything from paint to tires", "elegant, layered, timeless" and Beyl's "knockabout with a wooden leg" stay out as runs; paraphrase.
- Walter Bergeron (*One Table*) shares a surname only. Vic's Floridita visit is the Superhero row's. Berry, Donn Beach, "Let's get the record straight": out of guest text.
- Tyre coincidence: Oxford's tyres and the sheet's flat tyre. If whoYouAre uses a tyre and y-text uses his tyre jobs, own it in one plain clause.
- Any present tense about the chain: dated ("more than a dozen, last I read in 2015"), secondary.

- **Vic's orgeat was bought** ("French orgeat syrup", *Joy* pdf 327) and his ice was shaved, not crushed and mounded: "every piece of the show is Vic's own" overclaims. Say "the mint and the lime shell are Vic's own". Never set a bought orgeat against the real thing.
- His sugar was rock candy syrup (Oxford pdf 1211; "a dash" in his own account, *Joy* pdf 327); ours is 5 ml of 1:1 simple. His curaçao was "from Holland"; ours is a dry style. Never "his recipe", "his curaçao", "his syrup".
