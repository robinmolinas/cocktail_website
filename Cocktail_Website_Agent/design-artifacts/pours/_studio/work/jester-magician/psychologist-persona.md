# Persona card: The Elf/Imp (jester-magician) · Wren, r1

Source: `persona.py jester-magician` (xlsx row). Essence: cheeky, mischievous, a wicked sense of humour. "Trouble people let in on purpose." Sugar swapped for salt, keys hidden somewhere impossible, a coin from behind an ear, an innocent look while everyone works out what happened. "It never goes too far, because the point is the grin, not the victim." A house where nothing unexpected happens would drive them mad. Goal pleasure, fear boredom; Magician fears insignificance, dullness, inertia. Drivers: build connection / LEAVE A MARK.

## The one true thing (no sibling has it)
**Their mischief has rules nobody knows about.** Everyone sees chaos; in fact every trick is cut to fit one person and built so it can be put right in seconds. The salt goes back in the salt cellar, the keys turn up before anyone's late, the coin goes back in the pocket. For every trick people see, there are a few they decided against, because of who'd be on the end of it. That private line is why people let them in on purpose: the trouble is always the kind you'd want.

## Hidden, feared, given
- **Hides:** the rules, and the care in them. They'd rather be thought a menace than be caught being thoughtful. The innocent look covers the planning, not guilt.
- **Fears:** on the surface, a house where nothing happens. Underneath, the one trick that lands on someone the wrong way: becoming a bad day in someone's life. That fear is what keeps every trick small, and it also keeps every trick short-lived. So nothing they make lasts (LEAVE A MARK, unmet).
- **Gives (say it out loud, once):** permission to be surprised, safely. Around them a grown-up house still has play in it, and nobody gets hurt.

## Position (Ryu's, earned last)
Make one that stays. Their tricks are built to vanish by teatime; the next one doesn't have to. Build a surprise into something that lasts (a place, a thing, a habit) and let it go on catching people long after the moment, without them there. In their interest: it answers the fear of insignificance and the unmet "leave a mark", and it's still harmless, so it keeps their rule.

## Siblings, kept apart
- **Tease** (*I'll Tell You Later*): timing of a reveal everyone expects; owns watching it "reach their faces", "ordinary evening", "playing along". Elf: nobody knows anything's coming.
- **Alternative Comic** (*Unrehearsed*): a joke in the form, at an occasion; owns "straight face", "watching every face", "never explain". Elf: private, small, planted.
- **Wacky One** (*For Kicks*): a daft thing kept up publicly for life; owns "grow out of it", "never saw a reason to stop".
- **Stand-up**: words that name the everyday. **Morale Booster**: fun for a group, the list of flops. **Naïf**: earnest, not mischievous. **Wise Talker**: humour in hardship. **Scandalmonger**: the gasp at the pious (the Elf has no target). **Straight Man**: credit running the wrong way (keep any "who gets the credit" angle out of this pour). **Cynic**: wit against a lie.
- **Outside the family:** the **Trickster** (*No Accident*) owns "underneath the mischief, you care", "every trick is an invitation", "chaos / plan three moves ahead", being ignored. The Elf's rules are about who'd get hurt, never strategy. The **Surrealist** owns "a minute later" and "looks like something else". The **Mad Inventor** owns "someone in the doorway", "an ordinary job becomes the most interesting thing in the house". The **Trailblazer** (*Fine by Me*, same bar) owns "a way in" and "in plain sight, with a grin". The **Angel** (*Before the Room*) owns "at the door". The **Entertainer** owns practising for strangers.

## Traps
1. Gatekeeping: secret, password, getting in, "if you know". Affection, never exclusivity.
2. Cruelty: no victim, no "fooled", no "gotcha". Nobody is the butt.
3. Childish: "kid", "imp" as naughty child, "naughty". The guest is a grown-up with play in them.
4. Faces and straight faces (taken three times in this family).
5. Deception in the glass: nothing hidden from the guest, no lie about what's in it, no "truth at the end".
6. "Ordinary" as the gift (Tease, Mad Inventor): name the safety of the surprise instead.

## Story test
A surprise built into an everyday place or thing, played completely straight, harmless, and it **lasted**: it's still catching people long after it was made. The maker's joke is on record (their words); affection and motive may be signposted, but the event must be on a page. Out: hoaxes, deception about a drink's past, anything that reads as a secret or a way to keep people out, Prohibition.
