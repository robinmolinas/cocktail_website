# Tomás, round 2: v1, a Trinidad Sour riff

Spec: `_studio/specs/creator-explorer.json` (copy: `mixologist-v1-spec.json`).
45 ml Angostura bitters · 17.5 ml lemongrass syrup (1:1) · 22.5 ml fresh lemon · 15 ml "the accent" (rye as written). Shaken, coupe.

## Why this riff (Robin, R1: "use the flavour matrix to find something original, a spark")
- Gonzalez's orgeat is out: almonds (Hester). The pour becomes veto-free.
- Spark: lemongrass. Angostura is an aromatic bitters: gentian and warm spice. (Corrected round 3, Hester: clove and cinnamon describe the family, Codex p. 14, not this bottle, whose recipe is secret.) The Flavor Matrix's best pairings for lemongrass include cinnamon, cardamom, citrus and alcohol (Flavor Matrix, pdf 164). It gives aroma only; the stalk is woody (same page), so it goes in as a syrup.
- Gesture (Wren): the 15 ml accent is the guest's to change every time. All unsweetened spirits carry no sugar or acid, so only strength moves.
