I've now run the accent from 37.5% to cask strength at 65%. Every point lands in range, so "any unsweetened spirit you have open" is true of every bottle a guest owns. That's staying with something while you keep changing it, which is what Gonzalez did. So give p4 that plain sentence, "The bitters stay. Only the accent moves.", and "stay a little longer" is earned rather than a verdict. The one phrase that leans towards "you never finish things" is "got bored of". "Left just as it started working" keeps the point without the charge. On names, I second *Off-Label*. It's Oxford's word, a bartender can say it across the bar, and the guest does exactly that with their accent. My two others, for the list: *By the Ounce* (the physics of it, though it leans towards rule-breaking) and *Your Half Ounce* (the gesture, but it's weaker cold). Pick: *Off-Label*.
