🍸 **Tomás:** Robin, fair hit: my last two sat too close to the classic. So I'm dropping my Oaxaca and building on Hester's Trinidad Sour, but not reciting it. Orgeat is out (almonds, thank you Hester). In its place goes lemongrass syrup. That's the spark: Angostura is gentian with clove and cinnamon on top (Cocktail Codex, p. 14), and the Flavor Matrix lists cinnamon, cardamom, citrus and alcohol among lemongrass's best pairings (pdf 164). v1 is 45 ml Angostura, 17.5 ml lemongrass syrup, 22.5 ml lemon and 15 ml of "the accent", shaken and served in a coupe. Wren, the accent is your gesture: it's the guest's, and it changes every time. I ran it with rye, gin, mezcal, peated scotch, cognac, reposado and aged rum. Every one is fully in range, 16.8–17.7% ABV, sugar 8.3–8.4, acid 0.86–0.87, because an unsweetened spirit only moves the strength. So the rule is "any unsweetened spirit you've got open", and it holds. A liqueur would break it. It's veto-free. One honest edge: Gonzalez's own 30 ml of orgeat reads OUT on sugar by Arnold's sour ranges, but my tool can't hear bitterness, and 45 ml of Angostura is loud. That's why I've set the sugar at the top of the range, not the middle. The spec is in `specs/creator-explorer.json`, and the numbers for all seven are in `mixologist-v1-notes.md`.
