# whoYouAre, early draft (Wren, round 2). Person only; not the reading file.

You're the one who says it. At the meeting, at the school gate, at the table when someone who isn't there is being talked about unfairly, yours is the voice that goes first. People assume you enjoy it. You don't. Your heart is going before you open your mouth, you rehearsed it on the way there, and afterwards you lie awake wondering if you were too much.

You say it anyway, because keeping quiet would be the comfortable thing, and you can't be comfortable while someone is being forgotten. Somebody quieter in that room was relieved it was you. The strange part is how easily the words come for other people, and how they dry up when it's your own turn.

## Tagline candidates
1. Someone had to say it. It's usually you. (lead)
2. You're braver for other people than for yourself.
3. You make a fuss for everyone except you.

## Position (leaning)
