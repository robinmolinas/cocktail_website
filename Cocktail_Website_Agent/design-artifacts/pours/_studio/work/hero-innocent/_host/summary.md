- **The persona:** the Initiate is sure there's more in them than anyone has seen, and has no proof yet. One offhand "not quite" from the right person keeps them up all night. What they dread is being asked for it properly, in front of the person they wanted to impress. Their gift is that they take a hard word home and come back with something (Wren, r1).
- **The story:** Dale DeGroff and Joe Baum (*A Proper Drink* pp. 11–13; Oxford BULLSHOT, RAINBOW ROOM). Baum auditioned him to run a classic cocktail bar and gave only oblique instructions ("Joe never told me anything"). He tasted DeGroff's beef-broth drink and said "There's something wrong with it. Fix it." The answer came from beef with orange on a Chinese menu, and the orange is still in DeGroff's printed recipe. It was the plan's lead, accepted by Wren with conditions in r1. Backup (unused): Toby Maloney.
- **Versions that failed:** "off the chart" (the *Matrix* beef wheel lists citrus); "most people have never tried it" (replaced by Hester's sourced "once everywhere… half-forgotten now"); "missing" in the closing line (Baum said *wrong*); "the orange lifts it at the end" (the peel is smelt first, and the juice runs all through the drink); "That was all" (the tellings don't name the lemon or lime).
- **The drink:** a Bullshot, as DeGroff prints it: 60 ml vodka, 90 ml home-made plain beef broth, 5 ml orange juice, 5 ml medium dry sherry and a pinch of salt, shaken and served in a tall glass of ice, with orange peel squeezed over. Veto-free. Balance judged by hand (freeform; in the highball ranges). No spark: the orange is from the page. Closing line: *Squeeze the orange peel over it last. When nobody will say what's wrong, read every menu you're handed.*
- **"This is me":** "You'd never admit how much one sentence from the right person can move you."
- **Names:** *Work It Out*, 2–1 (Tomás and Hester; Wren had said she could take it, and she accepted). *Nobody Told Me* goes to Robin as the alternative.
