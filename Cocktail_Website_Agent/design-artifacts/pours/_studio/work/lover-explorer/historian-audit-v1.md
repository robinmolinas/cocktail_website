## Fences (Tomás's ask), for every line still to come (closing line, names, image brief)
- **Hangover roots (F22):** no cure, hangover, morning after, brunch, "pick-me-up" or "day-after". Oxford roots the whole family there (pdf 291–292), and none of it comes in.
- **Equal parts isn't a first:** the 1929 Broadway mix was equal parts tomato juice and gin (pdf 292). Never "the first" or "the only" equal-parts drink.
- **No year for the St. Regis drink** (C3: 1934 vs 1941). No Petiot, no Jessel, no Harry's Bar.
- **Never "his recipe"** for our glass. It was Lauryssen's, given to Gaige's book, and ours is changed (manzanilla, more lemon, no Worcestershire or cayenne).
