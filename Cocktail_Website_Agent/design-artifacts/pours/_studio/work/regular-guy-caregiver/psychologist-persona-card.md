# Persona card: The Good Samaritan (regular-guy-caregiver), Wren, round 1

Sheet: essence "Stepping in and helping out. Not passing by on the other side of the road." Goal belonging, fear exclusion (primary: exclusion, discourtesy, individuality; secondary: selfishness, indifference, cruelty). Example: the 2005 London bombings (ordinary people helping strangers). Story: everyday kindness, a seat for a stranger, quiet heroism, small acts that ripple.

## The one true thing
You see the people everyone else has learned not to see, and you treat them like anyone. The person on the pavement the crowd steps round, the one asking for change by the cash machine, the lost tourist, the person crying on the train. Not a rescue: a hello, a seat, a coffee, a name. For a minute, someone who'd become invisible is just another person in the queue again.

The parable's own question is "who is my neighbour?", and this person's answer is "whoever's in front of me". Their circle has no edge.

## Hidden
- They can't un-see. Everyone else has the knack of looking through people; they never learned it, and some days it's exhausting.
- The ones they walked past (late, tired, the third one that morning) stay with them far longer than all the times they stopped.
- Stepping out of a crowd is awkward for someone who'd rather blend in (primary fear: standing out). They do it anyway, and slip back in fast.

## Fear
Indifference becoming normal: a street where everyone looks away. Underneath, the exclusion fear turned outward: they know how easily anyone can end up on the wrong side of the glass, and that it could be them.

## Gift (say it out loud, Robin's rule)
For the person they stop for: being treated as someone, not a problem. For everyone watching: proof that stopping is ordinary. Kindness that belongs to everyday life, not to heroes.

## Siblings (Regular Guy) and cross-family neighbours
- Hidden Talent (gift unseen), Entrepreneur (started from nothing), Have A Go Hero (took on a big problem untrained), Bumpkin (plain pleasure), Prankster (mischief), One Next Door (the familiar one), Mechanic (makeshift), Scoundrel (charming rule-bender), People's Champion (calm lead, then steps back), Everyman (handed-down know-how). None is about strangers the world has stopped seeing.
- Owned nearby, keep out: the Nurse (*Everybody's*: room mishaps, towel/chair/name, same for everyone, Irish Coffee to arrivals); the Rescuer (*Off Duty*: emergencies, first to move); the Researcher (*Serviceable*: "people you'll never meet"); the Firefighter (*Anyone Would Have*: the shrug, "anyone would have", singled out = left out); the Healer (*Till Spring*: help easy to take); the Saviour (*Next One's Mine*: "put it on me", can't owe); the Fairy Godparent (*Their Night*: unasked, at the back); the True Friend (*Four Shares*: makes sure nobody is left out; "keep one cup back for yourself"); the Craftsman (*Not Only the Way*: "it was nothing"); the Entertainer (*Cold Water First*: strangers at a bus stop).

## Traps
1. "People you'll never meet" (Researcher). The sospeso's donor never meets the recipient; the reading must stand on the one who comes in, not on distance.
2. "Nobody left out" (True Friend). Ours is a stranger the world stopped seeing, not a friend at the edge of the group.
3. Arrivals, a cup handed out, the same for everyone (Nurse, mirror pairing, also a coffee drink). One person, one cup, never "everyone".
4. Gratitude not owed / easy to take (Healer). Never the gift.
5. Paying for others as a ledger (Saviour). No "put it on me", no books.
6. The shrug, "anyone would have", "it was nothing" (Firefighter, Craftsman).
7. Hero, saint, "good": the name already says it. No "quiet heroism", no praise; recognition only.
8. Rescue, emergency, medical, the bombings themselves: out.
9. Charity, pity, "the less fortunate" as a label for people: the person asking is a customer, not a case.
10. Scene: public, everyday, strangers (street, bus, café, queue). Never a party or a home.

## Story test
Ordinary people (not one hero) make room, at small cost and with no credit sought, for someone the world around them has stopped seeing, in an everyday place, and treat them as anyone. The habit holds or spreads.

## Verdict on the plan's lead (caffè sospeso)
Accept, re-angled. The crossing gap is retired: the mirror is the one who comes in later and asks, and gets a coffee at the same bar as everyone else. Conditions: no date or era; "Is there anything suspended?" and the fading/return only as "one reporter wrote" (NPR alone); never "never meet"; never gratitude; never a first giver; Bulgaria's self-immolations out; drink never a cup handed to arrivals, never Irish Coffee. Backup still needed (Boldt struck): something face to face.

## Position (leaning, for round 2)
You can't stop for every one, and the ones you miss stay with you. Leave something waiting for them: a cup paid ahead turns the ones you'll miss into ones you didn't. Gain: they walk on lighter. Wording to be checked against *Next One's Mine* ("lighter"), *Four Shares* ("one cup"), *Till Spring* ("put them down").
