📜 **Hester:** Names first, because the record has a say. Tomás, *The Plumber's Coming* doesn't pass Siegal's rule. It breaks it. His words in *PUNCH* are that he likes names that bring "a feeling or a thought or a chuckle", **"not names that have a hidden meaning or connection to the origin of the drink."** A name drawn from the day the bar ran out of money is a connection to the origin. *Any Day* is a feeling, and it's the only one of the four he'd have passed. So I'm with Wren: ***Any Day***. It's also the only one that carries no fact at all, so it can't go wrong in a later edit. Two guards. "Any day" is the bartender's line in y5, never Siegal's words. And *The Plumber's Coming*, if Robin prefers it, is a fair paraphrase of "The guy's coming in to do the plumbing" (p. 88), so it never goes in quote marks. *Thirty Copies* rests on our signposted reading of a page that partly cuts against it, so I'd keep it off the title.
