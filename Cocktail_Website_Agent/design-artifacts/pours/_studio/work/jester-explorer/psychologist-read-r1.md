# The Alternative Comic (jester-explorer): Wren's read, round 1

Sources: persona.py (xlsx row), family plan row, *A Proper Drink* pp. 234-235 (read whole, printed pages), sibling and cousin readings (Anyway, For Kicks, Is It Just Me, Cold Water First, Off-Label, The Other Berry, No Accident, Someone Else's Map).

## The one true thing
They don't play with the joke. They play with what the room expects to happen next. The fun, for them, lives in the second where nobody knows whether they're allowed to laugh, and they stretch that second on purpose. Not for cruelty and not to look clever: because whatever people feel after it is really theirs, not a reflex. Anyone can get the laugh people walked in with. They want the one nobody rehearsed.

## Hide / fear / give
- **Hide:** they care, a lot, whether it lands, and about the people in the room. They watch every face. They'd never explain a bit, because explaining feels like breaking it, so when it doesn't land they keep a straight face and carry it home alone.
- **Fear (surface):** boredom, but a precise one: the evening where everyone reacts on cue, the punchline you could see coming, being asked to "do the one you do" (a known act is a cage: the Explorer half).
- **Fear (under it):** that the strangeness reads as coldness. That people think they were the target, or were left outside it.
- **Give (said out loud, Robin's rule):** a real reaction instead of a polite one. The night people still try to describe days later. Permission, for one second, not to know how to feel.

Other traps: contempt for the audience ("they didn't get it"); edginess, discomfort as the goal; Andy Kaufman (his hoaxes are the Trickster's, his death never; not our touchstone); fine dining, price, luxury; "molecular"; the page's pun ("in the rock … on the rocks") is the writer's, never the Aviary's; "awkward" and "weird" as labels on the guest.

## Story test
Someone broke a drink's expected form on purpose, to get a real reaction rather than the expected one; the first room didn't know what to make of it (on the page); and what came next kept every bit of the strangeness and added company. Out: hoaxes, ingredient experiments, "then they got normal".

## Verdict on the plan's lead (the Aviary): accept, narrowed
Passes on the page (*Proper* 234-235): no bar to stand at; an Old-Fashioned, the most expected drink there is, sealed in a ball of ice you crack open; the Chicago crowd "received Aviary rather coolly at first"; Joly: "it lacked a sense of hospitality"; then "the drinks were just as bizarre, but now they came with a bartender joke and a chat". The coincidence I'd hand the room: Joly's criticism IS this person's hidden fear (strange read as cold), and the fix is this person's position.
Fences, from the page:
1. The page says "coolly" and "mixed feelings", not "puzzled". Write it in that scope.
2. The page doesn't say they changed *because* of the reception: Joly's arrival "altered" the approach. No "they listened and tried again" as fact; signpost it or leave cause out.
3. One mirror: the drink (In the Rock) and the turn (Joly). Achatz once, for the idea; Schoettler out (a third name, and "learned the classics from scratch" leans on the Old Master's ground).
4. "We had to talk him into our world": the sceptic brought inside. Good texture, one clause.
5. Joly told gently; no "lacked hospitality" as a verdict on the guest.

## Position (Ryu's turn)
Keep every bit of the strange. Stay for the chat. Explaining isn't what breaks it; leaving people alone with it does. (Not "explain the joke", not "let them into the practice", not "be normal".) Ends on what they gain: the room comes with them next time.

## For Tomás (direction only, no spec yet)
The strangeness belongs to the form and the serve, never to odd ingredients (*Off-Label*). The drink inside should be a proper, renowned Old-Fashioned the guest would want anyway: the joke is on how it arrives. Breaking the ice at home must be simple and not a show (no drink you perform), and must not echo *Half a Rim*'s clear-ice cutting. The "bartender joke" could live in the closing line.
