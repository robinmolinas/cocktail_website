# Wren's alternatives (jester-explorer)

## y4 without the rum (if Tomás keeps the bottles plain)
4. The cocktail I've made for you takes that idea home. Inside is the renowned classic, a rye Old-Fashioned with demerara sugar, Angostura bitters and orange, chilled and already diluted, waiting in a shell of ordinary cloudy ice from your own freezer. One tap of a spoon and it runs out into the glass. Nothing inside it is strange; the joke is entirely in how it arrives, which, if you ask me, is you exactly.
