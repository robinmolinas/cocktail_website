## Wren's Arnold Palmer gloss
**Sourced, one word softened.** *LI* p. 263: Arnold describes the Arnold Palmer as a mixture of iced tea and lemonade, named for the golfer; ESPN asked him for an *alcoholic* version. What went into those first versions isn't named. So "iced tea and lemonade" stays, and "with a spirit in it" becomes **"with alcohol in it"** (S1). Added to the card as F14.
