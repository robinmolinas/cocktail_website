## Guards (for Wren's reading and the audit)
- **1688 is milk punch in general**, not the curdled kind (audit v1 X3). Never put 1688 next to "curdled".
- **Not "invented"** for Franklin, Rockett or Arnold. Franklin *wrote a recipe down*. Behn only "possibly" (never state). First record 1688; earliest written recipe 1711.
- **The milk's direction.** "Liquor into milk" is Arnold's modern rule (LI p. 271). The old recipes put hot milk into the punch (Franklin; Oxford's Rockett adaptation). Never call our method Rockett's or Franklin's.
- **"Back through its own curds"** is Oxford's modern adaptation, not verified as 1711 wording: say it of *our* method, not of history. Franklin's word on record is "till it is clear", but that's the dossier only: **"clear" is not the reveal word** (Wren; Half a Rim).
- **"Keeps for years"**: not on the page. Oxford: indefinite storage without refrigeration, Dickens's bottles (undated). Never claim it for our punch.
- **"Rough brandy"** (Arnold's guess about Franklin): inference, out.
- **Royal warrant**: out (Oxford contradicts itself: Whisson vs Hudson; "mid-eighteenth century" vs Victoria).
- **Arnold Palmer** = iced tea and lemonade (LI p. 263); his versions were "alcoholic", spirit unnamed: "with alcohol in it", not "a spirit". He made "several", not "a batch".
- **Arnold's quotes**: "weren't very good" and "unintended side benefit" are his, under 15 words. "The tea flavor was still very strong" is his too.
- **Tamarind**: "reputed/known as", never "is the most acidic and sweetest". Colour ("amber") is Tomás's expectation, not a fact.
- **"The milk leaves the sour alone"**: Tomás's inference from LI p. 265; p. 266 says proteins also strip some colour and flavour. Signpost it or keep it out.
- **Siblings**: no "it was always there" (As It Was); no "rescued a bad drink" as the whole story (Half a Rim), since Arnold's is about what stays, not what's refused; "leave it once it breaks" sits close to No Accident's "Don't stir it". Say "let it rest".
- Franklin's recipient and date (James Bowdoin, 11 Oct 1763): Founders Online listing only, page not fetched. Say "a 1763 letter".
