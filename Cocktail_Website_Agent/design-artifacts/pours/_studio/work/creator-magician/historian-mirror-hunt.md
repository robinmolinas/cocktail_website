# Mirror hunt: creator-magician, The Makeover Artist (Hester, round 1)

Persona: takes raw potential and turns it into something extraordinary; "revealing hidden brilliance"; the transformation is almost miraculous. Fear: status quo, mediocrity; Magician's fear: unintended results, insignificance. Imagery: before-and-after.

## A. Toby Cecchini and the Cosmopolitan (person + drink) — my pick
**Story (sources' words where it matters).** A drink called the Cosmopolitan was already going round San Francisco; a 1987 newspaper called it a Kamikaze with cranberry. At the Odeon, most likely in 1988, Cecchini "didn't think much of it" and began tinkering: Oxford says he *re-engineered* it (fresh lime for bottled, Cointreau for cheap triple sec, the new Absolut Citron). It was the staff drink; customers started asking for it, and soon he watched other bars making it "wrong and badly".
**Why it mirrors.** A makeover in the literal sense: he didn't invent a drink, he saw what a crude one could be and remade it part by part, and his remake became "the version everyone means". The fear is in the second half: the makeover escaped him and went back to mediocre in other hands, then the next generation rejected it as the very thing to be made over. Unintended results, on the record.
**Sources.** *A Proper Drink* pp. 40–44 (his account, 2014); Oxford COSMOPOLITAN pdf 585–586, KAMIKAZE pdf 1128. Card: `fact-cards/cosmopolitan-toby-cecchini.md` (F1–F14, C1–C4).
**Risks.** C1: the original's red ingredient is contested (newspaper: cranberry; Cecchini: grenadine), so "he swapped in cranberry" is his account only. Not "invented". Year "most likely". A famous drink: needs Tomás's spark. **Sibling clash:** *Rosetta* already uses cranberry syrup.

## B. Clarified milk punch (the transformation itself) — runner-up
**Story.** Pour scalding milk into a spiced punch and it curdles, which looks like ruin; strain it back through its own curds and it comes out clear, smooth, and able to keep for years (bottles in Dickens's cellar). Dave Arnold rediscovered the trick rescuing a drink everyone agreed wasn't very good, and got an "unintended side benefit": silk.
**Why it mirrors.** The ugly middle of every makeover; the brilliance was in the punch all along.
**Sources.** Oxford MILK PUNCH pdf 1305–1306; *Liquid Intelligence* pp. 263, 267. Card: `fact-cards/clarified-milk-punch.md`.
**Risks.** No single person to mirror; Behn's invention only "possibly"; Franklin letter not yet fetched. **Sibling clash:** clarity/cloud is *Half a Rim* and *Rosetta*. Dairy.
