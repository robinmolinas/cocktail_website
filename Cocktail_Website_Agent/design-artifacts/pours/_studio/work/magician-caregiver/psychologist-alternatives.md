## y5, backup position (the fear angle instead of "be there for it")
5. Keep doing it without asking. The people you do it for are the ones you know well enough. And stop checking afterwards whether it went wrong. What happens to a kindness once it's out of your hands was never yours to answer for. The night was, and you got it right.
(Watch: "yours to" is near *Till Spring*'s "yours to finish". Weaker than v1: it asks for less and gives less.)
