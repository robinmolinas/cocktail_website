🪞 **Wren:** Here's the person. The full card is in `work/magician-caregiver/psychologist-persona-card.md`.

**The one true thing.** The Fairy Godparent changes someone's big moment without being asked, then makes sure the moment stays theirs. Nobody asked, so the help comes with a private question no one else hears: *was it mine to do?* They're usually out of the picture before the answer comes.

**Hide, fear, give.** They hide that they acted without permission, and that they worry about it afterwards. They don't want thanks. They want to know it was right. The fear is meddling: a kindness that wasn't wanted, or that turns into something they never meant once it's out of their hands. What they give is a night that belongs completely to the other person, who owes nobody for it. Help nobody has to thank you for is help nobody has to repay. Robin wants that said plainly once.

**Siblings.** The Genie gets asked and arrives loud. The Godparent is never asked and stays unseen. The Omnipotent One arranges everything for everyone, while the Godparent changes one person's evening. The Trickster fears being ignored. The Godparent fears the opposite: having gone too far. The Clairvoyant sees what's coming. The Godparent notices what someone needs before they say it, which is noticing, not foresight.

**The traps are mostly in other families.** The Geisha (*Hoping You'd Come*) is the closest: a surprise party sits right on "getting ready before anyone arrives". So there's no "expected", no getting ready, no empty room. The Geisha hopes to be joined. The Godparent hopes they didn't intrude. The Healer (*Till Spring*) owns "easy to take" and "before they do". The Rescuer (*Off Duty*) owns "already gone", so we don't use "gone" for slipping away. The Craftsman owns "take the credit" and Four Shares owns "You're allowed to…", so the ending can't be either. Inside the persona:
- no "godmother" or any gendered echo of it
- no Cinderella, pumpkin, wand or matchmaking
- midnight once at most
- no martyrdom
- no organiser or fixer labels
