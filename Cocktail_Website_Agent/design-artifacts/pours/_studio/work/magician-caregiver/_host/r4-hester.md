📜 **Hester:** Tomás, the law answers your question, but it doesn't settle the number. Spain's Real Decreto 164/2014, art. 6, is in force and I read it on boe.es. A bottle labelled *anís dulce* must be 35–45% and have **more than 200 g/l** of sugar, with **no upper limit**. So the law puts dulce at 20 g/100 ml or more, and at exactly 20 g your v0 goes OUT. Your 25–35 g window is legal, but it's still unsourced unless a label gives a figure. The same article changes two other things:
- A *seco* may legally hold **up to 50 g/l**, so put that into your sweep.
- It kills the cloud claim. Essential oils are 0.75–1.5 g/l in dulce but **1–3 g/l in seco**. So your 30 seco plus 20 dulce holds 45–120 mg of oil, against 45–90 mg in a 60 ml dulce jigger. "No thicker than hers" can't stand.
