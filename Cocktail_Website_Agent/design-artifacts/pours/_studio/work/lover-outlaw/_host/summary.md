- **The persona:** the Bad Influence doesn't want an audience, they want accomplices. Polite feels lonely to them; under every "come on" is a "please come", and the fear is being kept for the noise, or tamed and then dropped.
- **Story:** the plan's lead held. Wren accepted Employees Only (Dushan Zaric and Jason Kosmas, *A Proper Drink* pp. 153–157) with conditions in round 1: leaving Schiller's together, pouring by eye, the soup at closing, the staff who stayed because they "had a hand in creating this". The Brother Cleve backup wasn't needed.
- **The drink:** a rye Manhattan with a little dry orange curaçao (Employees Only's addition) and a sweet vermouth steeped about 12 hours with raw beetroot that the guest peels and slices by hand, so the drink marks its maker. Stirred, chilled coupe, orange peel. Fully balanced; veto-free. Spark from *Flavor Matrix* GRAPE (beet among grape's pairings), so the beet goes into the vermouth.
- **What failed or was struck:** "walked out" became "left"; Simonson's "nobody left" became Zaric's "very few ever left" (Hester, C5); the curaçao is never "the original" or "their recipe" (no page gives their measures; Byron 1884 not found). Tomás's first closing line repeated Wren's y5, so he took her wording. Hester's step-2 audit quoted five fixes to the reading (W1–W5: "other serious bars", Kosmas's "as he felt it", "Zaric was plain about it", "a bar he'd worked at", "one reason Zaric gave"), all landed in step 3.
