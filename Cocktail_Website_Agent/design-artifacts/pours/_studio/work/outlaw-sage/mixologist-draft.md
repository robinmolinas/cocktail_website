# outlaw-sage · The Devil's Advocate · Tomás's draft (v3, round 4)

Spec: `_studio/specs/outlaw-sage.json` (style `shaken`).

**Glass:** old-fashioned glass (rocks glass, about 300 ml), filled with fresh ice
**Contains:** nuts (amaretto), egg-white (raw egg white). Not veto-free.

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 45 ml (1½ oz) | amaretto | an Italian amaretto |
| 22.5 ml (¾ oz) | cask-strength bourbon | bottled at or near barrel strength; 55% or stronger |
| 22.5 ml (¾ oz) | fresh lemon juice | |
| ½ tsp (2.5 ml) | sherry vinegar | the Spanish kind (vinagre de Jerez); measure it, don't guess |
| 1 tbsp (15 ml) | egg white, lightly whipped | from a fresh egg; whip the white, then measure a spoonful |
| 1 | strip of lemon peel | over the top |

No sugar syrup: the amaretto brings all the sweetness.

## Method

1. Fill the glass with fresh ice and leave it to chill.
2. Separate a fresh egg. Whip the white with a fork for about 20 seconds, until it's loose and frothy, then measure one tablespoon. You won't need the rest.
3. Pour the amaretto, bourbon, lemon juice and sherry vinegar into a shaker. Add the spoonful of egg white last.
4. Fill the shaker with ice, close it, and shake hard for about 15 seconds.
5. Strain into the glass, over the fresh ice. A thin white foam will settle on top.
6. Squeeze the lemon peel over the foam, shiny side down, so its oils fall on the drink, then lay it on top.

Lemon with aged spirits (*Codex* p. 115). **The spark:** the *Flavor Matrix*'s Nut entry (pdf 184, printed 174) lists vinegar among nuts' best pairings. It's the only one of them that pushes against sweetness; the others are tropical fruit, honey, chocolate, roasted meat, caramel and popcorn. Amaretto counts as nut-flavoured here: Oxford ALMOND LIQUEURS (pdf 94) says it's commonly thought of as almond-flavoured, though it's generally made from apricot kernels. Acetic acid is the vinegar, the one common food acid with an aroma, used as a minor acid (LI p. 59, F23), so half a teaspoon is a taste you notice, sitting beside the lemon. Sherry vinegar over cider or wine vinegar is my craft call: the Matrix names no kind. The Nut wheel on pdf 185 appears to carry sherry and bourbon, but that page is OCR'd and I haven't rendered it, so it isn't cited. **Overlaps, owned:** sherry vinegar is in *Fair Measure*'s syrup, and balsamic is *Straight Back*'s float.
