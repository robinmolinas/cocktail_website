# Persona card: The Entertainer (magician-regular-guy)
Wren, round 1, 2026-10-01. Source: persona.py (xlsx row), plans/magician.md, plans/work/magician/psychologist-sketches.md, registry.

## The person
A pack of cards comes out at a kitchen table or a bus stop, and for one minute the ordinary world goes strange. It's for anyone: strangers included, no ticket. The hours of practice behind it are private. The flop is what sends them back to practise.

## The one true thing (what no sibling, and no pour yet, carries)
**They make the wonder, and they're the one person in the circle who doesn't get it.** At the reveal, everyone else is a child for a minute. The Entertainer already knows how it's done, and is watching faces: the gasp, then the laugh, then two strangers turning to each other, suddenly not strangers. What they practise for isn't the gasp. It's that laugh passing between people who'd never have spoken.

Working "this is me" lines (not final):
- "You don't practise so they'll be amazed. You practise so they'll laugh together."
- "Everyone else gets to be a child for a minute. You're watching their faces."

## Hide / fear / give
- **Hide:** the hours, yes, but more than that, how much the minute matters to them. The trick is the easiest way they know to reach a stranger. (Never "shy", "insecure", "needs attention": no diagnosis.)
- **Fear:** the flop. Silence where the laugh should be, in front of strangers, where there's no friendship to catch it. The sheet's "unintended results" in the hands: the card that isn't there.
- **Give:** for one minute, anyone is let in. Grown-ups remember being children, and strangers end up in the same laugh. Said out loud once (Robin's "say the kindness" rule).

## Against the siblings (and the pours that sit nearest)
- **Trickster** (*No Accident*): a trick with a point; fears being ignored. The Entertainer's has no point but joy, and fears the fumble. Never "ignored", "strings", "a trick with a gift inside".
- **Illusionist:** wants certainty to wobble, stays a mystery; the second look. The Entertainer wants the laugh and would happily be known.
- **Genie:** big, for the one who asked. Entertainer: small, for anyone.
- **Fairy Godparent** (*Their Night*): unseen, at the back or gone. The Entertainer is right there in the middle; the distance is that they're the only one not amazed. Own this in the reading's shape: never "at the back", "slip out", "not there".
- **Apprentice:** a runaway. **Alchemist:** permanence. Clear.
- Cross-family, the real dangers:
  - **The Pioneer** (*Nothing to It*): "nobody's ever been able to tell", the shrug, "let them hear how close it came". Theirs is hidden fear; ours is hidden care and a place outside the laugh.
  - **The Campaigner** (*Not Too Polite*): "People think it comes easily to you. It doesn't… you rehearsed it."
  - **The Minimalist** (*Half a Rim*): "The last version looks like it took a minute." (And "a minute" is in our own story: own it or rephrase.)
  - **The Morale Booster** (*Anyway*): the flop list, "falls flat, laugh about it with them", "tell the story of the one that didn't take". The Booster plans a night for a group they know; the Entertainer does one minute for anyone.
  - **The Photographer** (*As It Was*): the ordinary day as the hook. **The Rescuer** (*Off Duty*): hot toddy, "sit down".
  - **The Geisha**'s alternative tagline "It all looks effortless" (not shipped, still avoid).

## Traps
- "Looks effortless / it isn't" as the reading's point: owned three times over. Hours of practice may appear as a fact of the person, never as the recognition.
- Labels: card-trick nerd, party magician, showman, performer. A coin behind the ear as the reading's image (in two sheets).
- Showing off as the motive.
- A drink that's a show (room lesson: no drink you perform). A throw or flame at home.
- Magic puns, "abracadabra", "ta-da", "now you see it".
- Making the watching sound lonely or sad. It's a cost the guest recognises with a smile, not a wound.

## Story test (updated from the plan)
1. A showpiece that looks easy in public and took long private repetition.
2. An ordinary audience, not an elite.
3. The payoff is shared delight: the crowd's pleasure is on the record.
4. A risk or fumble admitted somewhere.
5. The joy spread or came back.
6. (New, nice-to-have) something about where the maker's attention was while the crowd watched: the arc, the crowd, not the blaze. If absent, one signposted line.

## Verdict on the plan's lead (the Blue Blazer)
**Accept the story; rule on the drink.** "Practise for some time with cold water" is the hidden hours in the maker's own words, and a crowd that came to watch the blaze more than drink is the ordinary audience whose delight is the point. It also gives test 6 for free, signposted: the one person at the bar not watching the fire is the one throwing it.
- The Blazer is the most performed drink in the books, and the room's lesson is no drink you perform. The show belongs in the story. The guest's glass is the drink under the show, the one the crowd barely noticed, made for once for the person who always makes the show.
- **No throw at home, hot or unlit.** An unlit throw is still near-boiling liquid in an arc, and the scald is Thomas's own warning. Cold-water practice may be an image in the reading, never a method.
- Idea for Tomás (his call): Islay smoke carries the fire without a flame. (Keep clear of "stand in", the Genie's.)
- Jerry Thomas is *Beside the First*'s: one owned clause. "Jays" never in our voice. Kansas City burn: dossier only. Higgins mixing out of sight: foil only if Hester's page gives a reason; never invent one.
- Backup (Huber's) is weaker for this person: "don't show off, do it the same way every time" is the Craftsman's or the Nurse's creed, not the Entertainer's, and nutmeg is a veto.

## Position direction (not final)
Not "let them see the fumble" (*Anyway*'s) and not "tell them how hard it was" (*Nothing to It*'s). Instead, get them inside the laugh: **let one person into the practice**, where the spills are, so for once the Entertainer is laughing with someone rather than watching. The cold-water rehearsal is its image. Alternative: let someone fool *them*, and be the one who gasps.

## Tone
Funny is allowed: this is a light person. One real smile in the reading, warm, never jokey all through. Plain words.
