## Names (my pick)
**Cold Water First.** It's Thomas's own advice (F2), and "first" is fair: he put the practice before proficiency. Read cold, it's also the person. *Under the Blaze* is factually clean ("blaze" is the 1883 bartender's word), but it puts fire in the title of a glass that has none. *Nobody's Stranger* is clean and has no story in it.
