**The one true thing:** this is the person who takes everyone out there, and the same person who turns everyone round. They suggested the hill, and they're the one who says "we're going back" two hundred metres below the top. When the edge and the people pull against each other, the people win every time, even when it costs them the thing they came for. And people go further with them than they'd ever go alone, *because* they trust them to turn round in time. No sibling has both halves. The Nanny (*Fair Measure*) is the brake but never proposed the trip. The Pioneer (*Nothing to It*) goes first and alone. The Rescuer (*Off Duty*) runs in after someone.

**Hide, fear, give.** They hide how much they wanted the top. The disappointment gets swallowed on the spot so nobody feels they cost it. They also hide the weight: counting heads, the one face they're worried about, the doubt they can't show because everyone is reading theirs. The fear is being trapped, and worse, having led someone into it. What they give is daring made safe: people stand somewhere they'd never have gone, and they come home. And privately, every turn-round goes in the file marked "didn't make it".

**The siblings and the traps.** *Fair Measure* owns "one more, then home", the text when they're home, and fairness done in the open. *Off Duty* owns the crisis scene of handing out jobs and "let them do the going". *Four Shares* owns "allowed to be looked after". *Left Standing* owns "take someone". *Brought Home* owns the return ticket and "no way back". *Nothing to It* owns "tell them you were terrified". *For Good* owns the disaster nobody sees, so our version is about the goal given up, not the problem avoided. *In a Minute*, *Why Not?*, *Straight Back*, *Just Knew* and *Fine by Me* share no ground once those are cleared. Also out: captain or boss labels, flattery, the sea and the ship, "Endurance" as a pun, "no man left behind", any pronoun borrowed from the four men, and heroics. The point is that he refused the heroic last dash.

**My story test:** someone responsible for a group in the unknown who, when stuck, holds them together with judgment and structure rather than heroics, and brings every one of them back. It has to show the others depending on them, being trapped, the cost carried unseen, and "everyone came home" on the page.
