# The Expedition Leader (explorer-ruler): Wren's persona card, round 1 (2026-10-01)

Source text I read in full for the turning back and the return: Shackleton, *Shackleton in the Antarctic* (Heinemann 1911), Project Gutenberg #69138, saved here as `psychologist-src-shackleton-pg69138.txt` (line numbers below are that file's).

## The one true thing
**They're the one who takes everyone out there, and the one who turns everyone round.** The same person who suggested the hill is the one who says "we're going back" two hundred metres below the top. When the edge and the people pull against each other, the people win, every time, even when it costs them the thing they came for. People go further with them than they'd go alone *because* they trust them to turn round in time.

No sibling has both halves. The Nanny (*Fair Measure*) is the brake but never proposed the adventure. The Pioneer goes first, alone. The Rescuer goes in after someone. The Leader leads people *into* the unknown and owns getting every one of them back.

## Hide / fear / give
- **Hide:** how much they wanted the top. The disappointment is swallowed on the spot so nobody else feels they cost it. Also the weight: counting heads, the one face they're worried about, the doubt they can't show because everyone reads theirs. And a small private guilt that they love the edge they're leading people towards.
- **Fear:** trapped, and worse, having led someone into it. Going so far that not everyone can get back.
- **Give:** daring, made safe. Other people get to stand somewhere they'd never have gone, and they come home. The order the Leader brings is a kind of safety.
- **What they privately call it:** failure. Every turn-round goes in the file marked "didn't make it".

## Siblings and neighbours: what's taken
- *Fair Measure* (Nanny): owns "one more, then home", "a text when they're home", the sensible one, "if I stopped holding it, it would come apart", and fairness done in the open ("so nobody could think they'd been given short measure").
- *Off Duty* (Rescuer): owns the crisis scene of handing out jobs ("you call, you hold this, stay with them") and the position "let them do the going".
- *Four Shares*: "You're allowed to be looked after too." *Left Standing* y5: "don't go alone. Take someone".
- *Brought Home* (Tourist): "the return ticket", "no way back", home as half the trip.
- *Nothing to It* (Pioneer): the high board, going first, "tell them you were terrified".
- *For Good* (Innovator): the disaster nobody sees because it was prevented ("Nobody remembers the problem"). The Leader's version must be about the goal given up, not the problem avoided.
- *Making the Calls* (Auteur): doing everyone's part. *Anyone Would Have* (Firefighter): practice so the day feels ordinary.
- *Fine by Me*, *Just Knew*, *Straight Back*, *Why Not?*, *In a Minute*: no shared ground once the above are clear.

## Traps
- Captain, boss, commander, "born leader": labels and flattery. Military cosplay.
- The sea, the ship, ice floes, *Endurance* as a name or pun (the sheet's own story, and four pours already sailed).
- "No man left behind" and any borrowed pronoun: all four on the march were men; the guest is "you" only. Avoid quoting "man could only do his best".
- Rations, shares, portions as the mirror (*Fair Measure*). Hunger may be context in a clause.
- "One more" anything (*Fair Measure*), "no way back" (*Brought Home*), "allowed to" (*Four Shares*).
- Heroics. The point is the opposite: refusing the heroic last dash.

## Story test
Someone responsible for a group in the unknown who, when stuck, keeps them together by organisation and judgment rather than heroics, and brings every one of them back. Must show: the others depending on them; the moment of being trapped; the response as structure; the cost carried unseen. Must hold on the page: everyone came home.
