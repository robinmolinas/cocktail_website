# Tomás, round 2 notes (provisional; no spec until Wren rules)

## Lead: the Bluff depot plum in a Scotch Old-Fashioned, built over one big cube
- 60 ml Highland blended malt Scotch (Mackinlay's replica / Shackleton blended malt recommended, pending Hester; substitute style: Highland blended malt)
- 10 ml fresh-plum syrup: equal weights ripe plums (stoned) and white sugar, blended cold, pressed through a sieve (Codex p. 47 blended-fruit syrup method). Fresh fruit = veto-free; dried/candied fruit would go in as nuts on the safe side.
- 2 dashes Angostura (or orange bitters), orange peel squeezed over.
- Style `built` (LI ranges: 27-32%, 6.5-8.5 g, acid 0-0.05%, dilution 24% fixed).

## Why this one
- Wren r1: a nod to the way home, not the Pole: "Carlsbad plums, cakes, eggs, plum puddings" laid down at the Bluff depot by Joyce's party (Shackleton 1911, src lines 3977-78). The plum is the luxury waiting on the way back.
- Old-Fashioned = made from what keeps (plan row). The plum makes it not-a-plain-classic (Robin 2026-09-30).
- Matrix: stone fruit's best pairings include fruit brandy and wine (pdf 236); plum appears on the Grain wheel (pdf 137, OCR: render before quoting). Plum-and-Scotch itself is my craft knowledge, unsourced.

## Guards
- Not the prep as the gesture: "Make it a day ahead" is *For Good*'s closing line; the night-before oleo is *Four Shares*. The syrup is just prep.
- No shares, no measured portions (*Fair Measure*), no sea.
- Plum puddings also appear on the outward Christmas (85°51'S, src 3462-3474, with "a drop of medical brandy"): the nod is to the depot on the return, not Christmas.
- Neighbours: *Nothing to It* (bourbon OF, no ice, small tumbler, stirred-flagged), *Standing By* (blended Scotch OF, small wine glass, no ice). This is built on one cube in a heavy double tumbler, Highland blended malt, plum.
