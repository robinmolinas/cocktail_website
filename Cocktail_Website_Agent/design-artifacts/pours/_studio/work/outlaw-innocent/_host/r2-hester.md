**What happened after the book.** The book is from 2016.
- **2019:** Bar Agricole won the James Beard Award for best bar program, after eight years as a finalist. Two sources: SFist, Feb 2020, and Vogler's own PUNCH essay, Jan 2021. In the essay he says he was "relieved, but not joyful".
- **2020:** every one of his bars closed (PUNCH 2021).
- **2022 to 2024:** Bar Agricole reopened in two more places, and both closed (Hoodline 2022; SFist 2024).
