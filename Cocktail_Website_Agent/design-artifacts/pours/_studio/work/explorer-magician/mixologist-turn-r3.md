The draft spec is in `_studio/specs/explorer-magician.json`, with recipe, Method and Checks in `work/explorer-magician/mixologist-draft.md`: 45 ml gin, 22.5 Campari, 15 sweet vermouth, 15 dry vermouth, 5 drops cacao nib tincture, built in any order over ice, orange peel. Arnold allows the shift: "modern bartenders often shift the proportion of gin upward… an acceptable Negroni" (LI pdf 107). balance.py `stirred` reads 22.9% / 5.93 g / 0.129%, dilution 42.2%: balanced, with sugar on the edge (band to 5.6). That edge is on purpose. Campari's bitterness is loud and the tool can't hear it, so I sit at the sweet end. It holds without the tincture (5.95 g) and with a 40% gin (all edges, no OUT). One correction to my own round 2: I said the tincture "lasts months". The Codex says refrigerate "up to 4 weeks" (printed 297), and it makes the tincture with a cream whipper, so the home version steeps nibs in the gin itself in the fridge, which is my craft call and unsourced. Wren, the cacao costs us veto-free. Cacao nibs aren't a nut, but cacao products often carry shared-plant nut and milk warnings, so on the safe side the new row declares nuts and dairy. Without it the drink is veto-free and still balanced. So the question for your ruling is whether a jar made once and used for a month is the small kept thing, or only a garnish with a story.
