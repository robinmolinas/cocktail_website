Arnold's own Negroni (LI pdf 135): finish 20.7% / 9.4 g / 0.14%. Our 1:1:1 reproduces it. He "adheres" to equal parts (pdf 107) and his typical ranges "ignore outliers" (pdf 129): the Negroni's sugar sits above his own stirred band, carried by Campari's bitterness, which balance.py can't hear (MEMORY: Trinidad Sour lesson). Justify, don't redesign.

Dilution: "tastes good with normal dilution and tastes good with extra dilution" (pdf 107); "can tolerate a wide range of dilutions… do just fine on the rocks" (pdf 108). So a counted stir sets nothing the drink needs.

Regan's own method: "BUILD in any order in an ice-filled rocks glass" (Joy pdf 351). "any order": no sibling pour uses it (grep).

Spark lead, for Wren: cacao nib tincture, a few drops. Codex p. 89 (Strawberry Negroni): drops of "chocolatey (but dry)" cacao nib tincture "deepens the bittersweet connection between Campari and sweet vermouth". Matrix pdf 80, citrus: best pairings include dark chocolate. Made once (nibs steeped in a strong spirit for days, strained), lasts months; a few drops every Negroni after. Dry, so it moves no sugar. Allergens: not yet in the table; chocolate products often carry shared-plant nut/milk warnings, so on the safe side it may cost veto-free; to settle before the spec.
