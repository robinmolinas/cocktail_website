## Wren's question: "famous"
**"famous" passes.** Oxford doesn't use the word: it calls *Joy* "his best-known book" and says it "helped to jumpstart the cocktail renaissance" (pdf 1635). The support is Simonson: "an authority figure he nonetheless is, one of the accepted grand old men of the cocktail resurgence" (Proper p. 62; reliable secondary, on the record). That carries "famous" in the trade, which is the epigraph's scope. "Bartender" passes too: twenty years behind New York bars (Oxford contributor note pdf 2272; REGAN pdf 1634). "Well-known" is no safer, so keep "famous". The epigraph's colon presents his instruction, not the whole recipe, and that reads true.
