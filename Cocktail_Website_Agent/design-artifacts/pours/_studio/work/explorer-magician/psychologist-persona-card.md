# Persona card: The Spiritualist (explorer-magician), Wren, round 1

## The one true thing (no sibling has it)
They go a long way (a retreat, a walk, a shelf of books) for something small enough to carry into an ordinary week. The trip isn't the point; the one line or the small practice that holds on a Thursday is. The test of everything they find is whether it survives the ordinary day.

## Hidden
- How often the glow fades. Back from the retreat, it lasts until the first full inbox, and they count that as failing.
- Where some of it came from. Scripture and *The Secret* sit on the same shelf. They'll pass on the line and leave the book out, because they've seen the eyebrow.

## Fear
A life that never asks what it's for (entrapment). Its quiet form: coming back and going back to sleep.

## Gift (said out loud once)
Whatever they keep changes how they meet people: they turn up kinder and more present, and they hand someone the right line at the right moment, without a sermon.

## Siblings and the family's pours (frames to keep clear of)
- Discoverer, *In a Minute*: stopping, slowness, time, a few steps behind. Ours isn't stopping for the world; it's turning inward to meet it.
- Pioneer, *Nothing to It*: scared every time, nobody can tell. Regan's nerves get one clause, never the anchor.
- Tourist, *Brought Home*: brings a find home to share by name. Ours brings back something to live by, not something to show.
- Sense Seeker, *Straight Back*: "somewhere else entirely". Ours goes away to come back here.
- Innovator, *For Good*: "ten minutes" is in its whoYouAre. Say "a few minutes" or "an hour early".
- Conservationist, *Left Standing*: restraint, what's left. Mad Inventor, *Why Not?*: the long way for fun.
- Outside the family: *Before the Room* (the Angel) owns "calm", breathing easier and "what you left at the door", so Regan's "baggage at the door" (Joy pdf 116) stays out.

## Traps
Wellness or cure; a faith given to anyone; "spirit" wordplay; "within" puns (the experience's name, and "tuition from within" is unattributed, pdf 118); mocking *The Secret* or the guest's shelf; the retreat as a label ("the one who's been to the ashram"); earnestness with no lightness; fear as the anchor; the arrival in New York as a "brave new start" (the Pioneer's).

## Story test
A search made on purpose (or a practice later given a name by one); one small thing kept; carried into ordinary working days for years, on record; the change is inward and shows in how they meet people. Better still, a lightness on record. Out: miracles, conversion, a cure, a monk legend, a pilgrimage told as an adventure.

## Lead verdict: Gary Regan. Accept, with conditions (see turn r1)
