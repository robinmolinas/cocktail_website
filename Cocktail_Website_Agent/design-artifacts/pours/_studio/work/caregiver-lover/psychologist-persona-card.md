# Persona card: The Understanding Spouse (caregiver-lover)
Wren, round 1. Source: persona.py caregiver-lover (xlsx row). Draft for the room.

## Who this is
- **Essence:** always there through thick and thin. Constant, reliable; knows when to give advice and when to just be there; presence speaks.
- **Goal / fear:** Service / Selfishness. Secondary (Lover): intimacy, connection; fears isolation, loneliness.
- **Drivers:** provide structure / BUILD CONNECTION.

## The one true thing (what no sibling carries)
Their care is aimed at **one bond, over years**, and it's made of **understanding, not fixing**. They know someone's worst side, in full, and they stay: not because they can't see it, because they can. Staying is a choice they make again on the bad days, quietly.

## What they hide / fear
- They'd never call it a choice. Calling it that would sound like keeping score, and keeping score feels selfish. So it gets heard as patience, or softness, or "you let them get away with it".
- The Lover fear underneath: being the one who understands, and not being understood back. A loneliness *inside* closeness, not outside it.
- The hidden habit: swallowing the disagreement. Understanding someone's reasons becomes a way of never saying "no, this hurt". Saying it feels like making it about themselves.

## Turned into a gift
Around them, the person they love doesn't have to be at their best to be loved. That's rare, and it's theirs.

## Proposed position (Ryu's, earned last)
Not "look after yourself" (Four Shares owns the cup kept back; True Friend owns "you're allowed to be looked after too"). Instead: **understanding isn't the same as agreeing.** Say the one thing you've been understanding your way around. Staying means more when the other person knows what it costs, and the bond is strong enough to hear it. (Hard truth, kind frame.)

## Sibling fences
- Craftsman (Not Only the Way): pride in method, "the way I know how to look after you". Avoid method, avoid "what you rarely say out loud".
- Nurse (everyday practical care), Rescuer (steps in at the crisis), Healer (transforms/fixes), Doctor (knows the answer), Nanny (structure imposed), Reassurer (lightens with humour), Angel (pure goodness): this one does NOT fix, rescue, lighten or know better. It stays and understands.
- Sculptor (Quite Alive, one person, made to measure): keep "one person" about *time and staying*, never about making something for them.

## Dignity guard (important)
The name says "Spouse"; the guest may be single, widowed, divorced, or give this to a sibling, a parent, an old friend. **The reading must not require a partner.** Write "the person you chose" / "the people you've stood by", never "your husband/wife/partner". No gender. No Marge (source example, not for the guest).

## What the story should mirror (for Hester)
Someone who stood by a person through their worst stretch, whose own part is barely in the record, and who understood rather than fixed. A marriage or long bond in drink history, or an ingredient that's always the quiet partner holding a drink together through every version (thick and thin). Bonus if the record shows one moment they *did* speak up.

## Candidate "this is me" line (v0)
*"You've seen the worst of them. That's not why you stay, and it's not despite it either. You stay because you understand it."*
