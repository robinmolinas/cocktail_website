Round 5: v2 with a mandarin peel instead of orange (Wren; garnish only, no numbers move), Hester's M1 (ordinary ice cubes), the wait written as Arnold's 90-second test, and Wren's closing line. Story: Jimmie Charters at the Dingo (Wren accepted r3). Spec: `_studio/specs/ruler-caregiver.json`. **The change from v1:** the rum-and-Bonal drink is gone. This is now a Manhattan made on cognac, the base of Jimmie's own Dingo cocktail, with a spoon and a half of mandarin liqueur, the one ingredient from that cocktail I've taken. As before, the drink's gesture is the wait: once it's stirred, it sits on its ice while the other person finishes talking.

**Glassware:** cocktail glass (about 180 ml), chilled. No ice in the glass.
**Contains:** veto-free.

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 60 ml | cognac | any VS or VSOP cognac, 40% or stronger |
| 30 ml | sweet vermouth | |
| 7.5 ml (1½ tsp) | mandarin liqueur | any mandarin liqueur made on brandy or cognac; for the Mandarin in the cocktail Jimmie invented at the Dingo |
| 2 dashes | Angostura bitters | |
| 1 | strip of mandarin peel | squeezed over the top, then dropped in; a clementine works |

## Method

1. Chill the glass in the freezer, or fill it with ice water while you mix.
2. Put the cognac, sweet vermouth, mandarin liqueur and bitters in a mixing glass or jar. Fill it with ordinary ice cubes (not crushed).
3. Stir for about 15 seconds, until the outside of the glass feels cold.
4. If someone's still talking, stop stirring and leave the spoon where it is. The drink can sit on its ice for a minute and a half or so without getting watery.
5. When they've finished, empty the serving glass and strain the drink into it.
6. Squeeze the mandarin peel over the top so its oils fall on the drink, then drop it in.

2. **To Each Other** (Wren's round-4 pick, conceded): the toast Jimmie got out of two people about to fight.
