# Hester: candidates, ruler-caregiver (round 2)

## Wren's lead: Plunkett and Masterson — FAILS on the primary record
Card: `fact-cards/masterson-plunkett-waldorf-1906.md`. The 1906 press (Tribune, Evening World, 23 June) has Plunkett as the man who'd been calling Masterson a coward, "submissive" / "glued" to his chair, and leaving in the panic; his friend is knocked down; the house detective ends it. No seat, no drink, no talk. Regan's (pdf 32) "remained calm and offered … a seat, a drink, and the chance to talk" is a later softening, and "fellow U.S. marshal" is unsupported. Not even a labelled legend: the man in it was the provoker.
