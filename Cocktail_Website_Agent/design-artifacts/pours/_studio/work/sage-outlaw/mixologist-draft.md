# sage-outlaw, The Lone Voice: drink v1 (Tomás, round 2; round 3: dilution justified, Hester's anchors applied; round 4 step 1: audit T1-T3)

Spec: `_studio/specs/sage-outlaw.json` (v1). Glass: small stemmed port glass (about 150 ml), chilled, no ice. Contains: veto-free.

## Recipe
| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 ml | ruby port | a young, non-vintage ruby |
| 40 ml | 10-year-old tawny port | any tawny with "10 years old" on the label |
| 25 ml | cold strong black tea | plain black tea (not Earl Grey or any flavoured tea), no sugar, no milk: 2 tea bags in 150 ml just-boiled water for 8 minutes, then cooled in the fridge |
| 1 strip | orange peel | |

## Method
1. Make the tea: steep 2 bags of plain black tea in 150 ml of just-boiled water for 8 minutes, twice as long as for a cup, so it comes out strong and a little drying. Lift the bags out, let it cool, and keep it in the fridge (it lasts two days).
2. Put the glass in the freezer for 10 minutes.
3. In a mixing glass or a jar, pour the ruby first, then the tawny, then the cold tea.
4. Fill with ice and stir for about 15 seconds, until the outside of the glass is cold.
5. Strain into the chilled glass, with no ice.
6. Squeeze the orange peel over the top so its oils fall on the drink, then set it aside.
