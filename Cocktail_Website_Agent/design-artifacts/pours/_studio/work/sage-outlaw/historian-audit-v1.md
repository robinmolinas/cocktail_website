## Fixes, old → new
**Wren (reading):**
- **H1** y1: "to join his uncle's port house." → "to join his uncle, a partner in a port house there."
- **H2** y1: "and a monopoly, the Douro Wine Company, set up in 1756, that taxed exports heavily" → "and the Douro Wine Company, whose monopoly dated from 1756, which taxed exports heavily"
- **H3** y2: "In 1844 he put it all in a pamphlet, without his name on it." → "In 1844 he set it out in a pamphlet, without his name on it."
- **H4** y2: "so plenty of people read it." → "so plenty of people wanted a copy."
- **H5** y2: "He was soon found out anyway, and bitterly attacked" → "He was easily found out anyway, and bitterly attacked"
- **H6** y3: "By the next year a continuation was in print." → "By the next year he had more on the same subject in print."

- **H8** y4: "then twice as much ten-year-old tawny, which brings more structure." → "then twice as much tawny, a blend of wines from several years."
- **H9** y4: "Port is sweet, and rather than" → "Port tends to be sweet, and rather than"
- **H10** epigraph: "Then an older one, at twice the measure." → "Then a tawny, at twice the measure."
