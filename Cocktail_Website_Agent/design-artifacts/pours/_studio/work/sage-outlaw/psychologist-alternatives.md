# Alternatives: The Lone Voice (sage-outlaw)

Wren. Kept out of the reading on purpose. The pick is held to the round-4 vote.

## Names
- **The Second Time** (my pick: the one true thing, the coming back after the cost, in a plain phrase anyone could say)
- On the Record (Tomás's pick; I could take it, but it leans on the record more than on the person)
- At Greater Length (the DNB's phrase: wry, but it reads as the story's, not the guest's)
- Still There (spends the "this is me" line, so no)
- Said Again

## Taglines
- **You don't like being the only one. You like pretending even less.** (v1)
- You'd rather be wrong than quiet. You keep checking. (risks "always right")
- The first time took nerve. The second time took you. (praise, so no)
- You'll say it twice if you have to. (too close in shape to *Not Too Polite*'s "It's usually you")

## Epigraphs
- **A young port goes in first. Then an older one, at twice the measure.** (v1)
- Two ports in this glass. The second says what the first did, at greater length.
- Port, twice. Nothing in it is there to make it go down easier.
- **Port goes in twice here: once in short, then again at greater length.** (v2)
- A young port goes in first. Then a tawny, at twice the measure. (H10; true, but it repeats the closing line's recipe order)
- Two ports in one glass. The second says it again, at greater length. (the Craftsman's epigraph shape)
