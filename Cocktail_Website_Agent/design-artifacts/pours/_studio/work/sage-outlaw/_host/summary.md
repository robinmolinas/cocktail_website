- **The persona:** the Lone Voice looks into the thing everyone is happy with, finds it doesn't hold, and says so, and doesn't enjoy standing alone. Part of them hopes they're wrong. They check it again, and it's still there. What they can't live with is the comfortable version becoming the official one with nobody having objected. Their gift is that, because they said it first, others get to say it second.
- **Story:** the plan's lead, accepted by Wren in r1 with conditions (from her own reading of the DNB entry): Joseph James Forrester and *A Word or Two on Port Wine* (1844). He published anonymously against the Douro Wine Company's monopoly, was "easily identified and bitterly attacked by the persons interested", and in 1852 set out the abuses at greater length to a Commons select committee. Hester's counterweights shaped the reading. He wasn't alone on the record, so the coldness belongs to the guest's scene, not his. Whether he was right is contested (C3), so the reading never says he was. He shipped port and was not a disinterested party (C4). Runner-up: the real Ramon Llull, not opened.
