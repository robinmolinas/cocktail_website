| story | On 1 April 1979 four friends from the Oxford University Dangerous Sports Club jumped from the Clifton Suspension Bridge in Bristol on elastic rope, Kirke in top hat and tails: theirs were the first recorded bungee jumps (GWR; NYT 2023). The costume is on record for Kirke only. | Something everyone called mad, done among friends, on April Fools' Day. | test 1, 2, 4 |
| gesture | The rope wasn't tested. Kirke, 2019: "We were called the Dangerous Sports Club, and testing it first wouldn't have been particularly dangerous" (GWR; NYT via BristolLive). He added that he was confident: clever friends had worked out how far the ropes would stretch (*Independent* 2023, via the *Bristol Post*). | The danger made into a joke, and a choice made with friends, not blind. | test 4; the gift (fear turned into a laugh) |
| feeling | As he tipped off the bridge: "The main thing going through my mind was 'Whoooppeeee'" (NYT, via BristolLive 2019). None of them made any money from it (ITV West Country 2019; NYT). | For the feeling, not money or a cause. | test 1 |
| cost | Police at first took it for an April Fool's joke, then arrested all four; by Kirke's account the police were "very good humoured about it" (ITV News 2019). Fined (GWR; "a small fine", NYT) and released on condition / the understanding that they'd never do it again (GWR; *Telegraph* obituary). | The sensible answer, on the page, and it came with a smile. A cost that ends in a laugh. | test 3, 5 |
| after | Six months later Kirke and others from the club jumped from the Golden Gate Bridge (WaPo 2023; *San Francisco Chronicle* archive: 9 Oct 1979, four detained); the club repeated it across the US and the world (GWR; *Telegraph*). | Nobody else got to decide it was over. Wren to weigh: a promise to the police, broken. | the fear (the choice taken away); Wren's call |
| motive | Kirke on why the club existed (to *Vanity Fair*, 2013, relayed by NYT and WaPo): what they hated was formal sports' "little, important bourgeois instructors" saying you had to pass "five-part exams" first. **The club's reason, not the jump's.** | Who's in charge: other people deciding, sensibly, what you may do. | the hidden thing (it's about who's in charge) |
