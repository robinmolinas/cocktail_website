## 0. My own correction first
**E2 was wrong.** My round-3 OCR highlight stopped one sentence early. Embury's CANADIAN ends: "…1 teaspoonful of sugar syrup to each drink. **Maple syrup is sometimes used in place of the sugar.**" (1958, 1961 and 2008 editions agree; the next entry is CANASTA.) Oxford pdf 1226 was right to list it as a maple drink. "Embury's CANADIAN has no maple" was mine, and it is struck everywhere: card, anchors, and this audit. Consequences: the maple isn't borrowed from his Collins. It's his own noted variant of this very drink. E4 (the "cutting down somewhat" note) belongs to a **different** drink, and it pairs less maple *with* added lemon.
