His CANADIAN is "straight Canadian whisky with 2 or 3 dashes each of curaçao and Angostura and 1 teaspoonful of sugar syrup to each drink". There's no maple in it. So "Embury's Canadian had maple" is out. What is on his page: his CANADIAN COLLINS says "use Canadian whisky and, in place of sugar syrup, use maple syrup". Near it is a maple drink he calls "a specialty of the Canadian Club of New York City", a club, not the whisky. Of that one he writes that it "can be improved upon by cutting down somewhat on the maple syrup". So you can say Embury paired maple with Canadian whisky, and that the man himself held it back.
