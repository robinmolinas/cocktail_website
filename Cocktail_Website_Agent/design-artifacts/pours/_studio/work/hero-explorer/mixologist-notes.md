## How Zirbenz is made (Wren's veto question)

| # | Claim | Source (tier) |
|---|---|---|
| Z1 | "a liqueur flavored with the fruit of the arolla pine" | Oxford, HAUS ALPENZ pdf 975 (primary) |
| Z2 | "draws all of its high alpine flavors and natural earthy red colors from the freshly picked fruit of the Arolla Stone Pine… The young full cones are still fresh and sweet"; pickers "ascend to high elevations then climb the trees to pick this wild pine fruit fresh"; "Handcrafted and bottled in Austria by Josef Hofer, a family distillery since 1797"; 35.0% ABV (750 ml and 375 ml); maker suggests "Combine with Scotch or aged rum for robust, complex toddies", and "après-ski" | Haus Alpenz product page, alpenz.com/product-zirbenz.html, fetched 2026-10-01 (importer's own page: secondary, marketing voice) |
| Z3 | Zirbenschnaps/-likör: red cones still full of sap, not yet woody, hand-picked late June to mid July, cut into 3-5 mm slices and steeped about 5-6 weeks in grain spirit; a second (Zirbengeist) method distils the kernels of woody autumn cones | de.wikipedia "Zirbenlikör/Zirbenschnaps", fetched 2026-10-01 (tertiary; Hester may want better) |
| Z4 | Pinus cembra cones "contain the seeds (or nuts)"; "The seeds are also harvested and sold as pine nuts"; "Pine cones cut into slices are used to flavor schnapps" | en.wikipedia "Pinus cembra", fetched 2026-10-01 (tertiary) |

**Call:** the whole young cone, seeds inside, is sliced and steeped. Pine nuts are in the steep. STUDIO-RULES 4 lists pine nuts under `nuts`. **Zirbenz = `nuts`.** Any stone-pine liqueur (Zirbenlikör) is made the same way, so no substitute style clears it. The only veto-free drink drops the bottle, and the bottle is the story. Recommend accepting `nuts` (the plan budgeted it: nine veto-free rows remain).
