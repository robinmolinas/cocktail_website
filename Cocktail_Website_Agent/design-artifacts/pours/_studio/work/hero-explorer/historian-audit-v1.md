## Passes (checked on the page)
- **y1:** student in Austria; Colorado ski towns (p. 240). "Mild-mannered… soft-spoken" is attributed to "one writer", and "brassy" → "loud" is fair. The "nothing safe or boring" quote is exact. "Went back… to find a pine liqueur he could bring over, and returned with Zirbenz" fits "went to Austria to find an example… ended up bringing over" (G2 kept). "Hofer family distillery has made since 1797": *PUNCH* 2015 ("made by the Josef Hofer distillery in Austria since 1797") and the catalogue 2026.
- **y2:** one pallet, sixty cases; no big distributor; one-man outfit; "sold as a drink for after skiing" (G13 kept); "struggled, though in some places it did well"; Denver bartender (Kenyon, Steuben's); Aspen's best store; "both coasts" = San Francisco and New York; Batavia arrack, "rum-like spirit from Indonesia" (p. 241). "Went and found it" holds for the example: Alpenz imports Batavia arrack (Oxford pdf 975). "For a decade, by one writer's account": dated by the 2016 book.
- **Wren's asks:** **"its importer" is right.** "Combine with Scotch or aged rum for robust, complex toddies" is on Haus Alpenz's page, not the distillery's. **"Years later" is right:** the quote is from *PUNCH* (3 April 2015), about ten years after Zirbenz arrived (2005, per Oxford and *PUNCH*). **The epigraph passes:** "could afford to buy only a pallet of sixty cases" (p. 240), and Simonson calls him an importer. One fence: "the pine in this glass" holds because y4 names Zirbenz first, as recommended. If the recipe ever drops the recommendation, the epigraph changes.
- **y4:** the order matches Method steps 1–5 (mug warmed first; pine, then rum, then dram, lemon, syrup, water; peel last). "Brown sugar syrup" is a fair plain gloss of demerara. "The find that comes next… the one after": Zirbenz came first and allspice liqueur later (Oxford pdf 975: "initially focused on… But in response to demands… began importing"). The remedy history is absent.
- **Closing line:** "a spoon of what came next" is true (Oxford's order, above), and "Pine first" matches step 2.
- **Image brief:** young cone, "picked fresh, per the importer", mittens for "sold as an après ski tradition", the bag for p. 240. No medicine, no relic, no pine nuts.
- **Checks:** the toddy line, the allspice page's "drying aromatic" for "toddy-style drinks" (fetched in this session, verbatim) and the nuts reasoning (G12) all pass.
