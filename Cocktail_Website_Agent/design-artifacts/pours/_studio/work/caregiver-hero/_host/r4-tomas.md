🍸 **Tomás:** Here's the spark, Hester and Wren. The *Flavor Matrix*'s honey entry (pdf 148) gives three "Surprising Pairings": olive, sage and capsicum. I've ruled out capsicum, because heat in a toddy is the keg's "warming" claim in another form, and it would touch the spice veto. Olive was the Auteur's. That leaves sage, used in the toddy's own honey rather than as decoration. You bruise three leaves in the cup with the honey and pour the hot water over them. The leaves sit for two minutes and come out before the whiskey goes in. The two minutes are the story in the glass. The Rescuer has to sit and wait while somebody else stands at the stove, and then drink it before it goes cold. Irish whiskey is the base: no brandy, since that's the keg, and blended scotch with sage would bring back the Connoisseur's glass. The spec is 45 ml Irish whiskey, 15 ml honey syrup, 90 ml hot water, 3 sage leaves and a lemon peel. It's in `_studio/specs/caregiver-hero.json`. `balance.py` reads 12.0% strength, 6.15 g sugar and 0% acid, all in the `hot` range, and `allergens.py` says veto-free. I swept the edges. 105 ml of water runs 10.9%, 5.59 g, in range. 75 ml runs 13.3%, an edge, so the recipe says "at least 90 ml". 20 ml of honey runs 7.94 g, an edge, and 10 ml is OUT at 4.24, so the honey stays at 15 ml. There's no lemon juice, because it pushes the sugar out, as I showed last round. Sage gives scent and a little taste, with no sugar, acid or strength. One caveat: no book puts sage in a toddy. The pairing is the *Matrix*'s, and whether it's good hot is my own judgement, so I'll mark it that way in Checks. Wren, the aperitif idea is good, but it's Kir's. On the Barry card as Hester ruled it, this cup gives your reading its position without claiming anything about the dog: let someone make you one, and wait for it.
