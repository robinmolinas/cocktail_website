# Alternatives: The Game-Changer (sage-magician)

Wren, kept apart from the reading (an alternative inside the reading counts as an extra "yours").

## Tagline candidates (the person)
1. *You take a yes more seriously than a no.* ← my pick (in the reading). It doesn't use "Most people… You…", which three registry taglines already use.
2. *You don't want followers. You want people who can see it too.*
3. *Before you change anything, you check what it'll change in them.*
- Dropped: *Most people worry nobody will listen. You worry everyone will.* (the r1 seed; formula clash)

## Names (the person, not only the story)
1. **Plain to See** ← my pick. What they do to an idea before they share it, and what the guest gets from them. It carries "obvious" without spending the word (*For Good*), and it doesn't repeat the "this is me" line.
2. *Worth Following* (Tomás's and Hester's pick). It answers the fear head on. I could take it.
3. *Eyes Open* (Tomás). Too close to the closing line.
4. *Once You See It* (Tomás). Too near *For Good* and the Surrealist.
