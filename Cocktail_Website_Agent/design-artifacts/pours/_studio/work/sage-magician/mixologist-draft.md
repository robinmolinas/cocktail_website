# Drink v1: The Game-Changer (sage-magician)

Tomás, round 2, 2026-10-02. Spec: `_studio/specs/sage-magician.json`. Plan row direction kept (Old-Fashioned, two whiskies from two countries, no named bottle, small tumbler, no ice), within Wren's r1 conditions.

**Glassware:** small tumbler (about 200 ml), chilled in the freezer, no ice
**Contains:** veto-free

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 ml | Canadian whisky, corn-based | Any blended Canadian whisky made mainly from corn (most are), 40% or a little stronger; not a 100% rye Canadian. Canadian whisky is often called rye, though corn is usually its main grain, with a little rye for flavour. |
| 30 ml | American straight rye whiskey | Any straight rye, 40-55%. This is the one made from at least half rye: the same name, and in a typical bottle the grains the other way round. |
| 7.5 ml (1½ tsp) | demerara syrup | Equal parts demerara sugar and hot water, stirred until clear, then cooled. |
| 7.5 ml (1½ tsp) | tamarind water | Break 25 g of sour, seedless tamarind pulp (a block from the world-food aisle; not "sweet tamarind") into a bowl, pour on 60 ml of just-boiled water, leave 30 minutes, mash with a fork and press through a sieve. Keeps a few days in the fridge. Blocks vary: if yours is very sour, use 1 tsp; if it's mild, 2. |
| 2 dashes | Angostura bitters | |
| 1 | strip of orange peel | |

## Method

1. Put the tumbler in the freezer for 15 minutes.
2. Pour both whiskies, the demerara syrup, the tamarind water and the bitters into a mixing glass or a jar.
3. Fill it with ice and stir quickly for about 15 seconds, until the outside is very cold.
4. Strain into the cold tumbler. No ice in the glass.
5. Hold the orange peel over the drink, skin side down, and squeeze it so its oils fall on the top. Rub it once round the rim and drop it in.
