1. **The verse, one version.** Minnick 2024, as I recorded it from the page in the plan: "If, as they say, God spanked the town / For being over frisky, / Why did He burn the churches down / And save Hotaling's whiskey?" There is a variant ("His churches", "spare"), which Wikipedia quotes from a 1962 *ABA Journal* essay. Use Minnick's only, never a mix. The "lie" is on Minnick's page: several clergymen called the destruction divine retribution for the city's sins.
2. **"Famous at once" / "the whole city": not on any page.** And "four lines the whole city repeated" was my own wording in the plan. I'm striking it. What is on the page: the verse was still being retold in 1962 and again in 2024. So write "people still quote it", or "people kept the line". Not "people repeated it" if that means at the time, because nothing records how it first went round.
3. **People saved the warehouse, yes, you get your clause.** Minnick: its staff, the fire department and the Navy. Wikipedia, citing SF Heritage (which I haven't read), says Hotaling's own employees moved thousands of barrels to an empty lot. "Its own workers among them" is safe.
4. **Your mirror has a hole on one side: "Field signed the jibe."** No page says how the verse first circulated, or under whose name. The brand-side story has him scribbling it on a walk to the station, which is legend. What's on the page is that it's credited to him and remembered as his. So: "the joke is remembered as his; the hope went out under another name." That keeps the shape and makes no claim about a signature.
5. **The hope side is passive on the page, so watch the verbs.** OAC says "no press pictures were permitted, and his fee was donated to charity". Neither clause says he decided it. "Gave the fee away" was my plan's shorthand too. Write "no press photographs were allowed" and "his fee went to charity". If you want it to be his choice, that's "I like to think". Better news from Wikipedia: he went on air under the made-up name "Cheerio", even signed his 1936 book about the show *by Himself*, and the show ran from 1927 to 1940. That's "more than twenty years later", not "twenty" (1906 to 1927 is 21).
