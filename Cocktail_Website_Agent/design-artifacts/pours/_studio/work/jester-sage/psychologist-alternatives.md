# The Cynic: Wren's alternatives (not guest text)

## Names (my pick: Against My Better Judgement; I could take *And Yet* (Hester) or *Between the Lines* (Tomás))
- **Against My Better Judgement**: hoping against your own evidence, in the guest's own voice; funny and true.
- And Yet (Hester): the turn from the joke to the hope in two words. Strong.
- Between the Lines (Tomás): good, but it describes the reading of text more than the person's hope.
- Other ideas: Still Hoping · No Pictures · The Other Way Round.

## Taglines
- Everyone hears the joke. Hardly anyone hears the hope. (pick)
- You only joke about it because you still care how it turns out.
- You laugh at how things are. Only because you know how they could be.
