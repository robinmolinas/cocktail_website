## Lineage, for Wren (not yet in any file)

- **L-a.** Bourbon, dry vermouth and bitters is a **Manhattan made with dry vermouth**, a classic on record in the nineteenth century. **The date is contested:** Oxford MARTINI (pdf 1244) puts a French-vermouth Manhattan in Byron's *Modern Bartender* the year after 1883, but *Imbibe!* (pdf 194) says the first Dry Manhattan recipe "turned up in 1891", in Byron's second edition. So no year, and never "the first". "A Dry Manhattan with a spoon of pear in it" is true. "A bourbon Martini" is Tomás's family label, not a name on any page. Our drink is never "the classic" as it stands (it has the pear).
- **L-b.** The Martinez's San Francisco story (Oxford pdf 1244) is a legend Oxford rejects. Out.
