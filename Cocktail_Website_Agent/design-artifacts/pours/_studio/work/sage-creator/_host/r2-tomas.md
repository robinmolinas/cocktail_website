It's a bourbon Old-Fashioned served up in a chilled coupe, and two things nobody sees carry it. **The sugar is corn.** Bourbon is at least 51 percent corn (Oxford CORN pdf 583), and the *Flavor Matrix*'s Corn chart (pdf 89, rendered) puts bourbon whiskey and popcorn on the same wheel. So the demerara syrup is steeped with home-popped plain popcorn, and the toasted corn sits under the whiskey where nobody can see it. **The water is measured, not melted.** I'm using Arnold's method (LI p. 152): bourbon, syrup and bitters go in the freezer the night before, and 30 ml of ice-cold water goes in at the last minute. No ice ever touches the drink. The numbers: **28.0% · 4.75 g · 0% acid**, in range on the stirred finished bands. 30 ml is this mix's own stirred dilution (balance.py: 45%, 31 ml).
