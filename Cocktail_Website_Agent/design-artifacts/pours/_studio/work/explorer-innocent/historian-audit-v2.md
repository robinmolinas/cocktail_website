## Reading: v1 items closed
- A6 ("renowned" → v2 y4 "a classic from Havana"): **closed.** "From Havana" holds: first printed in Havana in 1915 (Escalante, Hotel Inglaterra; Oxford pdf 715, E1). It's "a Cuban classic" for its popularity at home and abroad (E2).
- A7 ("in oak" → "years"): **closed.** "A wine the law won't let anyone sell until it has had years" holds for every DO Madeira (M2: canteiro not bottled before 3 years; estufagem not sold before 31 October of the second year after the harvest, so about two years or more).
