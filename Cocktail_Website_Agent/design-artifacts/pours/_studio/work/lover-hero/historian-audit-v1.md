## Tomás's rye lead (Bly's birthplace)
- **Birthplace:** Cochran's Mills, Pennsylvania, 5 May 1864. Sources: Elizabeth Ehrlich, *Nellie Bly* (1989), archive.org OCR highlight (incomplete); Wikipedia (Burrell Township, Pennsylvania; lead only). "Western Pennsylvania" isn't on any page I read.
- **Rye:** Oxford RYE pdf 1715: "Beginning in the late colonial period", rye was the spirit of choice "from the Appalachian front to the valleys of the Monongahela River in western Pennsylvania". That's a region and an era. It doesn't say rye was the whiskey of her county in the 1860s.
- **So:** "rye, long Pennsylvania's whiskey, where she was born" is the most both pages allow, and it's a coincidence, to be owned as one. "The base becomes hers" claims too much. Watch *Brought Home*'s "Two places go into this glass": a French-style wine and her home state's whiskey is that shape. My advice: keep it out of the reading, or give it one plain clause, never a theme.
