It's a Tequila Highball built the *Codex*'s classic way: 50 ml Fortaleza Blanco (recommended; or any blanco tequila milled with a tahona) poured over three cubes, stirred for three seconds, then 125 ml cold soda and one more stir (p. 199). **No lime, no sugar.** That's your condition, and it's physics as well: the *Codex* says blanco tequila is "earthy and vegetal" alone and "suddenly becomes citrusy" in a Highball (p. 208), and its root recipe drops the lemon wedge as "unnecessary at best" (p. 199). The soda brings the citrus out of the tequila itself, so nothing gets poured in to cover anything up. That's test (4) in the glass.
