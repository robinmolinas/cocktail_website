**The truer story is a little better than our plan.** In his account it wasn't only "a property". When his grandfather sold the business, with its two industrial distilleries and most of the fields, he kept the estate in Tequila, and on it an old distillery, La Fortaleza, which he had made into a museum. The grandson's own reason for starting is on the record: the tequilas on sale had lost something next to the ones he'd drunk with his grandfather, and he thought reviving the old place would be "a fun project". That's your Dude in his own words, and no crusade. His (4) is on record as a choice too: "we won't produce the most tequila, nor will we earn the most".
