# The Trailblazer (explorer-outlaw): persona card, round 1 (Wren)

Sources: persona.py explorer-outlaw (xlsx row), my Explorer sketch (`plans/work/explorer/psychologist-sketches.md`), the family plan row, *A Proper Drink* pp. 270-276 re-read whole this round, the registry, *Nothing to It* read in full.

## The one true thing
They don't ask, because a question hands someone else the key. Not contempt, not defiance: once you've asked, a "no" turns your own way into a fight, and they'd rather it never be a fight. So they just go, in plain sight, and say it without ceremony.
And the part no sibling carries: **the route they open turns into the signposted road they can't stand.** Once everyone's on it, it has their name on it, and people expect them to keep walking it. Their own mark becomes the fence.

## Hide / fear / give
- **Hide:** that they care whether it lasts. The shrug ("didn't know any better") says they did it for themselves; underneath, they wanted to leave a mark (the sheet's driver) and would never say so.
- **Fear:** being kept: the fenced field, being told where to go, and the quieter version, being held to the one thing they're known for.
- **Give:** after them, nobody needs to ask. Not proof that it's *possible* (that's the Pioneer's), but the sense that it was *allowed* all along, because nobody was ever standing there to say no. Said out loud once (Robin's rule): that's worth a great deal to the people who come after.

## Siblings (and this family's pours)
- **Pioneer, *Nothing to It*: DANGER, two ways.** It owns the shrug after, "make it look possible", "the others followed", "someone behind you decides they could do it too". And its story has the same shape as ours: a practice kept inside the trade (Blanton's own barrels), then someone put it out in public (its epigraph: "used to stay inside the trade. Then someone put it on sale"). Freeman's fat-washed spirits that never reached a menu, then Lee's menu, is that shape again, also with a Lee, also bourbon, also an Old-Fashioned. Keep apart by: credentials (Elmer T. Lee: decades under his boss; Don Lee: a customer, a regular behind the bar), permission not courage, and the route taken *elsewhere* by others ("every meat and nut under the sun"), never "followed".
- *Left Standing* (Conservationist): restraint, "you'd rather know it's there". Owns "take someone". Keep "there" out of our ending.
- *For Good* (Innovator): fixes an annoyance so nobody remembers it. We fix nothing.
- *Brought Home* (Tourist): goes and comes back to share. We don't come back.
- *In a Minute* (Discoverer): stopping, time. *Why Not?* (Mad Inventor): the elaborate route for fun (Lee's exploding cherries and popcorn are this colour: one clause at most). *Straight Back* (Sense Seeker): a taste that takes you. *Just Knew* (Spiritualist): a habit kept before it had a name.
- Outside the family: *No Accident* ("never break a rule": no "rule" anywhere in our title block); *Still Yours* (Trend Setter: liked it first, tempted to drop it when everyone arrives, position "keep it"): our "the road gets your name on it" is near it, so ours is about being *kept*, never about liking or dropping; *Beside the First* (Old Master: change one thing in what you're known for); *Not Too Polite* (Campaigner: a cause); the Thrill Seeker (reverse pairing, unauthored: the jolt and the fear, not the route).

## Traps
- Aggression or defiance as the point ("rule-breaking energy without aggression", sheet). Nobody is the jailer: not Eben Freeman (never timid: "fear of backlash" is Lee's guess, so it stays out), not Jim Meehan.
- Labels: rebel, outlaw, maverick, pioneer, trailblazer said to the guest; summit or peak puns; Hillary's quote (the sheet's); a borrowed swear.
- The speakeasy and PDT's hidden door: Outlaw ground, and it contradicts "done openly". The reading never mentions how you get in.
- Madrusan's "no stripes" is a *complaint* (nobody liked being told what to do by Don). Usable only as the fact that Lee came in as a customer; never as praise, never as Lee bossing anyone.
- Deragon's "parents come home / Look, it's working" casts Meehan as the parent, and Madrusan contradicts it on the same page ("PDT's origin story is tangled... conflicting accounts", p. 272). Dossier only.
- "First" / "invented fat-washing": Proper p. 275 says he didn't. Grant it, then correct it.
- Quotes with "we/our" ("We didn't know any better", "our regulars are bartending") trip the lint: paraphrase with attribution.
- "Allowed" in the ending is *Four Shares*' frame ("You're allowed to..."). "Already" is *Off Duty*'s. "Followed" is *Nothing to It*'s.
- Meat: the bacon is no veto, but the reading must make it sound worth having, never greasy or a stunt.

## Story test
Someone **without permission or credentials** went their own way **openly** (no trick, no secret); an **unpompous remark on record**; the route **stayed open and others took it places the maker never went**. Bonus that would make it land: **the maker's own wariness of being held to it, on record** (or signposted). Out: a cause, a trick, a watched first, taste-first, crime as glamour, a jailer.

## Verdict on the plan's lead (Don Lee, the Benton's Old-Fashioned): accept, re-centred
Re-read pp. 270-276 whole. It passes, and the page holds more than the plan quoted:
- Missing credentials, in a funny line: other bartenders loved the Monday nights because their own **regulars were now bartending** (Lee, p. 271). Lee was also a regular at WD-50, where he tasted Freeman's experiments (p. 271, p. 275).
- Unpompous on record: they didn't know any better, so they just put it on the menu (p. 275).
- Openly: on the menu, PDT's best-known and best-selling drink (p. 275).
- Route open and taken elsewhere: a whole genre, "every meat and nut under the sun" (p. 275).
- **The mirror of the hidden fear, on record:** Lee is proud of it and "a bit haunted by it": he says it's the drink that will probably follow him to the grave (p. 276). The route becomes the road with your name on it. That's the Trailblazer's fear in his own mouth.
Conditions: (1) the recognition rests on *not asking*, never on going public or on "first"; no epigraph of the "kept inside, then sold" shape; never "followed". (2) Freeman credited warmly, never timid. (3) No speakeasy, no Meehan as jailer, no "no stripes" as praise. (4) Fun and cherries: one clause. (5) No year (2007 vs 2008). (6) Tien Ho is "a regular", not a chef, unless Hester finds the page.

## Position direction (for the room to test)
- **Lead:** let it follow you. The road with your name on it isn't a fence: it's the mark you'd never admit you wanted, and you're still free to walk off it. (In their interest: it ends the restlessness that makes them leave their own best things.)
- **Alternative (harder truth):** ask once, and find that a no doesn't stop you. Freedom was never in avoiding the question. Riskier: it can read as a scold to someone who hates being told.
