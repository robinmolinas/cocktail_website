# Persona card: The Sculptor (creator-caregiver)

Wren, round 1. Written from `persona.py creator-caregiver`, the ten Creator siblings, the approved Visionary and True Friend, and my voice ledger.

## Core truth
For the Sculptor, care is measured in attention. They make things slowly and by hand *for someone*, because a shortcut, to them, would say "you weren't worth the time". The making is how they say what they find hard to say out loud.

## Shows / hides
- **Shows:** patience, fussiness even. The thing redone at midnight because one detail was off. People call it perfectionism.
- **Hides:** that it isn't about the thing. Every hour is for the person it's going to. They rarely say "I love you" or "I was worried about you"; they make something, and hope it's read. And the cost nobody sees: the evenings they disappear into it, sometimes away from the very person it's for.

## Hidden fear → hidden gift
- **Fear:** indifference, in both directions (Caregiver: indifference, selfishness; Creator: mediocrity). Doing anything carelessly for someone they love. And, quieter: that the hours won't be seen, or that the person would rather have had *them* than the thing.
- **Gift:** attention you can hold in your hands. They give people proof that someone looked closely at them.

## The "real me" line to aim for
Draft: *"A shortcut, to you, says 'you weren't worth the time'. So you never take one."*
Alt: *"You've never been good at saying it. So you make it, and it takes as long as it takes."*

## Truth vs. legend
The true story, told with its small, patient detail. A legend would feel like a shortcut. They'd love a real hand-made detail more than a grand claim. Kind, but true.

## Tone
Compassionate + inspirational → tender and unhurried. The bartender slows down for them. One warm smile allowed (the 2 a.m. redo is a little funny, and they know it).

## Distinct from siblings
- **The Obsessed** (creator-lover) is in love with the making itself. The Sculptor is in love with who it's *for*.
- **The Visionary** (creator-hero, approved) is about size and time, a work other hands carry on. The Sculptor is small scale, one pair of hands, one person. Nothing unfinished, no handover.
- **The Old Master** (creator-sage) seeks depth of craft; **the Makeover Artist** (creator-magician) transforms people. The Sculptor transforms material, as a gift.
- Cross-family: **the True Friend** (approved) already owns "you're allowed to be looked after too" and "keep one cup back for yourself". The Sculptor's ending must not be about receiving care.

## Traps
- The literal sculptor: chisels, marble, clay as the guest's life. The name already says it; most guests don't carve. Scenes must be ordinary (a cake, a card, a meal, a gift wrapped twice).
- A pun on "sculpt" in the drink or the name (ice spheres, "carved"). Only if the story truly calls for it.
- "Perfectionist" as a label, or as a flaw to fix. It's care, not a disorder.
- Flattery: "you make beautiful things". Say who they make them for, and what it costs.
- Telling them to settle for "good enough". It would betray the Creator half. The position has to honour the care, not ask for less of it.
- Endurance (the Visionary's five-minute shake). Here the effort is attention, not stamina.
