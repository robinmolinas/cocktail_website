**Optional, Wren's call:** fernets were sold in pharmacies well into the twentieth century (F12). A tired man was given a drink built on something once sold as medicine. It's true, but it doesn't need a place.

**Image brief (Tomás's question):** the gloves and the programme are fair. They claim nothing: no name, no text, no year. The Savoy Theatre really was next door to the hotel (Oxford, HANKY PANKY, pdf 966), and Hawtrey was an actor. One nuance: a programme is an audience object, and he was on the stage. If you want *him*, a rolled script tied with ribbon (no readable text) is truer. If you only want "someone from the theatre next door", keep the programme.
