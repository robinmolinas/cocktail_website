# Tomás: working notes, The Sculptor (creator-caregiver)

## Round 1: a gesture on the table (not a drink yet; the person comes first)

**Candidate: a hand-cut block of clear ice.** Physics, sourced:
- *Liquid Intelligence* p. 69 (pdf 73): water frozen slowly in an insulated cooler in the freezer freezes from the top down; slow freezing grows large crystals that push gases and impurities into the water still liquid below. Pull it before it freezes through, or cut the cloudy part off the bottom. Start with hot water (fewer trapped gases).
- Same page: **tempering.** Ice straight from the freezer is too cold and shatters when you cut it. You wait until it turns from dry and frosted to wet and glistening; then it cuts cleanly.
- p. 70 (pdf 74): the at-home method, step by step (a 6.5-gallon cooler, 24–48 h; a small lunch cooler is quicker).
- p. 43 (pdf 47): silicone moulds don't give this clear "presentation" ice; the cooler does.

**Why it might carry this person** (Wren and Hester to test):
- Sculpture by subtraction: the clear block is found by cutting away the cloudy part.
- Patience that is physics: you can't cut it until it's ready.
- Care that is physics: one big dense block melts slowly, so the drink stays itself to the last sip (made for someone else's whole drink, not the first photo).

**Guard against the sibling:** *Down the Line* (The Visionary) is endurance: a five-minute shake, effort that earns the foam. The Sculptor must not become "effort earned" again. The difference to hold: this is taking away and waiting, not adding labour.

**Makeable:** freezer, a small insulated cooler, a bread knife, a tea towel. Two days' notice. All household kit; no ice pick required.

**Open:** the drink that sits on it (stirred, spirit-forward, clear enough to see the ice through) waits for the person and the story.

- v1, 60/30 with Fernet at 5, 7.5 and 10 ml: **balanced at all three.** Finished at 25.7% ABV, 3.80–3.89 g sugar/100 ml, 0.125–0.132% acid, 44% dilution. (`mixologist-v1-fernet*.json`)

**The gesture I'm proposing: fit the Fernet to the person.** It's the one ingredient that changes the drink most per drop. Start with a barspoon (5 ml), stir, taste it from the spoon, and add more only if the person it's for needs it (up to 10 ml). Every stopping point between 5 and 10 ml is proven balanced, so the guest can't break it by paying attention. That's Coleman's hours spent on one man, in the glass: the drink is fitted to someone. It's not endurance (Down the Line) and not being looked after (Four Shares).
