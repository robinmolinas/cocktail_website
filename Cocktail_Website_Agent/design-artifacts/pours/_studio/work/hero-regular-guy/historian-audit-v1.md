## Wren's three asks
1. **"Plenty of bartenders still call it too much trouble"**: moot, because that line left with the Mojito y4. For the record: Oxford pdf 1343 gives no quantity ("bartenders who regard it as…"), so "some", and no "still" without a date.
2. **"After the Greek god of wine"**: holds, cited to Cartwright (*World History Encyclopedia*, 2012, secondary). p. 71 says "from Greek mythology" and gives no reason, so no line about why he chose it. Drop "at twenty-one" (H2).
3. **"Bénédictine goes into the Singapore Sling"**: yes, and it's on two pages: Oxford BÉNÉDICTINE (pdf 256) names the Singapore Sling among the classics it's "essential" to, and the 1913 order names it. Never "always". In the period it was "often replaced or supplemented by curaçao" (pdf 1803–1804).
