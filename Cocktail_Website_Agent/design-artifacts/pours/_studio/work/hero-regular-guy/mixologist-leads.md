## Lead: the Mojito as he made it at Enrico's (1996)
- Page: "It was the only place in San Francisco making Mojitos... Pretty simple drink, but nobody made them." (*Proper* p. 73). The persona's shrug inside out: anyone could have, nobody did.
- Oxford MOJITO pdf 1343: a common order ever since, despite "bartenders who regard it as too much trouble to make". Recipe there: built in a tall glass (lime and sugar stirred, mint muddled lightly, ice, light rum, soda, stir once).
- Crew and hidden hours: Waugh at Enrico's, "I picked mint all day long... My hands were dyed green" (p. 173). Hester: was Waugh there at the same time as Marco? Not on the page.
- The gesture: built in the glass, no shaker, nothing to spill (the clean coat), nothing to perform.
- Guards: Harrington got the Mojito going at Enrico's (p. 55; Nepove later "Mr. Mojito"), so never "his Mojito". Out: Drake / cholera "cure" (medicine), Havana and Sloppy Joe's (Cuban / Outlaw ground), Maloney's forgotten lime (p. 94), Saunders' Old Cuban and Gin-Gin Mule (pp. 124-129), Smuggler's Cove and tiki.
- Shape: white rum, tall glass, ice. No Mojito in the registry. Family: Superhero (white rum, nick-and-nora) and Problem Solver (white rum, pitcher) differ in glass; the Codex files the Mojito in the Daiquiri extended family, so the family would have a sixth daiquiri-family drink: flag. Moves the plan's gin / rocks-glass reservation (a gin Mojito in a tall glass sits on *Worth the Trip*).
- Veto-free in principle; prove after the ruling.
