# Alternatives: The Firefighter (hero-regular-guy), Wren

## y4 if the Singapore Sling is the drink (my r3 case)
4. The cocktail I've made for you is the Singapore Sling, the drink nobody behind that bar knew how to make. It's gin, cherry, the honeyed herbs of Bénédictine, pineapple and lime, long over ice. A lot of bottles for one glass, which is maybe why it caught two good bartenders out. I made it properly, every measure in its place, for the guest who waited that day and for every day you've practised so nobody waits on you. ## y4 if the Mojito goes plain (no Bénédictine)
4. … drop the last two sentences; the drink then carries only the practice, not the day it started. Weaker, and Robin's no-spark rule then needs the drink made interesting another way.

## y5 alternative (softer, affirmation)
5. You've been enough more times than you've ever let yourself count. Keep the hours if they help you. Just stop treating every day as the one you'll be found out on.
(Set aside: "count" is close to *Not Too Polite*'s closing line.)

## y4 on the Mojito + Bénédictine (v1, retired r5)
4. The cocktail I've made for you is a Mojito, a renowned classic. It's the drink he remembered making at his first job in San Francisco. "Pretty simple drink," he said, "but nobody made them." Plenty of bartenders still call it too much trouble: lime and sugar stirred together in the glass, mint pressed gently so it gives its scent without turning bitter, ice, white rum, cold soda. I added one thing, a spoonful of Bénédictine, a honeyed herbal liqueur that goes into the Singapore Sling. It's there for the night nobody could make that drink, the day all the practice began.
