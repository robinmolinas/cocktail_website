# outlaw-ruler · The Leading Edge · drink draft v1.1 (Tomás, round 4: Hester's S1, S2, S5 in; nothing in the glass changed)

Story ruled (Wren r1/r3: Maytag, centre moved to the 1993 distillery; quince kept r3). Spec: `_studio/specs/outlaw-ruler.json`. Matches reading v1's y4 once the round-4 word fixes are in.

**Glassware:** coupe (about 200 ml), chilled in the freezer
**Contains:** nuts (the quince paste, on the safe side; see Allergens)

## Recipe
| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 45 ml (1½ oz) | Old Potrero Straight Rye whiskey (48.5%) | recommended; the Toasted Barrel bottling (51.2%) works too; or any straight rye of 40-51% |
| 22.5 ml (¾ oz) | Cointreau | or any orange liqueur of the triple sec style, about 40% |
| 22.5 ml (¾ oz) | fresh lemon juice | squeezed the same day |
| 15 ml (1 tbsp) | loosened quince paste | plain membrillo (quince, sugar and lemon, with no nuts in the ingredients) mashed with an equal weight of just-boiled water; check the pack for a nut warning |
| 1 strip | orange peel | squeezed over the top |

## Method
1. Loosen the quince: put 30 g of quince paste (a slice about the size of a matchbox) in a cup with 30 ml of just-boiled water and mash it with a fork until no lumps are left. Let it cool. That's enough for three drinks, and it keeps in the fridge for a few days.
2. Put the coupe in the freezer.
3. Pour the loosened quince, the lemon juice, the Cointreau and the rye into a shaker.
4. Fill the shaker with ice and shake hard for about 12 seconds, until the outside is frosty.
5. Strain it into the cold coupe through the shaker's strainer and a small fine sieve held underneath, which catches any bits of quince.
6. Hold the orange peel over the drink, skin side down, and squeeze it so its oils fall on the top. Then lay it on the rim.
