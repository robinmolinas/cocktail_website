## Resonance test
- **As the guest.** I've seen the thing before it had a name, and I've kept the plan to myself. The microscope and the secret stills are me. "He'd do whatever the new boss said" surprised me, and then it landed: I don't have to hold everything. The fear (it goes quiet after you've gone) is said, and it isn't the end. The ask gives me something back: freedom for the next thing.
- **As the sceptic.** Swap test: *Kept* stays small by choice. Here "small" is one clause, and the story is going to the next edge. *Asked In* has an illegal still; here it's legal and secret from his staff. *Still Yours* is about liking it first; here it's the steering. Horoscope risk: "you catch it before anyone has a word for it" could fit any trendsetter, but the hidden plan and the fear of what it becomes make it this person. Weakest point: y3 carries a lot of history, but it's the turn.
