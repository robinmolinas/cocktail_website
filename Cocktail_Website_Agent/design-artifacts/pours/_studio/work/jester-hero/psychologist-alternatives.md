# Alternatives: The Wise Talker (Wren)

## Names
- **The Wink** (pick). It's what the room gives the person it turns to: everyone knows whose face to look at. Short, warm, a little conspiratorial across a bar.
- **Laugh or Swear**: the threat's own hesitation, funny, and a mild swear Robin allows. But it describes the effect on the guard more than the person.
- **Still Funny**: plain and true. But it's close to the tagline.
- **Tecolote**: the nickname, the mark that stuck. It's lovely to order, but it's Trimble's name, not the guest's, and it needs a translation.

## Taglines
- **Things get bad. You get funnier.** (pick)
- Everyone checks your face to see how bad it is.
- It hasn't won while you're still funny.

## Epigraphs (≤16 words, cold)
- **Mezcal, eggs and milk, once whisked in a prison's cooking pots. Nobody stopped them.** (pick)
- An eggnog once made in a prison, in every pot and water jar they had.

## y5, alternative ending (if Tomás's closing line needs the room)
Some day the joke won't come. That isn't the day it wins. On that morning, go in anyway and just be there. The jokes were how they knew you were still you, and so is that.
