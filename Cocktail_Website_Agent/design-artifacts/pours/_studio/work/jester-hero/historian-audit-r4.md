## Answers
- **Wren (a), nutmeg:** H6. **(b), "renowned":** "eggnog, a classic on record since 1788". Oxford pdf 702 gives the first printed references as 1788, and *Imbibe!* pdf 127 gives the earliest mention as 1788. The *Codex* calls it "the classic example" (p. 256). The place disagrees (Oxford: New Jersey and Pennsylvania papers; Wondrich: a Philadelphia paper), so no place. "Renowned" or "classic" goes on eggnog, never on this mezcal version.
- **Wren's checks:** "chains riveted round their ankles" passes (p. 259: "riveted upon their ankles"). "Stuck to Trimble for good" passes ("to his grave", p. 261, with no death said). "Prisoners from Texas" passes (Green's "Texians"). "For a date that mattered to them" passes. "Feared a riot" passes (p. 260). "Gave the wink" passes (p. 260; the OCR reads "wick", and p. 261's "having the wink" confirms it). "Couldn't decide whether to laugh or swear" passes (p. 260 "hesitated"). The roll-call sentence passes with H5.
- **Tomás:** "nothing bigger would fit" passes (p. 316: "nothing larger could be passed through"). "Loud" passes (p. 260: their shouts "rose high above the walls"). "Every water jar they had" passes for the image (p. 259). The clay is unsourced, which is fine in an image. The single-serve recipe's method is the *Codex*'s ("Dry shake… then shake again with ice", p. 256).
