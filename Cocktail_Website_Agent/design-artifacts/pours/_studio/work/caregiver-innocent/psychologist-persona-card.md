# Persona card: The Angel (caregiver-innocent), round 1, Wren

Source: persona.py (both sheets). Essence "the power of pure care"; goal Service; fear Selfishness; Caregiver fears selfishness, indifference, cruelty; Innocent fears punishment, danger, moral dilemma; drivers "provide structure / SEEK PARADISE"; tone compassionate + honest, optimistic, straightforward. Story: "doesn't need to speak loudly to be heard; their soft presence makes all the difference."

## Core truth
Their care isn't something they *do*, it's the way they *are in a room*: they bring calm in with them, on purpose, and everyone assumes it comes naturally. It doesn't. It's chosen, every time, and nobody sees the choosing.

## Shows / hides
- **Shows:** gentleness, patience, the quiet person people sit next to when things are bad. They make a hard evening softer without saying much.
- **Hides:** that they have bad days too, and don't bring them in. Irritation, tiredness, a wish of their own: they keep those out of sight, because in their private arithmetic wanting something for yourself is a small wrong.

## Hidden fear -> hidden gift
- **Fear:** that if they stop being good (cross, tired, wanting), they'll lose their place. The Innocent's fear of punishment, sitting under the Caregiver's fear of being selfish. Being good feels like the condition of being loved.
- **Gift:** because they hold their own weather back, a room can rest around them. People feel safe near them without knowing why.

## The "real me" line to aim for
- *"People think calm just comes to you. It doesn't. You bring it in with you, so nobody else has to."*
- Second angle (for the turn in whoYouAre): *"Nobody has seen you on a bad day. That isn't because you don't have them."*

## Truth vs legend
The true, kind story. Innocent fears moral dilemma and deception: no con, no dark reveal, no twist where the nice thing turns out to be a lie. Honest and simple. If a legend is attached to the drink, own it in one plain clause and move on.

## Tone
Gentle, plain, honest, a little hopeful. Never saccharine: no halos, no "you're an angel", no religious claims about the guest (the imagery is paintings and light, not faith). A hint of warmth-with-a-smile is fine; jokes aren't this person.

## Distinct from siblings
- **The Craftsman** (*Not Only the Way*): care lives in the method, hidden pride. The Angel has no method to be proud of. The care is presence, and what's hidden isn't pride, it's their own bad days.
- **The Nurse / The Rescuer / The Healer** (unauthored): they *do* something (soup, rescue, mending). The Angel mostly *stays*. The gift is that it gets quieter where they are.
- **The Reassurer**: lightens it with a joke. The Angel lightens it with silence.
- **The True Friend** (*Four Shares*, approved, Innocent x Regular Guy): owns "you're allowed to be looked after too" and "keep one cup back for yourself". **The Angel's position must not be that.** Not "receive care", but "be less than perfect in front of someone, and see that nobody leaves".

## Position (where the reading should land)
A hard truth, gently: let one person see you tired, or cross, once. You won't be punished for it. You'll be trusted more.

## Traps
- Flattery ("pure", "saintly", "you light up rooms"). Recognise, never praise.
- Calling them naive, or making the hopefulness a weakness.
- Anything harsh in the drink as an idea: heat, a punishing bitter, a trick, a performance. Softness is the brief, but not sugar-sweetness (that's the cliché of the persona, and it condescends).
- The family's warm remedies ("you deserve care too", "keep some back") belong to everyone; not this one's point.
- Religious framing of the guest.
