# Name Candidates v1 — The Angel (caregiver-innocent, rework)

## My pick
**Before the Room** — names the deliberate act: what they do before they
walk in (leave the long day outside, choose the calm). Not in the registry.

## Alternatives
- **What You Carry In** — direct, about the deliberate carrying.
- **The Quiet Half** — the side nobody sees; works for Kir and for the guest.
- **The Hour Before** — like "Before the Room" but softer; the preparation.
- **Both Halves** — names the structure of the reading; slightly on the nose.

- **The Rest of You** — used as the first pour's name; scrapped with that pour.
- **Boosts** — first pour candidate; off.
