**Location:** y3, embedded in "What exactly he had carried — what he set down before each of those receptions — nobody lifting a glass in those years would have thought to ask."
**Issue:** Inference — implies he carried something and made a habitual choice to set it down. Neither Oxford (pdf 1133) nor Joy (pdf 315) says this; sources only give the Resistance fact and the reception fact. **Verdict:** Safe as our reading. Not a block. Wren should confirm she's comfortable calling it our reading rather than fact.

### T2 — MINOR: "pressed into syrup" / "pressed from Dijon's black currants"
**Location:** y2 and y4.
**Issue:** Crème de cassis is a liqueur (fruit macerated in alcohol), not literally pressed into syrup. "Pressed from" evokes the fruit origin correctly but slightly mischaracterizes production. Not load-bearing for the mirror; the drink description works without the word "pressed."
**Verdict:** Wren's call. A change to "made from" or "from the black currants of Dijon" would be more precise. Not a block.
