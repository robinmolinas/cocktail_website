## What makes the Angel distinct from siblings
- Not method pride (Craftsman): no craft being shown.
- Not warm remedies handed to the ill (Healer, Nurse).
- Not structure imposed for their own good (Nanny).
- Not going in to fix something (Rescuer).
- Not pushing for change (Campaigner).
Pure presence. The care is BEING in the room. They walk in, and the rowdy
kitchen stills. Nobody notices they did it.

out once.
