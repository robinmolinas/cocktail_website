# Tomás: drink draft v2, lover-caregiver (The Geisha), round 5

**Build: COLD, made after Willard's own Apple-toddy** (ruled round 4; the hot alternative in `mixologist-draft-hot.md` is retired).
Spec: `_studio/specs/lover-caregiver.json` (style `highball`, a still one; filing in Checks).

**Glassware:** a small wineglass (about 250 ml), one plain lump of ice
**Contains:** veto-free

## Recipe
| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| about 60 g | roasted apple (roughly half a medium apple, peeled and cored) | wrapped in wet brown paper and roasted until soft, as Willard's recipe has it. Then left to cool. |
| 60 ml | brandy (grape brandy: a VSOP cognac, or any aged grape brandy of about 40%) | the captain's page says only "brandy"; Wondrich reads it as the grape brandy he preferred |
| 60 ml | cold water | |
| 1½ tsp (about 6 g) | sugar | Willard used as much sugar as brandy; I cut it to a spoonful and a half, or it would be more than four times too sweet |
| 1 | lump of ice | ordinary ice, one piece |

## Method
1. An hour or more before anyone comes, peel and core the apples, half an apple for each drink. Wet a sheet of brown paper (a brown paper bag soaked under the tap works), wrap each apple in it, and roast at 180°C (350°F) for 30 to 45 minutes, until the apples are completely soft. Willard buried his in the embers of a fire. Let them cool.
2. Put half a roasted apple and the sugar in the glass, and mash them together with a spoon.
3. Add the brandy and the cold water, and stir until the sugar has gone and the apple is loose in the drink. Do this before anyone arrives, in the glass or in a jug for several. It will wait.
4. When they come, give it a stir, add one lump of ice, stir a few more turns, and serve with the spoon in the glass.

**closingLine:** *Roast the apples. While they cool, call the one you'd most like to see.*
(Round 5: Wren's lead-2 shape, which rests on no oven time. "call" is new to the registry's closing lines; "walk in" is kept out (the Angel's reading has "when you walk into it"), and "hoping… come" is kept out because the name and y5 already carry it.)
