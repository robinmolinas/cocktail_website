# Alternatives: The Giggler (Wren, round 3). Not guest text.

## Names (registry-checked)
1. ***There It Goes*** (my pick, held until the vote): what you say when the laugh gets out, and what you'd say watching a paper plane leave your hand. Light, no pun on the persona name, and it doesn't spend the "this is me" line.
2. *Can't Help It* (Tomás's pick): true, and the guest's own words. I could take it. My worry: it leans on the involuntary part and reads a touch like an apology, which this guest has done enough of.
3. *Out Loud*: good, but it echoes y5 ("don't hold it in") and makes the proposal the name.
4. *Nothing Wrong*: lovely from Briars, but cold and out of context it reads as a defence ("nothing wrong with it"), and *Wrong*-words sit near *In Good Part*'s ground.
5. *In Stitches*: a stock phrase, and a bit tired.

## Taglines
1. *You find it funny first. Then everyone does.* (my pick)
2. *You don't tell the joke. You're why it lands.*
3. *The joke was never what set them off. You were.*

## Epigraphs

2. *Five drops of salted water. You won't taste them. You'll taste everything else more.* (14; teases the spark; held in case Hester rules on "adopted")
