# The Sex Symbol (lover-ruler): Tomás's draft, v2 (round 4, step 1)

Spec: `_studio/specs/lover-ruler.json`. This is the Mary Pickford from Oxford (pdf 1252: Bacardi, pineapple, grenadine, shaken, chilled cocktail glass), grown up the way Wren asked. The rum now leads, lime makes it dry, and the grenadine is made at home from pomegranate with thyme in it. It carries less sugar than Oxford's (6.48 g/100 ml finished against 7.25). **v2 (round 4):** the rum is now a 50% white rum, so the drink lands in range instead of going to Robin flagged (Wren and the host, r3/r4). The measures are unchanged.

**Glassware:** cocktail glass (about 180-200 ml), chilled
**Contains:** veto-free

## Recipe
| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 60 ml | white rum, 50% or stronger | unaged or lightly aged, not spiced or sweetened; labelled 50% ("100 proof" in the US) or a little stronger; anything from 50% to 63% works |
| 30 ml | fresh pineapple juice | or an unsweetened, not-from-concentrate juice |
| 20 ml | fresh lime juice | squeezed today |
| 12.5 ml | thyme grenadine | homemade, the day before: pomegranate juice, sugar and thyme (see Method) |

## Method
1. The day before, make the grenadine. In a clean jar put 250 g of pomegranate juice (100% juice), 250 g of white sugar and 6 sprigs of thyme, crushed lightly in your hand. Close the lid and shake until the sugar has dissolved, which takes a few minutes. Leave it in the fridge overnight, then strain out the thyme. It keeps about a week in the fridge.
2. Put a cocktail glass in the freezer.
3. Measure the rum, pineapple, lime and 2½ teaspoons (12.5 ml) of the grenadine into a shaker.
4. Fill the shaker three-quarters with ice and shake hard until the outside is too cold to hold. The pineapple will foam a little, and it should.
5. Strain into the cold glass. No garnish.
