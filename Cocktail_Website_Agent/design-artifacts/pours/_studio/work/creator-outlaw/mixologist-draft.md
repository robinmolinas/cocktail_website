## Method (v1; Wren to read)

1. Put the glass in the freezer for ten minutes, or fill it with ice and water while you make the drink and tip that out before you pour.
2. Drop three basil leaves into the shaker. Add the vodka, orange liqueur, lime juice and cranberry.
3. Fill the shaker with ice, close it, and shake hard for about twelve seconds, until the outside is almost too cold to hold.
4. Pour it into the glass through the shaker's strainer, with a small tea strainer held underneath, so no bits of basil or ice get through.
5. Hold the lemon peel over the drink, coloured side down, and squeeze it so its oils fall on the surface. Then drop it in.
