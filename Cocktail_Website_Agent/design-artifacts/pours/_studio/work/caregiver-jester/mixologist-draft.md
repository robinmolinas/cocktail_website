# caregiver-jester: Tomás's draft (v1, round 3)

Spec: `_studio/specs/caregiver-jester.json`. Riff on the Suffering Bastard as printed in *A Proper Drink* p. 264 (Oxford pdf 1767 prints another version; neither is "his recipe", Hester's C2). The recipe is mine.

**glass:** tall glass (highball), full of ice
**contains:** spice (ginger, on the safe side)
**serves:** 1

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 ml | brandy (cognac or any VS grape brandy) | the brandy and gin together are the Suffering Bastard's; the rest is mine |
| 30 ml | London dry gin | the other half |
| 20 ml | fresh lime juice | squeezed the same day |
| 35 ml | cooked ginger and cumin syrup | homemade, below, adapted from the *Flavor Matrix*'s ginger syrup; anything from 30 to 40 ml keeps it balanced |
| 2 dashes | Angostura bitters | |
| 100 ml | soda water, cold | poured on last |
| 1 sprig | fresh mint | give it a clap between your hands so it smells as you drink |

## Method

1. **The syrup (the day before, or an hour ahead).** Toast a teaspoon of cumin seeds in a dry saucepan for about a minute, until you can smell them. Add a 10 cm piece of fresh ginger, peeled and thinly sliced, plus 480 ml water and 200 g sugar. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 5 minutes, stirring until the sugar has dissolved. Take it off the heat, cover it and let it cool. Strain into a clean bottle and keep it in the fridge. It makes about 560 ml.
2. Fill a tall glass with ice. Pour in the brandy, gin, lime juice, syrup and bitters, and stir for a few seconds.
3. Pour the cold soda water on top, then give one slow stir, lifting from the bottom.
4. Clap the mint sprig once between your palms and tuck it in beside the ice.
5. **To take it to someone:** mix everything except the ice and soda in a clean bottle and keep it cold. Add the ice and soda when you get there, because soda goes flat in a bottle. The lime is best the same day.
