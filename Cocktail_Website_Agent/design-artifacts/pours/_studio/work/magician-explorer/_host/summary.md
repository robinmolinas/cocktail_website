**The person:** the Alchemist will try a thing forty ways without complaint, because every try can be undone. What they put off is the last step that can't be taken back. Underneath is a hope they won't say out loud until it works: that the ordinary thing can be made better. The gift is said out loud.

**Story:** Aeneas Coffey, the plan's lead, accepted by Wren in r1. He spent twenty-four years in Ireland's excise, left in 1824 at forty-four, years before any patent, and bought a Dublin distillery. His still began in wood and iron and was soon replaced by copper. Ireland's big firms scorned it (1878) and held out; Scotland took it up. Runner-up: Crow (backup), rejected because he has no step he can't take back (Hester r2).
