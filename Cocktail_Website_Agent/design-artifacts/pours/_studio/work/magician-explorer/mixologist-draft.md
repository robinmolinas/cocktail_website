# magician-explorer · The Alchemist: drink draft v3 (Tomás, round 5; spec v2, audit A5–A6 landed)

**Glassware:** coupe, chilled · **Family:** Martini (stirred) · **Contains:** none (veto-free)

## Recipe

| amount | item | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 60 ml | single grain Scotch whisky | a style, no brand: a younger bottling, 40% or stronger; past about twelve years it "tends to taste mostly of the barrel" (Oxford) |
| 25 ml | blanc vermouth | the pale, gently sweet kind (Dolin Blanc or similar), not dry and not red |
| 1 barspoon (5 ml) | calvados | apple brandy from Normandy; any calvados will do |
| 1 barspoon (5 ml) | simple syrup | equal parts sugar and water, stirred until clear |
| 1 strip | lemon peel | squeezed over the top, then dropped in |

## Method

1. Put the coupe in the freezer, or fill it with ice water while you mix.
2. Pour the grain whisky, blanc vermouth, calvados and syrup into a mixing glass or a jar.
3. Fill it with ice and stir for about 30 seconds, until the outside of the glass is frosted cold.
4. Stop there: more stirring keeps adding water. Empty the coupe and strain the drink into it.
5. Squeeze the lemon peel over the top so its oils fall on the drink, then drop it in.
