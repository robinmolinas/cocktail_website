# Alternatives, v1 (Wren)

## Names
- **Show Your Working** (pick): what the Genius asks of every authority, and what they do with their own answer.
- **Rum and Fruit Juice**: the answer refused; funny and intriguing, but it names the story, not the person.
- **The Whole Thing**: the gift (the answer laid out whole). Check "the whole thing" against *Down the Line* (it was theirs), so probably out.
- **Every Part Named**: the drink's proof; flatter.

## y5, if "ask it out loud" collides
So take the question you've carried longest to the loudest person you know, and let them ask it for you, once. Then listen to what comes back. You may get there years sooner, and on the day it all falls into place, you won't be the only one who's glad.
