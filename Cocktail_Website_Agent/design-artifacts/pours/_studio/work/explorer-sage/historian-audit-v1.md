## Image brief: facts and fences (Hester)
- **Facts it may show:** a tall glass of crushed ice (Codex p. 126 zombie glass); a mint sprig (pdf 2229); grapefruit, lime, cinnamon sticks, cloves (pdf 2229; FALERNUM pdf 761 for cloves); a homemade bottle of falernum with a handwritten, illegible list of contents (our method).
- **Fences:** no likeness of Berry, Haigh, Ray Buhen or Beach; no Tiki-Ti, Hollywood, Sunset Boulevard, film reels or trailers; no ring binder, old ledger, microfilm, cipher or code marks; no years or dates written anywhere; no flame (151-proof is flammable; also the flaming-lime-shell trope, Oxford pdf 259); no tiki mug, carving or skulls (the Codex's pirate mug is theirs); no absinthe or Pernod bottle; no "two per customer" sign or anything about strength; no padlock or vault (no secret-recipe romance); one glass.

## Names
- **Pick: *Show Your Working*.** It's true to the story in a way the others aren't. Berry didn't only give the answer. He showed where it came from (the 1950 binder, the headwaiter's notebook, the barmen), which is what the person asks of every authority and gives in return. Watch: it's a schoolroom phrase, so the reading never leans on school (the Einstein sheet's drill). Runner-up: *In Full*. *Every Part Named* describes the drink, not the person. *Spelled Out* leans on the code (Wren's trap 3).
