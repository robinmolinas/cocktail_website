- **The persona in one line (Wren):** the Genius holds one question for years alongside an ordinary life, because they need to *understand* it. What keeps it open is an answer that's true and explains nothing. They hide how long it's been, and they fear being stuck with an answer they can't get behind, or settling for it themselves. What they give: when they get there, they hand over the whole answer plainly, so nobody has to take it on trust.
- **The story chosen:** the plan's lead, narrowed. Jeff Berry, at twenty-one in 1979, asked "What's in this drink?" and heard "rum and fruit juice" everywhere (Proper p. 258). Ray wasn't being difficult: Beach had told him not to reveal. Writing trailers by day, Berry tracked down old barmen, microfilm and notebooks. By 1998 the question had narrowed down to one: the Zombie. Haigh, "the opposite of Berry", got him a recipe where alone he'd got "rum and fruit juice". **Runner-up:** John Clarke and the proof hydrometer (unused: thin on the person, navy lore).
