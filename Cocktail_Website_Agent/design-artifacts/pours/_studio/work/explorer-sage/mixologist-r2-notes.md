Story: I accept Wren's narrowing to the Zombie. Drink = the Zombie as Berry printed it (Oxford ZOMBIE pdf 2229; same recipe in Joy pdf 409), shaken, not blended.

## Sources opened
- Oxford ZOMBIE pdf 2229: 22.5 ml lime, 15 falernum, 45 gold Puerto Rican rum, 45 gold Jamaican, 30 Lemon Hart 151 demerara, 1 tsp grenadine, 6 drops Pernod, a dash of Angostura, 15 ml Don's Mix (2 grapefruit : 1 cinnamon syrup); blended 5 s with 3/4 cup crushed ice; tall glass, ice to fill, mint sprig.
- Codex p. 127, Zombie Punch (homage to Brian Miller's version): shaken with ice, strained over crushed ice in a Zombie mug; Velvet Falernum, House Grenadine, 2 dashes Pernod Absinthe. Codex p. 126: a zombie glass holds up to 20 oz.
- Oxford FALERNUM pdf 761: rum, sugar, lime; Caribbean spices "such as cloves, ginger, and nutmeg"; "Almonds are sometimes added". So a bought falernum = nuts (safe side).
- Ingredient table: ginger_beer and ginger_cumin_syrup are classed as spice, so no ginger goes in the home falernum.
- Matrix: citrus best pairings (pdf 80) include ginger (spice veto) and dark chocolate. Nothing earned a place. Recorded as consulted and set aside.
- Codex p. 52 cinnamon syrup uses an immersion circulator (niche kit): the home method will be direct heat (my craft call).
