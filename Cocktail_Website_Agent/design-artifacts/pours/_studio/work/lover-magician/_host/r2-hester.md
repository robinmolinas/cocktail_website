The claim we can make is "built from two things on his menu", never "his drink". A second problem: the shiso being strained out and tasted but not seen is already *Up Close*'s shape. Its closing line is "Strain the pineapple out. It's still there at the rim" (ruler-lover, a Lover neighbour). So the leaf needs another job in the method, or at least a closing line nowhere near that one. For the substitute line, Oxford SHOCHU (pdf 1784–1786) says it's usually 25%, either neutral (kōrui) or aromatic pot-still (honkaku). In US shops it's often mislabelled "soju", and Korean soju is a different spirit, so tell the guest to ask for shochu. If you split the base with gin, the *Flavor Matrix* pdf 288 lists juniper and shiso together under alpha-pinene (OCR only, so render it before you lean on it).
