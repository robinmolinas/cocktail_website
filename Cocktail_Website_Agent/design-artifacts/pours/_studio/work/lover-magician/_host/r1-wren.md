🪞 **Wren:** The Charmer. The sheet says they keep one card hidden, always. My read is that the card isn't there to keep anyone guessing. It's there so there's still a reason for the other person to stay. They're afraid that once someone has seen all of them, there's nothing left to stay for. **The one true thing:** *You don't keep something back to be chased. You keep it back so there's still a reason to stay.* The position grows out of it: they'd stay anyway, so turn the thing over for one person and find out. The full card is in `psychologist-read-r1.md`, with seeds in `psychologist-alternatives.md`.

**What they hide, fear and give.** They hide how badly they want the closeness they keep at arm's length. The fear is the Lover's isolation, sharpened: being seen too clearly, then left. The gift, said out loud: people come away feeling they've *found* something nobody else has. Being let in by them feels like a privilege, and that's worth something.

**Siblings.** The Flirt plays in the open, and the Charmer holds back. The Sex Symbol manages an image for a crowd, and the Charmer holds one thing back from the one person they're drawing close. **My test:** (1) something held back by design, not deceit; (2) a tender reason underneath; (3) it cost closeness, on the record; (4) someone saw past it, or the reveal was kind.
