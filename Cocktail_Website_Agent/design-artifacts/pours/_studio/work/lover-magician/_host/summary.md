- **The persona:** the Charmer always keeps something back. It isn't a trick: they're afraid that once they're fully known, there'll be no reason left for anyone to stay.
- **Story:** the plan's lead held. Wren accepted Angel's Share (Shinichi Ikeda and Shingo Gokan, *A Proper Drink* pp. 47–50) in round 1, with the sibling guard that the reading must not land on "they didn't have the words" (that's the Rough Diamond's ground): the reserve was chosen, and the language only explains it. The Innocent family had already released this story to Lover. Petraske's visit, Uyeda and "speakeasy" are kept out. The backup, Donn Beach, was not needed.
- **The drink:** a stirred Martini on a split base of shochu (30 ml) and London dry gin (37.5 ml), with blanc vermouth, half a teaspoon of rich syrup and one torn green shiso leaf. The leaf is stirred in, strained out and set on the table beside the glass as it's served. It comes from the two things Ikeda put on the menu: Martinis stirred the way he'd learned them, and originals with shochu and shiso. v1 had an EDGE on initial acid; v1.1 (more blanc, less syrup) is fully in range. Veto-free.
- **What failed:** Tomás's first idea for the leaf, "tasted, not seen", was *Up Close*'s gesture (Hester); the leaf's job became the handing-over. Gokan's "We were always outside" tripped the one-bartender lint inside a quotation, so Wren paraphrased it. The closing line was rewritten in round 4, and its new second sentence came in on Wren's objection and was checked by Hester in step 4. The epigraph went from "up a floor" to "upstairs" as a lint-only word-count fix.
- **"This is me":** "You don't keep something back to be chased. You keep it back so there's still a reason to stay."
- **Names:** *Worth Finding*, 2–1 (Wren, Hester); Tomás voted *Not All at Once*. Also *Without Notice* (Hester), *A Reason to Stay*, *The Last Thing*, *The Door at the Back*, *The Green Note*, *Something Green*.
- **Edges:** strength sits just under 21% (EDGE) with a 40–43% gin; it's fully in range at 45% or stronger. The bar's present state is unchecked, so the reading uses no present tense. No web visits.
- **Rule candidates:** none.
