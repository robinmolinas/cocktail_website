## What the rooms found hard

- **One family, one motif pool.** Caregiving is warm by default, so the hot drink, "nobody sees", "whoever", "it was nothing" and "since when" kept arriving in several rooms at once. The lint's motif check and Wren's swap test moved lines off siblings in nearly every room, and cost most of the late rounds.
- **The toddy pull.** At least four rooms reached for a hot drink early (hero, innocent, ruler, regular-guy). After your 2026-09-28 ruling, the rooms stopped discarding a category because a sibling used it, but still steered off the exact drink.
- **Medicine stories.** Priestley, Picon, cinchona, Scialom's hangover cure, grog against scurvy: the family attracts "it was once a cure". The rooms kept health claims out of every reading; Hester struck several ("didn't cure the sailors", "the one thing he wanted").
- **Crossings.** Parallel turns crossed often (story swaps in creator, innocent; name swaps in jester, sage). Each was named neutrally and settled on the current version.
- **Usage limits** cut at least eight rooms mid-round. Every room resumed with the agents' context intact.
