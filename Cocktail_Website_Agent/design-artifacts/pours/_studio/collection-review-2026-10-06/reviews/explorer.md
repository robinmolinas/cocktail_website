# Family review: Explorer (2026-10-06)

Reviewer notes in one paragraph: the Explorer family has the collection's best whoYouAre openings (the high board, the frost in the shadow of a fence, "Right. Turning round.", the jar that puts you back in a kitchen), and its drinks are the most genuinely home-made: a frozen Margarita prepared in freezer bags, a burnt-sugar flip, a fat-washed bourbon, a falernum written on its own bottle. Allergen lines all match the live tool. Two instructions can't be followed as printed. *Nothing to It*'s method step 3 ends on a colon with the strength bands missing (they exist in Checks). *For Good*'s step 3 adds the lime early but step 4 adds it again. Those are the P1s. What recurs: (1) **the readings run long and history-heavy**. Five of the collection's eight longest readings are Explorers, and *Show Your Working* and *Far Enough* now go past half history. (2) Small leftovers of research talk ("one of the books I mix from", "the book I took the method from") and one flat legend correction ("He didn't."). (3) A shared proposal shape: four of eleven end on "do it with someone" (Left Standing, Why Not?, Straight Back, Show Your Working). Each is earned, but it is now a family habit. Robin's tagline marks are respected below: `yes` applied, `no` kept, and the three `retry` rows (explorer-lover, explorer-magician, explorer-regular-guy) have new candidates built on behaviour, not the story.
