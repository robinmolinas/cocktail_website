# Family review: Caregiver (2026-10-06)

Reviewer notes in one paragraph: this is the most worked-over family (Robin's 26 edits all landed here), and it shows: the readings say the kindness out loud, the twists are tied to the stories in words, and the proposals mostly end on what the guest gains (Fair Measure, Not Too Polite, Till Spring). The recipes are honest and makeable at home. Every allergen line matches the live tool, and the only out-of-range number (Fair Measure, 9.5% ABV against a 10% floor) is Vernon's four-to-one by design. Three things recur. (1) **Nine of the eleven taglines are lifted from their own whoYouAre**, sometimes word for word ("You've seen them at their worst", "Everyone gets the same you", "You were never against the fun", "since when?", "people you'll never meet"), so the scroll-stopping line is spent before the reading starts and then comes back as an echo. That's the family's main fix. (2) **Research talk the rules have already ruled out** survives in three places (Kir's "Oxford and the *Joy of Mixology* both record", the Martinez's "the big Oxford book… that I lean on", the Suffering Bastard's "I've never found out"). (3) Small home-kit gaps: three homemade syrups have no keeping time, and two cocktail blocks repeat their Glass/Contains line. No gendered language about the guest, no bare "yours" for the drink, no archetype names.
