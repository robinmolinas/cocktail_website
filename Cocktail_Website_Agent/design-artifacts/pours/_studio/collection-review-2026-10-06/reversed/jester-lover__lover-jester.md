# Reversed pair: jester-lover / lover-jester


## jester-lover · The Tease · *I'll Tell You Later* (draft)

- tagline: You always know the perfect moment. It's never the first one.
- epigraph: A New Orleans coffee that keeps you waiting for the coffee.
- drink: old-fashioned · hot · cognac, kirsch, demerara_syrup, coffee_chicory, cinnamon_stick, lemon_peel, orange_peel, clove · two demitasse cups (small coffee cups, about 90 ml) on saucers, warmed with hot water first
- closing: Run the brandy down the peel. Then give someone else the coffee, and let the timing be theirs.

**whoYouAre**

You've had the best news of your week since lunchtime, and you haven't said a word. It can wait until the plates are cleared. The present comes out last, after a decoy that gets a groan. Someone asks why you're smiling, you say "I'll tell you later", and they lean closer. The real pleasure is watching it reach their faces.

You choose the moment with care, and you light up when it comes. It isn't a no that you dread. It's a flat "just tell me" from someone who's stopped playing along, or the day your little delays become what everyone expects. And there's one seat in the game you never take: the one where you're the one waiting.

So here it is: you give people something to look forward to. With you, an ordinary evening gets a reason to linger, and a tease is an invitation into the game.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. So, one evening soon, let someone else decide when. Let them keep their news until the plates are cleared, or hand you the present after the decoy. Sit in their seat for once and enjoy not knowing. You'll feel what you've been giving people all along, and you'll find the game is even better with two. I hope someone keeps you guessing.


## lover-jester · The Flirt · *Only Half Joking* (draft)

- tagline: Every tease is a compliment. The fun is seeing who catches it.
- epigraph: Named for a friend, with one letter too many. This glass finally has jasmine in it.
- drink: sidecar · shaken · gin_london_dry, lemon_juice, cointreau, campari, jasmine_tea_syrup · cocktail glass (about 180-200 ml), chilled
- closing: Put in the jasmine Harrington's spelling promised. Then pay one compliment with no tease around it, even if it comes out a letter off.

**whoYouAre**

You don't tell people you like them. You tell them their coffee order is a cry for help, that they've clearly been practising that walk, that you'll allow the hat, just this once. Then you watch. If they grin and send one back, something starts between the two of you that nobody nearby can quite follow.

Every one of those was a compliment. You just couldn't hand it over plain, because plain is too easy to turn down. Said as a joke, a no costs nobody anything, you included. What stays with you is the one who takes it at face value and looks a little hurt, or smiles politely and lets it drop, and the joke left hanging with only you holding it. And being teased by you feels like being picked. People walk away from you funnier and better-looking than they came in. That's what your teasing hands them.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. Next time someone plays back, don't send another tease. Tell them what the last one was hiding: that they're funny, that they're good company, that the hat is actually great. Once, plainly. It may come out clumsy. Harrington's came out with a letter too many, and it stuck. Plenty of people you like have no idea how much, and the ones you tell will play back hardest of all.
