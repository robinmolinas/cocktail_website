# Reversed pair: lover-sage / sage-lover


## lover-sage · The Romantic · *In Kind* (draft)

- tagline: You always ask the second question. You're still hoping someone asks you one.
- epigraph: This drink has its own ode. A bartender used to say it while pounding the ice.
- drink: old-fashioned · freeform · bourbon, rich_syrup, water, mint_leaves_pressed_out · julep cup (pewter or stainless steel; any metal tumbler of about 300 ml at home)
- closing: Pound the ice loud enough that someone asks. Then give them the long version.

**whoYouAre**

At dinner, a friend mentions the book that got them through a bad winter. The talk moves on. You don't. You ask what it was about that book, then about the one page they still think of, and half an hour later the two of you are somewhere the rest of the table never went. You take what people love seriously, and they remember who did.

You do the easy talk well enough: the trains, the weather. But you feel the evening sliding past on the surface, and later you write three pages to someone who wasn't there. At some point you started keeping the long version for the one person you're sure will follow it. Here's the thing you'd never tell the table: you've decided most rooms aren't the place for it. Sometimes you're right. But it means you can sit at a full table and still feel unmet. Not an empty room: a full one.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. Next time the talk at a full table stays on the weather, don't save the long version for later, or for the one person you're sure of. Give it to the room. Say what a book did to you, or why one song still gets you every time. Not everyone will follow. Someone will, and it may be the person you'd least have picked. That's how a crowded evening stops being lonely: you go deep first, and let them come after you.


## sage-lover · The Connoisseur · *A Brother's Care* (approved            # Robin, 2026-09-24 (incl. name + tagline))

- tagline: Some truths last because someone loved them enough to keep them.
- epigraph: Nothing in this glass is pretending. You'd know if it were.
- drink: martini · stirred · scotch_blended, sweet_vermouth, oloroso_dry, px_sherry, orange_bitters · Nick & Nora
- closing: Sip it slowly. It was made to be understood, not finished.

**whoYouAre**

You're a scientist of the senses. You notice what other people miss: the second flavour hiding behind the first, the moment a drink changes in the glass. And you've taught yourself to put it into words.

Among your friends, you're the one people ask before they choose: the restaurant, the song, the gift, the place to go. It isn't because yours is the loudest opinion. It's because you can always say *why*. That's your gift. You don't just know that something is good, you know what makes it so. It's also how you love people. When you find something wonderful you can't keep it to yourself, and nothing makes you happier than watching someone discover it through you. What you can't forgive is being fooled: the label that promises too much, the story told only to impress.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

4. Here's what I'd ask of you. When you do have this in front of you, take the first sip without naming anything. No notes, no verdict. Name it all on the second, if you still want to. Then try the same thing the next time anything delights you: a meal, a song, a person. Let the first moment go unexplained. You've spent years making sure pleasure tells you the truth. It will. You can put the notebook down.
