# Reversed pair: hero-jester / jester-hero


## hero-jester · The Storyteller · *Hold the Shark* (draft)

- tagline: Your worst days become everyone's favourite stories.
- epigraph: Its name went round the islands long before its recipe did.
- drink: daiquiri · shaken · rum_appleton_12, lime_juice, orange_curacao, orgeat_toasted_almond, simple_syrup · double old-fashioned glass (about 350 ml), packed with crushed ice
- closing: Take the almonds to gold before anything else: that's where the taste is.

**whoYouAre**

A flat tyre on the motorway, in the rain, with no signal. By Friday it's the best thing anybody's heard all month: there's a tow-truck driver who sang the whole way, a hailstone the size of a plum, and a lorry that people ask you to do again. All of it happened, more or less. You simply know which version deserves to be the one people keep.

What nobody at the table knows is that the bigger the story, the worse it really was. The forty minutes on the hard shoulder, cold, and sure nobody was coming, never make it in. Told plainly, a bad day might sound like weakness. Worse, it might sound like nothing much, and be forgotten by the weekend. So you tell it larger, until the bad day is the small thing in it. The people listening go home believing their own bad week will pass, and might even be funny one day. That's a real kindness, and it's why they keep asking for another.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. So keep telling them. The shark stays: people need it, and you're good at it. But once, tell one exactly as big as it was. I'd bet the table goes just as quiet. And what they'll remember afterwards won't be the shark. It'll be you.


## jester-hero · The Wise Talker · *The Wink* (draft)

- tagline: Things get bad. You get funnier.
- epigraph: Mezcal, eggs and milk, once mixed in prisoners' water jars. Nobody stopped them.
- drink:  · flip · mezcal, egg_whole, milk, rich_syrup · a small plain earthenware cup (about 300 ml), chilled in the freezer; any small tumbler will do
- closing: Fill the shell for whoever's on watch. On the days nothing's funny, show up all the same.

**whoYouAre**

It's been bad for a while now. Not one bad day: weeks of it, the kind where everyone has run out of things to say. And you're still funny. Not loud, just there every morning with something, the way some people bring coffee. When it gets worse, people glance at you before they decide how worried to be.

People say nothing gets to you. It all gets to you. You've just decided it doesn't get the last word. What you don't mention is that each day the next joke takes a little more finding, and that you sometimes wonder about the morning nothing comes, when everyone looking at your face sees what you've been holding off. And what you give them is the thing they need most: while someone's still laughing, they're still themselves, not just people something is happening to.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. Some day the joke won't come. That isn't the day it wins. On the morning you've got nothing, let it not come. Go in anyway, unfunny, and just be there. My guess is that on that morning someone else will find the joke for once, because you've been showing them how every day. I'd love you to be there to laugh at it.
