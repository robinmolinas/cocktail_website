# Reversed pair: hero-lover / lover-hero


## hero-lover · The Dashing Hero · *One Line* (draft)

- tagline: All that cool is for someone.
- epigraph: Ordered down to the half measure, shaken ice-cold, then named after one person.
- drink: martini · shaken-spirit · rum_jamaican_any, gin_gordons, vodka, lillet_blanc · deep champagne goblet (about 200 ml), chilled
- closing: The rum goes in first, under the order. Once, tell one of them they're the reason.

**whoYouAre**

The car slides on an icy bend with a friend beside you, and by the time it stops you've got them laughing. The wedding speech goes missing an hour before, and you've written a new one, with a joke about the old. People say nothing seems to touch you. They're wrong about why.

It's never for the room. It's for one person in it. The more someone matters, the cooler you get, because if the feeling showed, it might look like weakness. So you love in exact, unspoken ways: you know the date they'd rather not mention, and you never let on that you noticed. It was never the danger you were hiding. It was them. Around you, anyone you love gets to feel light, because the fear in the room comes to you and goes back as a joke. That's how you protect people. It matters more than the style ever could.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. No speech required. In his friend's copy of the book, Fleming wrote: "For Ivar, who mixed the first Vesper." So pick one of the people all that cool is for, and give them their line, plainly, on a day when nothing's gone wrong. It'll take more nerve than anything you've ever made look easy. And I'd bet it's what they keep of you.


## lover-hero · The Knight in Shining Armour · *Out of Your Way* (draft)

- tagline: Some people send their love. You bring it.
- epigraph: This cocktail is made at room temperature. Some things shouldn't arrive cold.
- drink: martini · freeform · red_bordeaux, rye, demerara_syrup, angostura, water · stemmed red wine glass (about 250 ml), at room temperature, no ice
- closing: Raise it to someone first. When one's raised to you, you've brought enough.

**whoYouAre**

Someone you love says it's a shame you can't make it. It's three hundred miles away, in the middle of the week. You don't argue. On the night, you're there, on time, in your good coat, and the whole room turns round. That's how you love. Not a card, not a call. You said you'd come, so you come, the whole way, with everyone watching. Anyone you love never has to wonder whether you'll be there, and everyone around them can see how loved they are. Nobody forgets being loved like that.

It was never the distance. It's the second before you see their face, when you don't know yet whether they're glad you came, or whether they'd have been fine without you. You'd never admit it, but somewhere along the way, being needed started to feel like the proof that you're loved. It isn't the only proof. Most of the time, it isn't even the best one.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. So go and see someone who's doing perfectly well without you. No occasion, nothing to fix, nobody to rescue: just because you'd like to see them. You'll feel that second at the door, the same as always. Let it come. When their face lights up, it won't be for anything you've done. That's what being wanted looks like, and you've had it all along.
