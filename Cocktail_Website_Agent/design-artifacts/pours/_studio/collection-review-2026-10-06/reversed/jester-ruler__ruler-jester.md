# Reversed pair: jester-ruler / ruler-jester


## jester-ruler · The Straight Man · *Don't Look at Me* (draft)

- tagline: You hardly crack a smile. You're having the best time in the room.
- epigraph: Named after a regular, mixed by the bartender. The regular denied being its author.
- drink: highball · highball · rye, lime_juice, apollinaris · large plain tumbler (about 350 ml), three ice cubes
- closing: Lime first, no sugar, not a word. Next time, let them catch you laughing.

**whoYouAre**

Everyone around you thinks the funny one is your friend: the one who waves both arms through every story, gets the order wrong on purpose and starts the singing. You're sitting beside them, saying very little. You let them run, then one quiet word, or just a look, and the whole table goes. They get the laugh. It comes off you. Most of them take you for the serious one, and you go along with it, since that's part of the act.

How much you're enjoying it is the bit you hide best. You'd never say it, but you need them to be ridiculous. Put you in a room where everyone behaves and you're bored within the hour. People think you put up with the loudest person at the table. You picked them. Your fear is being taken at face value, as the grown-up who doesn't get the joke. You get it before anyone. And that's a gift to everyone near you: next to you, they're at their funniest, because someone steady is there to play it against.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. You give everyone their best moment, and the one person nobody's watching is you. Once in a while, let it show. Let the loudest person at the table see you laugh. They've been playing to you from the start, and your laugh is the one they wanted most. The act survives it. They'll know you were having fun all along, and you get to have it with them, out in the open.


## ruler-jester · The Charismatic Leader · *Who's In?* (draft)

- tagline: With you it feels like a party. It's also going somewhere.
- epigraph: A bartending school went bust. Its party bar didn't. This drink comes in its big glass.
- drink: daiquiri · shaken · vodka, lime_juice, raspberry_cumin_syrup · a big glass (about 400 ml, a 14-oz highball or double old-fashioned), chilled in the freezer, packed with crushed ice and a straw
- closing: Taste it through the straw and make one call. Then pass the glass to whoever hasn't made a call yet.

**whoYouAre**

The last train's cancelled, and there are twelve of you on a cold platform. Someone sighs. You make a joke, it lands, and somewhere in the middle of it there's a plan, and everyone hears it: the café that's still open, the walk across town, a bet on who gets home first. Years later, it's the night everyone brings up.

Nobody calls it being in charge, because to them it feels like being asked along. You like it like that. Being obeyed bores you, and it would turn you into the boss. You seldom admit how much you enjoy having your hand on the wheel, and that when you make that first joke, you don't yet know it'll work. What you dread is the night it gets away from you, or the night the fun runs out and you're just someone telling people where to go. Around you, a plan falling apart is where the good part begins. People go further than they ever would on their own, and they come back glad they went.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. Before you go, a thought, and I mean it kindly. Somewhere near you is someone who never makes the first joke, because you always do. When the evening next falls apart, wait a moment. Leave it to them, and follow wherever it goes. You won't lose the wheel. What you'll get is something you almost never have: the fun of not knowing where you're going while someone else steers.
