# Reversed pair: creator-jester / jester-creator


## creator-jester · The Surrealist · *The Other Berry* (draft)

- tagline: You don't make things strange. You notice they already are.
- epigraph: Made like a strawberry daiquiri. There isn't a strawberry in it.
- drink: daiquiri · shaken · rum_white, lime_juice, tomato_vanilla_syrup · coupe, chilled
- closing: Tell them it's tomato before they taste it.

**whoYouAre**

You've started a hundred sentences with "this is going to sound weird, but…". Then you say what you saw: the thing that looks like something else, two ideas nobody thought belonged together. There's a pause, then a laugh. About a minute later, it doesn't sound weird at all.

None of it is a performance. You're just saying what's there. You laugh along, but you meant it, and it stings a little when it's taken as only a joke. So the strangest things you see, you mostly don't say. What you can't stand is the sensible version: an idea sanded down until it fits in. What you give people is a second look at things they thought they knew.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. You apologise in advance for things you'll turn out to be right about. Drop the "this is going to sound weird". It gives people a way to laugh it off before they've looked. Say what you saw, plainly, the first time. Then let them catch up. They usually do, about a minute later.


## jester-creator · The Wacky One · *For Kicks* (draft)

- tagline: You made it up. Now everyone says it.
- epigraph: Three coffee beans float on top. They're meant to be flies. It's a long story.
- drink: daiquiri · shaken · scotch_blended, irish_whiskey, lemon_juice, orgeat · coupe, chilled
- closing: Three flies, every time. If you ever stop, start again at three.

**whoYouAre**

Somebody once said something daft in passing, and you didn't let it go. You took it one step further, then another. Now there's a word people use without knowing it started with you, a thing you always wear, a little ritual nobody remembers agreeing to. It started as a joke, and it's still a joke. You just never saw a reason to stop.

At every age, someone has expected you to grow out of it. What you don't say is that you could. Normal isn't a mystery to you: you could do it tomorrow, and nobody would stop you. That's the part that frightens you, more than any look across a room: how quietly it could happen. Meanwhile, next to you, other people get to be a bit ridiculous too, and they are. They feel freer around you than they'd ever think to tell you. That's a bigger gift than being taken seriously.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. Some stretches of your life will need you to be serious, and when one comes, you'll manage it. It doesn't get to take the silliness with it. When it's over, pick up where you left off: the word, the ritual, the thing you always wear, all of it. You won't be the only one who missed it.
