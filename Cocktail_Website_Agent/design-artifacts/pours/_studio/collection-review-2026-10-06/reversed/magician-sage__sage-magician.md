# Reversed pair: magician-sage / sage-magician


## magician-sage · The Clairvoyant · *Already There* (draft)

- tagline: You can tell who someone will become. You can rarely say how.
- epigraph: The grappa here comes from one grape variety. Grappa made that way began in Friuli.
- drink: martini · stirred · grappa_nonino_monovitigno, blanc_vermouth_fig_leaf, simple_syrup · small tulip-shaped grappa glass with an open rim (about 150 ml), chilled, no ice
- closing: Steep the leaf and leave the figs. When you see where someone's heading, give them your reasons too.

**whoYouAre**

There's someone in your life that everyone has quietly made up their mind about. You haven't. You noticed something once: the evening they stayed behind to fix a thing nobody would ever have checked. Since then you've talked about them as if their turn is coming. Usually it comes. And when people ask how you knew, you can't really say.

That's the part nobody hears. Your reasons are so small that, said out loud, they'd sound like nothing, so you hand over the conclusion and keep the reasons. It's also why you can seem a little mysterious. Underneath sits a worry you seldom admit: that what you saw in someone was only what you hoped to see, and they went ahead because you said so.

And here's your gift to whoever is near you. In your eyes, nobody is ever finished. People grow into the place you kept for them, and plenty never find out who kept it. Most people only get that kind of faith from someone who loves them. You hand it out on far less.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. When you next see where someone's heading, show them what you saw, not just where you think they'll end up, the way the Noninos, as the family tells it, printed their working on the label: the evening they stayed behind, the small thing they fixed. Then they aren't carrying your hope. They're holding your reasons, and they can grow into them at their own pace. When they get there, they'll know they did it themselves. And you won't be the only one who can see it anymore.


## sage-magician · The Game-Changer · *Plain to See* (draft)

- tagline: You take a yes more seriously than a no.
- epigraph: Two countries, two whiskies, both often called rye. One usually holds only a little rye.
- drink: old-fashioned · stirred · canadian_whisky, rye, demerara_syrup, tamarind_water, angostura · small tumbler (about 200 ml), chilled in the freezer, no ice
- closing: Put it out there. They'll pick it up with their eyes open.

**whoYouAre**

You've seen how something could work better: the way the team runs its week, how the family shares out the holidays, the fix a friend has needed for years. You can see it whole. And before you say a word, you walk back through it, more than once, until you can explain it in a sentence. From outside it looks like confidence. It's closer to care. You know that if they like it, they'll actually do it.

What keeps you up is the yes: everyone's, and then finding out you were wrong. A mistake you make alone stays with you. A mistake people take up because of you travels with every one of them.

So you never hand anyone an answer to obey. You give them a way of seeing it, plain enough to use without you, and to notice if you've slipped. You never asked anyone to believe you. You showed them how to look. That's generosity, and not the usual kind: people leave you more capable, not more in need of you.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. Let me be honest with you about one habit. You keep the next idea back until you're sure, and you'll never feel quite sure enough. But look at what you've already done to it: you've made it simple enough to see through. People who take up an idea like that aren't following blindly. So share it before it's big. A flaw found among a few friends costs a laugh. And if there's no flaw, you get what you were after all along: people doing something better, their own way, because of what you showed them.
