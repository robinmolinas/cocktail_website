## creator-regular-guy · The Photographer · *As It Was* (draft)

- tagline: Everyone keeps the big days. You keep the Tuesdays.
- epigraph: The truest record of this drink is a book that never caught on.
- drink: daiquiri · shaken · gin_london_dry, lemon_juice, simple_syrup, maraschino, creme_de_violette · a stemmed cocktail glass (a coupe or any small stemmed glass), from the freezer
- closing: Hold it up to the light first. The colour was always there.

**whoYouAre**

"Hold on, don't move," you say, at the kitchen table, on an evening nobody planned. People laugh, because nothing's happening. You know something is: the light across the table, what everyone ordered, the thing someone said that they'll have forgotten by Friday. Other people wait for something worth remembering. You notice it's already here.

Ask you about it and you'll shrug: it's nothing, just a picture. Making a fuss of it would feel like showing off. The shrug covers a fear you don't say out loud: the blur. A whole year going by and looking like one day. Keeping the ordinary is how you make sure it counted. And the people around you get to see their own everyday life the way you saw it: worth keeping.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. One thing I'd ask. Stop calling it nothing. Somewhere in everything you've kept this year there's an ordinary day that someone else has already forgotten. Print it, and give it to the person in it. They don't know yet what their Tuesday looked like. You do.


## regular-guy-creator · The Hidden Talent · *Right Here* (draft)

- tagline: You thought you had to choose. You never did.
- epigraph: Four plants flavour this gin, they say. The last you taste comes first here.
- drink: old-fashioned · built · gin_tanqueray_london_dry_413, licorice_root_syrup, angostura · plain kitchen tumbler (about 250 ml), three or four ordinary ice cubes from the freezer tray
- closing: Make it where everyone is, not in the other room. Someone will ask about the finish.

**whoYouAre**

There's something you're good at that doesn't match the picture people have of you. A cake nobody believed you'd made. A song you only sing in the car. A drawing on the back of a work envelope. When someone finds out and says "you never said", you laugh and change the subject. Part of you has always believed you had to pick: be good at it, or stay one of them.

The truth is, you want both. And you've got one thing wrong: when it shows, nobody you love thinks you've got above yourself. They're proud it's one of theirs who did it. That's your gift to them, more than the cake or the song: it shows them what someone like them can do.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. So show it to the people who knew you first, before strangers. Make the cake for your own table. Sing with the window down on the way to your family's. Nobody will think you've changed. You'll find out you never had to choose, and they'll get to be proud of something that's theirs too.
