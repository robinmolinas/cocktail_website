## explorer-hero · The Pioneer · *Nothing to It* (draft)

- tagline: You went first, so they knew they could.
- epigraph: One barrel, bottled alone, used to stay inside the trade. Then someone put it on sale.
- drink: old-fashioned · stirred-spirit · bourbon_blantons, caramel_syrup, angostura · a small tumbler (about 200 ml), chilled in the freezer, no ice
- closing: Take the sugar off at amber. And when they ask, let them hear how close it came.

**whoYouAre**

The top of the high board, the whole pool gone quiet, everyone looking up. Years later it's a microphone, or a first day in a place where you don't know a soul. You've gone through it so many times in your head that you know every step of it. From the outside, it looks as if you were made for this.

You're scared every time. Nobody's ever been able to tell. What gets you up there isn't a lack of fear. It's a bigger one: the day you could have gone and didn't, and knowing it for the rest of your life. So you go. And afterwards, when people ask, you make it sound like very little. That isn't modesty. You've seen what happens when something looks impossible: nobody tries. So you make it look possible, and somebody behind you decides they could do it too. Hardly anyone knows you do that on purpose. It's one of the most generous things a person can do for whoever comes after them, and you do it every time.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. After the next thing everyone watched you do, don't make it sound like nothing. Find the person who's thinking of going next, and tell them you were terrified, and that you went. Your steadiness showed them it could be done. Your fear will show them they don't have to be fearless to do it. And you get back something you've been giving away every time you shrugged: how much it took.


## hero-explorer · The Quest Seeker · *Someone Else's Map* (draft)

- tagline: Far doesn't scare you. Finished does.
- epigraph: Sixty cases of the pine in this glass were all one importer could afford.
- drink: old-fashioned · hot · zirbenz, rum_aged, allspice_dram, lemon_juice, demerara_syrup, water · heatproof glass mug with a handle (about 250 ml), warmed with hot water first
- closing: Pine first, then a spoon of what came next. Then ask someone what they'd like you to find.

**whoYouAre**

The medal's still round your neck, or the last exam's handed in, or the record you hunted for two years is finally on the shelf. Everyone's pleased for you. And by that evening you're quietly reading up on the next one.

People call it restless. It isn't, quite. What you dread is finished: a comfortable life that asks nothing of you, a map with nothing left on it. Going after something far away and hard to get is how you find out what you're made of, and once you know, you need something new to find out about. And you never return empty-handed. You turn up with things nobody near you could have got, and you make far away look like somewhere people can actually go. Everyone you know lives a bigger life because you went. No prize on your shelf is worth as much.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. So the next evening you arrive and it goes flat, don't go looking for a bigger mountain straight away. Ask a friend what they've stopped hoping to find: the book that's out of print, the song they half remember, the place they always meant to see. Then go and get it. You'll never be short of somewhere to go, and a friend gets the thing they'd given up on. I hope your list stays long.
