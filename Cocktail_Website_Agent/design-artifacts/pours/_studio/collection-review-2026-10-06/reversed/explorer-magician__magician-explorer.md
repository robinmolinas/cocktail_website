# Reversed pair: explorer-magician / magician-explorer


## explorer-magician · The Spiritualist · *Just Knew* (draft)

- tagline: You've been doing it for years. You just never called it that.
- epigraph: A famous bartender's recipe for the classic behind this drink: build it in any order.
- drink: martini · stirred · gin_london_dry, campari, sweet_vermouth, dry_vermouth · a rocks glass, filled with ice
- closing: Take the bay leaf from the jar you never think about. Then name one habit that's quietly kept you going.

**whoYouAre**

There's a shelf somewhere in your home that would surprise people. Very old books next to very new ones, and a couple you'd rather nobody picked up. You've gone further than the shelf, too: the silent weekend, the long walk, the course your friends teased you about. Each time you return with one thing, a line or a habit, and you want it to last through an ordinary week.

Nobody sees how often it slips. By Wednesday the inbox has it, and part of you decides the real answer must be somewhere else, so you start looking again. What would scare you is a life that stops asking what it's for. But some of it has been working for years without your noticing. The walk you take before a hard day. The song you play in the car before you go in. You'd never call those a practice, and they've been holding you up the whole time. People feel it, too. You arrive more present than most, and sometimes you hand someone exactly the line they needed, with no lecture attached. That line was worth every retreat.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. Keep going away. It's where you find the words. But before you plan the next retreat, look at what already carries you through an ordinary day, the walk, the song, the hour you keep for yourself, and give it its proper name. It counts. You're further along than you think, and I'm glad of it for you.


## magician-explorer · The Alchemist · *One More Adjustment* (draft)

- tagline: Nothing is ever only what it is. Not to you.
- epigraph: The still behind this whisky was first built of wood and iron.
- drink: martini · stirred · scotch_single_grain, blanc_vermouth, calvados, simple_syrup · coupe, chilled
- closing: Stop stirring once the glass is frosted: every turn after that adds water. Then let whatever you've been adjusting go out as it is.

**whoYouAre**

Right now, something you've made is nearly there. A recipe, a piece of writing, a shelf, a plan. Everyone who's seen it says it's done. Only you can tell what's still off, so you change it, bit by bit, and you note each change so you can always find your way back.

You'll do a thing forty ways without complaint, because every one of them can still be undone. It's the last step you put off: the moment it's served, sent, signed or said, and can't be taken back. So it waits, sometimes long after it's ready. What you hide even better is the hope. People see someone careful, a little sceptical, who wants to see proof. Underneath, you believe almost anything can become something better, and you want that to be true more than you'd ever say before it works. Give you something everyone else has written off, and in time you'll hand back a better one. The people close to you start to believe things can get better, because they've seen you make it so.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. When something you've made is nearly there again and your hand goes back for one more change, ask who the change is for. If it's for the thing, make it. If it's for you, put it in copper: serve it, send it, say it. A result you can't take back is also one nobody can take away from you. And then, at last, you get to enjoy it finished.
