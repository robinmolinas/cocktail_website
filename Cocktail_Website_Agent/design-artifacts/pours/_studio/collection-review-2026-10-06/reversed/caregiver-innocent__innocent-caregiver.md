# Reversed pair: caregiver-innocent / innocent-caregiver


## caregiver-innocent · The Angel · *Before the Room* (draft)

- tagline: Everyone breathes easier when you arrive. Nobody asks what you left at the door.
- epigraph: Everyone expects the cassis. The basil gets there first.
- drink: highball · freeform · creme_de_cassis, champagne_brut · a flute or wide coupe, chilled
- closing: Let the Crémant carry the basil up. Then say one thing you'd usually leave outside.

**whoYouAre**

People think calm just comes to you. It doesn't. You bring it in with you, the way you'd bring a coat for someone who didn't think to. A rowdy kitchen goes quieter when you walk into it. Someone who was about to cry starts talking instead. Two people halfway through an argument remember that they like each other. Nobody thanks you, because nobody saw you do anything. You just came in.

Hardly anyone has seen you in a mood. Cross over nothing. Wanting the window seat, the last slice, an evening that's only yours. You keep all of that on the other side of the door. Somewhere you learned that being good is what keeps your place, and that wanting too much might cost you it. So the people who love you know half of you. It's the easy half, for them.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. So start with whoever you're surest of. When they ask where you'd like to sit, don't say you don't mind. Say the window. And when something small makes you cross, let it reach your face before you smooth it over. It's a small thing to ask, and it will feel enormous. I don't think anyone will leave. I think they'll finally meet the rest of you.


## innocent-caregiver · The Big Sibling · *Night Light* (draft)

- tagline: You were small too. You looked after them anyway.
- epigraph: Part of the taste in this glass comes from something that stayed behind in the barrel.
- drink: martini · stirred · fino, dry_vermouth, blanc_vermouth, camomile_honey_syrup, orange_bitters · a small stemmed cocktail glass, chilled, no ice
- closing: The fino needs only quiet company. Let one of them know you were scared too.

**whoYouAre**

You were small too. People forget that part. You were the one holding the smaller hand at the crossing, saving the last biscuit, staying awake because someone littler was frightened of the dark. No one had asked. It just seemed obvious that anyone you loved should be safe, and if nobody else was watching, you would. You were never less scared of the dark. You just had someone to be calm for.

You still do it. When something goes wrong, the calm voice comes out before you've decided anything. And underneath it, whenever someone you love gets hurt, part of you is already asking what you should have seen, as if it had been your watch. Here's what that gives them: the ones who grew up near you learned from you that the dark can be got through. Some of them are calm in it now, and they couldn't tell you where they got it.

**yours (proposal paragraph)**

5. Pick one of the people you watched over, one who's grown now, and tell them about one time back then when you were scared too, while you were the one being calm. They can carry it now. I think they'll be glad to know what reached you first, and prouder of you than you'd guess.
