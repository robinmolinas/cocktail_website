# Tagline review — recent pours

Reviewed 2026-10-02 against the complete guest-facing pour for every dossier added since `d0fd04e` (65 pours): cocktail, ritual, epigraph, `whoYouAre`, full `yours`, and closing line.

No pour dossier has been edited. These are recommendations for Robin's review.

## What is making the current set sound generated

The problem is mostly corpus rhythm, not weak psychological ideas.

- 52 of the 65 current taglines are two short sentences.
- 32 start with **You...** and another 16 start with a mass contrast such as **Everyone...**, **Nobody...**, **Most people...**, or **People...**.
- Many use the same reveal mechanism: public reading, full stop, private correction. For example: “Everyone sees X. Nobody sees Y.”
- Abstract praise recurs more often than observed behaviour. The full readings are much more human because they contain the cash machine, the bread, the wet Tuesday, the high board, the chair moved nearer the heater, and the sentence someone swallows.

The replacements below use those particular human details, vary their syntax, and usually make one clean observation rather than performing a twist.

## The current lines I would be least worried about

These are good in isolation and could survive if Robin prefers them. They mainly suffer from being surrounded by too many lines in the same cadence.

- *Left Standing* — “You'd rather know it's there than have it.”
- *Why Not?* — “There's an easier way. It just isn't as much fun.”
- *Someone Else's Map* — “Far doesn't scare you. Finished does.”
- *I'll Do It* — “You say yes before you know how.”
- *Go On* — “Most of the fights you've stopped never happened.”
- *The Long View* — “The worse it gets, the clearer you are.”

If any of those stay, the surrounding rewrites will give them back their force.
