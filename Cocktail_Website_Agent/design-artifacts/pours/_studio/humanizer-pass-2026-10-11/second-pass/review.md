### Off-Label · The Experimentalist

Meaning retained: motive: Start before the outcome is known and enjoy discovering it.; fear: Repeating a success exactly feels like a door closing; failed tries remain less visible.; gift: People learn to try, and to treat a failed attempt as the next thing to change.; story: Gonzalez's competition account and attributed non-win remain; curiosity is signposted as interpretation.; drink: The ounce-and-a-half bitters and lemon remain; lemongrass and the guest's half-ounce choice of unsweetened spirit change the accent.; proposal: Return to a half-tried idea and stay to see what it becomes.

**Before**

Hand you instructions and you'll follow them, right up to the moment you don't. You start things before you know what they'll be. That isn't a hole in the plan. It's the part you like best: finding out.

Some of what you try doesn't work, and you honestly don't mind. You rarely mention those ones, and there are more of them than anyone guesses. What gets under your skin is the opposite: something works, and everyone wants it exactly like last time. Being asked to repeat yourself feels like a door closing. Stand next to you long enough and people start saying what you say: "let me try something." You've shown them that something that didn't work isn't a disaster. It's just the next thing to change.

**After**

Hand you instructions and you'll follow them, right up to the moment you don't. You start things before you know what they'll be, because finding out is the part you like best.

Some of what you try doesn't work. You don't mind, though you rarely mention those ones, and there are more than anyone guesses. What gets under your skin is having something work and being asked to do it exactly like last time. It feels like a door closing. Stand next to you long enough and people start saying what you say: "let me try something." You've shown them that a failed try isn't a disaster, just the next thing to change.

### Go On · The Caring Leader

Meaning retained: Hears trouble before others, hides active listening and fear a missed warning costs someone; earns honest speech; Jimmie prevents fights but fears withholding credit; a waiting stirred Manhattan allows speech; trust also allows a clear no.

**Before**

You hear it before anyone else does. A joke at dinner that lands a little too hard. A voice going up half a note in a meeting. A group chat going quiet in the wrong way. By the time the others notice, you've already pulled up a chair, asked the one who's upset what they meant, and let them say all of it.

People take you for easygoing. You aren't. You're listening the whole time, because when people are in your care, you'd rather hear the hard thing tonight than watch it break something later. You could settle most of it with one word, and you rarely use it. You'd rather they finish. People say the real thing around you, because they're sure you'll hear all of it. You never mention how hard you work at it, or the fear underneath: that the one time you miss it, it all comes apart, and someone you were looking after pays for it.

**After**

You hear the joke at dinner that lands a little too hard, the voice going up half a note in a meeting, the group chat going quiet in the wrong way. By the time anyone else notices, you've pulled up a chair and asked the person who's upset what they meant. You let them say all of it.

People take you for easygoing, unaware of how hard you're listening. When people are in your care, you'd rather hear the hard thing tonight than watch it break something later. You could settle most of it with one word, but you rarely use it; you'd rather they finish. Around you, people say the real thing because they're sure you'll hear them out. You keep the effort to yourself, along with the fear that one missed warning could bring it all apart, leaving someone you were looking after to pay for it.
