## Review notes

- Shared sensory profiles may share words or a complete trio. Accuracy takes priority over making every row different.
- Creamy, silky and velvety describe different possible textures. The review ties them to cream or egg, anise oils, clarification, broth, syrup, shaking or stirring as appropriate.
- On the Record remains sweet from its port. Tannic describes the tea-and-wine drying grip; it does not call the drink low in sugar.
- In Good Part uses Plush for its soft foam and Tangy for its lemon and fruit-wine acidity, giving each word a separate job.
- Warming describes a strong spirit’s perceived warmth in the mouth. Steamy, Cosy and Woodland are tied to actually hot serves in their explanations.
- At the Time gains coffee flavour as the coffee block melts; the opening sip remains an Old-Fashioned.
- Is It Just Me alternates between apricot rye and pilsner in two glasses.
- Nutty in A Brother’s Care is oloroso’s aroma, rather than a new nut ingredient. Declared allergens are unchanged.
- The explanations identify genuine recipe uncertainty: the optional spirit in Off-Label, the remaining tamarind character after milk clarification, the floral Armagnac substitute, faint lavender through the egg shell, and different bottle or garnish choices.
