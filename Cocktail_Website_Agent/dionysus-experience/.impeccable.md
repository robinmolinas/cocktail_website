# Design tooling context

This is a thin context entry point, not a new source of product or visual
decisions. Read PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md before work. They and their linked
approved BMAD/WDS sources remain canonical; resolve disagreements there.

## Design Context

### Users

As documented in PRODUCT.md: cocktail enthusiasts, people seeking
self-discovery, partygoers and fans of immersive magical experiences.
The job is to discover a personal cocktail, feel seen, and want to share or
make it. Do not infer a different audience from implementation details.

### Brand Personality

Mystical, sophisticated, theatrical and intimate. Preserve the creator's
high-end speakeasy/fortune-teller atmosphere; see PRODUCT.md.

### Aesthetic Direction

DESIGN.md is the visual contract: warm paper and candlelit depths, purposeful
darkness, Playfair/Playfair Display/Jost and choreographed motion. Persona
imagery follows ../design-artifacts/persona-image-system.md and the settled
pour. Timber room/table; functional metal objects are allowed. No modern
industrial set, neon, SaaS cards or generic lifestyle cocktail photography.

### Design Principles

1. Preserve the locked brand system rather than apply generic anti-pattern
   advice mechanically; deliberate darkness/glass language is not itself slop.
2. One room, different person: accurate drink first, plausible personal traces
   second, ingredient cues only when needed. Physical blank tag; runtime name.
3. Immersion cannot erase legibility. Actual copy, names, motion and reduced
   motion must work on phones as well as desktop.
4. Keep geometry, browser evidence, material acceptance and user/editorial
   approval separate. Do not infer a release from one passing test.

This entry records existing instructions only. New strategic decisions belong
in the canonical design log, not this tooling file.
