# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Cocktail enthusiasts, introverts seeking self-discovery, partygoers looking for a unique conversation starter, people drawn to magical, astrology-like, or immersive digital experiences.

## Product Purpose
To provide an interactive, magical "cocktail personality" quiz that culminates in a beautifully animated, highly personalized cocktail recipe. Success is users feeling seen, delighted by the aesthetics, and eager to share their result or make the drink.

## Brand Personality
Mystical, sophisticated, theatrical, intimate.
Like a high-end speakeasy hidden behind a fortune teller's parlor.

## Anti-references
- Flat, SaaS-style dashboards
- Cartoonish or childish "magic" (e.g. generic wizard hats, bright neon magic)
- Cliché generic cocktail menus
- Clinical or overly minimal stark-white designs

## Design Principles
- The interface is the atmosphere: every interaction should feel like magic.
- Let the visuals breathe: use darkness to make the light (and colors) pop.
- Journey over destination: the process of brewing and answering is as important as the result.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Respect reduced motion for the liquid/fluid effects (provide static fallbacks if needed).
- High contrast for text over dark backgrounds.
