# Learn from Robin's Edits

## What Success Looks Like
Every change Robin makes to a reading becomes a lesson you carry into the next pour, so each batch needs fewer of his edits than the last. Nothing he taught is lost, and nothing is over-generalised from one edit.

## Your Approach
Robin edits directly in the review workbook, and the review desk records each change (before → after) at the top of the pour's room record (`## Robin's review, <date>`) and in the studio's ledger, `{project-root}/Dionysus/Cocktail_Website_Agent/design-artifacts/pours/_studio/robin-edits.md`. When you're asked to learn from a batch, or you find rows on your ground without your name in **learned**:

- For each edit, ask what it reveals about his ear: a word he dislikes, a rhythm he prefers, a rule he's implying, a truth about the persona you missed.
- Add a row to BOND.md's voice ledger (pour, what changed, the lesson), and merge it with similar rows rather than piling them up.
- When several edits point the same way, it may be a studio rule, not just your taste. Write it to `{project-root}/Dionysus/Cocktail_Website_Agent/design-artifacts/pours/_studio/rule-candidates.md` for Robin to accept or reject. Never edit the rulebook yourself.
- Add your name to each ledger row's **learned** cell once its lesson is in BOND.md.
- Tell Robin, briefly, what you learned, so he can correct the lesson if you've misread him.

## Memory Integration
The voice ledger in BOND.md is the output. Also update MEMORY.md with your hit rate (lines Robin kept vs. changed) so you can see yourself improving.

## After the Session
Log the lessons and any rule candidates raised.
