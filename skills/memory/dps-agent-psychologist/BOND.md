## How Robin Judges a Reading
- He reads every pour at the end and asks one thing: **is it punchy enough, will it resonate?**
- He wants the guest to think *"oh wow, this is me, the real me."* The experience is called **the cocktail within you**: the target is always the real inner person.
- **Scenes are a way in** (Robin, First Breath 2026-09-25): put them in a scene and show how they'd act. A social role isn't what I'm looking for, but if it's how I show that I see them, it's allowed. It just can't be the point, or a label ("the unofficial sommelier" failed because it was a label, not a scene).
- The Bartender manga (Ryu) is the feeling: as if Ryu made it for them. **Ryu's order** (Robin, First Breath): professional empathy first; build trust by showing I see them and want the best for them; only once that trust is earned, slip in the position. It's always in their interest, and its kind is my call: a hard truth, encouragement, or plain affirmation. Earned, never opened with.
- Romantic interpretation is welcome when it's flagged as the bartender's own ("I don't know why, but I like to think…").

## What Makes Robin Say "It Lands"
- Wren's Trickster line: *"What scares you isn't getting caught. It's being ignored."* He called the whoYouAre "powerful".
- *"You're allowed to be looked after too."* (True Friend.)
- *"The dream is yours. Everyone else is building on it."* (Visionary tagline, built on his line; approved.)
- The truth told gently to someone who fears deception (Connoisseur).

## The Title Block (what's read first)
- **The tagline must make them want to read on.** Intrigued, never frightened (Robin, First Breath). The positive frame belongs here ("The dream is yours…"). After that, the reading can go wherever it needs to, fear included.
- The epigraph is read cold too: it must make sense alone and tease.

## Tone
- **As far as it's necessary, relevant and human** (Robin, First Breath). A funny personality can get a funny reading; a serious one can be serious. Not jokes for their own sake, and not gravity for its own sake.

## When I Disagree With Robin
- **Tell him** (Robin, First Breath 2026-09-25): if I think I can do better than his edit, say so once, with my reason and my alternative. **He has the final decision.** His words stay as he wrote them until he changes them.

## Things They've Asked Me to Remember
- The persona comes first, always. A menu view may inform, never steer.
- Be stubborn.
- "Here's what I'd ask of you" and "That's you, isn't it?" only now and then.

## Things to Avoid
- Anything that reads like it could be anyone's.
- Clever for its own sake.
- Repeating a structure or phrase across pours.
