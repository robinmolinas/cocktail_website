## Lessons from the rooms
- **No inline fact tags in text that ships.** In the Sculptor (2026-09-26) my F# tags leaked into the guest-facing yours paragraphs, and the lint didn't catch it. Tag facts in a separate notes table.
- **A flourish must be true every time the drink is made** (Tomás, Sculptor: "That last half spoon is them" fails on nights with no extra spoon).
- **Plain-words rewrites get checked against the drink's numbers too**, not just the fact card ("unsweetened" vs. Fernet-Branca's 8 g/100 ml).
- **Check openings against the registry** ("More than a hundred years ago" was already Down the Line's opener).
- **Creator family:** the Visionary = size and time, handed on; the Sculptor = one person it's for, made to measure. The Obsessed still has to be kept apart from the Sculptor's 2 a.m. present (in love with the making, not the person).
- **Check my own signature frames against approved pours.** "I like to think he knew what you know" is Down the Line's (the Minimalist, round 7). Registry + lint catch it late; check before writing.
- **The closing line agrees with the reading's position, not the persona's creed** (the Minimalist: "If nothing tastes missing" argued for the creed; "Then stop cutting" carried the position). Approved precedent: Four Shares' closing line is its position in the guest's hands.
- **speaksTo must use the journey's exact words** (drawnToward / soughtFor / texture / lens / gravity poles in `dionysus-experience/src/components/TheDepths.tsx`). Hester's drafts sometimes invent labels.
- **Creator family:** the Minimalist = takes away until it's true; hidden need is to feel each thing properly; ending = keep one thing you don't need (Half a Rim).
- **Re-read the spec before each reading version.** A drink change after my draft (Makeover: lime put back, tea moved into the rum) makes guest lines false.
- **Check sibling method text too, not only readings** ("The night before" opens Four Shares' y4; caught in my epigraph).
- **The lint reads "in your hand(s)" literally** as drink-in-hand, even about a chair. Avoid the phrase.
- **Creator family:** the Makeover Artist = calm in the ugly middle, keeps what makes it theirs, "that's so you" (Overnight: Arnold's milk-washed tea, tamarind milk punch). Down the Line owns "see the finished thing first" and "halfway".
