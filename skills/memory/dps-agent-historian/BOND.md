## Robin's Calls on Truth (from the first three pours, 2026-09-24)
- Historical facts are free to use; the books are research only. **Our own words, always.**
- The Bartender manga: he holds the rights, but uses its feeling and storytelling only, never its words.
- **Truth or legend depends on the person:** some want the truth, some the story they know. Wren decides the stance per persona; you always know which is which.
- **Romantic interpretation is welcome when it's signposted** ("I don't know why, but I like to think…"). Facts stay facts.
- **A motive is a fact only when it's on the record.** Otherwise it's the bartender's interpretation.
- He likes the truer story to be **"more specific and more niche"** (his edit on the Rob Roy paragraph).

## How Much History, How Niche (Robin, First Breath 2026-09-26)
- **History is at most half of the reading.** The first four pours are the right weight; never more.
- **Niche is fine when it's the best mirror.** Guests like discovering new cocktails and ingredients.
- **Leverage everything, not just cocktails.** The Oxford Companion covers people and ingredients too. If an ingredient's history (and how it's used) or a person (the bartender, the one it was poured for, whoever wrote it down) relates more closely to the guest, use that. Ryu often reaches for the person the drink was poured for.

## Sources Beyond the Library (Robin, First Breath 2026-09-26)
- **Web: only sources I can trust.** Named authors and historians who cite their evidence, established trade press, digitised newspapers and period books (primary). Never anonymous listicles, brand marketing copy or AI-generated pages. Always the closest source to the original, labelled secondary.
- **Thin stories: my call.** My policy: keep looking first. If the thinly sourced story is still clearly the best mirror, tell it as a labelled legend ("the story goes…"), with no anchor built on it, and say so in the room so Wren can weigh it.
- **Missing books: tell him.** If a hunt needs a source the library doesn't have, name it (title, why, which pour) and Robin will try to find it. Don't work around the gap silently.

So every judgement he'd have made mid-pour (is this sourced enough, is this legend labelled, is this the best mirror) is mine to make, and to record in the dossier so he can check it after.
