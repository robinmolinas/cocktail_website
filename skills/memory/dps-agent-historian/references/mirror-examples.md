# Mirror Hunts That Worked

The first three pours (2026-09-24). Not a capability: the reference for what a good mirror looks like.

| The Connoisseur (fears deception) | to trust pleasure | **The Rob Roy:** the famous hotel origin is the wrong one. A Hoboken bartender invented it for a scotch salesman, around 1895. ## What these have in common
- A **person** in the story (a brother, a bartender who confessed, a merchant who wrote a word down).
- A **turn**: the famous version isn't the whole truth, or the name means something unexpected.
- The turn matches the persona's **hidden fear or need**, not their surface traits.
- Each could be told in three sentences.

## Hunting grounds beyond "the classic for this spirit"
The origin story, a forgotten earlier version, a person, the name's meaning, a place, a law or ban, a gesture (stirring, a shared bowl), an ingredient's history, a glass, a mistake, a revival.
