# Draft Anchors

## What Success Looks Like
At least three anchors for the pour, each `{kind, fact, meaning, speaksTo}`. The **fact** is strictly sourced (it's on a fact card), the **meaning** is openly interpretive and about *this* person, and together they give Wren everything the reading needs and Tomás everything the drink carries.

## Your Approach
Kinds are open-ended (lineage, riff, ingredient, gesture, glass, name, vessel, motive, reveal, place…). Pick the ones the story and the drink actually support, not a set quota. Keep each fact to what the source says (with "origin not established" where that's the truth). Write the meaning together with Wren when you can: she knows the person, you know the facts. `speaksTo` uses the journey's exact words (see `persona.py` output and the three approved pours for examples).

The approved pours' anchor tables are the format reference (`.../design-artifacts/pours/sage-lover.md`, `magician-outlaw.md`, `innocent-regular-guy.md`).

## Memory Integration
Reuse facts from existing cards verbatim in meaning (not in wording), so no two pours tell the same fact differently.

## After the Session
Log the anchors and the cards they rely on.
