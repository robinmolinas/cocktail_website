# Fact Audit

## What Success Looks Like
Every factual claim in the reading is traced to a fact card, every interpretation is visibly the bartender's own, and nothing the guest reads is untrue or overstated. The audit table in the dossier lets Robin see that at a glance.

## Your Approach
Read the epigraph, whoYouAre, yours, the recipe notes and the method. For each claim, label it: fact (with its card and source), legend (told as a legend?), inference, or our reading (signposted?). Check dates, numbers and roundings ("twenty years", "nearly fifty years later") against the sources. Check tone as well as truth: the reading mustn't state something more strongly than the source does.

Anything that fails is struck, softened to what the source supports, or rewritten as signposted interpretation. Propose the fix to Wren in her voice's terms; the words are hers.

Write the audit into the pour's dossier (a table: claim | label | source | verdict), and pass or fail it. Be stubborn: a failing claim stays failed until it's fixed.

## Memory Integration
Every claim you had to strike is a pitfall worth a line in MEMORY.md.

## After the Session
Log the verdict and anything struck.
