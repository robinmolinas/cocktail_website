# Persona Read

## What Success Looks Like
A persona card that the whole room builds on, and that holds a truth about this personality its ten siblings don't share. Hester can hunt for a drink with it, Tomás can design with it, and you can write from it. The card names the one line that should land hardest, before a word of the reading exists.

## Your Approach
Start from the full profile: `python3 {project-root}/skills/dps-tools/scripts/persona.py <pairing>` (both spreadsheet sheets, `archetypes.ts`, both archetypes' fears and tone, the siblings). Read the siblings' essences, and their readings if they're authored, because distinctiveness is the point. Then think like a clinician who likes people: what does this person show, what do they protect, what are they afraid of losing, what do they do *instead of* asking for what they need?

The spreadsheet is a starting point, not the truth. Its words can be clichés, and a few names are still gendered ("The Straight Man"). Go beneath them. Its fears are the most useful field: the primary archetype's fears (e.g. Sage: deception, conflict, **ambiguity**) often decide what the drink must never do.

The card, written to the pour's room scratch folder when in the room (otherwise shown to Robin):

- **Core truth:** one sentence, specific to this personality
- **Shows / hides:** what the world sees; what they rarely let anyone see
- **Hidden fear → hidden gift:** the fear, and how it turns into what they give
- **The "real me" line to aim for:** a draft of the sentence that should make them stop
- **Truth vs. legend:** do they want the true story, the con *and* the reveal, or the kind story they know?
- **Tone:** from the BRANDING tone lines, translated into how the bartender should sound for them (funny? tender? blunt?)
- **Distinct from siblings:** 2–3 contrasts with named siblings ("unlike the Mentor, this one teaches nobody; they study pleasure for themselves")
- **Traps:** what would betray this person (e.g. "leaving anything unresolved: Sage fears ambiguity"; "cleverness: the True Friend wants warmth")
- **speaksTo hints:** which journey answers (lens, drawnToward, soughtFor, gravities, texture words, flavours, vessel) this person is likely to give, in the journey's exact words

Log the core truth and the "real me" line in the session log. If a sibling comparison revealed something about the whole family, note it for MEMORY.md.
