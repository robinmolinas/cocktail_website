# Resonance Test

## What Success Looks Like
An honest verdict you'd sign: either *ready*, with the exact line that makes this guest think "this is me", or *not yet*, with what must change. Robin's review criterion is "punchy enough, will it resonate?", so this test is his proxy, run before he ever sees the pour.

## Your Approach
Read the reading twice. First as the guest (this personality, reading on a phone, drink not in hand). Then as a sceptic who has read the other 131. Load `references/resonance-calibration.md` if you need to recalibrate what passing and failing feel like.

1. **The "this is me" line.** Quote it. No line, no pass.
2. **Horoscope check.** Mark each whoYouAre sentence *specific* or *generic* (true of almost anyone). More than one generic sentence fails.
3. **Swap test.** Compare with the authored readings of the same-family siblings (`persona.py <pairing>` lists them; read their pour files). Any sentence that would fit a sibling is flagged and rewritten.
4. **Punch check.** Would the tagline, the first line of whoYouAre, and the last line of yours stop a scroll? Say which is weakest.
5. **Rule check.** Anything the lint can't see: does the proposal assume the drink is in hand? Is a romantic reading signposted? Is anything clever for its own sake?

The verdict goes into the room record, in your voice: the line, the marks, the flags, *ready* or *not yet* and why. Be stubborn: *not yet* stands until the reading earns *ready*. Say what would change your mind.

## Memory Integration
Use BOND.md's "What Makes Robin Say 'It Lands'" as the bar. When Robin later rewrites a line you passed, note the miss in MEMORY.md: your test should have caught it.

## After the Session
Log the verdict and the "this is me" line. Track your hit rate: lines you passed that Robin kept vs. changed.
