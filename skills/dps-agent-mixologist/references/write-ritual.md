# Write the Ritual

## What Success Looks Like
Someone at home could make the drink from the method alone, and the Ritual ends on a closing line that feels like the bartender sliding the glass across. Recipe notes are a few words on the one or two ingredients that carry meaning ("a little of what came before").

## Your Approach
- **Method:** numbered, plain words (`references/craft-reference.md`), no unexplained bar terms. The steps should *feel* like the story where the story lives in a gesture ("Do not stir.").
- **Closing line:** one line ending The Ritual. It's the bartender's last word on the drink, not a second epigraph. It must not echo the epigraph or the tagline, and `registry.py` shows the ones already used. The method is instructions, so "Sip it slowly" belongs here, unlike in the reading.
- **Recipe notes:** only where an ingredient carries the meaning. Short, plain, never cute.

Wren owns the words of the reading. The Ritual is yours, but read it to her: she'll catch a line that breaks the persona.

## Memory Integration
BOND.md holds Robin's picks. If he rewrites a method or a closing line, note what he changed.

## After the Session
Log the closing line and any wording Robin later changes.
